## Strict decisions by existing RQ

<a id="a11-specificity"></a>
**A11 — narrowing already integrated; no duplicate branch.** The reduced HPCS score supplies a shared-response observation, while independent lesion-specific additions remain the hypothesis. Main additionally records the eight-patient Kim association, unresolved beyond-shared contrast (q=0.0547), and a three-donor acute-injury comparison whose sample size and design do not decide tumour specificity. Those later results prevent describing the initial 19/23 contrast as independent evaluation or treating reduced HPCS as a malignant-cell classifier. Stress, cycling, composition and annotation remain rivals. A frozen increment beyond the shared component, assessed in independent within-study contrasts and with independent identity evidence, is the discriminator.

<a id="a12-context"></a>
**A12 — conditional recipient model retained; no new mechanistic claim.** The original between-cohort heterogeneity motivates testing recipient context beyond ligand RNA. The later exploratory pilot improves AT2 held-out RMSE from 0.4271 to 0.3244 while fibroblast improvement is unstable; inspected external cohorts fail their exact validation gates. Keep this as a nominated association pending independent transport, not IL-1-specific activation. A12-S1 already contains the unassigned-source identity problem; duplicating it as a new biological state would be wrong.

<a id="a13-increment"></a>
**A13 — later evidence narrows the proposed predictor.** Initial IPF/Kim coverage failures did not imply that every fibroblast-context analysis was impossible. The subsequently amended GSE308103 definition admits 12 paired triads. Its frozen fibroblast programme worsens primary held-out RMSE from 0.2283 to 0.2373 and every eligible predeclared sensitivity, so the tested score does not improve observed predictive performance there. This weakens that particular frozen predictor; it does not precisely exclude a meaningful biological effect, fibroblast feedback, other programmes or measurements. Same-patient histology, mixture and macrophage state remain rivals. Any newly selected programme would require a declared exploratory amendment and fresh evaluation, rather than relabelling the negative pilot as support.

<a id="a14-fate"></a>
**A14 — mechanism remains proposed; no new withdrawal evidence.** Separate the duration effect from fibroblast-specific IL-1 reception. Neither overlapping RNA signatures, missing KAC labels nor whole-section averages demonstrate failed recovery, irreversibility or malignancy. Verify withdrawal and engagement, and measure viable mature yield and traced descendants under recipient-specific interventions. Residual exposure, death/replacement and epithelial-only effects remain alternatives. This is already A14's main hypothesis and should not be presented as a newly supported branch.

## Integration recommendation

Add this chronological review as provenance under A11–A14 where useful, retaining their current main wording and decision status. **No additional biological branch passes the distinctness test from this article's later analyses alone.** Their useful contribution is to narrow score specificity, receptor-context claims and the A13 predictor after its completed negative pilot. Missing states, regions, identifiers and coverage are failures to identify an estimand, not zero effects.
