### What was missing versus already covered

**Already covered:** viable input/recovery, source attribution, biological-unit
nesting, nonmonotonic directions, receptor versus movement, intact protein versus
RNA, and the absence of direct functional evidence. More generic enrichment or
another expression atlas is not required to fill these gaps. F3 already names
perturbation history and linked recovery/state RNA; the RQ plans needed to carry
that distinction into their own endpoints without making F3 mandatory.

**Now made explicit in all three plans:** choose and record longstanding genetic
loss versus acute perturbation of established Tregs, the starting tissue/state,
perturbation-to-stimulation interval, and appropriate perturbation verification.
Either can answer a bounded genotype/perturbation question; they cannot silently
stand for one another. A catalytic-action claim needs target-engagement evidence;
gene-level Ezh2 RNA alone is insufficient. Model availability and exact timing
remain unknown, not filled with invented choices. Direct chromatin-target mapping
is not a prerequisite for these output comparisons.

**Now made explicit where discordance is interpreted:** RNA, protein and response
need the same preparation IDs, assigned condition and sampling window, with
aliquot links and recovery. Measurements from different papers or time windows
cannot establish that a post-transcriptional change caused the discrepancy.
Such linkage is needed for a discordance claim, not for every otherwise qualified
standalone genotype-response estimate. Protein accumulation also depends on
volume, degradation and uptake; AREG/IGFBP4 assays do not automatically measure
secretion kinetics.

**A33 optional bridge:** if both assays qualify, split independently sourced Treg
preparations into the declared TIGIT/AREG assay and a defined conventional-T-cell
suppression assay, with common responder input and explicit assay conditions.
Estimate the genotype response in each endpoint separately. AREG responsiveness
retained with weaker suppression, the reverse, or changes in both would each
narrow the functional account. Different P values are not evidence of selective
function, and a null needs sufficient endpoint-specific precision. This would
add an Ezh2-context comparison beyond the published distinction of the functions;
it would not prove repair, same-cell coexpression, or mediation. It is an option
within A33 development, not a new registered RQ or a required extra assay.

**A34:** the immediate movement-versus-fitness comparison still stands. Homing,
retention and tissue access remain a separate later bridge; no additional spatial
analysis is justified by the reviewed results alone.

**A35:** specify the initial responding population as lineage-identified Tregs
under native-IGF exposure. Establishing a Treg source and a Treg population response
does not by itself distinguish autocrine from Treg-to-Treg paracrine action. A
conditioned-medium effect on another recipient would require its own named
recipient and comparison; it does not complete the present response question.

### Tumor extension: informative possibility, substantial overlap

A future matched-model comparison could ask which immune-effector response
accompanies Ezh2 perturbation versus local depletion, retaining residual Treg
amount and tumor-recognition context. Wang Figure 7 already tests a particular
depletion comparator; Zhang Figure 2H–J already establishes an NKG2D-ligand
boundary in its system. Their different tumor and perturbation designs cannot
be joined into a new causal estimate. This possibility stays with article-level
Wang/Zhang reasoning and does not become A33, A34 or A35.

The Bockman lead has now been followed through a [complete main-paper read](BOCKMAN_FULL_READ_20261112.md), including visual inspection of Figures 1–7. This supersedes the earlier abstract-only access state and the overly broad cDC2/CD4-dependence shorthand. In its single-tumor comparison either cDC subtype can support control; CD8 contribution changes at the untreated distal site. The supplements remain separately access-limited. The revised extension asks about a qualified EZH2 intervention, viable Treg recovery and a defined DC functional response. Local depletion, cDC2 uptake, CD4 involvement and distant control are published premises, not proposed discoveries. Wang also already has a reprogramming/depletion comparison. See the full-read note for the remaining increment, alternative explanations and stepwise decisions.
