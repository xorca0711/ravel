## Checkpoint reviews and resulting sequence

1. **Model/source review completed.** Acute and longstanding perturbations
   cannot be pooled as one exposure. Use Wang S4D as a candidate qualification
   precedent while keeping actual implementation unresolved. The existing
   constitutive-genotype comparisons remain informative within their scope.
2. **Endpoint review completed.** Check of saved Panetti Methods confirmed
   transport/shedding inhibitors; check of the Loyher manuscript confirmed
   the antibody-versus-binding distinction. These findings change assay
   qualification before any new scoring or numerical plan. They do not
   change the registered A33–A35 working hypotheses or frozen illustrations.
3. **Numerical eligibility reviewed: not qualified for the requested contrasts.**
   No assessed record supplied the relevant Ezh2-by-response endpoint and
   biological-unit crosswalk. Do not launch an unrelated atlas or bulk-CD4
   analysis to fill that absence. Public files that might support a narrower
   standalone estimate remain bounded leads, not rejected sources. Reopen one
   only if its unit keys and endpoint would change a stated decision, under
   a new exposed/prospective contract as appropriate.
4. **Plans revised.** A33 qualifies extracellular AREG separately from the
   published staining assay; A34 validates receptor detection and considers
   general movement competence before attribution; A35 separates known
   recipient competence from unresolved intact protein source. Exact next
   comparisons and informative null/opposite outcomes stay in their plans.
5. **Tumor extension reconsidered after full reading.** The remaining
   contribution is a defined EZH2 intervention/DC-functional comparison
   with viable-lineage recovery, conditionally compared with local depletion.
   It does not rediscover local depletion, CD4 involvement, cDC2 uptake or
   distal tumor control. The [extension stages](BOCKMAN_FULL_READ_20261112.md#conditional-extension-beyond-bockman)
   retain site/recognition boundaries and the uptake-versus-presentation gap.

S1–S5 and F1–F3 keep their previously recorded completed dispositions;
F3 remains a measurement specification. This follow-up completes the requested
public-source and planning review; it does not execute the unresolved A33–A35
functional studies or the tumor extension. No experiment was scheduled, no
author contact occurred and no new RQ or human acceptance decision was created.
