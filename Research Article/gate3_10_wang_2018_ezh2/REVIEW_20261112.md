## What the review supports

The published premise connects EZH2 loss with altered Treg behavior and tumor
immunity. The executable comparison here is narrower: relative RNA abundance
in four deposited deficient and five control libraries after 96 hours of
culture. The library is the observed unit; the source does not establish the
number or independence of contributing mice/pools. The analysis therefore
describes these libraries without estimating population uncertainty.

- **The source-driven amendment is appropriate.** GEO and BioSample agree on
  four fl/fl, four +/+ and one fl/+ libraries, whereas S3C labels its controls
  fl/+. The report retains this discrepancy, includes a +/+-only sensitivity,
  and does not invent the missing biological-unit or processing crosswalk.
- **The calculations match the declared method.** Review covered all four
  scripts, both amendments, the source manifest, results and three measured
  figures. A separate standard-library calculation from the saved count matrix
  matched all nine library totals and size factors, and 154 contrasts across
  the nine anchors and five context genes (pooled control, +/+-only and nine
  fixed-factor omissions per gene; absolute tolerance 1e-12 for contrasts).
  It wrote no analysis outputs. This spot check does not independently repeat
  every Hallmark calculation; the recorded execution verification is reused.
- **The unfavorable and mixed observations remain visible.** Foxp3 RNA rises;
  Il2 and Ifng have sparse counts; Ccr2 crosses zero under library omission;
  Areg does not transfer uniformly across contexts. Il10 also shows substantial
  library sensitivity in magnitude despite a stable direction.
- **The Hallmark and cross-study claims stay within their measurements.** Raw
  enrichment scores and omission ranges are descriptive, not normalized GSEA
  significance or confidence intervals. The five-gene comparison preserves the
  different source scales and prior exposure. Reciprocal Igfbp4 RNA directions
  nominate a question but establish neither secretion nor IGF mediation.

## Limits that still govern the result

The source does not separate genotype-associated within-state RNA differences
from culture recovery, population composition or processing effects. PCA
separation and stable directions across the specified sensitivities do not
resolve these alternatives. Both population changes and within-state changes
could be biologically meaningful.

Median-ratio normalization measures relative abundance and depends on its
reference assumption; library-total sensitivity is not an absolute RNA assay.
Omission analyses keep normalization factors fixed, so they assess influence
under that normalization, not robustness to every normalization refit.

The newer mouse Hallmark collection and adapted ranking prevent an exact S3B
replay. Higher cultured Foxp3 RNA does not contradict tumor lineage tracing,
and inflammatory RNA ranks do not establish secretion, lineage conversion,
actual apoptosis or tumor protection. Those functional conclusions remain
attributed to the paper's experiments. S5 completes a source-grounded synthesis,
not a numerical reproduction of flow cytometry or tumor outcomes.

## Follow-up judgment

Closing the optional human tissue branch is justified: that comparison cannot
resolve the source or recovery ambiguities in this cultured mouse contrast.
No broader expression screen is needed merely to obtain a stronger narrative.

For stronger population inference or exact reproduction, first recover the
mouse/pool and processing crosswalk, resolve the control-label discrepancy, and
obtain the authors' panel/normalization/GSEA specifications. For a new biological
study, retain the proposed linked absolute viable-recovery and cell-state
measurements, and specify whether the target is the total genotype-associated
population difference or an RNA difference within recovered states. Conditioning
on a post-perturbation state alone would not identify a direct genotype effect.
These are conditional next steps, not authorization or evidence of bench readiness.
