# Activation, mitochondrial stress and the age dependence of CD38

**Question for interpreting C2.** CD38 consumes NAD+ and supports calcium signaling during activation. The biological consequences depend on which cells are studied, their metabolic state, and whether the endpoint is immediate proliferation or durable repopulation. An RNA signature alone cannot determine which function dominates.

**When an external analysis is justified.** If the Song count reconstruction points to an age-specific stress or activation program, recover the relevant *bulk sorted* subseries of GSE229137, donor/replicate structure and autophagy-gate labels before computing an overlap. Compare within-study contrasts and report uncovered genes. A related external state can give the Song pattern a more specific interpretation. The external study did not perturb CD38 in that contrast and cannot identify its downstream mediator. If Song shows little RNA separation but a clear transplant effect, close the transcript branch and explain which donor or direct metabolic measurement would be needed.
