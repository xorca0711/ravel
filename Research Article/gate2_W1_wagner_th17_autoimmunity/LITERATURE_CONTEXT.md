## What the comparison would teach

First establish identity and source concordance. Consistent reaction-level
behavior supports a more specific measurement; discrepancies constrain method
transfer. A within-state association still needs an independent endpoint before
supporting function. Missing units, linked history or exposure measurements leave
the corresponding biological comparison held. Equivalence needs useful precision
and a justified margin; non-significance alone does not decide it.
