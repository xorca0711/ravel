## Consequential interpretation checks

1. **Program selection and treatment are statistically dependent.** Vehicle
   measurements define the programs and enter DFMO contrasts. The CDFs describe
   selected genes; they do not independently validate lineage switching. The
   existing A/B alternative definitions change 1,150/1,034 gene categories.
   Those sensitivities are finished results, not missing work. A future fixed
   cross-dataset program comparison addresses this specific dependency in the
   receiving dataset, while retaining prior outcome exposure.
2. **The model unit and its uncertainty remain conditional.** Technical runs
   were collapsed; animal labels, not genes or cells, anchor R3. A has three
   WT labels; B has four WT and three KO labels. Fixed animal effects handle
   animal offsets but do not establish all residual independence assumptions
   across that animal's cultures. Independent arithmetic verification checks
   implementation, not model adequacy or the biological sample provenance.
3. **Interaction evidence must stay endpoint-specific.** The genome-wide
   source-threshold counts are 58 Th17n and 42 iTreg interaction genes, while
   the 12 fixed display genes yield no BH-significant interaction. Both facts
   remain visible. Unadjusted model intervals and BH calls answer different
   questions. No equivalence margin exists. Selecting a favorable interaction
   gene after viewing the table would be a newly exposed exploratory step.
4. **The source mismatch is unresolved.** The reconstructed baseline PCA uses
   3,879 genes versus the paper's 3,414. Undisclosed filtering/pooling and missing
   source workbooks prevent exact numerical reproduction. Matching the count
   by tuning thresholds would not resolve that uncertainty.
5. **The assays do not measure the same outcome or time.** R1 measures
   transcriptome-derived scores in 48-hour sorted IL17A-GFP-positive cells;
   R3 measures relative bulk RNA at 68 hours. Source functional endpoints at
   120 hours do not become paired outcomes for those RNA samples. There is no
   deposited Th17p ATAC group in the qualified 12-sample catalogue. No measured
   flux, tracked fate, secretion or lung repair follows from these proxies.
6. **Source dependencies limit replication claims.** PGAM and its Treg
   follow-up reuse GSE138266; preprints and successors are one publication
   lineage. Newly checked Hu/Wu SLE recruitment periods overlap at the same
   hospital, with no cross-publication patient-ID reconciliation. This is
   possible overlap, not proof of duplicated patients. Distinct Wagner series
   are not established as physically independent validation cohorts.
7. **New source qualifications matter before transfer to lung.** The 2014
   developmental JMJD3 paper has a 2023 figure correction. A 2021 acute-injury
   study measures short-term injury/ferroptosis-associated endpoints, not
   durable AT1 maturation. Neither establishes the proposed lung competence
   discriminator. The correction and remaining method limits are recorded
   in the [source update](LITERATURE_UPDATE.md).

The review found reasons to narrow interpretation, not a demonstrated arithmetic
defect requiring a replacement run. Existing outputs, figures, contracts and
unfavorable results remain unchanged. No claim grade or governance rule changes.
