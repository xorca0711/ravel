# P05 — glycolytic relaxation as a competence feature

**Wg-P05 · Predictive companion proposed for A1 · A8/A14 companions · Novelty provisional.**
[Prior branch](../branches/P05_lung_regulatory_competence.md) · [Derivation index](README.md).

**Framing qualification:** the [merged-standard audit](FRAMING_AUDIT_2026-10-04.md)
distinguishes this directional prediction from A1's regulatory explanation. A
biochemical rate can mark competence without being a causal regulatory feature.
Whether to retain it as supporting prediction or adopt a broadened A1 scope is
unresolved; the existing canonical hypothesis and guide remain unchanged.

## Observation → gap → question

There is no new lung result in the Wagner dataset. This derivation joins its
measurement-versus-function lesson to current lung questions and primary lung
evidence. Choi already tests inflammatory glycolysis, IL-1β withdrawal and late
AT1-marker recovery. Repeating those comparisons would reproduce a precedent.

**Question:** Among lineage-defined AT2-derived transitional epithelial
preparations after a common IL-1β exposure, does early epithelial glycolytic
relaxation after verified withdrawal predict later absolute mature AT1 output
beyond starting RNA state, survival and residual input?

**New proposed feature:** the early change in epithelial glucose-to-lactate
production after withdrawal, measured biochemically rather than inferred from
a glycolysis RNA score. This is a nominated assay concept, not a locally
validated feature. Slower relaxation is hypothesized to predict less subsequent
AT1 maturation. A baseline rate and its early change must be distinguished.

## Strongest rival

The feature reports surviving cell number, baseline transition composition,
remaining IL-1 activity, or stromal metabolism/support. It may simply restate
the starting RNA maturation component. Predictive value would establish a
marker of competence, not an intrinsic causal regulator.

## Proposed tuple, unit and endpoint

Model: lineage-labeled primary mouse AT2-derived cultures entering a source-
comparable transitional state, with matched stromal context, common IL-1β
history and a withdrawal-versus-continued-input comparison. Source day-14
withdrawal and day-21 maturation provide an anchor; the early biochemical
sampling window is not yet qualified or frozen. No ODC1/JMJD3 perturbation is
nominated from the T-cell results.

Independent AT2-source animal is the biological unit; retain stromal donor and
culture block identities. An epithelial-resolved assay is essential. The
published whole-organoid ECAR assay cannot establish epithelial attribution,
and a destructive assay in sister cultures links preparations rather than
single cells. If attribution or linkage is infeasible, this proposed feature
is held rather than replaced by total lactate or an RNA score without amendment.

Primary later endpoint: absolute lineage-derived mature AT1 output relative
to the declared starting lineage input, using independently qualified mature
identity and morphology. Also retain absolute viable output and state fractions.
Marker intensity alone is insufficient. A barrier-function assay is a separate
qualification before any repair-function claim; AT1 output is not whole-lung repair.

Prediction compares the fixed baseline transition/maturation RNA information
with the added early biochemical feature. Split by independent animal and keep
all transformations within training data. A8's existing RNA component remains
unchanged as a comparator. Adding P05 does not redefine it. No linked eligible
dataset, performance margin or sample size is presently established.

## Informative outcomes and routing

| Outcome | Interpretation and action |
|---|---|
| Feature improves out-of-animal prediction after baseline and input qualification | Supports a competence marker; causal intervention is a later, separate question |
| Improvement disappears after viable amount, state or residual-input accounting | Favors the corresponding rival and limits the metabolic feature |
| Whole-culture association lacks epithelial attribution | Niche/metabolic mixture finding only; not an epithelial competence result |
| No added information with informative precision | Retain the RNA baseline for this endpoint; do not select another metabolic feature merely to recover significance |
