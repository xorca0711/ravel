# P03 — persistent function after transient polyamine restriction

**Wg-P03 · Proposed biological question · Outcome-exposed; novelty provisional.**
[Prior branch](../branches/P03_state_shift_selection.md) · [Derivation index](README.md).

## Observation → gap → question

The [local RNA reconstruction](../MODEL_RESULTS.md) shows a DFMO-associated
shift in selected Th17/Treg programs. Definitions substantially change program
membership; shared vehicle samples create selection dependence. No tracked
cell history or drug-free suppressive endpoint is present in these data.

**Question:** During source-matched Th17 polarization, does the DFMO-associated
regulatory RNA response persist as drug-independent suppressive function, or
does it resolve into a transient response or selective survival/expansion?

The proposed starting population is naive mouse CD4 cells undergoing Th17n
polarization, with Th17p as a separately analyzed context comparison. This is
a differentiation question. It must not be described as conversion of already
committed Th17 cells; a mature-cell conversion experiment would be another scope.

Wagner already rests differentiated cells between 68 and 120 hours for selected
readouts. This proposal does not claim the first delayed or rested endpoint.
That source description does not establish verified pharmacodynamic recovery
or drug-free suppressive function with resolved founder contributions.

## Hypothesis and strongest rival

The provisional hypothesis is that a subset of the Th17n response persists after
effective drug activity and the initial metabolic perturbation have resolved,
and is associated with later suppressive function. A stronger effect in Th17n
than Th17p is a context prediction to test, not an observed functional result.

The strongest rival is transient pharmacological suppression or preferential
recovery of pre-existing regulatory-prone founders. Residual DFMO effects,
continued metabolic depletion and carryover into responder cells can also mimic
durable suppression. Removing extracellular drug alone does not establish
recovery from inhibition of ODC.

## Proposed tuple, unit and endpoint

- Population/condition: mouse naive CD4-derived Th17n (TGF-β1/IL-6), compared
  separately with Th17p (IL-1β/IL-6/IL-23); source-matched vehicle/DFMO exposure,
  followed by a qualified recovery period. Cytokine and nutrient histories must
  be held or varied explicitly, not changed together without identification.
- Unit: independent animal-derived preparation; cultures, cells and founder
  lineages are nested observations. Sister cultures can link RNA and later
  output at preparation level; they do not trace the same individual cell.
- Early endpoint: fixed RNA program and separate Foxp3/IL-17 protein measures.
  The source's 68-hour RNA time is a reference, not evidence for a new schedule.
- Later primary endpoint: suppression of an independent responder-cell response
  after verified recovery, at a declared viable input and responder denominator.
  Record absolute functional-cell yield as well as per-input activity. A weak
  responder response caused by carried-over drug is not suppressive-cell function.
- Discriminator for selection: prospective founder/ancestry and viability/division
  information independent of the outcome signature. Post-treatment strata alone
  cannot identify a direct effect or demonstrate conversion.

Recovery interval, assay validity, metabolic recovery criterion, meaningful
effect and unit-level precision are unknown. No protocol or sample size is frozen.

## Informative outcomes and contribution

| Outcome | What it would teach |
|---|---|
| Persistent function after verified recovery, with broad founder contribution | Supports a durable population outcome in this context; same-cell reprogramming still needs the ancestry evidence |
| RNA/protein shift but no later suppression | Separates regulatory resemblance from functional competence |
| Response disappears after recovery | Supports reversible dependence on the perturbation |
| Function is confined to preferentially surviving founders | Supports selection rather than broad lineage redirection |
| Drug/metabolic recovery or assay carryover cannot be qualified | Durability remains unresolved; do not interpret a wash step as proof |

Puleston already addresses proliferation-independent lineage effects. Carriche
already tests spermidine-driven regulatory differentiation and an iTreg
suppression assay. Thus neither “beyond proliferation” nor “polyamines generate
suppressive cells” is the increment. The proposed distinction is **transient
DFMO during the specified polarization, verified recovery, persistent function
and founder contribution together**. [Primary locators and access limits](SOURCES_AND_GROUNDING.md).

P04 can be a mechanistic companion if this scope is retained. E1/E2 can qualify
the RNA description but cannot answer the functional question. The HIVEP1 and
other restricted-text leads remain novelty-review gaps before global promotion.
No present result establishes the provisional hypothesis.
