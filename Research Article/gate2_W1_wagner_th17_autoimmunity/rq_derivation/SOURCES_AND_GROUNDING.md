## Primary findings and consequences

| Primary source / exact locator | Inspected finding and its role in derivation |
|---|---|
| [Wagner 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.05.045), Fig. 6G/H legend and STAR Methods “Inhibitors and metabolites” | Inhibitors enter during differentiation. The legend describes resting from 68 to 120 h, and RNA cells from the Fig. 6G experiment. P03 cannot be called conversion of committed cells or the first delayed readout. Pharmacodynamic recovery and linked raw functional-unit maps are not established locally. P04 discriminates assay from time rather than denying the source's stated experimental relationship |
| [Puleston 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2021.06.007), previously audited S1J–L/S2A/S4A–B | Growth/division-independent lineage effects are already precedent. P03 needs a specified persistent functional outcome and selection discriminator, not another general claim beyond proliferation |
| [Carriche et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaci.2020.04.037), online 11 May 2020, January 2021 issue; [author-hosted full paper](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/21486/1/21486oa.pdf), Methods p. 337, Results/Discussion and Fig. E3/E4/E6 | Supplementation can favor a regulatory phenotype; E6 directly tests suppression by spermidine-exposed iTregs. This narrows P03 to the specified DFMO/Th17 polarization and recovery context. E6 is not a demonstrated test of persistent function by DFMO-exposed Th17-derived cells. The selected Results, culture/suppression methods and supplementary legends were inspected; no full reanalysis or same-sample independence audit |
| [Choi 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2020.06.020), Results Fig. 4/S6 and Fig. 7D–H; organoid and ECAR methods | Withdrawal/recovery and glycolytic intervention are already tested. ECAR is measured in processed organoid material and normalized by cell number; it is not automatically epithelial-specific or a nondestructive longitudinal assay. P05 therefore nominates epithelial-resolved early biochemical relaxation and linked later output as an assay qualification problem before prediction |
| [Yadav 2025](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188734), Fig. 3/S3C–D, Fig. 5D, Fig. 6G and discussion | Establishes ornithine supply/proline/collagen and a human peripheral-blood neutrophil–lung fibroblast coculture. Alpha-SMA is not interchangeable with collagen. The murine source and human source differ. P06 uses the human source model as a nominated context, not new discovery or proof of every IPF source |
| [Hamanaka 2025](https://doi.org/10.1042/BCJ20253033), published Results Fig. 3–6 and relevant culture/tracing discussion | Recipient arginine requirement and context-dependent precursor use are established. The proposed P06 increment is source-dependent net balance of substrate consumption/delivery within observed exposures, not arginine starvation or extracellular ornithine uptake alone. The published successor supersedes the 2024 preprint; no additional independent study is counted |
| [Lee et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s12276-024-01170-w), Results Fig. 6–7 and PRODH/ROS methods | OAT-related changes include a proposed PRODH/ROS/TGF-β signaling route. An OAT perturbation cannot be interpreted uniquely as loss of collagen precursor supply. P06 needs direct routing and alternative-signaling measurements. Targeted publisher sections/legends were read; the full supplement and raw cohort/data provenance were not audited |

The Carriche and Lee records had no correction/retraction returned by the
targeted DOI searches. That is a bounded lookup, not an assurance of absence.
Older review findings remain, including the corrected 2014 lung JMJD3 figures,
possible Hu/Wu clinical cohort overlap, PGAM's signature-sign ambiguity and
reused GSE138266. They are not independent evidence for these new hypotheses.

HIVEP1/ALDH5A1 restricted full texts remain explicit gaps. Neither regulator is
nominated here from an abstract. Their exact comparisons must be checked before
a claim of novelty or promotion of an overlapping enzyme mechanism. No source
or local result establishes the proposed P03 persistence, P04 time-resolved
dependency, P05 prediction or P06 compensation.

## Why derivation did not trigger another biological run

| Uncertainty that could change the question | What existing evidence permits | What is actually missing |
|---|---|---|
| R1 information beyond RNA and reaction aggregation | Source-specific annotation/comparator audit under a new exposed E3 contract | Qualified GPR mapping and biological preparation units; orthogonal measurements for biological claims |
| P03 persistent suppression versus a transient/selected population | E1/E2 can test robustness/transport of RNA descriptions | Pharmacodynamic recovery, independent responder function and founder/viability information |
| P04 endpoint selectivity versus delay | Direct RNA genotype interactions already executed | Linked assays at common times and qualified deletion/precision |
| P05 early biochemical predictor | Published withdrawal premise and current A1/A8/A14 baselines | Epithelial-resolved early measurements linked to later lineage-supported mature output |
| P06 net source–recipient balance | Published source/recipient premises and a concrete human model | Joint exposure range, source-specific allocation, substrate routing and collagen endpoint on independent donor units |

Additional RNA fitting cannot manufacture these missing outcomes. Deferring
such fits saves compute without relaxing evidence requirements. Any later
analysis must reference these cards, the exposed results and the review in a new
prospective contract. Numerical sample sizes, effect margins and external access
are not filled in to make the design appear ready.
