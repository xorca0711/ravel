# P06 — the balance of arginine consumption and ornithine delivery

**Wg-P06 · Proposed context/model comparison · Novelty provisional.**
[Prior branch](../branches/P06_ornithine_context.md) · [Derivation index](README.md).

## Published premises → gap → question

Yadav establishes a myeloid ARG1/ornithine–fibroblast circuit. Hamanaka shows
that extracellular arginine can support fibroblast activation without substantial
arginine catabolism by the fibroblast. These are compatible findings in different
systems: source-cell metabolism is not recipient-cell metabolism. No local
Wagner result measures either lung circuit. [Exact source locators](SOURCES_AND_GROUNDING.md).

**Question:** In human neutrophil–lung fibroblast crosstalk, does recipient
arginine sufficiency modify the net collagen response to myeloid ARG1 activity,
by balancing arginine consumption against ornithine delivery?

The specified source is human peripheral-blood neutrophils; the recipient is
primary human lung fibroblasts, initially studied within a defined TGF-β context.
This is a source-supported model nomination, not evidence that blood neutrophils
represent every IPF lung source. Healthy/IPF recipient status is a later declared
modifier, not an unbounded search for a responsive subgroup.

## Hypothesis and strongest rival

The proposed hypothesis is that reducing source ARG1 can lower ornithine-derived
collagen substrate while preserving extracellular arginine, and that the latter
partly offsets the collagen decrease when recipient arginine availability is
limiting. Where arginine is already sufficient, the ornithine-supply contribution
may dominate. Neither a crossover nor its threshold is presumed to exist.

The strongest rival is an ornithine-supply effect without biologically relevant
arginine compensation over the actual source-conditioned range. Additional
rivals are cytokine changes, altered myeloid/recipient survival, glutamine/proline
availability, general translation stress and OAT-related signaling rather than
substrate routing. Complete artificial starvation would not establish a boundary
that occurs in the nominated source–recipient system.

## Proposed tuple, unit and endpoint

Compare source ARG1-active and source-restricted contexts, while independently
varying/restoring recipient arginine and ornithine exposure within the range
actually measured in the system. Keep citrulline/glutamine and inflammatory
inputs declared and controlled. A recipient-only defined-medium comparison
qualifies the substrate logic; it cannot establish source causality by itself.
Total ARG1 inhibition of a coculture cannot be assumed source-specific.

Measure extracellular substrates across the exposure interval and distinguish
recipient incorporation into proline/collagen from polyamine pools. Primary
functional endpoint: newly produced/deposited collagen, reported both as absolute
output and relative to independently measured viable recipient exposure. Alpha-
SMA, collagen RNA, translation-stress signaling and proliferation are separate
readouts. OAT perturbation alone does not uniquely identify substrate routing.

Independent myeloid and fibroblast donors, and their pairings, define the design;
wells and isotope measurements are nested. Shared donors across pairs induce
dependence. Exact donor crossing, timing, source-specific perturbation validity,
metabolic tracing and useful effect/precision must be qualified before freezing.
No donor count, physiological concentration range or bench capability is invented.

## Informative outcomes and contribution

| Outcome | Interpretation and action |
|---|---|
| Arginine preservation offsets lost ornithine contribution within the observed exposure range | Supports the joint-balance boundary; quantify both contributions without calling a universal effect |
| Collagen tracks ornithine despite independent arginine control | Favors the supply-dominant explanation in this context |
| Arginine controls collagen but source activity never changes it enough | Recipient nutrient requirement is confirmed, but the proposed source-level compensation is unsupported |
| Effects follow cytokines, viability or signaling without route evidence | Supports the alternative; do not label the residual a metabolic-transfer mechanism |
| Only complete starvation creates the pattern | Artificial nutrient boundary; no demonstrated relevance to the qualified coculture |

This is model discrimination and a context boundary, not the discovery of ARG1,
OAT or ornithine-driven collagen. The joint comparison must still be checked
against the closest experiments before novelty promotion. The prior A3 ornithine
set remains ineligible; A9 supplies exposure-versus-response logic but retains
its AREG receptor question. P06 therefore stays article-local rather than being
silently merged into either RQ or into the Th17 mechanism.
