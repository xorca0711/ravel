# P04 — JMJD3 dependence of DFMO responses versus delayed kinetics

**Wg-P04 · Proposed source-replication/model-discrimination companion · Outcome-exposed; novelty provisional.**
[Prior branch](../branches/P04_jmjd3_endpoint_dependence.md) · [Derivation index](README.md).

**Framing qualification:** the [merged-standard audit](FRAMING_AUDIT_2026-10-04.md)
finds that the expected endpoint directions remain unspecified. The source's
aggregate Treg program cannot substitute for the nominated Foxp3 RNA endpoint.
This is a useful comparison to develop, not yet a complete directional biological
hypothesis; no source-aligned directional revision is adopted by this note.

## Observation → gap → question

The [R3 model](../MODEL_RESULTS.md) estimates direct KO-minus-WT DFMO interactions.
None of the 12 fixed display genes passes BH q ≤ .05 in either lineage's
interaction; the genome-wide source-threshold interaction sets are nevertheless
nonempty. These are different statements. Wide intervals and nonsignificance
do not establish equal genotype responses.

The source compares RNA at 68 hours with protein/secretion measurements at
120 hours. Those measurements have not been linked to the same biological
preparations locally. Endpoint and timing can therefore explain apparently
different dependencies.
The source legend describes the RNA cells as coming from the Figure 6G
experiment; the missing local unit map must not be mistaken for proof that
the authors used unrelated biological preparations.

**Question:** In Th17n differentiation, is the apparent JMJD3 dependence of
DFMO-induced regulatory output endpoint-specific, or explained by a delayed
response that becomes coherent when RNA, protein and secretion are compared
at common times?

## Hypothesis and strongest rival

The provisional hypothesis is that the genotype modifies the protein/secretory
response differently from the corresponding RNA response even at aligned times.
The strongest rival is shifted kinetics, compounded by baseline composition,
incomplete functional deletion and unequal precision. A common response may
appear selective when only one assay/time passes a significance threshold.

## Proposed tuple, unit and endpoint

Use source-comparable WT and JMJD3 conditional-deficient mouse CD4 preparations,
Th17n polarization and vehicle/DFMO. iTreg is a separate context comparator;
GSE162382 contains no Th17p group. Independent animal is the unit, with paired
cultures and linked aliquots nested. Functional deletion must be checked by an
appropriate assay; total Kdm6b RNA cannot establish it.

Nominate paired Foxp3 RNA and protein responses as the primary endpoint pair;
IL-10 secretion is an additional distinct endpoint, with viable cell exposure
and absolute output recorded. Source 68/120-hour collections anchor a proposed
common-time comparison for all assays, rather than assigning one assay to each
time. Qualification must show that these times capture the response; no dense
unbounded time scan is proposed. RNA aliquots and later cells are linked by
preparation, not asserted to be the same cell.

For each endpoint estimate `(DFMO − vehicle)KO − (DFMO − vehicle)WT` directly,
on its declared scale. Joint kinetic models, cross-endpoint scale comparisons,
multiplicity and useful precision need prospective specification. Different
q values or incomparable measurement units do not establish heterogeneity.
An endpoint-selectivity claim requires informative evidence for the relevant
responses, including adequate precision where an effect is claimed to be absent.

## Informative outcomes and contribution

| Outcome | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Distinct genotype responses persist at aligned times with qualified precision | Supports endpoint-selective dependence in this system; does not identify a direct chromatin target |
| Responses align after a time shift | Favors the kinetic rival over endpoint-selective regulation |
| Similar responses with prospectively justified equivalence precision | Weakens selectivity; current nonsignificant interactions cannot supply this conclusion |
| Deletion, specimen linkage or precision remains uncertain | Dependency remains unresolved |

Wagner already establishes the broad endpoint contrast; Li/Liu already constrain
universal JMJD3 interpretations. The increment is discriminating timing from
endpoint dependence using linked units. [Source review](SOURCES_AND_GROUNDING.md).

Proposed routing: mechanistic companion to P03 or source-reproduction/model
discrimination task. It does not need a separate global RQ unless review finds
a distinct biological contribution. Neither RNA–ATAC agreement nor this design
alone proves demethylation, binding or formal mediation. R4/R5 remain held;
no new numerical or laboratory execution is authorized by this card alone.
