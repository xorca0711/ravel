# P01 — information supplied by the reaction representation

**Wg-P01 · Proposed measurement question · Outcome-exposed.**
[Prior branch](../branches/P01_score_specificity.md) · [Derivation index](README.md).

## Observation → gap → question

The [R1 analysis](../SOURCE_SCORE_RESULTS.md) reconstructs condition-associated
reaction scores from the deposited outputs, while the source expression and
reaction metadata are locally available. They share a transcriptomic origin;
agreement with another RNA signature is not external validation. Preparation
identities and historical solver equivalence remain unresolved.

**Question:** Within source-defined Th17p versus Th17n, which reaction-score
associations retain information beyond matched enzyme/GPR RNA summaries,
technical variation and source neighbor sharing?

The decision is whether the representation justifies a reaction-specific
orthogonal measurement. It is not whether a high-scoring enzyme is causal.

## Hypothesis and strongest rival

Some reaction contrasts contain reproducible information beyond a fixed matched
RNA comparator. The strongest rival is that their apparent increment arises
from shared input expression, gene coverage, technical depth, activation or
whole-cohort smoothing. A missing preparation map also prevents separating
culture effects from cell-level separation.

## Unit, contrast and endpoint

Use the original 48-hour IL17A-GFP-positive mouse CD4 populations: Th17p
IL-1β/IL-6/IL-23 and Th17n TGF-β1/IL-6. Biological preparations would be the
independent units; currently verified cells are only descriptive observations.
Fix the gene–protein–reaction logic, direction and compartment, RNA comparator,
reaction family and discrepancy metric before a new calculation. Do not select
the best comparator or reaction after inspecting performance.

The current endpoint can be contrast/rank agreement and residual descriptive
information. Prediction requires enough independently mapped preparations,
train-only normalization/neighbor construction and held-out biological blocks.
It remains a hold with the current unit information. A biochemical endpoint is
an additional measurement, not obtainable by relabeling a score.

## Informative outcomes

| Outcome | Interpretation and action |
|---|---|
| Increment persists under a qualified comparison | Motivates a prespecified orthogonal reaction measurement; does not validate flux or pathogenicity |
| Increment disappears under matched RNA or mapping controls | Prefer the simpler representation for this endpoint; preserve the unfavorable result |
| Only whole-cohort smoothing creates the increment | Report dependence on that construction; do not call it held-out prediction |
| Units/mapping cannot be qualified | Restrict to source descriptive audit or hold the unresolved comparison |

## Prior work, contribution and readiness

[eFPA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44320-025-00090-9) already examines expression
versus network information; implementation differences limit transfer of its
Compass comparator. The contribution here is source-specific measurement
validation. [The prior audit](../PRECEDENT_REVIEW.md) supplies exact methods
locators. PGAM's unresolved signature sign precludes using that signature as a
fixed external criterion. E3 in the prior review is the bounded local next step,
after mapping qualification and a new exposed contract. No causal mechanism,
effect margin or independent sample size is nominated.
