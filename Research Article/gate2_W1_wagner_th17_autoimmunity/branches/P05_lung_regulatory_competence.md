# Wg-P05: a conditional lung regulatory-competence bridge

**Question/decision:** Within a defined alveolar transitional state, could an
early metabolic/regulatory feature improve prediction of independently measured
mature output beyond current RNA state? Decide whether the owner's bridge idea
can sharpen existing A1/A14, rather than allocate a duplicate global question.

**Published starting point:** Wagner motivates separating metabolic prediction
from orthogonal function. Choi's IL-1/glycolysis and withdrawal findings already
establish a closer lung premise. The current A1/A14 dossiers identify missing
early/later linkage and the need to separate history from residual input.
No Wagner-derived epithelial feature has been qualified.

**Hypothesis:** A nominated early metabolic/regulatory feature carries additional
information about later mature alveolar output within comparable RNA states.
**Strongest rival:** persistent input, cell loss, growth or baseline state explains
both the feature and later output.

**Tuple/unit/endpoint:** Exact epithelial population, feature, source/model,
exposure history and recovery interval remain **unspecified**. Independent
animal/donor-derived preparations must link early measurements to later absolute
lineage-supported mature output and an independently qualified functional readout.
Reuse A1/A14 endpoint/source qualification rather than duplicate their search.

**Discriminator and action:** Incremental prediction in independent units could
support a marker, not a causal regulator. Loss beyond baseline/viability/input
controls weakens it. Without linked units, stop at context review; do not substitute
pseudotime, Treg-like scores or AT1 marker expression for later function.

**Limit/hold:** Th17 evidence does not nominate ODC1 as an established alveolar
mechanism. This remains a conditional extension: feature, model, meaningful
effect, variance, timing and laboratory access require qualification. A1/A14
hypotheses and their existing holds remain unchanged.
