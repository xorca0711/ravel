## Historical branch definition

**Updated precedent boundary (4 October):** Yadav directly tests the broad
source-supply/recipient-use mechanism, including OAT and proline/collagen
readouts. Hamanaka's published 2025 successor constrains transfer to cultured
human fibroblasts. A new contribution requires a justified boundary comparison;
species or media labels alone are insufficient. [Audit](../PRECEDENT_REVIEW.md).

**Question/decision:** Which nominated lung-context boundary changes the
relationship between verified extracellular supply, recipient metabolic use
and functional response relative to Yadav's established circuit? Decide whether
a compartment-specific boundary test is justified; the exact tuple is still open.

**Published starting point:** Yadav 2025 provides the close lung circuit;
Wagner studies intracellular T-cell metabolism in a different setting. The
Niethamer W1/A3 analysis remains pseudobulk with no-hit corrected pathway tests;
A9-related RNA compatibility does not establish biochemical transmission.
No source/recipient metabolite calculation is performed here.

**Hypothesis:** A recipient-specific metabolic or functional difference remains
after its actual exposure/supply is accounted for. **Strongest rival:** altered
source abundance, supply, cell growth or general tissue damage explains it.

**Tuple/unit/endpoint:** A particular species, source/recipient pair, timing and
perturbation are not yet nominated. Qualify those first. Independent donors or
animals/preparations are units; co-culture wells and repeated assays remain
nested. Required measurements distinguish extracellular substrate abundance,
recipient uptake/routing, absolute viable recipient output and a context-valid
functional endpoint. RNA associations are a separate descriptive endpoint.

**Discriminator and action:** Recipient differences at comparable verified
exposure motivate recipient control; loss after supply accounting favors the
source explanation. Without direct supply/routing measurements, retain both.
An ODC/polyamine route cannot be inferred solely from ARG1 or ornithine RNA
compatibility, nor from spatial proximity.

**Limit/hold:** Avoid renaming Yadav's established mechanism as a new result.
Mouse macrophage and human neutrophil contexts require separate source mapping;
neither is established for every injury cohort. This is a proposed context or
compartment extension related to A3/A9, with no accepted mechanism, qualified
new cohort or confirmatory design.
