# Wg-P03: state shift versus growth and selection

**Updated precedent boundary (4 October):** Puleston S1J–L/S2A and S4A–B
already test growth and division-stratified lineage-marker changes. The residual
comparison concerns Wagner's exact drug/condition/endpoint context versus
genetic Odc/hypusine disruption. Durable suppression and tracked conversion
remain separate endpoints. [Primary-source comparison](../PRECEDENT_REVIEW.md).

**Question/decision:** In Wagner's exact DFMO conditions, which observed changes
support a context-specific response beyond altered proliferation, survival or mixture,
given that a division-independent lineage effect is already a published precedent?
Decide which additional functional measurement is needed before interpreting
Treg-like RNA as cell-state reprogramming.

**Published starting point:** Wagner Fig. 5/S5 and Fig. 6 contain perturbation,
rescue, growth/viability controls and bulk RNA. Puleston 2021 provides a close
lineage-fidelity precedent and cautions against a universal tolerogenic reading
of polyamine depletion. This package has no new biological result.

**Hypothesis:** Some state-associated changes persist within comparable viable
cell/division strata. **Strongest rival:** selective recovery of pre-existing
subsets or reduced growth produces the aggregate shift. Drug and genetic
perturbations can also have distinct consequences.

**Tuple/unit/endpoint:** Th17n and Th17p separately, control versus the exact
source perturbation, source-defined collection; independent animal/preparation
with nested cultures. Keep viable absolute yield, division-stratified protein
expression, bulk gene response and rescue endpoints separate. A denominator
change is part of the result, not a normalization nuisance to discard.

**Discriminator and action:** Concordant within-stratum changes across qualified
endpoints weaken a pure growth explanation; disappearance supports it. Bulk-only
availability permits aggregate replication and a remaining selection ambiguity.
Do not match or adjust on a treatment-induced variable and then label the
residual a direct causal effect without an identification argument.

**Limit/hold:** No lineage tracking or durable suppression endpoint is supplied
by bulk RNA. The assay-level source values/unit joins required for the full
comparison remain missing. Mechanistic novelty and a useful effect margin are
unresolved; R3 alone cannot finish this branch.
