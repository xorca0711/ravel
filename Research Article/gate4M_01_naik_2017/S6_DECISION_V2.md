## One supported next comparison

**Published finding → repository observation → remaining question.** Hamdan
reports enhanced recall in paired organoids. Naik S4/S4B show why a difference
after challenge, a baseline difference and a differential response need separate
interpretations. In Hamdan's two RNA patients, does the post-challenge I–NI
difference remain a larger within-history response after subtracting that
patient's concurrent untreated-control I–NI difference?

For a declared expression transform, define per patient:

`B = I_control − NI_control; P = I_TNF − NI_TNF`

`R_I = I_TNF − I_control; R_NI = NI_TNF − NI_control`

**`A = P − B = R_I − R_NI`.**

The endpoint is the **magnitude of the RNA contrast at one 24-hour endpoint**,
not response speed. The [S7 method](S7_HAMDAN_RESPONSE_METHOD_V1.md) specifies
the exact 16 libraries, per-patient normalization, zero semantics, all-gene
reporting and finite-library sensitivity before numerical execution. It uses
concurrent controls, not the separate baseline triplicates or patient-4-only
higher challenge. Patients 4 and 83 are reported separately. Their library
suffixes do not supply extra patients or matched culture units.

This is a justified **measurement/estimand extension in a separate human study**,
using Hamdan's own source data. It is not independent replication of Hamdan or
Naik, an exact reconstruction of STEM, a novel mechanism or a causal estimate
of inflammatory history. History and anatomical site/culture composition may
remain associated within a patient; pairing does not remove those alternatives.

### Relation to the closest published analysis

The recovered Hamdan supplement, Methods pp. 3–4 and Fig. S7 p. 18, orders four
categorical conditions: NI-control, NI-stimulated, I-control, I-stimulated. It
names STEM normalization but does not qualify the exact input transform or
membership here. A monotonic illustrative vector `[0,1,2,3]` has equal stimulation
increments and `A=0`; monotonic ordering alone is therefore a different estimand
from this interaction. This mathematical distinction does **not** show that any
actual gene has that behavior, that the authors ignored baseline, or that their
biology is wrong. Main Methods name DESeq2 while supplement Methods name
pairwise edgeR; both statements are preserved, without selecting a purported
author pipeline. S7B's patient-83 panel also repeats patient-4 condition text.

### Informative outcomes and stopping point

| Prospective outcome | What it would change |
|---|---|
| Positive post contrast with positive A and induced R_I, consistent across both declared normalizations | Supports an enhanced relative RNA response for those finite donor/gene records. Agreement between the two patients is descriptive concordance, not population confirmation. |
| Positive post contrast with zero/negative A | The post difference alone is insufficient to describe enhanced response; the concurrent baseline offset changes the interpretation on that scale. |
| Positive A with both responses negative | Describes less repression, not induction. Preserve both constituent responses. |
| Donor, normalization, zero/low-abundance or library-range sensitivity | Restricts the scope of the response description; do not select the favorable donor, method or gene subset as the answer. |
| No informative difference beyond the explicit decomposition | Retain the faithful within-source description and stop this bounded comparison. Do not add pathways, scores, peaks or another cohort solely to obtain a positive extension. |

No biological effect margin, p-value, pathway mechanism or target selection is
invented. Relative bulk expression and the `+1` transform do not identify absolute
RNA molecules, cell-state autonomy, epigenetic causation or functional repair.
