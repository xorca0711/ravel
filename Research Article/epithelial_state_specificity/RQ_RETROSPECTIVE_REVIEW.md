## Strict review of candidate branches

| Related RQ | Biological framing after review | Decision and next discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| A5 | Adult repair may recruit a developmental component while adding context-specific programmes | **Already incorporated and strengthened by a later, distinct test.** Do not add a duplicate "developmental reuse" branch. Independent cohort replication remains conditional on reconciled animal/state identities; function and shared ancestry remain separate |
| A7 | Cebpa loss may attenuate AT2 identity in both reference and transitional cells, compressing their difference | **Already incorporated; retain as conditional.** Replicated age-matched genotype-by-state effects with states defined independently of Sftpc and scored identity genes distinguish broad attenuation from a state-selective component. One-well contrasts cannot decide this |
| A8 | A maturation-specific component may add information about independently measured mature AT1 contribution beyond shared transition | **Already incorporated; retain the narrower current question.** Freeze component definitions before the outcome. Signature overlap is a measurement problem, not positive evidence for or against a maturation mechanism |
| A1 | Similar RNA may coexist with different regulatory states and responses | **Supporting measurement context only.** ES1 contains no newly linked regulation-to-fate evidence; pooled chromatin results do not become state-specific because RNA panels are now scored |
| A11 | Lesion-associated additions may accompany a shared plasticity response | **Use the later test's actual boundary.** A positive primary contrast does not satisfy the stronger beyond-shared criterion, nor does it establish neoplastic specificity without non-neoplastic comparators |

All useful contributions are already represented in the current canonical
hypotheses. The appropriate addition is a dated provenance link to this review,
not another biological branch. **No new register hypothesis is recommended from
ES1 alone.** The retained conditional candidates are the existing A7 interaction
and A8 independent-outcome tests; neither is strengthened by counting technical
seeds, overlapping gene lists or already analyzed cohorts as new replications.
