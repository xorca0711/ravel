# Ng26-P01 panel-to-source manifest

S0, 19 November 2026. “Readback” means the deposited table supports the stated readout under a future exposed contract. “Reconstruction” means a new calculation with separately specified processing. All panels reuse Nagaraja et al. Source locators below refer to the [version of record](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10258-4), PMC13083248.1 workbooks and the [versioned source inventory](sources.tsv). The inventory records the URL, cached bytes and SHA-256 for each file. Table numbers refer to individual workbooks in MOESM5.
