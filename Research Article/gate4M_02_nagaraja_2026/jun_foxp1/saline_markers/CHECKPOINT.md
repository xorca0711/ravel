For each site, the planned output shows each mouse, each arm’s mean and range, both study-specific DSS-minus-control differences in percentage points and every one-mouse omission. A shared nonzero direction surviving all six omissions in each study supports retention for marker follow-up. Magnitude and baseline variation stay visible even for stable directions. Omission-sensitive concordance is fragile; opposite signs or an exact tie weaken the shared-marker interpretation. A study-specific difference can remain informative without identifying its cause.
