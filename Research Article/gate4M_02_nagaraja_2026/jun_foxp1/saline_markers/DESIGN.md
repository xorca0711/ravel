# Do methylation differences recur at eight flanking CpGs after colitis?

## Purpose and prior exposure

This owner-requested descriptive marker screen belongs to Ng26-P01. The original studies measured methylation after different DSS regimens. The [previous qualification](../external/QUALIFICATION.md) located eight exact, measurable mm10 dyads in five of the original fragments. The remaining question is whether a DSS-history difference at the same assayed cytosine has a recurring direction in the two mouse cohorts. Either direction is informative. Each probe is reported in fixed ID order with its fragment and flank.

The site selection, strand, coverage, all prior Nagaraja results, and existence of the external beta values were known. Earlier qualification code read the targeted beta cells to test availability and range. Earlier work also accessed native methylation and regional differences. The external saline arm means, DSS-minus-water differences, directional matches and stability results were not calculated before this design. Treat it as an exposed exploratory screen.

## Specimens and source scales

- **External, GSE283386.** Three saline + water mice (GSM8661258–60) and three saline + 2.5% DSS mice (GSM8661261–63). DSS lasted five days. Isolated male C57BL/6J colonic epithelium was collected 14 days after withdrawal. Use the deposited MM285 array beta at each original probe ID. Map matrix columns by verified chip position.
- **Nagaraja, GSE316762.** Three DMSO control mice (GSM9459788–90, M7/M8/M9) and three recovered DMSO mice (GSM9459782–84, M1/M2/M4). The recovered specimen records specify day 23 after withdrawal following three 7-day DSS cycles with 10-day breaks. The GEO sample protocol describes three vehicle administrations beginning at day 20 and sacrifice 24 hours after the last. GEO also has a generic epithelial cell-type field; its sample titles and DNA extraction explicitly identify whole-colon OCT sections. Use this tissue label. The paper’s general in-vivo inhibition methods describe another duration; sample-linked records govern the clock for these six libraries.
- **Strand.** The old crosswalk fixes all eight unmasked probes, each in a distinct dyad. The array’s interrogated cytosine is the native plus-C coordinate on a plus probe or the C opposite the reference G at plus-C + 1 on a minus probe. Use the one original EM-seq track value covering that base. The five minus probes require their own strand values. The three plus probes use plus-C directly. No regional mean or dyad averaging. No individual is matched between studies.

## Frozen comparison for the next step

1. Include exactly the eight prequalified probe IDs and six distinct mice in each study. Preserve both study IDs, original sample names/arms, source fraction/beta, assayed strand and reference coordinate. Validate one finite fraction in [0,1] per study, mouse and probe. A zero is measured. If an expected value is missing or identities/coordinates disagree, report the source conflict and hold execution for review; do not substitute a site, mouse or strand.
2. At each site, independently within each study, compute **100 × (mean DSS-history fraction − mean corresponding control fraction)**. Weight the three mice equally in each arm. Positive indicates higher methylation after DSS. Show group means, individual mouse values, per-arm minima and maxima, and both within-study differences in percentage points. The studies share a direction convention, with different baselines, preparations, platforms, recovery times and independent mice. Do not pool them. Report the descriptive difference between the two estimates (external minus Nagaraja) with those limitations.
3. Call positive, negative or exactly zero using arithmetic on the deposited decimal strings as rational numbers, before any display rounding. A tie in either cohort has its own category, including ties in both. For every site omit each of the six mice in each study once; recalculate the difference with two mice in the affected arm and three in the other. Report all omissions, their minima/maxima, and whether any becomes zero or reverses sign. These ranges describe dependence on the observed mice. Baseline ranges and individual values reveal small contrasts and extreme specimens.
4. Show joint increases and joint decreases separately. A nonzero same-direction change that survives every omission in both cohorts is worth retaining **for follow-up as a recurrent marker**. Always report its actual magnitude and baseline spread. A concordant change that reaches zero or reverses under omission is fragile. Opposite signs or a one-cohort tie deprioritize a shared-direction interpretation and preserve the separate study observations. If both differences are exactly zero, record a joint tie. Even stable opposite signs suggest context dependence only; tissue, clock, assay and injury schedule vary together. Exact ties describe the deposited precision. No minimum effect, P-value cutoff or biological equivalence margin is assumed.
5. The reporting units are eight sites in five selected fragments. Original mice supply biological replication; co-located probes and repeated measurements from the same mice do not. Keep every site and its parent interval. No cohort-pooled effect, independent-sites test, gene ranking or causal/regulatory-target inference.

**Review boundary.** Step 1 verifies specimen identities, same-strand native source fractions and deposited external availability, then freezes the numerical contract. The external effect calculation and any recurrence or deprioritization label require the next owner instruction.
