# S2 · Clonal resemblance and colitis history in mouse organoids

Fos-family motif accessibility was more similar among clonemates than under global clone-label shuffling. The difference weakened when cells assigned to multiple clones were excluded. Colitis-derived lines had higher mean clone scores on average, with considerable overlap among the six mice. These results use the same organoid data as Nagaraja et al. [Published Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083248/#Fig2) · [S2 figures and source values](../FIGURES.md#s2--organoid-clones-and-the-six-mouse-lines)

## Fig. 2h · What Table 4 reproduces

The release has 4,815 scored cell–clone memberships from 4,193 cells. The tagged code groups by **clone token across all lines**, so cells assigned to multiple clones contribute to each. Each of 128 clone tokens receives equal weight in the observed median of within-clone sample variances. Each of 1,000 global shuffles preserves clone sizes. The code squares each shuffle’s median **SD**, then uses the mean and sample SD of those squared values for a two-sided normal-tail test. BH adjustment uses the finite tests in each complete 299-feature run. [Author R code](https://github.com/snagaraja13/colitis_memory/blob/v1.0.1/3_SHARE_TRACE/04_SHARE_TRACE.variance_testing.R) · [Supplementary Data 2](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13083248.1/41586_2026_10258_MOESM4_ESM.txt) · [Table 4 archive](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13083248.1/41586_2026_10258_MOESM5_ESM.zip)

Across those 280, the maximum difference in null means was .00587 in squared score units, and every difference was within 3.19 joint Monte Carlo standard errors. ## Fig. 2i · Which clones and mice carry the history difference?

The full release gives 80 control and 52 colitis **history–clone** medians. Four tokens occur in both histories. Control and colitis crossbars are −.202 and +.289. At the paper’s threshold of Fos >1.25, 2/80 control clones (2.5%) and 8/52 colitis clones (15.4%) qualify. The unpaired clone-level Welch P is .0164. Separating tokens by line gives 81 control and 54 colitis groups, with Welch P = .0956. Restricting to clean-exclusive cells gives 76 and 52 groups, with P = .146. These clone tests reuse mice within each history. **The published panel draws 74 control and 49 colitis points.** Those counts, both crossbars (−.209 and +.275), and the high fractions (2/74 = 2.7%, 6/49 = 12.2%) match the released history–clone groups with at least five memberships. ## Fig. 2j · Published RNA-program association

Nagaraja et al. report Spearman ρ = .52 between the **mean** AP-1 motif accessibility and mean P20 RNA score across clones. AP-1 correlations with the HNF4/PPAR-associated maturation programs P9 and P30 were −.36 and −.14. Table 5 provides program markers. The matched cell- or clone-level P20 scores needed to refit the association were unavailable in the qualified sources. These coefficients therefore remain author fits. They do not establish AP-1 regulation of P20 or a common independently measured cell state.
