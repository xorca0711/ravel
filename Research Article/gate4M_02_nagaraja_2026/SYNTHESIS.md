# What persists after colitis, and what do organoid relatives share?

**Final S6 synthesis · 20 November 2026 · Ng26-P01.** Recovered colonic epithelium showed a higher fraction of FOS-positive crypt cells and a higher mean Fos-family accessibility score in stem/progenitors. Organoid relatives resembled each other on that accessibility measure under the authors’ global comparison. Much of the resemblance weakened when relatives were compared with other cells from the same mouse line. The tissue and organoid studies used different specimens. Their agreement identifies a reporter worth following through a lineage, while leaving the origin and carrier of the persistent state unresolved. [Nagaraja et al., Figs. 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083248/) · [Ten figures and their source tables](FIGURES.md)

## What do clonemates share?

**Released score rows reproduce the observed dispersion.** S2 recovered all 295 defined median clone variances in Table 4 from 4,815 memberships in 4,193 physical cells. For Fos, variance was 1.864 against the authors’ global null mean of 2.158 (q = .00570). The replay used the same assignment rule with a new random stream. Hmga2 crossed the .05 boundary between the published test and the replay. Equal weighting of the three lines per history gave a +.321 Fos-score difference in mean clone medians, with one control line above two colitis lines. Fig. 2i’s plotted counts and high fractions match a five-cell minimum, but the published p = .038 remains unresolved. The clone mean Fos–P20 RNA correlation (ρ = .52) and clone/history variance components remain author fits. Matched program scores and the original model inputs would be needed to refit them. [Dispersion and history results](s2/RESULTS.md) · [Published-point audit](../../analysis/research/runs/ng26_s2_fig2i_audit_v1/panel_tests.tsv)

**Donor line changes the expected resemblance.** On the same 3,572 exclusively assigned cells in 121 clones from six mice, S5 found that the Fos variance deficit fell from 10.36% under global exchange to 4.29% under within-line exchange (empirical lower-tail p = .172). Observed variance remained below the within-line null in every mouse omission. Restricting exchange further by Hnf4g score bands gave a similar variance deficit (+.0863 in squared score units). Those bands share the ATAC assay. The RNA matrix and technical records yielded one compatible RNA candidate for 4,062/4,193 ATAC cells, but lack independently assigned differentiation labels and a fully validated pairing map. A residual lineage effect conditional on independently measured state remains unestimated. [S5 conditional estimates](s5/RESULTS.md) · [Cell pairing evidence](../../analysis/research/runs/ng26_s5_v1/rna_candidates.tsv)

**Fos was the only reporter to cross both published thresholds among 50 exact tissue–organoid matches.** Etv2, Spi1 and Spib gained accessibility in recovered tissue and Ctcf lost accessibility, with weak clonal evidence. Hnf4g and Hhex showed clonal evidence with subthreshold tissue changes. Runx1 and Tcf7l2 had clonal hits and lacked a recovered tissue test. None of the 50 passed the full within-line adjusted threshold in the Ravel extension. The Fos deficit kept its direction in 58/64 half-clone draws, 64/64 three-quarter draws and all six line omissions. These are sensitivity results within the observed donors. The probability of detecting an unknown family in a larger cohort is unestimated. [Family inventory, thresholds and sampling results](post_s5/RESULTS.md)

## Can another intestinal dataset test the same question?

**No external quantitative comparison qualified.** Arozamena et al. studied regeneration after intestinal irradiation. Its Ravel package compares the deposited LGR5- and KRT20-lineage libraries with a shared control on fixed-locus fragments and RNA. At *Tiam1*, the direction depended on promoter versus gene-body measurement. The source does not establish independent animal identities or aligned cell states for these library contrasts. Injury and recovery timing, state, motif-score definition and animal unit would all need alignment with the colitis experiment. Hamdan’s within-patient UC organoids supply human evidence for persistent regional accessibility and recall, without the traced clone and donor structure required for the Nagaraja comparison. Epidermal TF-retention and CpG/H2A.Z studies supply candidate mechanisms for colon. [Arozamena primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-79014-6) · [Arozamena library-level result](../ungated_arozamena_2026_inflammatory_memory/ACCESSIBILITY_RESULTS.md) · [Human colon and epidermal evidence](post_s5/MAINTENANCE.md)

**Closeout decision.** The available measurements support persistence of a tissue reporter and sensitivity of clonal resemblance to mouse line. The next discriminating evidence would link clone identity, independently measured state and chromatin across divisions in additional mouse-derived colonic lines from both histories. Connecting maintenance to early adenomas would additionally require a lineage or specimen link to the lesion endpoint. S6 closes the article-local package using the existing receipts.
