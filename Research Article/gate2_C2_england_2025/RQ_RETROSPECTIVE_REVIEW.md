Do not imply an IL-1 mechanism under A12 solely because several signals are present. |

## Exact suggested register additions

<a id="a2-coordinated-output"></a>
**Under A2:** “**Conditional candidate, related to the coordinated epithelial-output branch (England E-N4).** Comparable mutant epithelial states may coordinate EGFR-ligand and SPP1/DLK1 RNA outputs, rather than separate states supplying each signal. Test predefined genes within independently defined neighbourhoods and separate state abundance from within-state expression. A positive RNA result would nominate a source programme, not establish secretion, a recipient response or a common delivery mechanism. Depth and division of labour among cells remain alternatives.”

<a id="a8-maturation"></a>
**Under A8:** “**Conditional candidate, related to the incomplete-maturation branch (England E-N5).** Mutant mixed-identity cells may acquire early AT1 features without the late component associated with productive repair. The observed gate dependence makes independent state definition and biological-unit holdout essential; FU_B's in-sample calibration is not validation. Require late protein, morphology or traced mature output separate from the predictor genes. Failure after independent annotation would favour classification or context effects.”

<a id="a14-feedback"></a>
**Under A14:** “**Conditional candidate, related to the endogenous-feedback branch (England E-N7).** Persistent activity after mutant-state entry may reflect deficient feedback induction or continuing input overwhelming intact feedback. Their distinction requires comparable input, linked activity/feedback measurements and recovery after verified withdrawal. A single Nfkbia RNA value or an inhibitor-induced marker shift does not establish feedback failure or durable mature repair.”

<a id="a18-signal-interaction"></a>
**Under A18:** “**Conditional candidate, related to the SPP1/DLK1 interaction branch (England E-N8).** The source's reduced combined organoid-size response motivates interference between recipient responses, but saturation, survival and geometry remain alternatives. Judge the combination against a prespecified non-interacting expectation, with independent preparations and separate cell-number, formation and maturation outcomes. This tests signal interaction; it does not identify in-vivo range or necessity.”
