# Nb3: framing the eight annotated extension candidates

**Updated 1 October 2026.** All eight annotations are retained as biological
candidates. The first derivation developed E1/E8 into A22 and E4 into A23;
the other entries previously received only brief disposition notes. This file
supplies their hypotheses, evidence, competing explanations and discriminating
outcomes. Missing data limit what can be concluded; they do not invalidate a
biological question. These are post-analysis proposals, not preregistration or
eight independent discoveries.

The [extension review](reports/EXTENSION_REVIEW.md) owns the initial results;
the [follow-up report](reports/FOLLOWUP_RESULTS.md) owns the completed sensitivity
and pathway analyses. [Figures 1–8/S1–S3](FIGURES.md) display those results.
Unless identified as a published finding, a mechanism below is a hypothesis.
The screen supplies technical-well contrasts, with independent biological units
unresolved. Human S5 numeric reproduction remains failed; human findings below
refer to the separately checked count-derived analysis described in the
[concordance audit](reports/CONCORDANCE_AUDIT.md).

## E1 — Epithelial identity and fibroblast immune signalling

**Question.** Does NKX2-1-dependent AT2 identity maintain fibroblast chemokine
competence beyond changes in epithelial amount?

**Hypothesis and context.** An identity-dependent epithelial signal maintains
fibroblast chemokine production; loss of that input favours a wound-associated
response. NKX2-1 acts in the epithelial compartment in this model. The proposed
fibroblast effect is an intercellular response, not evidence that epithelial
NKX2-1 directly binds fibroblast genes. Chemokine-rich fibroblast populations in
the healthy human lung provide biological context, although the bronchial
CCL19/CCL21 populations in [Madissoon et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01243-4)
are different from this culture and its marker panel.

**Evidence and rival.** NKX21 lowers the AT2 and fibroblast chemokine panels
and raises wound markers through reference, marker and well omissions. All
seven chemokine marker contrasts are negative. The paired cross-target
AT2–chemokine association is modest (rho 0.210, or 0.151 with depth covariates).
IFN and TNF/NF-kB context is correlation-sensitive; no mediator is selected.
Changed cell composition, epithelial amount or shared culture stress could
produce the same bulk pattern.

**Discriminating outcome.** An identity-associated change in fibroblast
chemokine secretion and recipient immune-cell response that remains after
epithelial/fibroblast amounts and stress are independently measured would
support the hypothesis. RNA reduction alone cannot establish reduced immune
activation or recruitment. **Disposition: proposed [A22](../../RQ_Specified/A22_epithelial_identity_niche_response/README.md).**

## E2 — Transitional state and capacity to complete alveolar repair

**Question.** Do IFN-dominant and hypoxia-dominant Krt8-associated programmes
differ in their capacity to resolve into mature AT1 cells?

**Hypothesis and context.** Krt8-associated transitions contain different
maturation capacities; programme persistence, rather than Krt8 positivity
alone, predicts failure to resolve. Published work places transitional states
in both repair and fibrosis ([Strunz et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17358-3);
[Kobayashi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-020-0542-8)). Neither reference
establishes that the two Nb3 bulk patterns are those exact cell states.

**Evidence and rival.** CSNK2A1 has an IFN-dominant pattern and BECN1 a
hypoxia-dominant pattern in the fixed panels. This demonstrates perturbation
response differences, not two trajectories. The rival is one repair sequence
sampled at different times or with different proportions of cells and stress.
The data do not assign an IFN branch as productive or a hypoxia branch as
irreversibly arrested.

**Discriminating outcome.** Different mature AT1 contribution or recovery
after comparable initial state, injury burden and observation time would
support distinct maturation competence. A common temporal path with similar
eventual outcomes would favour the rival. **Disposition: distinct candidate
within existing [A1](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a1)/[A8](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a8)
state-versus-maturation questions; no additional global ID assigned.**

## E3 — Lipid and organelle homeostasis in AT2 renewal and maturation

**Question.** Do ELOVL1-dependent lipid composition and ATP6V0E-dependent
organelle acidification couple AT2 renewal to differentiation competence?

**Hypothesis and context.** Two separate homeostatic processes could change
receptor processing or epithelial differentiation competence and thereby alter
the balance between expansion and maturation. ELOVL1 supports C24 sphingolipid
synthesis ([Ohno et al., 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20937905/)); the
human ATP6V0E1 gene encodes a V-ATPase component involved in organelle
acidification ([NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/8992/)). The source
target label **ATP6V0E** is retained here; gene identifiers in saved analyses
are not being renamed. Neither lipid composition nor organelle pH was measured
in Nb3. The receptor-processing connection is a proposed mechanism.

**Evidence and rival.** Both perturbations have lower AT1-marker scores that
retain direction under local sensitivities. Their Wnt-panel intervals include
zero; even the CTNNB1 activating comparator fails the broad Hallmark Wnt
readout. Therefore neither Wnt activation nor Wnt independence is established.
Elovl1 RNA does not decrease, and RNA alone does not establish editing.
Alternatives include altered Wnt signalling, general fitness effects or a
different mixture of epithelial states.

**Discriminating outcome.** Validated functional perturbation, the appropriate
lipid or acidification phenotype, and an effect on renewal/mature AT1 output
beyond survival would support a homeostatic mechanism. A calibrated Wnt
activity readout is needed to decide whether that mechanism converges on Wnt.
**Disposition: two mechanistic arms of a retained candidate, related to
[A4](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a4)/[A8](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a8); not a
validated Wnt-independent pathway.**

## E4 — SLC34A2, phosphate homeostasis and transition entry

**Question.** Does SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis constrain entry
into a transition-associated state before broad AT2 identity collapse?

**Hypothesis and context.** Transport disruption could initiate epithelial
homeostatic stress and a transitional programme before secondary mineral or
inflammatory injury. Npt2b/Slc34a2 loss causes alveolar microlithiasis in the
published mouse model ([Saito et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aac8577));
this supplies a phosphate-homeostasis context, not proof of the proposed
phosphate-to-state sequence in this screen.

**Evidence and rival.** Krt8, Sprr1a and Clu all increase; the transition-panel
effect is +0.524 and retains direction under the tested omissions. AT2 identity
attenuation is smaller than with NKX21, but cannot be called absent. Bulk
composition, general stress or secondary microlith/inflammatory injury are
alternative explanations. Krt8 positivity does not establish a fibrotic fate.

**Discriminating outcome.** A transport-linked, within-state transition
response preceding secondary injury, with restoration of homeostasis tracking
state recovery, would support the hypothesis. A signal restricted to changed
cell proportions or following secondary injury would weaken it.
**Disposition: proposed [A23](../../RQ_Specified/A23_slc34a2_transition_homeostasis/README.md).**

## E5 — ERBB2/ERBB3 dependence in normal AT2 cells and cancer

**Later review and execution:** the [external E5 report](reports/E5_EXTERNAL_FEASIBILITY.md)
specifies a receptor/context epithelial-function hypothesis and a 14-library
descriptive pilot. ERBB3 and EGFR show different inflammatory-marker responses
under irradiation; sparse AT2-marker coverage prevents a normal AT2 renewal
conclusion. This adds a context-dependent model to the shared-dependency
hypothesis below. ERBB2 and the cancer/clinical branches remain separate.

**Question.** Is an ERBB2/ERBB3 requirement for normal AT2 renewal retained
in lineage-supported or AT2-like lung cancers, and could loss of normal-cell
function contribute to treatment-associated alveolar injury?

**Hypothesis and context.** A shared receptor requirement could make normal
alveolar renewal vulnerable while supporting some cancers. This contains
three separate claims: normal AT2 function, cancer dependency and a mechanism
of clinical injury. They require separate evidence. Dependency does not prove
a cancer's cell of origin. Clinical ILD reported with the HER2-directed
antibody–drug conjugate trastuzumab deruxtecan provides drug-specific context
([Li et al.](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2112431)); it does not
establish on-target loss of AT2 renewal, equate all ILD with fibrosis, or
generalize to HER3 or all HER2/HER3 therapies.

**Evidence and rival.** ERBB2/ERBB3 screen perturbations reduce organoid
coverage. This does not distinguish survival, proliferation, differentiation
or non-epithelial effects. The source DepMap release and line denominator
remain unresolved, so no independently reproduced cancer comparison is
claimed. Tumour-specific addiction and drug payload/context effects could
explain cancer sensitivity and clinical injury without a shared normal-cell
mechanism.

**Discriminating outcome.** A matched normal-cell functional requirement and
a lineage-context cancer dependency would support the first two links. The
clinical mechanism would still need independent evidence separating receptor
inhibition from drug-specific injury. **Disposition: retained receptor-function
candidate with a separate translational branch; not absorbed into A22/A23.**

## E6 — Which compartment mediates the EGF requirement?

**Question.** Does EGF support alveolospheres through epithelial EGFR,
fibroblast EGFR or a serial interaction between the compartments?

**Hypothesis and context.** Direct epithelial trophic signalling and an
indirect fibroblast-mediated response are competing, potentially coexisting
models. EGF withdrawal from a mixed culture cannot identify its essential
recipient.

**Evidence and rival.** Both compartments express EGFR, while the epithelial
EGFR screen estimate for coverage is near zero. Incomplete functional
perturbation evidence prevents calling epithelial EGFR dispensable. Expression
cannot establish either compartment's requirement; separate species-specific
CPM normalizations also do not establish a quantitative receptor ratio.

**Discriminating outcome.** A compartment-resolved functional response with
validated receptor perturbation would distinguish direct epithelial dependence,
indirect niche support and joint requirement. **Disposition: distinct epithelial
requirement question, related to [A9](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a9)'s fibroblast
AREG-recipient context; EGF and AREG are not interchangeable interventions.**

## E7 — Local epithelial growth and fibroblast heterogeneity

**Question.** Do locally expanding or identity-altered AT2 cells induce
fibroblast states, or preferentially retain and expand pre-existing states?

**Hypothesis and context.** Local epithelial signals may change the state of
nearby fibroblasts, producing distance-dependent response variation. Induction
within a fibroblast population is distinct from selecting its abundance.

**Evidence and rival.** Paired bulk wells support epithelial–fibroblast
response associations but resolve neither heterogeneity nor spatial proximity.
Fibroblast composition, matrix variation and shared injury exposure are rivals.
The spatial treatment/animal/grid metadata remain unreconciled; a local
paracrine gradient has not been demonstrated in the reproduction.

**Discriminating outcome.** A within-state fibroblast response associated with
epithelial state and distance, after accounting for cell abundance and shared
sample context, would support induction. A change restricted to proportions
would favour selection. Distance association alone would still not identify a
causal ligand. **Disposition: spatial/single-cell extension of A22, related to
[A13](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a13), with the communication direction reversed.**

## E8 — Lung fibroblast identity and the immunotherapy bridge

**Question.** Does epithelial NKX2-1 identity maintain a lung-associated
fibroblast programme that contributes to immune recruitment, and is that
relationship retained in NKX2-1-low lung cancer?

**Hypothesis and context.** Epithelial identity may maintain a stromal programme
that includes, but is broader than, chemokine competence. The chain is
epithelial identity → fibroblast programme → immune-cell recruitment → tumour
treatment response. Each arrow is a separate biological claim. The proposed
route from epithelial NKX2-1 to fibroblast transcription is indirect signalling;
a fibroblast-intrinsic NKX2-1 mechanism has not been demonstrated here.

**Evidence and rival.** Current support overlaps E1: chemokine/wound changes
and a lower PLIN2 sentinel. One sentinel cannot establish lung specificity.
No clinical outcome was analysed, and the notes' NKX2-1/immunotherapy association
was not independently evaluated in this reproduction. Tumour genotype, general
dedifferentiation, fibroblast composition and direct tumour–immune effects could
produce the clinical association without the proposed fibroblast route.

**Discriminating outcome.** A coherent programme distinguished from comparable
non-lung fibroblast states would address specificity; secretion and recruitment
outcomes would address immune function. A separate tumour cohort with relevant
genotype, treatment and immune context would be needed for the clinical branch.
**Disposition: the chemokine-response core shares A22; tissue specificity and
the cancer/immunotherapy bridge remain explicit, unresolved branches.**

## Allocation and next interpretation

A22 and A23 were prioritized because their local RNA patterns survived the
completed sensitivity checks. This is evidence readiness, not a claim that
they have greater biological importance than E2/E3/E5/E6/E7/E8. E2 and E3 already
have usable descriptive signals for further hypothesis refinement; E5/E6 need
functional recipient/dependency evidence; E7 and E8 need additional spatial,
tissue or tumour contexts. More re-scoring of the same bulk wells cannot resolve
these missing outcomes.
