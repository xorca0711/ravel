# Nb3: biological question derivation

**Proposed 1 October 2026 after the completed follow-up.** The owner requested
hypothesis-based RQs grounded in analysis or established primary research.
These use the register's hypothesis/context → evidence → prediction/rival →
decisive outcome → readiness structure. They are post-analysis proposals,
pending the owner's retain/reject decision, and add no claim grade.

## Questions carried forward

| ID | Biological hypothesis and context | Evidence that motivates it | What would distinguish it from a rival |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A22](../../../RQ_Specified/A22_epithelial_identity_niche_response/README.md) | NKX2-1-dependent epithelial identity maintains fibroblast chemokine competence; loss favours a wound-associated response beyond a simple change in epithelial amount | NKX21 has lower AT2/chemokine and higher wound RNA through local sensitivity checks; the global AT2–chemokine relationship remains modest | Independent preparations with measured epithelial amount/state, fibroblast abundance, secreted chemokines and recipient response; distinguish identity-dependent communication from composition/shared culture stress |
| [A23](../../../RQ_Specified/A23_slc34a2_transition_homeostasis/README.md) | SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis constrains an epithelial transition-associated stress response before broad lineage collapse | All three transition markers increase; gene/well/control omissions retain direction; identity effects are much smaller than NKX21 and cannot be declared absent | Time-resolved within-state measurements, phosphate/transport readouts and independent perturbation/restoration evidence; distinguish homeostatic stress from cell-mixture shifts and secondary microlith/inflammatory injury |

A22's functional direction is a hypothesis inferred from marker data. Healthy
human lung spatial profiling identifies chemokine-rich fibroblasts near immune
structures, supplying context for a stromal immune-response question; those
bronchial CCL19/CCL21 populations are not the same culture population or marker
panel studied here ([Madissoon et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01243-4)).

A23 connects two established observations without claiming their causal link:
epithelial Npt2b/Slc34a2 loss can produce pulmonary alveolar microlithiasis
([Saito et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aac8577));
Krt8-positive transitional states occur during repair and can persist in fibrotic
lungs ([Strunz et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17358-3)).
The proposed phosphate-to-state relationship remains unmeasured in Nb3.

## Where each annotated extension belongs

All eight now have explicit hypothesis/context, evidence, rival and
discriminating-outcome cards in [Candidate hypotheses](../CANDIDATE_HYPOTHESES.md).
The table below records allocation, not rejection of candidates whose decisive
data are missing. A22/A23 replace the initial Nb3 A19/A20 labels because remote
main already contains Nb2's A19–A21; the
[correction record](RQ_ID_CORRECTION_2026-10-01.json) preserves that check.

| Original extension | Disposition after analysis | Why |
|---|---|---|
| E1: NKX2.1 and fibroblast immune pathways | A22, priority 1 | The RNA phenotype has local support; causal pathway and recipient function remain open. Hallmark significance is too correlation-sensitive to name a mediator |
| E2: two transitional routes | Existing A1/A8 context; no new ID | Bulk stress-marker divergence does not establish trajectories or productive versus arrested fate |
| E3: ELOVL1/ATP6V0E | Conditional candidate linked to A4/A8 | Reduced AT1-marker phenotype warrants follow-up, but broad Wnt intervals, failed Hallmark positive-control calibration and incomplete functional editing evidence prevent a Wnt-independent mechanism claim |
| E4: SLC34A2 and identity | A23, priority 2 | Coherent transition-marker induction is distinct from the broad NKX21 pattern; phosphate mediation and temporal order require testing |
| E5: HER2/HER3 and cancer dependency | Distinct epithelial-function candidate; later external pilot completed | [GSE306184 pilot](E5_EXTERNAL_FEASIBILITY.md) nominates receptor/context-dependent inflammatory RNA; normal AT2 function, source DepMap cohort and clinical causality remain unresolved |
| E6: EGFR recipient requirement | Related to A9 recipient context, with epithelial requirement kept separate | Both compartments express EGFR; the screen does not resolve compartment-specific necessity or dispensability |
| E7: fibroblast heterogeneity/local paracrine signal | Related to A13; conditional spatial/single-cell branch | Bulk contrasts cannot resolve cell-state proportions or local distances; spatial metadata gates are still unmet |
| E8: lung-specific fibroblast programme and immunotherapy bridge | Chemokine-response core shares A22; broader branches retained | PLIN2 is one sentinel; tissue specificity, immune recruitment and tumour treatment response each require separate evidence |

This avoids counting E1 and E8 as independent discoveries and avoids duplicating
A10's growth prediction or A2/A15's AREG/integrin questions. A22 reverses the
direction of A13's existing fibroblast-to-epithelial prediction question; A23
nominates a specific homeostatic cause rather than relabelling A1's general
state-discrimination question.
