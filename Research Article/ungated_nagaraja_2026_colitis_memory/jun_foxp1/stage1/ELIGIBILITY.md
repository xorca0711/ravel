## Late native accessibility by mouse

Both original BGZF exports were read in full. The extended export contains 135,120,041 unique native fragments and the main export 435,283,259. The IGVF analysis records listed no fragment index and the tested sibling TBI URLs returned 404. Full-file counts supply the library denominator. The working view uses uniquely assigned barcodes with ≥300 autosomal unique fragments. Native overlap counts can be scaled per million such fragments in the mouse. The ≥1 and ≥500 views retain the same late donor counts. The paper sorted only EPCAM-positive cells at 50 days and later. The late estimates therefore describe the sampled epithelium across states. Independent stem/progenitor labels, author singlet calls and the original fitted chromVAR scores remain unavailable. **Why the 79-day control is held.** Two technical pool records share one barcode map and the same three nominal specimens. Both list DSS39 control m10 (IGVFSM0940ZCKQ). The map assigns its control barcodes to DSS46 control m10 (IGVFSM5944AGZN), a different donor also listed at day 50. This conflict affects 24,634 raw barcodes in the two 79-day adapters, including 5,970 at the working depth floor with 9,157,490 autosomal fragments. The 50-day mouse uses its own pool-supported fragments only. Live IGVF records and the release MD5 confirmed the discrepancy. A corrected specimen-to-barcode crosswalk is required to identify the 79-day control donor. **Export suffixes.** The main export has 10,222 literal `-2` barcodes. Original barcode FASTQ headers independently identify the P1_62-2 library. Of these, 10,029 suffix-only barcodes resolve to a unique specimen and enter the eligible pool before depth filtering. A further 96 identify a specimen but share a core barcode with another token, and 97 lack an exact map row. The extended export contains no suffixed identifiers.
