# S5 · Fos-family resemblance within mouse-derived organoid lines

**Restricting cell exchange to the mouse line reduced the Fos-family variance deficit from 10.36% to 4.29%.** The remaining estimate was negative in every leave-one-line-out comparison but lay within the conditional null distribution. The original prediction about independently annotated differentiation states remains unresolved. Each of 121 line-qualified clones had at least two eligible cells. Each physical cell had exactly one assignment in the full cleaned map and occupied exactly one clone slot in every shuffle. The observed statistic is the median within-clone sample variance. Each of 5,000 null draws squares the median within-clone SD, following the archived statistic. Mouse-line restriction reduced the absolute deficit by 61%. All six lines permitted exchange, with at least eight clones per line and 960,341 unordered pairs of cells from different clones within the same line. The 18 line-by-Hnf4g tertiles each contained at least five clones and 99 cells. All three null means had Monte Carlo SE ≤.0013. The reported intervals describe shuffles of these cells. **Membership, size and line influence.** S2’s global deficit was −.2928 after qualifying all memberships by line and −.2189 after clean exclusivity. Seven line-qualified groups lost all their cells under exclusivity. Requiring at least five clean cells kept 3,509 cells in 101 clones. The within-line deficit was then −.0756 (3.74% below null). At the two-cell minimum, omitting each mouse in turn gave within-line deficits from −.1294 to −.0661. Thus, the sign survives removal of any one mouse, while the magnitude depends on the reference population. Adding Hnf4g bands barely changed the full estimate. Hnf4g comes from the same ATAC assay and may itself be shared among relatives; it cannot establish whether differentiation accounts for the remaining resemblance. **RNA linkage improved, but state labels remain missing.** The actual organoid HDF5 matrix contains 77,229 barcodes and raw counts including *Car1*. The eight measurement sets are related as a multiome and use the same multiplexed specimen. Four pairs share technical library basenames. We inspected the first 65,536 compressed bytes of each technical barcode file and matched R1/R2/R3, compatible pool adapters and independently mapped specimen. Among 4,193 scored ATAC cells, 4,062 had one RNA candidate, 92 had two and 39 had none. In the 8k pool (ATAC P1 58, RNA P1 53), 223 of 224 had a unique same-specimen candidate, including 187 primary-analysis cells across all six lines. In the three multi-adapter pools, same-specimen matches favored particular adapter pairs but also crossed pairs. No full ATAC barcode matched an RNA barcode literally. The matrix has no deposited state-label field, and the tagged clustering notebooks refer to external assignments. Neither a compatible barcode nor a single RNA marker establishes an independently assigned differentiation state. The published mixed model estimates variance components for clone and colitis history. S5 instead asks how much resemblance survives exchanging physical cells within each donor line while preserving clone sizes. The results support a substantial contribution from between-line structure. They give limited evidence for an additional within-line effect and do not distinguish state, genotype, shared culture or inheritance.
