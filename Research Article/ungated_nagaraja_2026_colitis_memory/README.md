# What persists in the colonic epithelium after colitis resolves?

Nagaraja et al. followed mouse colonic epithelial cells from injury into recovery, then asked whether descendants of individual organoid cells share a chromatin state and how prior colitis changes early adenoma growth. These are three distinct comparisons. The chromatin question for this package is whether a subset of recovering stem/progenitor cells retains unusually high accessibility at AP-1 motifs, and how much of that pattern can be attributed to which epithelial states and clones were sampled. [Nagaraja et al., Nature (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10258-4)

| Evidence | What it can resolve |
|---|---|
| Mouse tissue, Fig. 1 | Frequency of AP-1-high stem/progenitor cells across mice and recovery times, alongside FOS protein and RNA measurements |
| Lineage-barcoded organoids, Fig. 2 | Similarity of motif accessibility among descendants in the same culture and its association with a regenerative RNA program |
| Binding and methylation assays, Figs. 2–3 | Which sequences can support AP-1/FOX binding and which regions change methylation alongside accessibility |
| Early mouse adenomas after *Apc* loss, Fig. 4 | Lesion size and regenerative program expression after colitis, with lesion number assessed separately |

The earlier Naik package measured a persistent difference between sets of distal regions in pooled mouse epidermis. This paper offers a way to examine heterogeneity among individual cells and relatives in mouse colon. Arozamena provides a closer intestinal repair comparison. The three papers have different tissues, designs and endpoints. [Naik mouse skin results](../gate4M_01_naik_2017/distal_m24_baseline_v1/RESULTS_V1.md) · [Arozamena intestinal memory](../ungated_arozamena_2026_inflammatory_memory/README.md)

**S1 result, 19 November 2026.** In the three mice sampled 21 days after final DSS withdrawal, 16.42% of CD44-positive crypt cells stained for FOS on average, versus 0% in three controls. The deposited mean Fos-family motif delta was +0.986 in recovered stem/progenitor cells. Only 104 of 260 genes upregulated in the released injury comparisons had an adjusted P value at early recovery. The original individual-cell threshold fraction and extended chromatin curve remain unresolved.
