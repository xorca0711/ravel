# What persists in the colonic epithelium after colitis resolves?

**Final S6 synthesis, 20 November 2026.** After DSS withdrawal, recovered mice had more FOS-positive CD44-positive crypt cells than controls. The released tissue fit also showed higher mean Fos-family accessibility in stem/progenitors. In separate organoid cultures, related cells resembled each other under the published global shuffle. Much of that resemblance weakened when cells were compared within their mouse line. [Read the final synthesis](SYNTHESIS.md) · [Ten figures and their source tables](FIGURES.md) · [Nagaraja et al., Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10258-4)

| Biological question | What the available evidence supports |
|---|---|
| How much persisted during tissue recovery? | FOS staining was 16.42 percentage points higher in early recovery (3 mice per group). The authors’ mean stem/progenitor Fos-motif difference was +0.986. Original per-mouse high-ATAC fractions remain unresolved. RNA test coverage changed substantially by stage. [Protein counts, Table 3 fit and RNA missingness](s1/RESULTS.md) |
| How much did organoid relatives resemble each other? | The score-row replay recovered all defined observed Table 4 variances. With exclusive clone membership, the Fos variance deficit fell from 10.36% under global exchange to 4.29% within mouse line. Independent differentiation-state adjustment remains unresolved. [Clone replay and history comparison](s2/RESULTS.md) · [Within-line estimates and RNA linkage](s5/RESULTS.md) |
| Which families supported both comparisons? | Fos alone passed both published thresholds across 50 exact matches. Its smaller within-line deficit persisted in direction in all six donor omissions. No reporter passed the full within-line adjusted threshold. [Family evidence and clone sampling](post_s5/RESULTS.md) |
| What could carry memory through recovery? | After five days of AP-1 inhibition, whole-colon methylation moved toward control by different amounts at *Dcaf5* and *Rbm27*. Binding, drug-on EdU and washout diameter each measure another part of the response. The molecular carrier remains unknown. [Locus and drug results](s3/RESULTS.md) · [Maintenance assessment](post_s5/MAINTENANCE.md) |
| What changed after tumour induction? | The within-mouse fraction of gross lesions over 1 mm rose from 31.69% to 59.07%, with similar gross counts. A separate microscopic cohort had higher tumour-area burden. Spatial P20 is a plot-derived RNA comparison in one section per history. [Mouse, lesion and section evidence](s4/RESULTS.md) |

The closest external intestinal studies provide biological context. Arozamena’s irradiated-intestine library contrasts lack the common recovery stage, cell state, feature definition and verified animal units needed for a quantitative comparison. The earlier Naik study concerns epidermis. **JUN–FOXP1 extension and external closeout.** All 63 sequences gained raw footprint signal with FOXP1, but the magnitude barely tracked the day-102 accessibility difference (partial rank ρ = −0.015). Day 50 was inverse (−0.370) and weaker on the fold scale (−0.121). The independent Yin libraries support an isolated-factor comparison for a shorter CpG–AP1 context. The independent colon array measures eight exact flanking CpGs in five fragments at day 14. Neither study tests late paired-factor occupancy or methylation-dependent maintenance.
