# Source and unit notes for S3

- **EdU and organoid diameter.** Fig. 2l in the source workbook has one percentage per labelled organoid-line replicate within arm. The denominator is DAPI-positive cells. Fig. 2n has one diameter per organoid and a group label. Its 311 rows omit the well and line identifiers. The caption describes eight to nine wells per group. [Source Data Fig. 2](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13083248.1/41586_2026_10258_MOESM8_ESM.xlsx) · [Paper, Fig. 2 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083248/#Fig2)
- **Measured footprint scores.** Table 6 has one row per coordinate, motif class and library. All 63 intervals match the motif start/end keys in the tagged region list. The numerator is the footprint score in the defined-protein condition, and the denominator is the score in the JUN+FOS library at that locus. The two deposited no-TF libraries are retained individually. Their locus-wise average is an explicit check of the summary sheet’s “Control” convention. The source library inventory identifies each named protein condition once. [Table 6 archive](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13083248.1/41586_2026_10258_MOESM5_ESM.zip) · [Tagged regions](https://github.com/snagaraja13/colitis_memory/blob/v1.0.1/7_inVitroFootprinting/Regions.csv) · [GSE316763](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE316763)
- **Regional methylation.** For each bigWig, UCSC `bigWigToBedGraph` extracted unzoomed intervals with `-chrom`, `-start` and `-end`. The arm64 executable SHA-256 was `8eeab2f2ae48e321ff5db8e0654ab6e97718902bfa8c89ff0c0f4446f46bad60`. Coordinates are mm10, zero-based and end-exclusive. The Thbs1 window interprets the rounded Mb labels on Fig. 2p. The two tissue windows follow the printed base coordinates on Extended Data Fig. 6e. The source tables preserve GEO URLs, accession and per-slice hashes. A one-base flank on each side of the reference window permits checking a CpG at a boundary. Uppercase bases were retrieved from the UCSC sequence API. Only reference-plus cytosines in CG dyads enter the regional summaries, one value per site. Fractions are kept at track-export precision. Missing signal and a measured zero remain distinct. These per-position fractions contain no numerator or coverage, so read weighting and cross-strand count pooling are unavailable. [UCSC reader](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/admin/exe/linux.aarch64/bigWigToBedGraph) · [UCSC sequence API](https://genome.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/help/api.html) · [Organoid GSE316761](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE316761) · [Whole-colon GSE316762](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE316762)
- **Fig. 2o.** The authors filtered methylation within ±500 bp of ATAC peak summits for a maximum of at least 10% and an SD of at least .05. They compared the retained 58,454 regions to line-pseudobulk ATAC fold changes using mean RPM by history. IGVFDS5536ZQMO supplies a pooled ATAC fragment file and RNA matrix. GSE316761 supplies six P4 methylation fraction tracks labelled M1–3 and M7–9. The mouse labels agree with the organoid line names; they do not establish identical aliquots or passages for both assays. The retrieved references do not provide the fitted 58,454-row joint table, per-library methylated/coverage counts at its exact windows or the corresponding author per-line RPM matrix. Fig. 2p displays history-level ATAC tracks and per-replicate methylation. A three-kilobase methylation readback cannot supply the missing genome-wide coefficient or a paired within-mouse effect. [Organoid ATAC/RNA analysis set](https://data.igvf.org/analysis-sets/IGVFDS5536ZQMO/) · [Public peak and motif references](https://github.com/snagaraja13/colitis_memory/tree/v1.0.1/ref) · [Published Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083248/)
- **Extended Data Fig. 6d.** The tagged script requires a peak-by-sample methylation matrix, the aligned motif annotation, treatment metadata and a precomputed chromVAR background-index matrix. It ranks variable Fos-annotated peaks and uses the unique background rows selected for those targets. The release contains the tissue peak reference and motif matches. The precise ordered set of 500 selected peaks, its 36,367 matched controls, the saved background indices and read-count/coverage inputs for recreating the original peak matrix were unavailable in the inspected release. The GEO `RAW.tar` here packages per-mouse supplementary fraction bigWigs. The paper describes 100 background matches per selected AP-1 peak before deduplication. The same-locus readback therefore leaves the paper’s set-level Wilcoxon tests as published estimates.
