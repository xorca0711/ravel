Extended Data Fig. 6c–d examines five days of inhibition in recovery. The authors report greater methylation loss at 500 selected AP-1 peaks than at 36,367 matched background peaks in recovered versus control vehicle mice (P = 2.71×10⁻⁴⁷). Within recovered mice, the inhibitor-versus-vehicle change did not distinguish the two peak sets. The tagged code and reference annotations omit the exact selected IDs and matched-background matrix. The genome-wide comparison remains author-reported. At the two illustrated windows, *Dcaf5* retained much of the between-arm difference, whereas *Rbm27* approached the control-vehicle mean. This supports incomplete restoration during the tested window. Tissue composition and the molecular carrier remain unresolved. These mice provide no methylation-to-tumour mediation estimate.
