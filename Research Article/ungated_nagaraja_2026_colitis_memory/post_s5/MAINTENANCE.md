# What could maintain the recovered colonic state?

After five days of T-5224 in recovered mice, previously inflamed whole colon still showed hypomethylation at some illustrated AP-1 regions. The extent depended on the locus. This constrains what reversed during the treatment window. The experiment does not identify the molecular carrier passed through epithelial divisions. [Nagaraja et al., Extended Data Fig. 6c–e](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13083248/#Fig10) · [S3 source audit and contrasts](../s3/RESULTS.md)

| Explanation | Evidence it accounts for | Observation that would distinguish it in colon |
|---|---|---|
| DNA methylation helps carry memory | In ED6d, the authors reported stronger methylation loss in recovery at 500 selected AP-1 peaks than at 36,367 matched peaks. The recovered inhibitor–vehicle change did not distinguish those sets. In the ED6e readback (3 mice/arm, 6 common CpGs/locus), *Dcaf5* was 17.72 percentage points below control vehicle and regained 3.39 points with inhibitor. *Rbm27* was 10.39 points below control and regained 8.67. | Within the same epithelial lineage, does a change in methylation at a memory locus alter later accessibility or recall across divisions after the initiating exposure ends? The exact genome-wide peak IDs and matched matrix are unavailable. |
| Methylation follows TF occupancy, accessibility or proliferation | Both ED6e loci moved toward control by different amounts. Organoid *Thbs1* was 12.20 points lower after colitis in the S3 readback. Fig. 2p reported a regional accessibility–methylation association (ρ = −.51). Either direction of causation, or a shared upstream change, fits those endpoints. | Time order and reciprocal dependence of occupancy, methylation and accessibility at the same loci in comparable cells. A correlation across regions and terminal whole-colon means lack this information. |
| Other proteins or chromatin features maintain accessibility | S3 reproduced higher JUN/FOS binding with FOX factors at selected sequences in a defined-protein assay. In recovered mouse epidermis, Larsen found JUN, ATF3 and p63 at memory domains after FOS declined. Cowley found that persistent epidermal domains were CpG-rich, disfavored stable nucleosomes and retained H2A.Z. | Direct occupancy and nucleosome/histone measurements at persistent colonic loci during recovery, followed by evidence that the retained feature is required for subsequent accessibility or recall. Neither epidermal finding identifies the colon carrier. [Larsen et al., Fig. 5C–G](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8500942/#F5) · [Cowley et al., Figs. 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz6830) |
| Selection of clones or changing cell composition | Whole-colon methylation averages mix cell types. S5's Fos variance deficit shrank when cells were exchanged only within donor line. A persisting clone can also carry a genotype, regional identity or differentiation bias. Human UC epithelium provides a precedent for selection of clones with alterations in inflammatory signaling. | Linked lineage identity, genotype and state abundance over time, with the molecular state measured within comparable surviving lineages. This distinguishes expansion of previously different cells from an acquired state retained by their descendants. Both processes can coexist. [Nanki et al., organoid sequencing and functional IL-17 results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1844-5) · [S5 results](../s5/RESULTS.md) |
| The inhibitor covered a limited window | ED6d sampled a five-day recovery intervention. ED6e showed partial and heterogeneous reversal. Drug-on organoid EdU and diameter after a 20-day washout are different assays. Diameter retains a size difference by history. | Colonic target engagement over the actual interval, with measurements at establishment, maintenance and recall in matched lineages. A late intervention can miss an earlier requirement, incomplete engagement or slower methylation turnover. [S3 drug and washout results](../s3/RESULTS.md) |

Two human colon comparisons narrow the question. Howell et al. found that sigmoid epithelial disease-associated CpG values persisted between diagnosis and repeat endoscopy in pediatric UC and Crohn's disease. Their organoid retention experiment used Crohn's disease samples and controls. Hamdan et al. found persistent differences in accessibility in paired inflamed-region and uninflamed-region UC organoids, with stronger transcriptional responses on restimulation. These studies support persistence in human colonic epithelium and motivate distinguishing methylation from retained accessibility within a lineage. Neither assigns the carrier at the mouse ED6 loci. [Howell et al., Fig. 5A–E](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6381389/) · [Hamdan et al., Figs. 3 and 5C–D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13196144/)

**Adenomas.** In S4, prior-colitis mice had a higher mean within-mouse fraction of gross lesions over 1 mm (59.07% versus 31.69%), with similar mean gross counts (24.4 versus 25.0). A distinct microscopic cohort showed a larger mean tumour-area fraction (0.486% versus 0.183%). Lesion-level P20 above a score of 3 occurred in 12/137 versus 2/117 calls from one section per history. No lineage or mouse crosswalk links the ED6 methylation measurements to an individual growing lesion. These results qualify the later phenotype while leaving methylation-to-growth mediation unestimated. [S4 estimates and specimen limits](../s4/RESULTS.md)
