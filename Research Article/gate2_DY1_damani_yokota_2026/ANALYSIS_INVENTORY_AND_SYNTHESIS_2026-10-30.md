## What the combined evidence now supports

**1. A more specific measurement question.** The paper's highlighted discrepancy
is late bulk RNA versus separate bulk ATAC, with scRNA contributing composition
information. In the external WT benchmark, about 56% of mapped rows share a
direction, versus about 63% in the source-nearest-TSS subset. F1 shows that unequal
regions-per-gene weighting explains little of those aggregate fractions. This
narrows one technical explanation; it does not eliminate mapping error, cell
mixture, normalization or uncertainty. The two tested mappings largely share
assignments, so their agreement is not independent mapping validation.

**2. Accessibility and transcription remain distinct endpoints.** The external
cofactor comparison has broad Ensembl-mapped RNA/ATAC Spearman correlation about
0.016 under the source IL4 label and 0.052 under CTRL. This is a descriptive result
over dependent mapped rows, not evidence for a 50% null or absence of specific
regulation. Different source-paper selections and count models prevent treating
these global summaries as a contradiction of its published cofactor mechanism.
They motivate testing transcriptional output separately from accessibility.

**3. The human disease bridge is stronger for one RNA summary than another.**
The matrix-associated IPF difference stays positive after every individual
component/donor omission in the displayed primary and external populations.
The primary FABP4-panel estimate is small, already has an interval spanning zero,
and changes sign when FABP4 is omitted. External negative panel coupling retains
its original imprecise interval even though omission estimates remain negative.
These support differentiated RNA-summary interpretation, not matrix production,
protective repair, secretion or macrophage-to-epithelial causation.

**4. The STAT6–EGR2–PPARγ/RXRA and ARG1 story remains a candidate explanation.**
Late RNA, motif associations, accessibility and external binding maps do not
identify earlier signaling, same-cell occupancy or maintenance. The paper's
ARG1 functional comparison is a distinct evidence type; compartment targeting
and unlinked molecular/functional cohorts prevent NAM-specific causal mediation
in this repository. Sparse human ARG1 does not provide a simple cross-species
confirmation. None of these limitations negates every published functional result.

## Consequence for the next question

The defensible next discriminator is whether discordance remains within comparable
macrophage states with qualified paired measurements, and how it varies across
observed stimulation times. A persistence claim additionally needs verified
cessation/continuing-input information. The available analyses do not substitute
pseudotime, late snapshots or group means for those measurements.

This connects to A1/A8's regulatory/RNA versus later-output distinction and A14's
persistence versus continued-input distinction. A2/A22 still require actual source,
delivery/reception and recipient evidence; A19 retains its functional-reserve
endpoint. Their hypotheses, negative findings and authority remain unchanged.
Existing question-folder context notes point to article-owned evidence.

**Practical disposition:** all currently eligible F1–F4 numerical work is done.
The gain is a narrower, better supported question and more reliable interpretation
of the human RNA summaries; it is not confirmation of a chromatin-memory or ARG1
mediation chain. New access/identity/measurement evidence, rather than another
reweighting of the same tables, is needed to cross the remaining inference gaps.
