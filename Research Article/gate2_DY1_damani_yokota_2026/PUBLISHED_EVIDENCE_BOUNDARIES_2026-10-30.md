The important discrepancy is therefore **bulk RNA versus separate bulk ATAC**.
The scRNA study adds a different question about state composition and RNA within
states. It cannot identify within-state accessibility in the separate bulk ATAC
samples. A significant result in one assay and an imprecise result in another is
not by itself evidence of a poised enhancer or unchanged transcription.

The published motif analysis used pooled pathway leading-edge genes and an
expressed-gene promoter background. A proposed broader accessible-peak analysis
would be an extension with its own universe and confounding assessment. It is
not an exact replay merely because the same TF name appears.

## Statistical and causal distinctions already decidable

- A library, tag or nominal replicate is not a verified independent animal/pool.
  The reference CSV names a condition; counts alone cannot supply the missing
  source-identity relationship. Distinct independently collected pools can be valid
  units without individual mouse names; the missing item is the source-specific
  library/tag-to-independent-pool map and overlap assurance.
- A peak call or narrowPeak signal is not a consensus-region read count. A valid
  file format cannot establish the paper's normalization, processing version,
  outlier identity or differential accessibility.
- Exon-bin hits are not automatically unique gene-level fragments. A declared
  exon-profile diagnostic may describe libraries, while exact gene-level
  reproduction remains unqualified.
- A trained genotype-versus-control comparison can estimate the stated
  conditional genotype effect when units and design qualify. The missing
  untrained genotype comparator specifically prevents a training-by-genotype
  interaction claim; it does not invalidate every genotype comparison.
- The reported targeting includes NAMs and alveolar macrophages, so a
  compartment-level functional result is not automatically NAM-specific.
  Establishment, maintenance and later execution are also distinct questions.
- Separate omics and functional cohorts cannot support a fitted causal
  mediation chain. No reformatting, proxy score or additional regression repairs
  that missing linkage.
- A single later snapshot does not distinguish response speed, peak amplitude
  and resolution. Early histology or RNA-state changes do not establish later
  lineage-derived mature epithelial output.

## Implication for the existing research framing

The paper supports keeping three questions distinct: persistent regulatory
association beyond measured mixture, the context of ARG1-associated output,
and durable epithelial consequences. Those were already proposed in the
[original review](../../docs/research_reviews/2026-10-29-nam-memory/README.md#proposed-questions);
this assessment does not introduce a new hypothesis or nominate an intervention.

The available evidence supports their motivation, but not a complete
STAT6–PPARγ/RXRA–ARG1–epithelial repair chain. The next informative evidence is
question-specific: source-qualified comparable states and assays for the
regulatory association; an actual linked biochemical/recipient endpoint for an
output claim; and an early-to-later lineage/function link for maturation.
These are missing-evidence categories, not an operational experiment package.

A1/A8 retain their linked mature-output requirements; A2 retains its AREG
availability/delivery question; A14 retains its cessation/reception distinctions;
A19 retains its functional reserve endpoint; A22 retains its focal protein-output
question and unfavorable broad-predictor evidence. Their
[existing contextual notes](README.md#architecture-and-evidence-boundaries)
remain the proper routing. No RQ is promoted, ranked or retired.

Current-evidence synthesis can be completed despite held numerical jobs. A
mechanistic conclusion cannot. The [job assessment](JOB_ASSESSMENT_2026-10-30.md)
separates those dispositions and records what would be needed to reopen each
