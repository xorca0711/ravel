## Execution amendment, 29 October 2026

GEO's 27 July 2026 ATAC processing narrative uses Seq-N-Slide/Bowtie2/edgeR with GC correction, whereas the paper uses seq2science/bwa-mem2/DiffBind-DESeq2. NarrowPeak signal is not a read-count matrix. Deposited bulk RNA files contain Ensembl exon-bin identifiers, whose aggregation cannot silently be described as unique gene-level fragment counts. Units, count semantics and source-version uncertainty must be qualified before inference. Source qualification does not revise an A-series hypothesis.

The published PPARgamma/EGR2 and ARG1 precedents are premises. Any newly used target set requires a primary-method audit before numerical use. Human GSE136831 observations are shared with existing Ravel evidence. No search performed here establishes novelty or laboratory readiness.
