## Search/access record and plan decision

Actual service: web search and official publisher/PMC/GEO pages. Primary indexed
records supplied the relevant sample lists, figure text and data-availability
statements. Direct PMC/GEO opens sometimes returned browser challenges; the
publisher full-text endpoint returned 403. These are access limits, not proof
that bulk files are unavailable. Exact downloaded bytes and metadata results
belong to the analysis chat's retrieval records/contracts.

Queries included `"GSE110465" "Samples"`,
`"The transcription factor EGR2 is the molecular linchpin" "GSE"`,
`"PMC7608752" "GSE151015"`, `"GSE151015" "Samples"`,
`"PMC7608752" "24 h" "ATAC"`, `"GSE115505" "SubSeries"`,
`"GSE151015" "ATAC-seq replicate"`, `"PMC7608752" "Figure 1" "Figure 2" "24"`,
`"GSE115504" "Supplementary file"`, `"GSE107456" "Samples" "RNA"`,
and `"PMC7608752" "Figure 6" "ATAC-seq"`.

Plan adjustment: assess each assay and RNA subseries separately. A missing
matched chromatin time course need not prevent an eligible RNA-only reference
description. Conversely, an RNA-only result cannot resolve RNA-ATAC timing or
NAM persistence. This preserves the original E6 question while avoiding both a
blanket hold and an unsupported cross-assay claim. No additional perturbation
screen or intervention prioritization is introduced.
