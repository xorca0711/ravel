## What this question could add

The contribution is a baseline measurement/model-discrimination analysis:
determine whether an interpretable mismatch remains, then assess its consistency
with existing regulatory evidence. It is not discovery of the axis. A
non-infectious reference can test the behavior of the comparison method in a
better specified count system; it cannot fill missing original sample identities,
turn a stimulus endpoint into post-cessation memory, or become independent NAM
replication. No finite search or schematic certifies novelty or laboratory readiness.

## Current hypothesis and rival

The dossier asks whether the late NAM bulk RNA–ATAC pattern remains interpretable
under defensible measurement, mapping and RNA-composition analyses, and consistent
with independent STAT6–EGR2–PPARγ/RXRA evidence. Measured RNA-composition sensitivity
does not resolve composition in the separate bulk ATAC preparations. Alternatives include state/origin mixture,
measurement and annotation choices, RNA uncertainty, cofactor/output requirements
and continued niche input. These can coexist. The comparison uses separate
baseline history/control assay contrasts; independently collected preparations
are the intended units, with exact deposited source-to-pool mapping unresolved.
No individual longitudinal or paired multiome structure is inferred.

## What the comparison would teach

A robust pattern would support model consistency, while a pattern removed by
valid measurement/mapping choices would reduce the need for that explanation.
A mixture-associated RNA difference would identify a contribution, not the
within-state ATAC component. A synthetic diagnostic can establish a statistical
failure mode, not explain a biological dataset. ARG1 molecular association,
enzyme output and causal mediation remain separate questions.
