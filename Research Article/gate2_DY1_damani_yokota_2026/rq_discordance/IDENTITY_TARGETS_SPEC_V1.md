# DY1-P02 fixed identity-target view v1

3 November 2026. Exposed descriptive reuse of immutable non-infectious cultured
macrophage reference effects. [PS3 rationale](../../../docs/research_reviews/2026-11-03-primary-candidate-literature/ANALYSIS_PLAN_V1.md).

Question: is the six-target RNA pattern and its mapped accessibility uniformly
consistent with one identity interpretation? This is a context-bound measurement
screen, not selection of a NAM intervention or proof of memory. The global F1/F4
summaries cannot answer a target-specific direction question. Selection is from
literature before inspecting the six saved effects, never from their magnitude,
p-value or agreement. Existing prohibitions on favorable selected-feature rescue
remain: all six targets, all three contrasts, both mappings, broad/proximal
universes, all zeros and opposing regions must be retained.

Targets: Irf4 and Pparg are named in Verwaerde 2025-online/2026-issue primary
abstract (PMID 41386231; full text not accessible, no regulon extracted). Pparg,
Rxra, Stat6 and Egr2 reflect the previously read Daniel 2018 Fig. 3-4 and Daniel
2020 Fig. 6/8 premise. Arg1 is a separate measured endpoint, not a catalytic or
repair readout. Deochand 2024 full primary Results, Fig. 1b-c and 5/S5 were read;
Pparg/Egr2/Arg1 are source targets. This BMDM reference does not reproduce an
IL13 alveolar-macrophage switch, infer that IRF4 suppresses PPARgamma here, or
supply NAM units/paired assays/post-withdrawal timing.

Use exact original RNA symbol-to-Ensembl map; require one identifier per target.
Read only frozen WT IL4-minus-CTRL effects at offset 1 (E2), and frozen cKO-minus-WT
within IL4 and CTRL (F4). Copy point estimates and all uncertainty fields exactly;
never refit counts, subtract assay scales, form genotype-by-stimulus interactions,
rank targets or infer binding from proximity. Counts are read ONLY for identifiers.

For each target/contrast, retain every mapped region under author-Ensembl and
nearest-TSS-symbol assignments, all-annotated and <=2kb subsets. Missing regions
are absent coverage, not zero effects. Report counts of positive/negative/zero
region differences and range, with no independent-peak test or averaging into an
activity score. Source nearest-TSS distance is not proof of regulation of the
assigned gene. Both source universes and mapping boundaries remain in force.

Original source-reported mouse/preparation units: RNA WT3 per group; cKO IL4=3,
CTRL=4; ATAC4 per group. Exact preparation/litter/batch dependence remains unknown.
WT sensitivity envelopes and F4 conditional Welch intervals are inherited
conditional descriptions, not verified population confidence intervals. No new
p-values, selection, power/equivalence margin or human acceptance. Verify exact
selected-row parity, output completeness and mapping cardinality independently.
Stop on a hash, identifier, membership or output parity mismatch.

Figure clarification: ATAC dots display point differences only; complete inherited
uncertainty and both mappings/subsets remain in tables. Opposing dots and proximity
do not establish true biological discordance or regulatory linkage.
