Search/read date: 29 October 2026. This bounded pass supports a non-infectious
human observational adaptation of stage 7, not novelty clearance or an
experiment package. Published findings and repository measurements are separate
evidence; repeated use of GSE136831 is one lineage.

The coordinating reviewer separately inspected the supplied Damani-Yokota paper's
human Fig. 5j/ED9b (SLC40A1/FOLR2/F13A1/STAB1/SELENOP) and Fig. 5n/ED9c human
highlighted genes. That review supports the list provenance, not exact cluster
reproduction: author reclustering/model assignments are unavailable. Our
two-of-five detection proxy and one/three-marker sensitivities are analyst-defined.
No identity marker enters the outcome panels. See the exact
[prospective numerical specification](HUMAN_NUMERICAL_PLAN.md).

The prior reviewed [JCI 2025 source-attribution precedent](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188734)
reported predominantly neutrophil ARG1 in human IPF specimens (primary Results
and Fig. 1/3–4, recorded in the existing review). No neutrophil annotation occurs
in this atlas. ARG1 results therefore concern captured annotated cells only and
cannot adjudicate tissue-wide dominance or enzyme activity.

## Contribution and interpretation boundary

Published human macrophage/epithelial associations → reused Ravel atlas with
qualified donor identities → remaining sensitivity to annotations, disease and
recipient composition → frozen donor descriptions and within-disease comparisons.
Concordant and discordant/null results are equally informative about this
adaptation. Primary Macrophage/floor20 narration is fixed before outcome exposure;
proxies cannot replace it because they look stronger. Clinical adjustment remains
descriptive and incomplete. These measurements cannot establish secretion,
matrix production, spatial signaling, human immune history, mature repair or
causation, and do not test A1/A22's remaining discriminators.
