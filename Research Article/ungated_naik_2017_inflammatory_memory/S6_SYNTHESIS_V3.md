## Published premise → observation → completed informative comparison

Naik 2017 already links inflammatory history, persistent accessibility and wound
response; its Fig. 5c already distinguishes IL-1beta transcript and protein.
Larsen 2021 and Cowley 2026 already develop establishment/recall/maintenance
mechanisms, with reused source datasets explicitly identified. Hamdan 2026
already separates barrier/clonogenic endpoints from enhanced recall/closure;
Falvo 2023 already reports stimulus/endpoint-dependent consequences. The precise
source versions, inspected figures, actual searches and access limits remain in
the [literature context](LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md). These are published precedents,
not new explanations discovered by this reanalysis.

Naik S4/S4B then showed why post-challenge, baseline-subtracted and alternative-
reference contrasts can mean different things. Hamdan's matched-donor source
made the explicit concurrent-control interaction executable. Its published
four-category STEM ordering and this interaction are distinct estimands; exact
STEM transform and membership remain unqualified. S7 now demonstrates the
distinction within the all-gene deposited source while retaining mixed results.
This does not directly test the paper's selected profiles, consensus peaks,
pathways or functional experiments, or establish that its authors ignored baseline.

The contribution is therefore a **justified measurement/estimand extension**
following source reconstruction, in a separate human study but using that study's
own data. It strengthens the need to specify the response comparison and its
constituent responses; it does not independently validate either paper's biology.
No new mechanism or target was required to make this comparison informative.
