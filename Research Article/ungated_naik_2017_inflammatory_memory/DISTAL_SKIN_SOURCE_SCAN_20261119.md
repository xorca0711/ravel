# Mouse epidermal distal accessibility after IMQ recovery

19 November 2026 · Nk17-P01 · Step 1 source qualification after S7 · For coordinator review

**The strongest directly usable source is Cowley Data S2, which supplies a transient comparator as well as memory coordinates.** Its 934 Memory and 933 Resolved regions can be given the same promoter exclusion and assessed in two post-IMQ and two age-matched naïve pools at 24 months. The libraries, units and file sizes are identifiable. The two-year libraries contributed neither peaks nor differential tests to the domain selection, but Fig. 1 already displays their behavior. This would be an exposed, within-study description. A second design can avoid using recovery data at *any* stage of region selection by discovering regions solely from Larsen’s four new D6 peak files. All four files were recovered. No recovery signal was extracted or new biological contrast estimated in this step. ## What changed since S3 and S6C

Naik reported 44,414 D6-induced peaks. Fig. 3b,d identifies 2,041 shared D6/D30 inflammation-induced peaks, Fig. 3c shows selected loci through D180, and Extended Data Fig. 4g counts overlap of D6 with D30 or D180. The article asks whether the chromatin response persists in recovered epidermal stem cells. Ravel previously qualified all 12 original mm10 BigWigs but recovered positional ranks only around GENCODE M9 protein-coding gene boundaries. Each width covered 21,760 distinct windows for 21,775 genes on chr1–19/X. D6/D30 and D180 have different sequencing platforms, and the deposited signal transformation is unresolved. S3 and S6C therefore remain valid for their own within-library ranks. S7 measured human post-challenge RNA. None supplies a set of distal mouse intervals. [Naik Fig. 3 and Extended Data Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature24271) · [S3 amendment](S3_AMENDMENT_V1.md) · [S3 qualification](S3Q_RESULTS_V1.md) · [S3 results](S3_RESULTS_V1.md) · [S6C method](S6C_D180_METHOD_V1.md) · [S6C results](S6C_RESULTS_V1.md) · [S6 synthesis](S6_SYNTHESIS_V3.md) · [S7 results](S7_RESULTS_V1.md)

The unresolved comparison is whether recovery libraries show a history difference at distal regions selected during acute inflammation, relative to a specified set of comparator regions. The observed unit is the sequenced cell pool. Regions and genomic bases are repeated features of that pool. An exposure-associated difference could reflect which basal or upper-follicle populations were present in the sorted skin, differences in initial accessibility, age, or technical processing. This endpoint cannot by itself identify a target gene, causal enhancer, cellular recall, or repair benefit.

### Cowley’s coordinate tables and the meaning of “distal”

The supplementary PDF’s **p. 26** identifies the files. The complete Data S2 workbook has one worksheet with Chromosome, Start, End and Type, 2,801 unique and valid triples on chr1–19/X. It contains **934 Memory, 933 Resolved and 934 Unchanged**. The Methods give a target of 934 for each. Use the deposited rows and retain the one-row discrepancy. Data S2 has no distance-to-TSS or distal column. [Supplementary captions](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Supplementary_Material.pdf) · [Data S2](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Data_s2.xlsx)

- Data S3 and S4 contain 75,642 D6 and 979 D30 coordinate-bearing DESeq2 result rows respectively. The 934 Memory regions were induced at both D6 and D30, with adjusted P < 0.05 and positive log2 fold change. Resolved regions were selected from D6-induced regions near the zero D30 Wald statistic. In the notebook, the aging exclusions for Resolved and Short-term are commented out. The executed lines use the D30 or M12 results and drop missing adjusted P values before ranking. Unchanged was a downsample of 16,897 regions passing the authors’ stability criteria across D6/D30/M12 controls and history contrasts. S3 is an induced subset of the 220,884-region universe. The full count matrix/eligible BED referenced by the notebook was not recovered here. [Data S3](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Data_s3.xlsx) · [Data S4](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Data_s4.xlsx)
- Data S5 has 143 reported Y1 rows, comprising 131 positive and 12 negative, all flagged significant by the published adjusted-P rule. Intersecting the coordinate lists identifies the reported 117 long-term Memory domains. The authors selected 117 short-term Memory domains around the zero Y1 Wald statistic. The other D30 Memory regions have neither designation in Data S6. Thus D30 is selected for the Memory versus Resolved comparison and Y1 is selected for the Long-term versus Short-term comparison. Neither contrast at its defining time tests persistence independently. [Data S5](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Data_s5.xlsx) · [Data S6](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13295011.1/NIHMS2170334-supplement-Data_s6.xlsx)
- Data S6 provides **117 Long-term and 117 Short-term** annotated regions, with transcript, distanceToTSS and annotation fields. The exact author label `Distal Intergenic` covers **48 Long-term and 42 Short-term** rows. This gives an immediately identifiable 90-region subset. Examples in the sheet’s one-based inclusive notation are chr6:82908812–82909878 (Long-term, nearest annotation Sema4f) and chr11:82072958–82074331 (Short-term, Ccl11). Introns can also be far from promoters. The label therefore answers a narrower question than a fixed TSS-distance rule. The paper uses mm10 TxDb.Mmusculus.UCSC.mm10.knownGene transcripts for gene proximity. Nearest-gene assignments are positional. - **Coordinate convention matters.** Every S6 row satisfies width = end − start + 1. All 234 join exactly to S2 Memory after subtracting 1 from the S6 start and preserving the end. None joins as an unchanged triple. Adding 1 to the S2 start likewise matches all 934 Memory and 933 Resolved rows to S3, and all 934 Memory rows to S4. The notebook builds GRanges then uses `export.bed`. Treat S2 as BED-like zero-based, half-open, and S3–S6 as GRanges-like for these joins. For other sources, check the convention independently.
- For a future **Memory versus Resolved** comparison, predefine a single outcome-independent promoter exclusion for both complete S2 classes. One possible definition is no overlap with any annotated transcript TSS ±2 kb on the agreed mm10 chromosomes. Pin the exact transcript annotation, version and convention before filtering, and report intragenic and intergenic eligibility. This analyst-chosen rule differs from both the S6 author label and S3’s protein-coding gene windows. No new intersections with promoters were performed.

## Informative comparisons and review decision

1. **Most direct late description.** Acquire and qualify the four M24 tracks. Before signal access, freeze either (a) all S2 Memory versus Resolved regions passing the same distal rule, with Unchanged as a stability-selected context, or (b) the 48/42 author-annotated S6 intergenic LT/ST regions if the target is longevity classes. In each library summarize fixed-interval coverage by class, then compare the class difference between the two history and two age-matched control pools. Keep per-pool values visible. Shared separation in both history and controls would point to intrinsic class differences. A larger separation in history pools would describe a late history-associated difference, potentially influenced by initial peak strengths, floor/coverage effects, or mixture. A small or reversed separation remains informative. With two pools/group, regional resampling cannot supply additional mouse replication. Fig. 1 already exposed the overall late trajectories.
2. **Most informative comparison with recovery-independent selection.** Use only the four *new* WT Larsen D6 BEDs, excluding Naik and every D30/M12 input from discovery. A candidate membership rule could require a called region in both D6 IMQ pools and neither D6 control, alongside a background reproducibly called in both groups. Freeze coordinate overlap, promoter exclusion, size/acute signal comparability, reference and coverage rules before examining recovery. If reliable matching for acute signal strength or actual differential accessibility is required, obtain raw D6 reads/counts under a separate decision. The public peak files support call membership only. Compare the fixed regions in the accession-distinct M24 mouse pools, or first qualify a lower-cost **relative-rank** description in the four local Naik D180 libraries. For the latter, specify one common interval universe for rankings and use per-library class summaries, since Naik signal units are unknown. Failure of the acute call class to separate at recovery would narrow any generalization from previously recovery-selected classes. This design separates discovery from the tested libraries. The team and published late results are shared, and animal-level independence remains unverified. It would add a selection-independent comparison without making the already published late observation novel.
3. **Original reproduction.** Preserve Naik’s accessible Fig. 4c exemplars as identified. Reproducing genome-wide original retained/transient membership still requires the versioned original peak/coordinate universe and its exact comparisons, or a separately authorized raw-read reconstruction with explicit discrepancies. Cowley and Larsen define different universes and use overlapping original samples. The existence of either later table does not resolve this blocker.

## Other primary evidence and contrary observations

- Gonzales et al. (2021), Fig. 2A–F and GSE165312, follow hair-follicle stem cells after wounding and their progeny in regenerated epidermis. Niche-specific regions can be lost or gained by D80, and the published classifications use the later states. Cowley also reuses this dataset. It could test generalization to a related injury with a different lineage history and reference tissue. It cannot supply an independent naïve/post-IMQ cohort. [Gonzales study](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abh2444) · [GSE165312](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE165312)
- Levra Levron et al. (2023), Fig. 1 and Extended Data on overlapping wounds, trace Lrig1 progeny after skin injury. “Distal” there describes undamaged skin away from the wound. Distance to a mouse skin wound and distance from a genomic promoter answer different questions. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01120-0)
- The healed-psoriasis keratinocyte study, Fig. 4e, reuses Naik’s mouse data and shows an acute S100a signal approaching control by D30 despite persistence at related human loci. This is a concrete reason to retain a transient comparator and allow locus-specific resolution. The human cohorts differ in disease and exposure. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xjidi.2025.100430)
- The 28 October 2026 mouse IMQ study on diet at disease onset implicates resident CD4 T cells in recall under that dietary condition. Fig. 4l–p uses depletion and skin grafts. It supplies a competing cellular source for a whole-skin recall phenotype. Its immune-cell ATAC signal cannot stand in for sorted epidermal ATAC. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11181-4)
