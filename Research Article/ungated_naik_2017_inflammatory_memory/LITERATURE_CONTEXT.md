attenuated baseline RNA and altered subsequent response. The comparison tuple

## What the comparison would teach

Retained accessibility with attenuated RNA and baseline-adjusted recall would
support the published association within this source. Baseline offsets, absent
or inconsistent recall, and dependence on region definitions would narrow it.
Unknown units or incompatible sampling could leave it descriptive/unestimated.
None of these outcomes is independently replicated skin memory or proof that
an endogenous element causes repair. Faster closure and barrier competence
must remain distinct, as the human organoid precedent makes concrete.
