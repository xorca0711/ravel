## Resident populations

In Fig. 1A–B, the gated populations are CD11b+ F4/80+ macrophages subdivided into MHCII-low/DNAM-1-low LCMs, MHCII-high/DNAM-1-high SCMs and intermediate transitional cells. ICAM2+ GATA6+ LCMs are an additional nested gate. Frequencies shown in A–B use total live events and B also reports absolute recovery. The control claim of over 80% LCMs and under 10% SCMs uses CD11b+ F4/80+ cells instead. The published summary pools six experiments and reports 9/6 control/KO mice for LysM Gata6, 5/7 for LysM Klf2 and 4/5 for CX3CR1 Klf2. [Pestal et al., Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11899981/#fig1)

| Perturbation | Recovered populations | Residual marker phenotype |
|---|---|---|
| LysM Gata6 loss | LCM frequency and number fell. SCM and transitional frequency and number had no reported significant change. | Lower F4/80 and absent GATA6, with ICAM2 and TIM-4 retained in the LCM-adjacent clusters. |
| LysM Klf2 loss | LCM gate fell from approximately 17% to under 1% of total cavity cells. Transitional cells increased. Most CD11b+ F4/80+ events mapped to SCM and transitional regions. | The few cells meeting the broad LCM gate had lower F4/80 and lacked ICAM2 and GATA6. |
| CX3CR1 Klf2 loss | LCM frequency and number were strongly reduced against the matched Cre-negative controls. | The few residual cells lacked ICAM2 and GATA6. |

Fig. 1C–D uses equal cell counts per genotype after downsampling. Cluster placement describes residual phenotype. Frequencies and absolute recovery come from Fig. 1B.

## Competition

In Fig. 2A–B the unit is the irradiated recipient, with three to four hosts per group in the illustrated experiment. Compare conditional donor and B6.SJL competitor contributions within each recipient and subset against the congenically distinct control donor mixture. Control donors contributed approximately equally to LCMs and SCMs. Both deficient donors contributed poorly to LCMs while retaining SCM contribution. The Klf2 deficit was more complete than the Gata6 deficit, with a smaller separation between mutants than under homeostasis. The authors report no significant competitive loss in the other surveyed tissue macrophage groups. The shared host localizes a relative competitive deficit to LCM recovery. Terminal donor share cannot distinguish entry, retention or survival. [Pestal et al., Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11899981/#fig2)
