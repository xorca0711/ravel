# Nb4 RQ literature screen

**Screened 2 October 2026 UTC / 3 October KST.** Targeted primary-source screen,
not a systematic review or a claim that an unlocated study does not exist.
Searches combined lung adventitial fibroblast/C3/epithelial identity,
MYRF/AT1/maturation, and the exact titles below. Publisher/PubMed indexed
abstracts and result excerpts were available; several direct full-page requests
failed. No newly screened paper supplied numerical inputs for this sequence.

| Primary study | Established context | Consequence for Nb4 |
|---|---|---|
| [Atakkatan et al., Cell Reports 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41319296/), DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2025.116641 | Reports a C3/IL-33 adventitial fibroblast niche and functional involvement in airway allergy | Adventitial C3 expression or immune function is not a novel proposition. The narrower question concerns subtype-specific responses to epithelial identity changes in a defined context. |
| [Prasad et al., JCI Insight 2025](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/184141), DOI 10.1172/jci.insight.184141 | Links intracellular complement with metabolic and inflammatory states in pulmonary vascular fibroblasts | C3 RNA cannot identify secreted versus intracellular complement or its activation. Keep these mechanisms separate. |
| [Hoffman et al., Nature Communications 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63735-1), DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-63735-1 | Reports pathogenic fibroblast activation by aberrant intermediate epithelial cells and a TGF-beta-associated secretome in preclinical fibrosis systems | Generic epithelial-to-fibroblast activation is established context; Nb4 must address a specific subtype interaction and endpoint rather than claim that direction as novel. |
| [Single-cell division tracing study, Nature Communications 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-46469-4), DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-46469-4 | Reports adventitial fibroblast activation and epithelial regenerative support after epithelial depletion | Subtype-specific epithelial support is already motivated; repair versus inflammatory settings and direction of influence cannot be pooled. |
| [Luna et al., Developmental Cell 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41558485/), DOI 10.1016/j.devcel.2025.12.011 | Demonstrates a developmental mesothelial role for MYRF | MYRF must be checked against mesothelial identity; an adult AT1 maturation role does not follow from this developmental result. |
| [Postnatal lung regulatory map, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9012447/) | Uses AT1 regulatory and chromatin context including the MYRF locus | Marker/regulatory context is prior evidence, not an independent mature-function endpoint. |

These sources narrow the candidate framing. They do not establish the exact
adult human subtype-by-epithelial-identity interaction, nor prove its novelty.
Dataset accession, raw measurement, replication and linkage audits would be
required before analyzing these studies as new cohorts.
