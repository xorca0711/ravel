# Nb4: narrowed research-question candidates

**Derived after reproduction, sensitivities, external pilot and genome-wide
diagnostics, 2 October 2026.** Conditional extensions of existing questions;
no new A-number or claim grade.

## Leading candidate

> Does loss of alveolar epithelial identity change C3-associated output and
> selected chemokine responses differently in alveolar and adventitial
> fibroblasts, beyond changes in fibroblast subtype composition?

This extends [A22](../../../RQ_Specified/A22_epithelial_identity_niche_response/README.md)
in the context of [A13](../../../RQ_Specified/A13_fibroblast_beyond_macrophage_il1b/README.md).
[Nb3](../../gate2_N2_nabhan_2026/reports/FOLLOWUP_RESULTS.md) owns the epithelial
perturbation motivation; Nb4 adds normal human subtype context. These datasets
do not jointly measure the proposed interaction.

The latest merged [A22 identity–amount extension](../../../RQ_Specified/A22_epithelial_identity_niche_response/reports/identity_amount_v2/RESULTS.md)
weakens the broad operational predictor under target omission and plate transfer.
Nb4 does not reverse that result: subtype-specific modification remains a
conditional focal question requiring a different, outcome-linked design.

C3's original fibroblast contrast survives assay/depth checks and transfers to
four external single-cell donors. CXCL2/IL32 change direction in some donor or
nuclear settings; CCL2/CXCL12 are heterogeneous. Whole-set complement enrichment
has opposite directions between cohorts. Thus C3, individual chemokines and
the broad complement set must remain separate endpoints.

Adventitial complement expression is already motivated by the source atlas;
that observation is not a novelty claim. The proposed question is whether
baseline subtype identity modifies the response to epithelial identity loss.
A targeted literature review remains necessary before formal registration.

| Rival explanation | Discriminating evidence |
|---|---|
| Subtype composition explains the bulk response | Paired within-subtype responses, with proportions measured separately |
| A pan-fibroblast response independent of subtype | Similar responses in both subtypes; little subtype-by-perturbation interaction |
| Handling or ambient RNA explains the RNA contrast | Paired preparations, localization and measured protein with technical covariates |
| C3 tracks identity without mediating function | Perturbation/rescue evidence and a recipient functional endpoint distinct from RNA or abundance |

A later eligible study should estimate the **difference of paired perturbation
effects between subtypes**, with donors as biological units. C3 is a focal
endpoint; CCL2, CXCL2, IL32 and CXCL12 are separate secondary endpoints with
prospective multiplicity handling. Do not substitute a whole-complement score
after seeing results. Normal atlases cannot estimate this interaction. No
power claim follows from the present two-source/four-external-donor comparison.

Retain conditionally if subtype-resolved effects persist after composition and
preparation checks. Reframe toward composition if within-subtype effects
disappear. Keep the complement-wide interpretation held unless independently
coherent pathway evidence appears; Nb4 currently does not provide it.

## Secondary candidate

> Does a MYRF-associated human AT1 maturation component add information about
> mature epithelial contribution beyond a broad AT1 marker program?

This belongs under [A8](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a8).
MYRF expression specificity survives comparators and a depth diagnostic, but
the atlas has no lineage contribution, mature function or perturbation endpoint.
The source already nominated MYRF; rediscovering expression is not the RQ.
Require independent outcome-linked data before advancing beyond reference use.

## Held branches

AT2-s-to-mouse-Wnt-stem-cell equivalence remains held for inadequate matched
human donor coverage and absent homologous fate testing. TBX5/pericyte RNA is
a reference observation; mural function, perfusion and causal regulation are
unmeasured. New species contrasts require reconciled animal age, study and
orthology. These are explicit data limits, not completed negative experiments.
