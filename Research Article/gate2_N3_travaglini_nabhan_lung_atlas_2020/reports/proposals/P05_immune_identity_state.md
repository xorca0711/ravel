# Nb4-P05: reproducible myeloid identities versus recurring states

**Status: planned.** [Master contract](README.md) · [source ledger](SOURCES.md).
S01 discovery; full myeloid S03 or qualified source studies from S04 for normal
validation; S05/S06 for a separate IPF extension.

## Question and alternatives

Do the original IGSF21-, EREG- and TREM2-labelled myeloid populations represent
reproducible molecular identities, or does a shared activation/state program
create apparently separate clusters? Source-era “dendritic” naming must first
be reconciled with current myeloid annotation; the plan does not assume that
all three labels are homologous dendritic subtypes.

The identity hypothesis predicts donor/study-reproducible multigene structure
within an independently defined lineage. The state hypothesis predicts the
same program across multiple identities or contexts. Batch, doublets, rare
sampling and dissociation are competing technical explanations.

## Measurement and units

Primary outcome: out-of-donor and out-of-study reproducibility of the
candidate multigene population after lineage and activation are represented
separately. Report per-donor classification/assignment agreement, candidate
coverage, multigene contrasts and assignment uncertainty.

IGSF21/EREG/TREM2 are tested sentinels, not sufficient annotation rules.
Candidate identity labels must have a sensitivity version excluding these
genes and the test program. A stable transcriptional cluster is not, by
itself, a new ontogenetic lineage.

## Ordered analysis

1. Recover original cells, label hierarchy and donor/assay/region coverage.
   Build a source-to-current myeloid crosswalk from independent lineage
   features; retain uncertain assignments and mixed identities.
2. Reconstruct source neighborhoods descriptively, then recluster within
   qualified myeloid lineages with a frozen resolution/feature sensitivity
   grid. Check donor domination, doublet flags and depth dependence.
   Do not select the visually cleanest UMAP as the primary result.
3. Compare a continuous state representation with a discrete-population
   representation using donor-held-out prediction or reconstruction criteria,
   identical eligible inputs and model-complexity control. Estimate
   identity-by-state patterns in donor pseudobulks.
4. Freeze multigene definitions and transfer into independent normal samples.
   Repeat with sentinel genes excluded and with activation/stress gene
   exclusion as an explicitly different measurement, retaining both results.
5. Only after normal qualification, compare IPF/control within independently
   assigned lineages. Test whether the program recurs across identities or
   expands within one identity. Spatial context can test localization when
   regions are independently annotated; RNA coexpression cannot establish
   a new lineage or lineage transition.

## Decision, figures and next step

Retain “reproducible molecular population” if the broader pattern survives
sentinel omission and transfers across studies. Prefer a state question when
a continuous shared program explains the observations. Mixed evidence supports
an identity-by-state model, not forced classification into one answer.
If no independent lineage discriminator exists, record that unresolved
ambiguity and the needed protein/ontogeny evidence.

Planned panels: lineage crosswalk and coverage; donor-faceted UMAP;
independent marker/program dot plot; held-out reproducibility; state gradients
within lineage. Caption focus: **“Separating myeloid identity from shared
activation tests what the atlas populations represent.”**

First deliverable: three-population annotation and coverage audit.
P04 and P05 can share audited immune inputs but answer different questions;
neither depends on a positive result in the other. A3/A6 own overlapping
macrophage mechanisms. Capillary intermediates belong to a separately scoped
P08 extension, not a pooled immune/endothelial clustering exercise.
