# Nb4-P06: AT2 signaling heterogeneity and the AT2-s label

**Status: planned; the earlier matched source comparison failed its coverage gate.**
[Master contract](README.md) · [source ledger](SOURCES.md). S01 discovery;
S03/S04/S07 candidate human replication; S02 only after P01 qualification.

## Question and alternatives

Does human AT2-s denote a reproducible transcriptional subdivision, or a
continuous axis of Wnt-component, metabolic and stress variation within AT2?
A discrete state should generalize beyond the donor and assay that make it
visually separable. Depth, regional sampling, cell cycle, damage and a
label-defining marker are rival explanations.

The notes nominate WIF1/HHIP/CA2, WNT5A/LRP5/CTNNBIP1/TCF7L2 and
CP/GSTA1/CYP4B1 contexts. These are source-informed candidate gene groups,
not validated Wnt-activity, quiescence or stem-cell scores.

## Measurement and units

Primary estimand: donor-held-out support for discrete versus continuous
multigene structure in eligible AT2 cells, with donor-specific program
distributions and AT2-s contrasts where both populations are observed.
Use prespecified predictive comparison and complexity control, not a UMAP gap
or a significant difference in the marker used to define AT2-s.

The prior source gate is retained; do not lower its cell floor and describe
that as the same test. A new all-AT2 continuous-state analysis is a separately
declared estimand. Subsampling cells measures technical stability, not
biological replication. No absent class is filled with zero expression.

## Ordered analysis

1. Audit per-donor/region/assay AT2 and AT2-s counts, annotation source and
   candidate-panel measurement. Mark exactly which prior matched contrasts
   are unavailable. Qualify a larger independent human cohort if necessary.
2. Freeze pan-AT2 inclusion using features independent of the tested groups.
   Reconstruct the source label, but perform de novo state characterization
   without that label and show both representations.
3. Compare a continuous latent-factor model with a two-component alternative
   using the same count-aware/predeclared transformation, donor-held-out
   likelihood or reconstruction and explicit complexity control. Test
   sensitivity to library depth and technical zero inflation before
   interpreting apparent bimodality.
4. Map fixed signatures to independent human donors; repeat after removing
   each sentinel and after anatomy/assay restrictions. Estimate each gene's
   contribution so a pathway label cannot hide opposed components.
5. Add a human–mouse comparison only after P01's label/orthology audit.
   Compare programs and their context; do not equate a mouse Wnt-responsive
   population with human AT2-s solely by shared marker expression.

## Decision, figures and next step

Retain a subdivision question if it transfers across donors/studies beyond
technical rivals. Narrow to a continuous-state question if that representation
generalizes better. If neither is estimable, preserve the hypothesis and
identify the exact missing donor/state coverage.

A stemness, quiescence, self-renewal or AT1-output claim needs linked activity,
time and fate/function measurements. A4/A7/A19 own overlapping signaling,
identity and reversibility questions; A8 owns mature contribution. Do not
replace their independent endpoints with a new RNA score.

Planned panels: coverage and donor-faceted embedding, single-gene/program
distributions, held-out model comparison, cross-cohort transfer and optional
ortholog dot plot. Caption focus: **“AT2 heterogeneity is examined as both
a population boundary and a continuous transcriptional landscape.”**

First deliverable: the rare-state eligibility map and an independently sourced
human validation shortlist. Models remain unselected until this map establishes
which comparison is scientifically interpretable.
