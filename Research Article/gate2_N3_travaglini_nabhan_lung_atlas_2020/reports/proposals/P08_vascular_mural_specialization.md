# Nb4-P08: pericyte identity, vessel context and contractile programs

**Status: planned; no linked perfusion or contractility endpoint qualified.**
[Master contract](README.md) · [source ledger](SOURCES.md).
S01 source; full S03 vascular/mural and spatial data are candidate validation.

## Question and alternatives

Is the TBX5-associated pericyte program a stable identity related to vessel
context, or does it vary with a broader contractile/hormone-response state?
Nb4 reproduced selected TBX5 expression contrasts; it did not establish
vascular function or a master regulator.

Start with pericytes versus vascular smooth muscle and qualified neighboring
mesenchymal classes. Alternatives are mixed mural identities, doublets,
vessel-region sampling, depth and shared MYH11/CNN1/TAGLN-associated
contractile expression. Hormone-receptor RNA is a nomination, not endocrine
responsiveness.

## Measurement and units

Primary contrasts are donor-level TBX5 and independently defined pericyte-program
expression across independently annotated mural identities and, where measured,
vessel beds. Secondary associations relate those measurements to a separately
specified contractile program. Exclude TBX5 and tested contractile genes from
the sensitivity identity classifier; show label dependence.

Vessel-bed inference requires anatomical labels or independently annotated
imaging. Endothelial markers in the same tissue piece do not prove that a
particular pericyte is adjacent to a particular vessel. Spatial cells/fields
nest within donors; RNA and image donors are paired only if IDs verify it.

## Ordered analysis

1. Audit pericyte/smooth-muscle coverage, label provenance and measured vessel
   context across source donors/assays. Reuse archived TBX5 results as exposed
   source evidence, not a new discovery.
2. Freeze independent mural identity and contractile programs, gene coverage
   and region strata. Compare donor pseudobulks with all eligible comparator
   populations; do not retain only the comparator that maximizes TBX5.
3. Estimate within-identity associations and identity-by-vessel contrasts only
   where units support them. Display sensitivity to library depth, mixed
   identities, gene omissions and anatomical restrictions.
4. Transfer the frozen measurements to full S03 or another qualified cohort.
   Use independent spatial annotations to test vessel context and localization;
   receptor/ligand coexpression supplies compatibility only.
5. Open a separate endothelial extension only after a new contract: test
   aerocyte/gCap/intermediate reproducibility and vessel-bed specificity.
   VWA1/HSPG2/PLVAP/MYC/HBEGF are source-context candidates, not a single
   interchangeable capillary score. Do not mix mural and endothelial cells
   into one clustering endpoint.

## Decision, figures and next step

Retain a regional mural-identity question if donor/study and independent
anatomical evidence agree. Narrow to a contractile-state association if the
pattern follows that program within identities. Missing anatomy limits the
claim to cell-class expression; it does not establish absence of specialization.

Mechanistic TBX5 regulation, hormone-triggered contraction, capillary support
and perfusion each require their own linked measurements. A21 owns the
Fzd4/endothelial-integrity mechanism; an overlapping capillary-function
extension belongs there. A purely descriptive TBX5 contrast is not a new RQ.

Planned panels: mural coverage; identity/contractile dot plot; donor contrast
forest; anatomically annotated vessel context; optional separate endothelial
state figure. Caption focus: **“Mural transcriptional specialization is
interpreted in the context of vessel identity and contractile state.”**

First deliverable: an anatomy-and-label evidence table separating observed
vessel context from inferred context. The next biological experiment should
follow whichever rival remains unresolved, not a presumed TBX5 mechanism.
