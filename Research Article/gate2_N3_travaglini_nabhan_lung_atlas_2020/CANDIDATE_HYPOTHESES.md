# Paper-local questions, not accepted mechanisms

**Current analysis label: Nb4.** This planning document precedes execution; see the [completed evidence and gallery](README.md) and [RQ derivation](reports/RQ_DERIVATION.md). Historical TN2020 run identifiers are preserved.

These questions originate in the owner's context notes and source reading.
They remain paper-local; none is assigned a global A-number.

| ID | Question and candidate explanation | Principal rival | Evidence needed to discriminate |
|---|---|---|---|
| TN-Q1 | Does AT2-s mark a reproducible human AT2-associated signaling phenotype? | Donor, assay, sampling and annotation differences | Consistent within-donor contrasts in an eligible independent human cohort; functional stemness requires separate lineage/self-renewal evidence |
| TN-Q2 | Do alveolar and adventitial fibroblast identities differ in chemokine-associated programs relevant to A13/A22? | Anatomy or abundance explains the contrast | Matched region/subtype coverage and recipient context; secretion and recruitment outcomes are separate evidence |
| TN-Q3 | Is MYRF associated with mature human AT1 identity beyond broad epithelial identity? | It is a correlated marker or a technical feature of captured AT1 cells | Donor-reproducible specificity in an independent reference; linked perturbation and later mature contribution are needed for a regulatory/fate claim |
| TN-Q4 | Is TBX5 selective for a pericyte program rather than shared mural identity? | Mural labels or vascular location explain enrichment | Independent pericyte/vascular-muscle distinction and a functional endpoint before a contractility claim |
| TN-Q5 | Which mouse-derived identity markers remain specific in human lung? | Age, sampling, assay and orthology differences mimic species divergence | Matched source designs and pinned orthology; leave incomparable populations unresolved |
| TN-Q6 | Which immune tissue-association signals persist within a lineage across matched blood/lung subjects? | Different immune subtype composition or transient recruitment | Verified paired subjects and subtype-matched coverage; longitudinal or independent residence evidence for durability |

**Priority:** TN-Q1 coverage and TN-Q2 identity context, followed by TN-Q3/TN-Q4
specificity if eligible. TN-Q5 is a transfer audit. TN-Q6 remains a secondary
scope because only two subjects have matched blood in the published design.
Broad cell-cell communication ranking is not the first deliverable. Existing
Table 5 outputs may support an audited source reconstruction later; they cannot
establish causal signaling, secretion or immune recruitment.

Positive, contradictory and inconclusive results all have a decision: retain an
eligible descriptive question, narrow the population/interpretation, or stop
until the missing design is available. Do not substitute another expression
score for a missing independent functional endpoint.
