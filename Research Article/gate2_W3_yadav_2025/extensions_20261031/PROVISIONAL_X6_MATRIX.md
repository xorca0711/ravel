## Unchanged substrate-balance question

[Wg-P06](../../gate2_W1_wagner_th17_autoimmunity/rq_derivation/P06.md) asks whether,
in human peripheral-blood neutrophil–primary human lung-fibroblast crosstalk within
a defined TGF-β context, recipient arginine sufficiency modifies the net collagen
response to source ARG1 activity. The hypothesis is partial compensation:
reducing source ARG1 could preserve arginine while reducing ornithine supply.
The strongest rival is supply dominance over the **actual source-conditioned
exposure range**. Neither compensation nor a crossover is established here.

The intended biological units are independent source and recipient donors with
declared crossing; wells and repeated measurements nest within them. Exact donor
crossing and source-specific perturbation validity remain unknown. The primary
endpoint remains newly produced/deposited collagen, both absolute and relative
to independently measured viable recipient exposure. Collagen RNA, alpha-SMA,
proliferation, protein concentration and repair function remain distinct.

## Informative outcomes and decisions still available

These are prospective interpretations from the unchanged P06 card, not observed
checkpoint-B outcomes. Substrate exposure, source specificity and actual donor
units must first qualify a comparison; no doses, sample size or effect margin
are invented.

| Qualified future observation | Interpretation that would be supported | Decision boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arginine preservation offsets reduced ornithine contribution within measured source-conditioned exposures | Supports a context-dependent balance | Quantify both contributions; do not claim a universal crossover |
| Collagen follows ornithine despite independently characterized arginine exposure | Favors supply dominance in that context | Preserve the unfavorable compensation result |
| Recipient arginine affects collagen, but source activity never changes its exposure enough | Supports recipient nutrient requirement, not source-level compensation | Do not extrapolate an artificial starvation boundary to the coculture |
| Effects follow cytokine changes, cell loss, translation stress or OAT/PRODH/ROS signaling without route evidence | Supports an alternative explanation | Do not relabel the residual effect as substrate transfer |
| A pattern appears only under complete deprivation | Supports an artificial nutrient boundary | Actual system relevance remains unsupported |

The missing measurements are therefore concrete: source-conditioned absolute
substrates across exposure, source-specific intervention validity, recipient
routing if a transfer claim is intended, and collagen with independent viable
recipient exposure. Source and recipient survival and cytokine context address
specified alternatives. Extra arms, tracers or pathway assays are justified by
the intended inference, not by a requirement to eliminate every conceivable rival.
Model access, timing, donor crossing, assay performance, relevant effect size and
precision remain unknown. This matrix is not an experiment package.

The epithelial candidates remain separate questions. A source intervention may
reduce collagen yet fail to restore mature epithelium, or support RNA may coexist
with matrix RNA. Neither is a contradiction without linked functional measures.
Direct epithelial fate/function evidence is the missing discriminator; a positive
substrate result would not fill it automatically.
