## Published premise and repository observation

Yadav provides intervention, protein and localization evidence for the nominated
immune–fibroblast circuit. Our source reanalysis preserves those selected effects,
but fibroblast Il6 genotype RNA is heterogeneous and the cell classes include
substantial mixed signals. Protein assays not previously reconstructed are a
concrete next comparison. The broader repo distinguishes source RNA, delivered
protein, receptor competence, matrix production and mature epithelial function.

## Working comparisons and consequential alternatives

- X1 tests dependence on a fixed existing mixed-cell category. Stability does not
  exclude ambient RNA, doublets or preparation confounding; dependence limits the
  original core-label interpretation. No threshold is tuned after seeing effects.
- X2 tests published source-value directions across explicit contexts. RNA/protein
  disagreement may reflect timing, units, composition or distinct preparations;
  it does not identify post-transcriptional control.
- X3 separates sample-level time/state expression from state abundance. State
  definitions, cell capture and cross-sectional sampling remain alternatives.
- X4 asks whether the nominated culture context represents the relevant tissue
  measurement. Assay, donor, lobe, passage and disease-stage confounding must be
  distinguished before attributing a difference to culture or progression.
- X5 asks whether ARG1/IL6R patterns survive source/capture and inclusion choices
  within qualified human contexts. No expression-positive preselection, mixed
  species integration or common lineage is presumed.
- X6 retains P06's compensation-versus-supply hypothesis and OAT/ROS, cytokine and
  viability alternatives. A separate functional bridge is needed for epithelium.

This is measurement validation, source reproduction and conditional context/model
comparison. A contribution beyond the closest published experiment is not yet
established for the new external branches; qualification may end in a justified hold.
