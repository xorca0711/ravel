## Wg-P06 remains a substrate-balance question

The [unchanged P06 card](../../gate2_W1_wagner_th17_autoimmunity/rq_derivation/P06.md)
asks whether recipient arginine sufficiency modifies the net collagen response to
source ARG1 activity in **human peripheral-blood neutrophil–primary human lung
fibroblast crosstalk within a defined TGF-β context**. The hypothesis is that
arginine preservation can partly offset lost ornithine contribution when
recipient arginine is limiting. The strongest rival is supply dominance without
meaningful compensation over the **actual source-conditioned exposure range**.
A crossover and its threshold are not presumed.

Independent source/recipient donors and declared crossing would define a future
design; wells and repeated measurements would nest within them. Primary function
remains newly produced/deposited collagen, both absolute and relative to an
independently measured viable-recipient exposure. No exact crossing, dose, effect
margin, sample size or laboratory capability has been invented.

| Published premise / existing evidence | Missing discriminator and informative outcome |
| --- | --- |
| [Yadav](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188734), Fig. 3/S3C–D, 5D–F and 6G, motivates the nominated circuit/model; the repository reconstructs those same sources. | Actual source-conditioned extracellular arginine/ornithine exposure and source-specific perturbation validity are needed. Coculture-wide inhibition is not automatically a source-specific effect. |
| [Hamanaka 2025](https://doi.org/10.1042/BCJ20253033), Results Fig. 3–6, supplies the recipient arginine-requirement premise; it supersedes the 2024 preprint, not an independent replication. | If arginine preservation offsets lost ornithine contribution within the measured range, it supports the context boundary. If recipient arginine matters but source activity never changes its exposure enough, the recipient requirement stands while source-level compensation remains unsupported. |
| [Lee 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s12276-024-01170-w), Results Fig. 6–7, and the source OAT/rescue panels retain the PRODH/ROS/TGF signaling alternative. | OAT perturbation alone does not identify precursor supply. Recipient incorporation into proline/collagen, separated from polyamine pools and signaling, is needed for a transfer-route claim. Collagen tracking ornithine despite independently characterized arginine exposure favors supply dominance in that context. |
| Existing relative metabolites, protein and culture observations do not jointly measure exposure, route and function. | Effects explained by cytokines, source/recipient survival, glutamine/proline, translation stress or signaling must remain alternatives. A pattern restricted to artificial complete deprivation does not establish relevance to the nominated coculture. |

and [literature context](LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md) retain completed reads, versions and
