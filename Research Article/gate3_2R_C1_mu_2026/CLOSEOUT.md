## S5. What Ross and Zeng distinguish

Ross et al. (Figs. 2–4) selectively depleted myeloid-biased HSCs in aged mice and measured hematopoietic and immune outcomes. That perturbation supports precursor composition as a causal route in their model. Mu's MPP3 frequency and lower mature myeloid fraction are compatible with altered composition. Mu's separate CBC shows a larger lymphocyte denominator with little absolute neutrophil or monocyte change, so these data do not establish the same output change as Ross. Greater or lower IL-6-high fractions can also arise through selection among CD68-positive states. [Ross et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07238-x)

Zeng et al. (Figs. 1–3, 5 and Supplementary Tables 8–9/12) compare human HSC subsets and progeny following exposure and recovery in a xenograft design. Their recovery comparison makes persistent accessibility and RNA changes interpretable within subsets. Mu's β-glucan phase precedes harvest and rechallenge, but the harvested fraction is lineage-negative marrow and differences can follow altered subset mixture or continuing SIRT3 expression. The four aged HSC RNA rows confirm a bulk respiratory-transcript direction under continued genotype exposure. They do not establish the within-HSC program required by the plan for a Zeng signature comparison. No scoring was performed. [Zeng et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10522-7) · [Verified literature context](LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md)

## S6. What still requires withdrawal or rechallenge

The ED1c–d contrasts support a priming-history-dependent reduction in progeny cytokine response. A matched secondary challenge after recovery and withdrawal of the initiating cue and controlled withdrawal of SIRT3 expression would distinguish retained effects from continuing regulation. Tracking independently identified donor HSC subsets and progeny would distinguish changed cell supply from changes within a lineage. The aged blood and liver readouts support changes in composition and macrophage phenotype, with cohort and section linkage unresolved. Transfer into a young host and antibody blockade support a transferable donor contribution and an IL-1β-sensitive recipient step. Serial donor tracking after transfer and a matched recipient challenge would test persistence. Fig. 7b supports a recipient endurance contrast across lineage arms while the mixed inverted-screen results limit its extension to other functions. Linking inflammatory readouts to the same recipients and timepoints would address a separate mediation question. The aged HSC RNA libraries belong upstream and have no individual linkage to recipient physiology. [Results and frozen evidence](RESULTS.md)
