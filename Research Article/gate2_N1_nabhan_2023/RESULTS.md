# Nb2: initial execution and the questions it leaves open

The accessible analysis plan has now been executed: all 18 source bulk libraries,
an adapted Figure 4 reconstruction, direct agonist comparisons, GO/Hallmark
sensitivity, and human/mouse receptor-context analyses. The paper namespace is
**Nb2**, within the requested `gate2_N1_nabhan_2023` folder. Ten source propositions
and eight owner-ordered hypotheses remain registered separately.

The principal bulk result is coherent direction with limited inference. Relative
to withdrawal, both receptor agonists increase the source Wnt/AT2 panels. The
three resolved Hippo-associated genes are higher for Fzd5 than CHIR across all
cross-arm library comparisons, but the individual genes do not pass genome-wide
FDR <0.05. Fzd6 is less consistent across libraries. Crim2 remains unresolved;
Crim1 was not substituted. Public metadata do not resolve culture/preparation
identity, and PCA shows marked library heterogeneity. No samples were deleted
to reproduce the published totals.

The most useful exploratory correction is that impressive oxidative-phosphorylation
enrichment weakens when gene correlation is estimated. It should not become a
metabolic mechanism claim. The source-like Hippo observation is a better-defined
follow-up premise, still requiring independent state and later-function evidence.

In human IPF KRT5−/KRT17+ cells, FZD1 is detectable but FZD6 remains higher in all
three eligible participants, including the depth-standardized comparison. Only
one IPF donor passes the main transitional-AT2 coverage floor. Fibroblast control
coverage is also too sparse for the intended disease comparison. In mouse, Fzd4
is prominent across capillary, arterial and venous states, so the vascular proposal
must distinguish general endothelial identity from regeneration-specific action.

**No new hypothesis is validated and no claim grade changes.** These results
advance the repository sequence from reading to measured observations and
specified next tests. Exact reproduction still needs source preparation/design
metadata, original atlas access/version recovery and functional unit-level tables.
See [execution/replay](EXECUTION.md), [stage ledger](metadata/stage_status.tsv),
and [numerical verification](metadata/execution_validation.json).
