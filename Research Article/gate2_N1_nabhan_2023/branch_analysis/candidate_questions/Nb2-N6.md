# Nb2-N6 candidate: does fibroblast subtype determine the Fzd1/Fzd2 functional balance?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** Nb2-N6. **Connections:** A13 epithelial plasticity/niche context
and A15 fibroblast activation; neither current outcome is replaced by RNA scores.

**RQ.** Do Fzd1 and Fzd2 have different effects on epithelial support and matrix
remodeling depending on fibroblast subtype and injury stage?

**Proposed hypothesis.** Fzd1-high and Fzd2-high receptor contexts mark different
fibroblast programs; a receptor's benefit depends on subtype and stage rather
than its abundance or fibroblast proliferation alone. A uniquely beneficial
Fzd1 effect is a stronger, currently unsupported hypothesis.

**Observed premise.** In five paired day42 samples, AF2 has more Fzd1, while AF1
has more Fzd2, support-ligand RNA and ECM RNA. The Fzd1/Fzd2/support directions
remain in all eight floor20 pairs; floor100 has no eligible pairs. Within AF1,
the Fzd1–support association is weak and changes sign under omission.
[Executed N6](../RESULTS.md#n6-fibroblast-subtype-changes-the-fzd1-interpretation).

**Test specification.** Unit: independent fibroblast source/preparation, retaining
epithelial-recipient identity where cocultures are used. Compare receptor-specific
effects within AF1 and AF2, then estimate receptor-by-subtype and stage differences.
Keep epithelial mature output and durable matrix deposition/remodeling as separate
endpoints. Include Fzd2/7 comparators and independently measured target engagement.
The source Fzd1/2/7 antibody and broad agonist studies cannot isolate Fzd1.

**Rival and falsifier.** Subtype composition or general growth could account for
the association. A Fzd1-specific benefit weakens if Fzd2/7 yield comparable effects
or subtype matching removes it. ECM and support genes may rise together, so an
RNA dichotomy of beneficial AF1 versus harmful AF2 is not assumed.

**Current decision:** retain as a subtype-specific niche hypothesis. The
[2025 Fzd2 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41257888/) motivates a concrete
comparator; the current atlas does not establish accelerated healing or fibrosis benefit.
