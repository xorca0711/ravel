# Nb2-N1 candidate: does a biological off-period permit maturation between bouts?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** Nb2-N1. **Question owner connections:** A4/A8; A14 supplies a
duration analogy for another pathway, not ownership of this Wnt question.

**RQ.** Does verified termination of Fzd signaling increase mature alveolar
descendant output between regenerative bouts while retaining a responsive AT2 pool?

**Proposed hypothesis.** Expansion and maturation have different temporal
requirements: an effective off-period permits differentiation that sustained
signaling constrains, without exhausting future regenerative capacity.

**Observed premise.** The AT1-associated score is higher at48 than24 hours after
CHIR withdrawal in all library-omission comparisons, but its conditional interval
is wide and Wnt/cycling directions are unstable. Fzd agonists preserve more
AT1-associated RNA than CHIR, not more than withdrawal48. [Executed N1](../RESULTS.md#n1-withdrawal-supplies-a-maturation-associated-lead-not-a-dosing-answer).

**Test specification.** Unit: independent starting lineage/preparation, with
within-unit repeated observations explicitly linked. Compare continuous versus
intermittent receptor stimulation; define in advance whether cumulative exposure
or initial expansion is the matching target. Measure actual target engagement
during the off-period, mature descendants per starting labeled population,
survival, residual AT2 capacity and response to a later bout separately. The
primary hypothesis concerns the schedule effect on lineage-linked mature output;
AT1 RNA alone is a supporting measurement. Set a useful-effect margin before
examining the new outcomes.

**Rival and falsifier.** Selective cell loss or reduced growth can mimic declining
AT2 markers. The hypothesis weakens if off-periods lower cell number without
increasing mature descendant output, or eliminate subsequent regenerative capacity.

**Current decision:** retain as a specific, connected timing question. The public
CHIR-withdrawal libraries cannot establish intermittent Fzd sufficiency; no such
schedule or serial culture dataset was recovered.
