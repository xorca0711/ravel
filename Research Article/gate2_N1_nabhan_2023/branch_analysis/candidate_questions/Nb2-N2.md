# Nb2-N2 candidate: which YAP-associated output accompanies differentiation competence?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** Nb2-N2. **Connections:** A1 regulatory context, A5 program reuse,
A8 mature contribution, A10 independent organoid outcome.

**RQ.** Does Fzd stimulation produce a selective, reversible YAP/TEAD-associated
component that can coexist with AT2 identity and later maturation, rather than
the broad response to sustained Hippo-kinase loss?

**Proposed hypothesis.** Receptor-level stimulation induces a restricted output
whose duration and starting state determine later differentiation competence;
the source three-gene increase is insufficient to identify that state.

**Observed premise.** Fzd5−CHIR and Fzd6−CHIR source-panel shifts survive omissions,
but the projected sustained-YT response changes sign under omissions. The latter
also scores higher in atlas AT1 than AT2, so it is not specific to maladaptive
persistence. Only34/49 up and47/48 down disjoint reference genes map in Nb2.
[Executed N2](../RESULTS.md#n2-the-source-yap-associated-component-does-not-establish-a-common-broad-state).

**Test specification.** Unit: independent culture/donor with linked early and
later observations. Measure a target-specific YAP/TEAD readout, canonical Wnt
output, AT2 identity, cycling/stress and subsequent mature output separately.
Establish same-cell coexistence rather than bulk co-enrichment. Compare transient
and sustained receptor stimulation with an appropriate downstream reference in
the same context. A mediator claim requires a discriminating perturbation, not
adjustment for treatment-induced cycling. Shared targets do not separate YAP
from TAZ; MST/LATS activation and YAP/TAZ activation have different meanings.

**Rival and falsifier.** Mechanical stress, altered mixture or generic growth
could explain the small panel. The hypothesis weakens if the component does not
track target engagement, is not reversible, or predicts persistent abnormal
states instead of lineage-linked maturation.

**Current decision:** prioritize with N1/N3, while retaining an unmeasured
target-specific activity gate. Gaona is a contextual perturbation comparator,
not proof of a Fzd-mediated mechanism or a validated fate classifier.
