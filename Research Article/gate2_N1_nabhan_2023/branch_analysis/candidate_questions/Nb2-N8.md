# Nb2-N8 candidate: what endogenous input makes Fzd6 relevant to alveolar repair?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** the pre-existing Nb2-N8 companion, distinct from the owner's
seven focused branches. **Connection:** N5 input/context and N3 starting state.

**RQ.** Is an endogenous ligand/co-receptor context sufficient and necessary for
a Fzd6-dependent alveolar response, or is the synthetic agonist accessing a
capacity that endogenous inputs do not normally use in that state?

**Proposed hypothesis.** Fzd6 competence becomes relevant only under particular
ligand, co-receptor and epithelial-state combinations. Synthetic Fzd–Lrp
activation demonstrates a capacity without identifying the physiological input.

**Observed premise.** Fzd6-associated canonical target induction is present under
synthetic stimulation in Nb2; source ligand/withdrawal arms and atlas expression
provide compatibility context. They do not include ligand-selective perturbation
crossed with Fzd6 dependence. A list of candidate Wnt transcripts cannot identify
receptor use or signaling direction. [Executed N8](../RESULTS.md#n8-endogenous-fzd6-input-remains-a-distinct-missing-perturbation).

**Test specification.** Unit: independent source/recipient preparation. Require
a defined endogenous input, verified receptor dependence, appropriate co-receptor
context and a linked response in the intended epithelial state. Distinguish
ligand availability, functional receptor engagement and downstream competence.
Specify input-by-receptor and input-by-state comparisons before outcome inspection.

**Rival and falsifier.** Other receptors may mediate the endogenous input while
the synthetic reagent bypasses that specificity. A proposed endogenous mechanism
weakens if its response persists without Fzd6, or occurs only with the synthetic
cross-linking context.

**Current decision:** retain as an eligibility-limited companion, not an executed
ligand-identification result or a new A-series question.
