# Nb2-N4 candidate: is recovery after verified Fzd5 loss compensatory?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** Nb2-N4. **Home:** this Nb2-specific perturbation question.

**RQ.** After target scope and the completeness of receptor loss are matched,
does sustained Fzd5 loss elicit an alternative functional route that acute
receptor inhibition does not?

**Proposed hypothesis.** A delayed adaptive response can restore growth/signaling
after verified receptor loss; it must be distinguished from retained protein,
unedited-cell selection and Fzd8-related differences in the blocking condition.

**Observed premise.** The source's Fzd5 editing score is65%; its inhibitory
antibody targets Fzd5/8. There are no KO or inhibitory-antibody libraries in
GSE208770, and no linked editing/protein/growth unit map was recovered.
[Executed N4](../RESULTS.md#n4-the-compensation-contrast-is-not-identifiable).

**Test specification.** First require matched target scope, verified functional
loss and comparable starting context. Unit: independent preparation, with early
and later measurements linked. Identify alternative receptor/regulator changes
alongside actual functional recovery, then distinguish their contribution from
residual unedited cells. A genotype-by-time comparison needs verified identity
and timing; agonist RNA and baseline coexpression cannot substitute.
Transcript-decay-dependent adaptation is a precedent from
[El-Brolosy et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1064-z), not an
automatic consequence of this particular deletion.

**Rival and falsifier.** Recovery that follows residual receptor protein,
unmodified clones or broader antibody specificity favors those explanations.
No functional recovery after verified loss, or no dependence on the proposed
alternative mechanism, weakens the compensation hypothesis.

**Current decision:** the current mechanistic test is ineligible. Retain the RQ
with a comparability-first gate; no compensation result is claimed from the
receptor/feedback table generated for agonist conditions.
