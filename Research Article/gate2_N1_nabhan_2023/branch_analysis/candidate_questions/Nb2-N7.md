# Nb2-N7 candidate: Fzd4-dependent renewal or general endothelial maintenance?

Paper-local interpretation draft. Not registered under `RQ_Specified`; its scope
may be merged, reframed or dropped after synthesis of the branch results.

**Derived from:** Nb2-N7. **Home:** Nb2 vascular extension.

**RQ.** Does Fzd4 contribute specifically to capillary renewal and descendant
production beyond its contribution to endothelial survival and barrier function?

**Proposed hypothesis.** A context-dependent Fzd4 response may contribute to
general-capillary renewal, even though receptor expression is broadly vascular.
Specific progenitor function requires an effect on lineage output that is
distinguishable from altered survival or general vascular performance.

**Observed premise.** CAP1/CAP2 marker differences agree in all eight paired
day42 samples, and CAP1 has more Fzd4 than CAP2 in8/8. Arterial and venous expression
is also high. CAP1's pooled Fzd4–cycling rho+0.738 survives depth/floor checks, but
within-round rhos are **−0.400 and+1.000** (n4 each). This inconsistency limits the
lead before any functional interpretation. [Executed N7](../RESULTS.md#n7-a-vascular-association-weakens-under-experimental-round-scrutiny).

**Test specification.** Unit: independent animal/preparation with source round,
tracing window, genotype and sex resolved. First replicate the within-capillary
association with a validated finer state crosswalk. A mechanistic test then needs
verified receptor perturbation linked to self-renewal, aerocyte/other descendant
identity and vascular performance. Estimate lineage contribution per starting
population alongside survival and barrier endpoints; do not define a progenitor
by Fzd4 positivity or a cycling score.

**Rival and falsifier.** A round/substate effect, or general endothelial loss,
could explain reduced renewal. Progenitor specificity weakens if receptor effects
are fully accounted for by survival/barrier changes or fail to reproduce within
matched source cohorts. Broad expression alone does not falsify contextual function.

**Current decision:** retain as a conditional hypothesis, with replication and
lineage/function gates ahead of a stem-cell mechanism claim. Gillich's capillary
lineage evidence supplies biological motivation, not a Fzd4 dependency result.
