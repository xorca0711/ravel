The completed exposed GSE160332 description covers activated human CD8 RNA
after WT IL-10, 10-DE, super-10 or vehicle. 10-DE responses are broadly weaker;
super-10 has mixed gene-specific directions rather than uniform amplification.
Its IFNG/IL18RAP changes are higher than WT and GZMB/IL22/SOCS3 lower across
the specified scale/omission cases. CCL1 remains omission-sensitive.

Donor/preparation identities and pairing remain unknown. These are finite-library
RNA observations, not independent replication, myeloid suppression, secretion,
functional protection or a population treatment effect. The published Fig. 5
already reports related patterns from this source; see the
[primary-source interpretation](../../docs/research_reviews/2026-11-08-saxton-reading/POST_RESULT_LITERATURE.md).
