fibrosis experiments do not establish a mechanism in Peng's cancer samples.

## Informative outcomes and limits

Concordant detection and positive-cell intensity would localize the aggregate
RNA pattern to both measured distribution components. A depth/confidence-sensitive
pattern would narrow that interpretation. Discordance is retained, not rescued
by a new score. Shared mapping genes (IL1R1, IL1R2, IL1RN, TNFAIP3), ambient RNA,
mixtures and selection prevent an independently validated within-state claim.

Adjacent-spot association that changes after depth/composition adjustment would
identify sensitivity to broad RNA organization. Persistent or heterogeneous
association remains spatial RNA description. Graph distance is in lattice hops,
not micrometers; same-spot RNA, cross-spot association and cell contact stay distinct.
All patients and contrary results remain visible. No novelty or acceptance claim.
