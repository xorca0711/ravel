## Closest published premises and limits

The existing review checked recent 2024–2026 primary work and older close
precedents. Its coverage/access record is retained; this source recovery does
not claim an additional comprehensive literature or correction/retraction audit.

- [Choi 2020, Fig. 7E–H](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487779/):
  persistent IL-1beta/withdrawal and maturation were already examined. Generic
  IL-1 withdrawal is an established premise, not a novel project generated here.
- [Hill 2023, Fig. 3e–g and Extended Data 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7614604/):
  macrophage IL-1beta and mutant epithelial tumor promotion are a close functional
  precedent. Peng adds early-lesion pathology and spatial organization.
- [Han 2024, Fig. 4 and lineage results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07113-9):
  the prior KAC atlas/signature and reused GSE222901 share provenance with part of
  Peng's evidence; paper count is not independent replication.
- [Cardoso 2026, Fig. 4 and Extended Data 8–10](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10399-6):
  epithelial AREG–fibroblast EGFR remodeling is a relevant reciprocal-niche
  alternative. The earlier review read indexed result/figure text, not the full
  methods/supplement; this is not established in Peng's exact patients or a
  validation of A13's different proxy.

The intended contribution is **source qualification**, then **shared-source
reproduction**, with a conditional **measurement/model-discrimination extension**
under A12. Independent-cohort transport requires its own eligibility. A repair-
versus-cancer fate experiment remains a different longitudinal question. No
novelty, mechanism, clinical interception benefit or combination synergy is
certified by this folder or its ungated label.
