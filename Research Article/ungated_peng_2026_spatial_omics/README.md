## Question and contribution

Can author-defined pathology, epithelial state and measured spatial context be
joined at a defensible sample level, sufficiently to distinguish a within-state
neighborhood association from a change in epithelial mixture or tissue packing?
The immediate purpose is **measurement/source qualification**, not a new test of
carcinogenesis. The endpoint is join and annotation availability. The patient is
the intended biological unit; lesions, sections, cores, cells and overlapping
neighborhoods are nested observations. Metadata work permits unresolved
independence but cannot support population inference.

The paper adds spatial and pathological context to Ravel's interest in epithelial
states, reception and subsequent outcomes. A KAC RNA state, reactive type II
pneumocyte morphology (RPII), an inferred CNA clone and a later functional fate are
different measurements. The paper connects some of them; it does not make them
interchangeable. Human cross-sectional ordering is not lineage tracing.
