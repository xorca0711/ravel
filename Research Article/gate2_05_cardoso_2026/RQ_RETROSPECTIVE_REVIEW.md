## Strict contribution decisions

<a id="a2-source-state"></a>
**A2 — accepted conditional source-state branch.** A change in epithelial state mixture, or in expression within a state, could alter the available epithelial AREG source relative to myeloid sources. This is an upstream candidate under A2; it does not answer delivery versus abundance. The corrected 10-donor molecule-matched gap supplies motivation, with only 6/10 positive differences. Decompose a frozen within-donor source contrast into predefined state proportions and within-state expression, and require transport to independent donors. Depth, annotation and changing myeloid composition are rivals. Failure to predict held-out gaps would weaken this nominated RNA-source model. Protein release and controlled delivery remain separate requirements for A2's mechanism.

**A9 — no new branch.** Its current hypothesis already asks whether receptor competence modifies response to AREG, explicitly using the 7/22 coverage observation. Add this review as provenance if helpful; do not present re-reading the same correction as additional support. A same-state, common-depth receptor screen is enabling evidence only; verified engagement and receptor-specific functional responses remain decisive.

**A13 — accepted constraint, no positive feedback claim.** Injury-generic fibroblast expression and unreplicated tumour contrasts require any fibroblast-context candidate to distinguish state abundance from within-state programme changes, and shared injury from tumour context. E4 does not explain the mechanism sustaining tumour expression. Marginal AREG correlations are not tests of fibroblast information beyond macrophage IL1B, so they must not be used as support or a negative result for A13's joint model.

**Rejected promotions.** Do not add a tumour-specific “second signal” from retained Runx1/Pdgfrb; a new Fst/Runx2 lineage from marker co-detection; a species-specific ligand mechanism from unmatched deposits; or ADAM17-mediated sort escape from RNA. Each requires evidence absent from the executed contrast. Their rejection as current RQ contributions does not establish biological absence.

## Suggested addition under A2

> **Conditional candidate, related to the source-state branch (Cardoso review, 1 October 2026).** Does epithelial state composition versus within-state expression explain the donor-specific, molecule-matched epithelial–myeloid AREG gap? The corrected gap is positive in 6/10 donors at 1,000 UMIs. Predefined states, fixed RNA sampling and independent donor evaluation can test this upstream source model; they cannot establish secretion, delivery or recipient activation. Changing myeloid composition and annotation remain rivals. See [the retrospective review](RQ_RETROSPECTIVE_REVIEW.md#a2-source-state).

The register already incorporates the main A2/A9 corrections. This proposed addition makes the conditional branch explicit without replacing their existing biological tests. [Study overview and figures](README.md) · [Historical trial index](trials/README.md).
