## Strict contribution decisions

| Existing RQ | Proposed interpretation | Decision, rival and discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| **A1, regulatory distinction of transitional states** | Preserved chromatin could explain reversibility despite low AT2 RNA. | **Do not add as a supported branch.** M2/M3 withdraw the premise. A1 already retains the valid broader hypothesis. Normal differentiation, arrest and measurement depth remain rivals; independent lineage/function endpoints plus a calibrated regulatory assay are needed. A negative accessibility direction alone cannot select a fate. |
| **A4, sequential Wnt/IL-1 response** | Axin2/Il1r1 overlap, co-accessibility or a deletion-induced Wnt change might identify a shared responsive lineage. | **Already represented as a conditional question; no duplicate.** Sparse transcripts, failed controls and unverifiable target engagement do not distinguish stable subsets from temporal states. Reporter history, present activity and receptor function must be separately measured in the same lineage. Do not read the withheld A2 Wnt contrast as a biological result. |
| **A5, development versus adult repair** | A transitional RNA component may be reused in development. | **Already incorporated; retain the specificity control.** Neonatal two-marker positivity motivates the question but does not establish a shared mechanism. Developmental immaturity and injury transition need broader independently defined panels and source-appropriate biological units. |
| **A8, maturation beyond shared transition** | Late AT1 differentiation may require an additional programme. | **Already incorporated; no duplicate.** The source biology motivates a maturation endpoint; local seed/threshold dependence limits the small RNA proxy. A measured mature-cell outcome distinct from the predictor is needed. |
| **A14, exposure duration and recipient compartment** | Persistent IL-1 exposure might impede recovery, with a separate fibroblast contribution. | **Source-paper motivation, not a new result from these libraries.** Current A14 already separates the hypotheses and asks for verified withdrawal, target engagement and recovery outcomes. The local static library comparison adds no withdrawal mechanism or fibroblast-specific necessity. |

The later analyses mostly **narrow or retract premises** rather than add a
distinct mechanism. No additional canonical branch passes the distinctness
test here. Preserve the useful hypotheses under their existing cards without
reusing failed-gate values or calling same-laboratory consistency replication.
