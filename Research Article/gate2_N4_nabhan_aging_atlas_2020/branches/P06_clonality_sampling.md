# Nb5-P06 Immune clonality versus sampling and compartment selection

## Question and decision

Does an age-associated change in observed repertoire clonality persist after
accounting for sampling and cell-subset composition? Decide whether a later
study should investigate repertoire structure or first resolve measurement bias.
This is an agent-proposed extension of the immune-repertoire source results,
separate from the owner's microglial-state candidate.

## Hypothesis and strongest rival

The hypothesis is a within-animal repertoire concentration difference across
age groups within comparable sampled compartments. The strongest rival is
different numbers of successfully assembled receptors, clone-detection
probability or changing B/T subtype and organ representation. A single clone
sampled across organs must not become several independent clones or animals.

## Required metadata and measurement

Need Supplementary Table 9 or equivalent author clonotype assignments linked
to cells, animals, age, tissue, lineage/subset and sequence-assembly status.
Recover receptor chain and clonotype definitions exactly; do not infer
clonotypes from expression clusters. Total captured, assembly-eligible,
successfully assembled and assigned cells need separate denominators.

The unit is mouse. Primary proposed endpoint: the fraction of successfully
assembled cells belonging to a non-singleton clonotype within a declared
compartment, accompanied by sample size and clone-size distribution. If the
source uses a different denominator, reproduce it separately and explain the
adaptation. This is receptor-defined relatedness, not a founder-lineage assay.

## Bounded analysis and alternatives

1. Reconcile published totals and clone assignments without reassembling reads.
   Mark whether only aggregate source arithmetic or animal-level data are available.
2. Freeze tissue and subset comparisons from coverage, then quantify per-mouse
   clonality with assembly success and tissue representation alongside it.
3. Use bounded repeated subsampling within mice over a justified common
   sampling range; report sampling sensitivity and dropped mice. Subsampling
   cannot remove all non-random assembly failure.
4. Assess whether the age comparison persists after supported compartment
   restriction and leave-one-mouse-out checks. Any diversity statistic added
   beyond the primary endpoint is a declared secondary family.

Stable comparisons motivate a repertoire-structure hypothesis. Collapse under
sampling control favors measurement selection. Missing cell-to-clone-to-mouse
links restrict work to descriptive source reconstruction; pooled fractions
cannot supply mouse-level inference.

## Further RQ frame and limit

Neither clonality nor clone size establishes antigen specificity, immune
protection, proliferative rate or clinical benefit. A future discriminator
would need independently measured repertoire function or longitudinal clone
information in a qualified model. No antigen or intervention is selected.
No direct equivalent global RQ is assigned; A17's founder-lineage growth
question remains biologically distinct.

**Additional-analysis frame:** per-mouse clonality and assembly coverage,
clone-size distributions and sampling-sensitivity curves. Sequence reassembly
is outside the initial plan and would require a separate contract.
