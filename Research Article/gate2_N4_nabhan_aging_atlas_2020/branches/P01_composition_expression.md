# Nb5-P01 Composition and within-type ageing

**Current continuation (3 October 2026):** Lung/bladder composition accounting is complete for its frozen stage. Official metadata transfer does not restore a renal epithelial denominator among the previously sampled 24-month kidney cells; whole-kidney interpretation remains held.
See the [completion audit](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md) before the earlier execution checkpoint below.

**Question:** For qualified tissue and cell-type strata, how much of a measured
age association reflects captured composition, detection frequency or
within-type expression? The decision is whether a candidate needs an
abundance measurement, a within-state functional measurement, or better data.

**Working hypothesis:** Some age associations persist within a stable cell
type after captured mixtures and technical coverage are considered.
**Strongest rival:** Differential recovery or shifts among subtypes generate
the apparent expression change. Sex, processing and selective survival are
additional rivals. This is a measurement question; the broad separation is
already established source logic, not a novelty claim.

**Unit and endpoints:** Mouse, with tissues, assays and cells nested within
mouse. Report captured type fraction within a fixed parent compartment;
marker-detected fraction within that type; and a specified expression summary
within that type. Every endpoint keeps its own denominator. Source-panel
aggregation is a separate descriptive reproduction.

**Discriminator:** Compare observed mixture summaries with a fixed-reference
mixture on the same linear measurement scale and common type support. Estimate
within-type age contrasts separately. Fix reference weights from metadata and
a declared adult reference before outcome inspection; include the interaction
term in any composition/expression decomposition. A residual term is not a
causal effect. Do not combine log means as if they were linear totals.

**Actions:** Attenuation after standardization favors mixture accounting;
stable within-type differences justify a bounded functional follow-up;
inconsistent assay or animal results restrict the association; inadequate
coverage produces a hold. Captured proportions cannot establish absolute
tissue abundance, and RNA cannot establish senescence, repair or secretion.

## Organ-specific follow-up frame

A bounded kidney example can ask whether increased expression within a type
coexists with reduced captured representation. This is a subanalysis of the
same composition/expression estimand, not a duplicate global RQ. Any proposed
compensation mechanism additionally needs independently measured tissue function
and absolute abundance; a favorable RNA decomposition alone cannot establish it.
Freeze the source-defined genes and relevant types before inspecting estimates.

**Additional-analysis frame:** a per-mouse decomposition figure with observed
and fixed-mixture values, plus explicit common-support and uncertainty panels.
