# Primary precedents and contribution boundaries

3 October 2026. This targeted pass complements the [earlier source ledger](../rq_derivation/SOURCES.md).
Primary publications support the facts below; the proposed contribution and
routing are agent interpretations. Search topics included bladder stromal versus
epithelial ageing, microglial middle-age sex/region states, CD8 clonality within
naive/memory states, and paired-organ repertoire ageing. Review articles and
search summaries were used only to locate primary sources, not to establish
new biological claims. Literature completeness and novelty are not certified.

| Target | Closest primary evidence | Consequence for the registered question |
|---|---|---|
| A24 | [Al-Naggar et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14399) describe umbrella-cell senescence-like features across the lifespan; [Meguro et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-024-00704-1) examine aged fibroblasts in bladder tumour permissiveness | Differentiation and cancer context are explicit rivals/limits. Neither observation establishes an age-dependent stromal cause of normal barrier loss. The proposed population/function bridge remains missing. |
| A25 | [Li et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-023-00479-x) report sex-dependent microglial ageing with a female middle-age pattern | Generic intermediate states and sex divergence are already known. The remaining model comparison concerns reproducibility beyond mixtures/continuous responses in a specified region/sex. Published clustering does not substitute for this comparison. |
| A25 | [Hammond et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6655561/) and [Jin et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08350-8) establish lifespan or region-resolved microglial context | These constrain novelty and offer possible transport sources, subject to age/region/animal mapping. Jin's two-age design cannot by itself test an intermediate-age configuration. |
| A26 | [Shifrut et al. 2013](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2013.00379/full) report differing spleen/marrow CD4 repertoire ageing | Tissue-dependent repertoire ageing is prior knowledge. A26 preserves a separate CD8 subject and paired-state comparison; it does not claim to discover tissue dependence in general or treat CD4 as equivalent to CD8. |
| A27 | [Ahmed et al. 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19124721/) report repeated sequences and reduced diversity within aged naive CD8 cells | This is a closer precedent than broad CD8 abundance. A27 must not claim the first within-state age effect. Its initial aim is defined-state quantitative attribution/replication in spleen. |
| A26/A27 | [Mogilenko et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33271118/) report clonal GZMK-positive ageing-associated CD8 cells | Clonal ageing populations and multi-organ immune context are established. Novel functional claims do not follow from the local repertoire results. |

Ahmed's PubMed abstract and figure descriptions were read; the PMC HTML open
encountered a browser challenge. Shifrut's primary publisher full text was read.
Li's publisher text was rechecked. Previously read primary-source material and
the eight deposited GEO design snapshots were reused, not independently
rediscovered or counted as new replication.

An additional current lead, [Birder et al., 5 August 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-65316-8),
addresses urothelial ageing and 8-aminoguanine. Its indexed primary abstract was
available, but full-page retrieval failed. It is retained as a closer epithelial
ageing lead for A24, not treated as a completed stromal/barrier-methods review or
a reason to nominate a drug. The precise overlap requires full-text inspection
before making a stronger novelty claim. No therapeutic recommendation is made.

## Access remains distinct from independent evidence

- GSE207932 has female 3/14/24-month microglia in different contexts; baseline
  controls and individual units must be qualified before A25 transport.
- GSE145562 pools contributing mice before sequencing. It cannot supply the
  proposed paired-animal A26 or individual-animal A27 inference without a
  demonstrated identity recovery.
- GSE180128 is reused by the bladder senescence paper; that reuse is not a
  second independent atlas. GSE247123 is female bulk and GSE253338 is an aged
  reporter-selected stromal source, not a qualified normal barrier comparison.
- The NeMO brain landing page and other listed expression-file pointers are
  public access leads. No new expression archive, independent-cohort effect or
  laboratory capability was qualified in this review.

The earlier deposit hashes and exact source identities remain in the source
ledger. Different source releases, shared animals, cell partitions and internal
held-out folds never become independent study replication.
