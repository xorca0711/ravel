## What counts as overlap

We compare the organizing biological question, subject/population, context,
contrast, endpoint and result that would distinguish the leading rival.
Sharing a tissue, broad pathway, analytical method or vocabulary is insufficient
to merge questions. Conversely, a new dataset or demographic sensitivity alone
does not automatically create a new biological proposition.

A24 does not assert that all fibroblasts support epithelia by one mechanism.
A25 does not universalize an epithelial state model to microglia. A26 explicitly
asks whether tissue environments differ, and A27 does not generalize a spleen
CD8 result to CD4, marrow or immune function. These distinctions implement the
owner's heterogeneity instruction without labeling each technical sensitivity a
new RQ. New source-specific targets can remain important even when the broad
phenomenon already has precedent.

## Scientific assessment and interpretation limits

**A24:** biological focus is distinct, but the causal bridge is weakly grounded:
opposing compartment RNA directions motivate a question, not a stromal cause.
No actual stromal subtype or barrier assay is qualified. Registration preserves
the question while keeping its population/function nomination task explicit.

**A25:** population and model rivals are clear enough for question registration.
The proposal is currently an exposed RNA-organization/model-comparison aim.
Model identifiability, feature selection, region/sex scope and external units
remain unresolved. A better fit does not prove a discrete state or disease role.

**A26:** the paired-organ estimand is distinct. Its source gate is currently held,
because the completed exact subtype follow-up does not support marrow CD8.
The spleen/marrow focus is a new, outcome-exposed narrowing; it is not a frozen
primary analysis or a positive result selected by changing eligibility.

**A27:** the biological unit and local-clone endpoint are explicit, but fine
states and receptor denominators need qualification. Within-state age-related
clonality has precedent. The defensible initial contribution is quantitative
attribution and replication, not discovery of a new ageing mechanism.

**A3 extension:** normal-age reference data help assess a rival. They cannot
separate injury history from age, current context and processing when those
factors are confounded. Negative CAMERA findings and the ineligible ornithine
set remain adverse evidence; no replacement programme is selected.

All proposals retain exposed-outcome status, mouse-level units, separate
denominators and honest precision limits. No effect margin, mechanistic
mediator, sample size, independent replicate or favorable interpretation was
invented to complete the review. No claim grade changes.
