## Observation and gap

The [repertoire results](../EXTENSION_RESULTS.md#p06-compartment-local-repertoire-concentration)
and [CD8 follow-up](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md) show an 18-month mean above the
24-month mean for some endpoints. Different animals supplied each age group.
This is not evidence of within-animal recovery, reversal or a universal peak.

**Provisional question:** Does a specified middle-to-late-age contrast recur
across independent age cohorts after differences in population composition,
sex, sampling and survival-related selection are addressed?

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is cohort and survival selection, alongside changing cell
states and sparse sampling. An age-window interpretation predicts a reproducible
contrast for the same endpoint and biological population in independent cohorts
with comparable observation. Repeated measurements, where feasible for that
tissue and endpoint, would help distinguish within-animal change from cohort
replacement. Persistence of a cross-sectional contrast alone cannot rule out
selection. Loss after supported restriction favors a population-composition rival.

## Unit and endpoint

The unit is the animal, or the animal with repeated observations when that
design is actually available. Use separate fixed interval contrasts for the
parent P02 or P06 endpoint, not a fitted mechanistic curve through three means.
Sampling ages, estimand, meaningful magnitude and precision require prospective
justification. Brain sampling is terminal in the present sources; those animals
cannot provide a longitudinal cellular trajectory.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Schaum et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32669715/) already describe
organ-specific temporal ageing patterns. Their companion relationship with
TMS requires animal-overlap qualification before calling a comparison independent.
Li's microglial series offers another age schedule but does not remove sex,
region or study differences; see [source ledger](SOURCES.md). No qualified
independent, individually mapped repertoire age series was established here.

## Grounding and review decision

First define the parent endpoint and obtain age, animal, sex and inclusion or
attrition metadata sufficient to assess selection. If those data are missing,
retain interval-specific descriptive contrasts and the selection uncertainty.
Do not fit a flexible age curve solely to generate a peak or reopen failed
strata. This companion question becomes a distinct proposal only if review
identifies an age-window mechanism and a feasible discriminating design.
