## Observation and gap

The [owner note](../NOTE_RECONCILIATION.md) asks whether an intermediate microglial
state could inform Alzheimer-related biology. Age-associated atlas clusters
motivate that idea but do not establish a transition or an intermediate state.
The [current recovery](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md#what-was-recovered) supplies
all-age count-valued expression and exact identities for the source microglia.
The actual state-versus-mixture comparison remains unrun.

**Provisional question:** Within a defined brain region and sex, is a middle-age
microglial configuration reproducible across animals and studies, and does it
require a state component beyond mixtures of young/old distributions or a
smooth age response?

The possible biological increment is the region- and sex-specific configuration
and its reproducibility. It is not the generic existence of ageing microglia.

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is redistribution among existing microglial populations,
possibly combined with continuous age responses, regional sampling and sex or
processing differences. A configuration-specific explanation predicts coherent
cell-level structure within a region that generalizes to held-out animals and
cannot be adequately predicted by endpoint mixtures or a continuous baseline.

A mean-expression residual alone cannot distinguish a discrete state from
nonlinear regulation. An endpoint-mixture explanation predicts that changing
weights suffice; a continuous explanation predicts similar or better held-out
performance without an additional state. Overlap or unstable performance leaves
the models unresolved. Neither a UMAP island nor a post hoc marker list decides.

## Unit and endpoint

Mouse is the independent unit, with cells and regions nested. The proposed
endpoint is animal-level out-of-sample predictive adequacy of explicitly fixed
RNA-distribution models, with cell-state coherence as a separate requirement.
No numerical acceptance margin is invented. The current design has 6/4/4 mice
at 3/18/24 months; all 24-month mice are male. Therefore an all-sex comparison
does not identify a sex-adjusted full-age trajectory. A male-only exploration
has particularly sparse middle-age animals and cannot establish a sex interaction.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Li et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-023-00479-x) already report
different ageing patterns by sex, including a female middle-age pattern.
[Hammond et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6655561/) establish
lifespan-related microglial diversity. [Jin et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08350-8)
offer region-resolved young/old data, but no matching third age.
GSE207932 supplies female 3/14/24-month control and challenge contexts; only
qualified baseline controls could address normal ageing. Different middle ages
and regional coverage restrict transport. Animal-level mapping remains pending
in these external sources; see [access limits](SOURCES.md).
