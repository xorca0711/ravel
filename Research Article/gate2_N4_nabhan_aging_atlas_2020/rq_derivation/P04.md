## Observation and gap

The [repertoire extension](../EXTENSION_RESULTS.md#p06-compartment-local-repertoire-concentration)
shows different age patterns across thymus, spleen and marrow. Different
conditional sampling depths prevent direct between-tissue ranking. The
[subtype follow-up](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md) qualifies only spleen CD4/CD8
comparisons; it has not established a cross-organ, matched-state CD8 effect.

**Provisional question:** For the same immune identity in the same animal, does
an age-associated change depend on tissue context or reflect a shared systemic
association? A concrete proposed example is CD8 repertoire concentration within
matched differentiation states, conditional on P06 population qualification.
This example narrows a broad frame; other biologically justified endpoints
remain possible after review.

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is comparison of non-equivalent cell states, receptor
recovery or circulating populations across organs. A tissue-context association
predicts a reproducible age-by-tissue difference within paired animals and
comparable states at a common observation scale. A shared association predicts
similar contrasts after those restrictions. Wide uncertainty cannot establish
equality. Shared receptor sequences indicate observed distribution, not migration
direction or local expansion.

## Unit and endpoint

Independent animals supply paired organ measurements. For the repertoire example,
the endpoint is a prespecified within-animal difference in local clone
concentration at a comparable state and sampling depth, followed by its age
contrast. Multiple organs are not independent animals. Meaningful magnitude,
variance and precision remain unknown; no systemic-versus-local causal estimate
is available from the current cross-sectional data.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Zhang et al. 2021](https://elifesciences.org/articles/62293) examine shared and
cell-type-specific programmes using the same atlas; that is reused evidence.
[Schaum et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32669715/) establish
organ-dependent temporal patterns. Thus a broad shared-versus-specific scan
alone is not a novel biological question. GSE145562 includes several organs but
pools animals, limiting the proposed paired-animal test; see [source ledger](SOURCES.md).

## Grounding and review decision

Proceed only if a shared state, paired animal identity and comparable denominator
can be qualified. The current missing non-spleen exact subtype strata are a
material hold. Do not loosen labels merely to manufacture cross-organ support.
Without a separate biological discriminator, retain this as a P06 context
extension rather than duplicating it as a global RQ. A local niche mechanism
would require evidence beyond a paired association.
