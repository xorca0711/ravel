# CD8 repertoire concentration within differentiation states

**Nb5-P06 · Provisional distinct-question proposal · Scientific review pending.**
[Original branch](../branches/P06_clonality_sampling.md) · [Package](README.md).

## Observation and gap

The [subtype follow-up](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md#completed-biological-follow-up-cd4cd8-repertoire-concentration)
finds higher older spleen CD8 mean local-repeat fractions after broad subtype
restriction and conditional depth standardization. The depth is only five
CD8 cells per mouse; there are 4/3/4 mice at 3/18/24 months. CD4 has no
24-versus-3 difference, and its 18-month signal depends on one mouse.
[Figure 11](../EXTENSION_FIGURES.md#figure-11-subtype-resolved-repertoire)
retains these contrasting outcomes. CD4/CD8 restriction does not distinguish
naive, memory and activation states or remove receptor-recovery differences.

**Provisional question:** Is older spleen CD8 repertoire concentration mainly
redistribution among differentiation states, or does concentration also change
within comparable states?

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is expansion of the representation of an already concentrated
state, together with differential receptor recovery. A within-state explanation
predicts a reproducible age contrast under a fixed, independently defined state
composition and comparable receptor ascertainment. A redistribution explanation
predicts that the contrast attenuates once those conditions are satisfied.
Residual uncertainty with sparse states leaves both explanations open.

## Unit and endpoint

Animal is the independent unit. The proposed primary endpoint is the
state-specific fraction of receptor-reconstructed cells belonging to a clone
observed repeatedly within the same animal and tissue, at a declared common
sampling depth. Report all CD8 cells and receptor-recovered cells separately.
Define states without using clone size or a signature selected from the target
age contrast. State definition, adequate coverage and meaningful precision need
qualification; the current eligibility minimum was not a power calculation.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Mogilenko et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33271118/) already report
clonal GZMK-positive ageing-associated CD8 populations. Mere rediscovery is not
novel. The possible increment is the specified within-state-versus-redistribution
discriminator and its transport across independently identified animals.
[GSE145562](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE145562) contains
expression and receptor data, but its design pools three mice per age before
sequencing. Without recovered individual identities, those mice cannot be
counted as three independent single-cell replicates. See [source ledger](SOURCES.md).

## Grounding and review decision

First qualify finer state labels and receptor denominators in the local data,
then seek an external cohort with individual animal identity. If supported
states collapse to insufficient cells, report that hold without lowering the
threshold after inspecting effects. A later functional question could ask
whether repertoire concentration predicts independently measured response
breadth, but function is unmeasured here and is not a preselected consequence.
No migration, immune impairment, antigen specificity or clonal growth rate is
inferred from this cross-sectional endpoint.
