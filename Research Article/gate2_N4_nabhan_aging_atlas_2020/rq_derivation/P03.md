## Observation and gap

The atlas provides a normal-age lung reference, but no compatible age/history
contrast has been qualified. The [A3 dossier](../../../docs/research_dossiers/A3.md)
retains age/processing confounding, negative CAMERA results and an ineligible
seven-gene ornithine set. W1 is pseudobulk, not a completed Compass analysis.

**Provisional refinement:** Does a fixed macrophage RNA endpoint associated with
prior injury persist within comparable origin/state at matched age and present
tissue context, beyond its normal-age association?

This develops A3's existing biological question. It does not create another RQ
or use the ageing atlas to rescue an unsuccessful pathway result.

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is normal ageing, origin replacement or continuing local
stimulation, compounded by processing differences. A history association should
remain within an age-compatible, independently resolved origin/state comparison
with comparable present context. Loss under these restrictions favors the
corresponding rival. A normal-age association alone cannot estimate an
injury-specific residual or establish intrinsic memory.

## Unit and endpoint

Animal within study is the unit. The endpoint is one predeclared, versioned
macrophage RNA programme in a common cell identity, with population abundance
reported separately. Which eligible programme should be primary remains a
scientific choice; no replacement pathway is nominated here. State and origin
must not be defined by the same endpoint being tested. Biological margins,
precision and a meaningful functional consequence remain unspecified.

## Precedent and independent evidence

Persistent macrophage effects are already represented in the [A3 primary-source
review](../../../docs/research_dossiers/packages_2026-10-03/A3.md).
[Angelidis et al. 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08831-9)
and GSE124872 offer a separate normal-age lung reference. Public metadata and
processed-file links are accessible; exact macrophage-state and animal
compatibility still require qualification. Its RNA and proteomic components
must not be treated as matched measurements from the same cells.

## Grounding and review decision

Build a source eligibility map for the specified endpoint and comparison before
any new biological fit. If age/history is completely confounded with study,
concatenation or integration cannot identify the target contrast. Stop there
and retain the confounding record. A functional history-versus-environment
comparison remains a separate bridge. This proposal concerns existing host-cell
evidence only; it specifies no exposure procedure, flux, immune training or
repair mechanism. Any accepted refinement should update A3 through its existing
review process while preserving its negative findings.

## Alignment with the current A3 scope

Main PR #134's [specificity review](../../../docs/research_dossiers/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a3)
requires a nominated programme and biological consequence before A3 becomes a
fully specified functional question. Both remain unresolved here. This ageing
reference is a supporting comparison, not a replacement for A3's organizing
biological hypothesis or evidence of a new mechanism.
