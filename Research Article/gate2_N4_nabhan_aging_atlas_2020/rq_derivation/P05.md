## Observation and gap

The [local results](../EXTENSION_RESULTS.md#p01-tissue-remodelling-and-within-type-rna)
show divergent marker detection, RNA magnitude and cell-compartment patterns.
A senescence-related transcript cannot identify why a differentiated cell
expresses it. This matters biologically because a normal barrier-cell phenotype
and loss of maintenance would imply different interpretations of ageing.

**Qualification question:** Which cell-resolved measurements can distinguish
constitutive terminal differentiation from an age-associated dysfunctional
phenotype in bladder urothelium and stroma?

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest biological rival to a dysfunctional ageing interpretation is
normal terminal differentiation, alongside population replacement and assay
detection differences. Dysfunction should be associated with a separately
measured loss of barrier maintenance within a resolved cell context. Stable
function despite marker positivity supports the alternative interpretation.
Neither outcome is established by an RNA panel alone.

## Unit and endpoint

Mouse is the independent unit; cells and image fields are nested. Marker RNA,
protein and the independent barrier endpoint must remain separate quantities.
Cdkn2a gene-level RNA does not resolve protein isoforms, proliferation arrest,
secretory output or senescence. An endpoint qualification exercise must define
cell identity, assay performance and the biological comparator before any
classification rule. No universal score threshold or effect margin is available.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Al-Naggar et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14399) make differentiated umbrella
cells a direct competing context. [Meguro et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-024-00704-1)
address aged fibroblasts in a cancer setting; their selected population and
functional context cannot be transferred wholesale to normal urothelium.
The [source ledger](SOURCES.md) records that GSE180128 is reused by the former
paper and that GSE253338 selects aged reporter-defined stromal populations.
Neither supplies automatic independent validation of a generic atlas score.

## Grounding and review decision

Document which proposed assay measures differentiation, abundance, protein or
function, then qualify the smallest panel capable of resolving P01's rival.
If only RNA data are accessible, retain an RNA-localization interpretation and
hold dysfunction claims. This is a prerequisite with a biological rationale,
not a demand for more generic artifact detection or an automatic standalone RQ.
The same separation of RNA and function applies to P03's macrophage endpoint,
without transferring bladder markers into lung macrophages.
