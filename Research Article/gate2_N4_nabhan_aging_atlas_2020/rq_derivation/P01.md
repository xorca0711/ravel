## Observation and gap

The [local decomposition](../EXTENSION_RESULTS.md#p01-tissue-remodelling-and-within-type-rna)
shows that the bladder aggregate Lmnb1 detection increase coexists with a
urothelial decrease and a mesenchymal increase. Cdkn2a detection declines in the
captured bladder universe. These are equal-mouse RNA summaries of broad source
annotations, not absolute cell counts, fibroblast identity or senescence burden.
[Figure 8](../EXTENSION_FIGURES.md#figure-8-within-type-profiles-and-fixed-composition-animal-estimates)
shows why tissue averages cannot identify which compartment changes.

**Provisional question:** Does ageing alter the contribution of defined stromal
populations to normal urothelial barrier maintenance, beyond changes in
epithelial differentiation and population abundance?

This is a biological hypothesis prompted by compartment divergence, not a local
finding of stromal causation. The missing connection is between independently
resolved stromal state/abundance and a measured epithelial maintenance endpoint.

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is epithelial-intrinsic ageing or altered differentiation,
combined with capture and subpopulation shifts, without a consequential change
in stromal support. A stromal contribution predicts a reproducible relation
between a specified stromal change and barrier performance after epithelial
composition is resolved. A later comparison separating stromal context from
epithelial age would need to change the barrier endpoint to support causation.

If only captured fractions differ, or epithelial differentiation accounts for
the association, this supports the rival and narrows the question. Failure to
detect an association with poor precision does not establish no stromal role.

## Unit and endpoint

The unit is an independently identified animal. The proposed functional endpoint
is bladder epithelial barrier integrity measured by an independently validated
assay; assay choice, meaningful effect, variance and sample size remain unknown.
Population-resolved abundance per tissue area and cell-resolved RNA/protein are
supporting measurements. Fields of view and cells are nested observations.
The current RNA detection proportion is neither the functional endpoint nor a
substitute for absolute abundance. No mediator is selected.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Al-Naggar et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14399) report senescence-like
features in differentiated umbrella cells across the lifespan.
[Meguro et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-024-00704-1) study an aged
fibroblast niche in bladder cancer. These constrain interpretation; neither
establishes the proposed normal-maintenance connection. Novelty remains
provisional. The [source ledger](SOURCES.md) distinguishes the reused bladder
atlas GSE180128, female bulk GSE247123 and aged reporter-selected GSE253338.
None is currently qualified as an independent, paired stromal/barrier test.

## Grounding and review decision

First qualify stromal and epithelial identities, age/sex and animal linkage in
the external bladder sources. Continue to biological analysis only if a
compatible endpoint can distinguish differentiation from dysfunction. If no
functional linkage exists, retain a population-resolved descriptive question;
do not substitute a generic senescence score. P05 supplies measurement
qualification. The proposed question's scope and access require review before
an executable design; the existing lung P01 evidence remains available separately.
