## Observation and gap

The [recovered brain design](../EXTENSION_COMPLETION.md#what-was-recovered) has
both sexes at 3 and 18 months but only males at 24 months. Male-only sensitivity
analyses elsewhere in the package do not estimate an age-by-sex interaction.

**Provisional question:** Does sex modify a specified microglial configuration
or CD8 within-state repertoire contrast, after comparable region/state and
sampling are established? These are alternative applications for review, not
an authorization to scan all genes, tissues and sex contrasts.

## Strongest rival and discriminating prediction

The strongest rival is age-by-sex design imbalance and different state or
region composition. A modifier requires a supported difference of age contrasts
between sexes in a balanced biological comparison. A significant result in
one sex and a nonsignificant result in the other is not evidence of interaction.
A wide interval leaves the modifier unresolved; a male-only effect cannot be
generalized to females.

## Unit and endpoint

Independent animals cross-classified by age and sex are required. Use the
already specified P02 or P06 endpoint; its age-contrast difference by sex is
the target estimand. Regional measurements and cells remain nested. Effect
margin, variance and sample-size justification are unresolved. Sex itself does
not identify a hormonal mechanism.

## Precedent and independent evidence

[Li et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-023-00479-x) already report
sex-divergent microglial ageing. Their female single-cell series does not supply
a matched male single-cell trajectory; the both-sex bulk series has a different
endpoint. [Jin et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08350-8)
include both sexes but two ages, suitable only for a separately qualified
two-age modifier. GSE208386's deposited processed filename indicates batch
correction; count scale and preprocessing require inspection before reuse.
Details and access limits are in the [source ledger](SOURCES.md).

## Grounding and review decision

Specify which parent question and age contrast could change interpretation,
then require a complete animal-by-age-by-sex design for that contrast. Current
TMS data cannot answer the full 3-to-24-month interaction. No new fit can supply
the missing older females. Retain this as a contextual refinement unless review
identifies a distinct biological discriminator and qualified independent design.
