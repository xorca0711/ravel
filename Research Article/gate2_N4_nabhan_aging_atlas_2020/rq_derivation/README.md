## Proposed questions and their place in the repository

These are proposed relationships, not retain/reject decisions or a priority order.
All eight branches and A0–A23 remain available.

| Card | Biological question or supporting task | Proposed relationship |
|---|---|---|
| [P01](P01.md) | Does ageing alter stromal support of normal urothelial maintenance, beyond epithelial differentiation and population redistribution? | Potential distinct question; functional connection is a hypothesis |
| [P02](P02.md) | Does a region- and sex-qualified middle-age microglial configuration require more than an endpoint mixture or smooth age response? | Owner's potential CNS question; independent reproducibility comes before disease interpretation |
| [P03](P03.md) | Does a fixed macrophage history-associated RNA endpoint differ from normal ageing within comparable origin/state and present context? | Refinement of existing A3, not a duplicate RQ |
| [P04](P04.md) | Is an age-associated immune change shared across organs or dependent on tissue context within the same animal and state? | Context extension; CD8 repertoire is a concrete example for review |
| [P05](P05.md) | Which cell-resolved measurements distinguish differentiated barrier cells from age-associated dysfunction? | Biological measurement qualification supporting P01; assay cautions also support P03 |
| [P06](P06.md) | Is older CD8 repertoire concentration mainly differentiation-state redistribution or a change within comparable states? | Potential distinct repertoire question; functional consequences remain separate |
| [P07](P07.md) | Does sex modify the specified age association within the same region or immune state? | Conditional modifier of P02/P06, not a broad sex scan |
| [P08](P08.md) | Is the observed middle-to-late-age contrast reproducible across age cohorts after population and selection differences are addressed? | Age-window extension; no trajectory inferred from cross-sectional mice |

The broad existence of microglial ageing states, clonal ageing-associated CD8
cells and organ-specific ageing programmes is already published. The possible
contribution lies in a narrower discriminator, not renaming those observations.
Bladder compartment differences suggest a maintenance question but do not show
stromal causation or barrier failure.
