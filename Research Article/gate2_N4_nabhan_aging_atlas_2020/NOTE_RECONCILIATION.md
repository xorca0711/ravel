## Interpretation checks proposed here

Retain separate denominators for all recovered cells, cells in an annotated
type, marker-detected cells, assembled receptor sequences and image-defined
cells. A change in one denominator does not establish change in another.
Cdkn2a RNA alone cannot define senescence or distinguish protein isoforms;
capture and detection can affect its apparent frequency. Neither M1/M2 labels
nor Shannon entropy measures a single functional axis. Mutation inference
needs sequence-level error controls. Trajectory ordering and gene overlap
cannot establish treatment direction, disease causation or functional repair.

[Keren-Shaul et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.05.018) is a direct
disease-associated-microglia precedent. It prevents presenting generic overlap
with disease-associated microglia as a new discovery. The exact distinction
between an intermediate state, a mixture and a disease response still needs a
targeted primary-source review; exhaustive novelty is not established.
