# Literature context for Nn26-P01

8 November 2026. This execution retains the comparison in the prior
[fit review](README.md) and [dated search/access record](DATA_USAGE.md#search-and-access-log).
It does not propose novelty, a new mechanism or a laboratory experiment.

The primary source is [Navarro Negredo et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.01.016),
the supplied final PDF and accepted-manuscript PMC XML. Figs. 3–5 test distinct
immune readouts; Fig. 5 already tests the T-cell/interferon contribution to
microglial responses. Fig. 6G/I/J measures neural-marker endpoints; Fig. 7G/H
shows tests against chance, whose direct between-group tests are in S4.
The [correction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.06.019) changes Fig. 2K
colors and the duplicated Fig. 4J representative image; it must accompany
interpretation of those panels.

The repository previously established source pooling and assay/dose distinctions,
but had not inspected S1/S4. These tables now support a panel-level arithmetic
audit, not new independent evidence. Their panel letters and some labels differ
from the final paper; endpoint names and source coordinates govern joins.
Reported mechanism, reconstructed measurement and unresolved functional
mediation remain separate.

The prior review covers the direct Saxton precedent, reused Dulken/Liu/Buckley/Sun
sources and the contrary chronic astrocytic IL-10 model of Sanchez-Molina et al.
(2022). Its access limitations remain; no new novelty conclusion is drawn.
The 8 November follow-up queries were
`"10.1016/j.immuni.2026.01.016" "supplemental"`,
`"Navarro Negredo" "mmc5" 2026`, and the correction DOI.
Public publisher CDN retrieval, rather than search hits, supplied readable S1/S4.
No browser challenge was bypassed and no author was contacted.

The next informative comparisons are those already in the plan: direct
animal-level D25K/control cognition tests, neurogenesis and nesting contrasts,
then within-dose RNA description if the object and its aliases qualify.
Reproduced contrary or null results limit the corresponding claim. Neither a
reanalysis nor the source paper counts as independent replication of itself.
