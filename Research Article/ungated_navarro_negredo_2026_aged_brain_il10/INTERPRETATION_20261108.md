# Interpretation after cloud RNA execution — 8 November 2026

This note connects the [qualified RNA description](RNA_RESULTS.md) and
[animal audit](RESULTS.md) to their published source. It does not change frozen
measurements, propose a new mechanism or record human scientific acceptance.
The question is which reported immune RNA patterns remain visible in the
deposited broad cell populations, while keeping assay and source limits explicit.
The RNA unit is an observed condition library/pool, not an independent cell or
mouse replicate. Endpoints are mean stored log-normalized RNA and detection.

## Published findings and the repository observations

| Primary finding | Repository observation | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Navarro Negredo et al. (2026), Fig. 3F–G, report microglial IL-10-related RNA responses, including reduced Tnf | Tnf is lower than old control in both supported WT/Mut alias pairs; the complete 28-gene vector has mixed directions | A related pattern already reported in this source. Broad deposited microglia differ from the author's downstream filtered population; no universal suppression score or secretion inference |
| Fig. 4C–D reports a stronger WT-associated microglial IFN response than engineered IL-10 | Several explicit IFN markers are lower in Mut5/Mut1 than their WT partners, often still above control; Mut1 Stat1 exceeds WT1 | Same-source descriptive agreement for selected markers, with retained exceptions. Missing exact hallmark membership prevents reproduction of Fig. 4C's score |
| Fig. 3K reports Gzmb expression among Gzmb-expressing T cells | Both Mut aliases have lower all-T and positive-cell Gzmb means than their WT partners; WT5 detection is below control despite a higher mean | Conditional intensity, prevalence and all-cell expression answer different questions. The old control contains only nine T cells; RNA does not establish expansion or animal-level uncertainty |
| Fig. 5B–F tests T-cell/IFN-gamma contributions in microglial co-culture; Fig. 5G–I tests combined IFN-gamma/IFNAR blockade in vivo | The RNA contrasts are compatible with parts of that reported response separation | The perturbation explanation belongs to the published experiments. These reused pooled RNA observations neither independently test mediation nor establish the route to cognition |
| Fig. 6G/I/J and Fig. 7G/H/S4 distinguish neural markers, ligand comparisons and chance tests | The animal audit preserves nonsignificant direct D25K/WT neural-marker comparisons and nonsignificant D25K/control cognition tests | RNA cannot upgrade these separate comparisons or join unlinked group averages into a causal chain |

The primary source is [Navarro Negredo et al., Immunity 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.01.016).
The [correction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.06.019) changes Fig. 2K
color labels and the representative WT image in Fig. 4J; the authors report
unchanged quantitative analyses. The owner-supplied final-format paper,
accepted-manuscript XML and archived author code were available for this review.
[Archived captions and source manifest](../../analysis/research/runs/nn26_completion_archive_v1/source_archive_manifest.json)
and [marker/code locators](../../analysis/research/runs/nn26_qualification_review_v1/marker_manifest.json)
retain exact source versions and access provenance. Fig. 3E's Stat3 caption
versus Socs3 code discrepancy remains unresolved, with both genes reported.

This is reused source data, **not independent replication**. The exact
accession/barcode/dose crosswalk and downstream cell exclusions remain absent.
WT5/Mut5 has an indirect main-figure dose association; WT1/Mut1 keeps its alias
labels, and WT50/Mut50 expression contrasts remain held. A source crosswalk
could change those eligibility decisions. Further resampling cannot supply
independent animals, an age-by-treatment comparison, lesion repair or mediation.
The [existing literature context](LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md) retains older precedents,
contrary findings and search limits; this interpretation adds no novelty claim
or new numerical analysis.
