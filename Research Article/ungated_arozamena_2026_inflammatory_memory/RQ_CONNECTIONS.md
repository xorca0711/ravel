# What the Arozamena reference can add to current RQs

1 November 2026. This article-local reference tests the portability of a published
molecular description. It does not adopt a new canonical hypothesis or upgrade
any RQ's evidence. [Source qualification](SOURCE_QUALIFICATION.md),
[prospective RNA comparison](RNA_EXTENSION_BRIEF.md), and
[originating review](ORIGINATING_REVIEW.md) distinguish evidence from proposals.

The biological framing is **persistent molecular state versus altered response
capacity after recovery**. Arozamena's intestinal results are a published premise:
Figure 3 reports persistent RNA patterns, Figure 5d/e reports nonparallel RNA and
accessibility at selected loci, and other cohorts measure later regeneration.
These are different endpoints. A deposited molecular difference alone cannot
identify regulatory memory or connect a specific early cell to later function.

The currently executable RNA question concerns the survival of an expression
description under fixed measurement choices. Even agreement across all four
deposited contrasts would leave within-state regulation versus composition
unresolved. Disagreement would constrain the scope of reproduction, not by itself
refute the author's ISC-restricted or functional findings. Source-derived RNA QC
is deliberately labelled a subset of the original joint QC.

Original within-state, full common-peak and later-function jobs retain their
specific prerequisites. A smaller fragment-based extension, if qualified, would
be a separate measurement adaptation with finite-library interpretation. No
novelty, mechanistic acceptance or laboratory readiness is certified here.
