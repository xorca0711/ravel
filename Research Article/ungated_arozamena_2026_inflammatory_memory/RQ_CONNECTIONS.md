# What the Arozamena reference can add to current RQs

1 November 2026. This article-local reference tests the portability of a published
molecular description. It does not adopt a new canonical hypothesis or upgrade
any RQ's evidence. [Source qualification](SOURCE_QUALIFICATION.md),
[RNA results](RNA_RESULTS.md), [paired results](ACCESSIBILITY_RESULTS.md), and
[originating review](ORIGINATING_REVIEW.md) distinguish evidence from proposals.

The biological framing is **persistent molecular state versus altered response
capacity after recovery**. Arozamena's intestinal results are a published premise:
Figure 3 reports persistent RNA patterns, Figure 5d/e reports nonparallel RNA and
accessibility at selected loci, and other cohorts measure later regeneration.
These are different endpoints. A deposited molecular difference alone cannot
identify regulatory memory or connect a specific early cell to later function.

The currently executable RNA question concerns the survival of an expression
description under fixed measurement choices. Even agreement across all four
deposited contrasts would leave within-state regulation versus composition
unresolved. Disagreement would constrain the scope of reproduction, not by itself
refute the author's ISC-restricted or functional findings. Source-derived RNA QC
is deliberately labelled a subset of the original joint QC.

The [reviewed RNA result](RNA_RESULTS.md) now shows that disagreement: the
GSE329778 LGR5 panel changes direction under RNA-QC selection. Its value for A1
and DY1-P02 is to make selection and measurement definitions explicit before
calling a difference regulatory. For A14/A8, the absent early-to-later unit join
remains decisive; a stronger RNA pattern would not fill that gap.

The [reviewed GRCm39 adaptation](ACCESSIBILITY_RESULTS.md) completes that
four-locus common-region comparison in the third deposit. All 128 paired cases
remain available. LGR5 Tiam1's negative TSS-window and positive gene-body contrasts
persist across the fixed QC/weight cases; LGR5 Cd74/Cd83 RNA reverses under RNA
QC while TSS fragments remain negative. Neither result resolves within-state
regulation. The opposite-strand counting sensitivity changes no contrast sign
in its 64 comparisons, but does not identify physical molecules or establish a
biological equivalence margin.

This sharpens the existing article-local question: **does an RNA/accessibility
description survive explicit region and population definitions, and, separately,
does a qualified early molecular state link to later measured function?** The
first part is partly answered for four loci in finite deposited libraries; the
state-resolved and functional parts remain held. It is measurement validation
and bounded model discrimination of an already published finding, not a new
discovery of memory or discordance.

Original within-state, full common-peak and later-function jobs retain their
[specific prerequisites](README.md#stepwise-disposition-ledger). A provenance-
qualified author annotation could reopen within-state/composition description;
a separately reviewed operational annotation would instead define a new exposed
adaptation. Actual longitudinal unit links are still necessary for the functional
question. No novelty, mechanistic acceptance or laboratory readiness is certified.
