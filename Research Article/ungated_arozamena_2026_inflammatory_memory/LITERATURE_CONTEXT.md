## Repository observation and unresolved contrast

The [Saxton reanalysis](../gate3_08_saxton_2021_il22/reassessment_v1/RESULTS.md)
found a robust selected-panel distinction within six colon libraries, smaller
broad-IFN contrasts and an unstable three-target subset. It has no ATAC or
longitudinal functional join. [DY1-P02](../gate2_DY1_damani_yokota_2026/rq_discordance/README.md)
distinguishes bulk RNA/separate bulk ATAC from scRNA composition information;
the remaining NAM mechanism is not established by an epithelial reference.

The unresolved measurement contrast here is whether the new intestinal source
supports within-state RNA/accessibility differences distinct from state abundance,
and separately whether early molecular and later regeneration records actually
join. These questions require the deposited design, not a presumed mechanism.

## Current question and principal alternatives

Arz1-P01 is a descriptive replication/model-discrimination candidate. Its primary
estimand is not yet fixed because biological units, states and assay correspondence
remain unverified. Composition/lineage selection, technical depth, mismatched
features, assay pairing and continued input are consequential alternatives.
Persistent accessibility alone does not identify the cause or establish functional
memory. A same-cell paired measurement still cannot replace independent animals.

## Informative outcomes and stopping point

Verified within-state differences would support a source-level distinction from
measured composition, while leaving unmeasured selection and continuing input
unresolved. Composition-dominated, sparse or normalization-sensitive patterns
are retained. Missing pairing holds Q4; missing later-unit joins holds Q6 without
preventing eligible source QC or a correctly scoped descriptive Q3.
