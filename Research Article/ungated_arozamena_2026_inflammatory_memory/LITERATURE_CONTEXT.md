## Repository observation and unresolved contrast

The [Saxton reanalysis](../gate3_08_saxton_2021_il22/reassessment_v1/RESULTS.md)
found a robust selected-panel distinction within six colon libraries, smaller
broad-IFN contrasts and an unstable three-target subset. It has no ATAC or

The unresolved measurement contrast here is whether the new intestinal source
supports within-state RNA/accessibility differences distinct from state abundance,
and separately whether early molecular and later regeneration records actually
join. These questions require the deposited design, not a presumed mechanism.

## Current question and principal alternatives

Arz1-P01 is a descriptive replication/model-discrimination candidate. The original
within-state estimand is held because the author state map was not located in the
documented coverage. The [prospective RNA adaptation](RNA_EXTENSION_BRIEF.md) fixes
four deposited-library contrasts and weighting/RNA-only QC sensitivity. Shared
stable gene identifiers support this narrower comparison, but no population unit
or state label is inferred. Composition/lineage selection, technical depth, mismatched
features, assay pairing and continued input are consequential alternatives.
Persistent accessibility alone does not identify the cause or establish functional
memory. A same-cell paired measurement still cannot replace independent animals.

## Reviewed paired observation and retained biological gap

The [completed Q4a report](ACCESSIBILITY_RESULTS.md) now supplies 128 paired
finite-library cases for the four source-selected loci. LGR5 Tiam1 changes
fragment-contrast sign between TSS windows and gene body in every fixed QC/weight
case; selected RNA signs change with RNA-only QC. The independent raw review
passed all fragment records and contrast calculations. The maximum effect of
the two prospectively declared record definitions is 0.00270523 log2(CPM+1),
without fragment-contrast sign changes in those 64 comparisons. This does not
settle the source's opposite-strand record semantics or establish equivalence.

The contribution remains a robustness/measurement description of the paper's
Figure 3j and Figure 5d/e premise. Region, selection, composition and source
identity are explicit limits on calling discordance regulatory memory. Missing
