# Silent clearance before and after cargo uptake

**Question for interpreting B1.** A low inflammatory response to one encounter can coexist with substantial cellular adaptation to the engulfed corpse. Before calling a cell “silent,” identify the endpoint: cargo uptake, sensing of a nucleic acid, cytokine production, corpse processing, or capacity for a subsequent uptake. The paper's TLR9 restoration and interferon experiments bear on sensing. They do not exhaust what engulfment induces.

### What to carry forward

GSE93820 and Roberts's figures belong to one study. GSE83222 supplies a different contrast in bone marrow, large intestine and spleen. It cannot reproduce a peritoneal culture withdrawal. Use it to ask whether particular RNA components follow **cargo status** across tissues only if that question survives the internal comparison. A signal restricted to one tissue is a boundary worth describing. To establish induced remodeling within previously identified high-uptake cells would require their pre-encounter state or a time-resolved exposure comparison. B2 can explain aspects of tissue identity without establishing how sensing changes after cargo arrival.
