# GSE83222 cargo-status boundary comparison

GEO pairs DsRed-positive and DsRed-negative sorted resident-macrophage fractions from common parabiotic mouse pools. Bone marrow, large intestine and spleen each have three pairs spanning two library batches. The preparation is the pool, with each condition represented by one library per pair. The same animals may contribute across tissues. [Series metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE83222) · [A-Gonzalez et al., Fig. 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5413334/)

The workbook has one gene-ID column and 18 libraries on the deposited TMM-normalized CPM scale. Use all six prespecified recognition and signaling-regulator genes from S1, joining on exact stable mouse Ensembl gene IDs and retaining absent or ambiguous rows. Estimate cargo-positive minus cargo-negative as a paired log₂(CPM + 1) difference, show each pair and average within tissue. The endpoint is association with the detectable cargo marker. Preferential uptake by a pre-existing subset, persistence of the label, and differences in cargo composition can affect this endpoint. The comparison cannot identify induction or reproduce peritoneal withdrawal.
