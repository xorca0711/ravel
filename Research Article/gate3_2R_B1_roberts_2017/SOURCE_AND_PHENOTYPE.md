The apoptotic challenge is labeled 1 macrophage to 10 apoptotic cells. Per-experiment TNF values, animal nesting and variability are unavailable in the panel. S2C reports preserved uptake, supporting the ligand/cargo dissociation. The [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE93820) contains TXT and HDF5 quantifications. TXT header fields distinguish `est_counts` and `tpm`. The descriptive replay uses transcript TPM summed through unambiguous transcript-to-gene mappings from Ensembl GRCm38 release 79. Unmapped targets are counted in the audit and remain part of the deposited TPM denominator. This is a fixed-panel descriptive replay, with a different scale from the published Sleuth/DESeq2 workflow. A gene must have one gene ID and at least one indexed release-79 transcript in each compared library. Partial reference coverage is flagged. Expression below 1 TPM is flagged, kept visible, and handled with a fixed 1 TPM offset for log contrasts. No inference is based on individual libraries as mice.

The SOFT map names GSM2462968–70 as experiment 1 and GSM2462971–73 as experiment 2. Each has a 0 h baseline and two 60 h libraries. Animal, pool and library nesting are unresolved. GEO titles and treatment fields identify the programmed minus/plus AC, deprogrammed plus AC and AC-only libraries. The Fig. 3G legend describes one experiment and averaged technical duplicates. Neither the SOFT nor the manuscript identifies a specific 60 h unstimulated GSM pair for that challenge. Its fold change is held. The programmed fold is computed from group-mean TPM, with AC-only mean TPM compared against the programmed unstimulated reference and an alternative flag against stimulated TPM to expose denominator sensitivity. A flag is a contamination screen, with no cell-of-origin assignment.
