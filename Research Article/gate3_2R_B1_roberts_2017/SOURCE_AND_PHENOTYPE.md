The opening comparison asks whether restored TLR9 expression permits a TNF response to CpG and to apoptotic thymocytes in Tim-4-positive peritoneal macrophages from constitutive RosaTlr9 mice. The published Fig. 2F shows a robust CpG response in RosaTlr9 and baseline-level TNF with apoptotic cells in both genotypes. LPS elicits TNF in both genotypes. The apoptotic challenge is labeled 1 macrophage to 10 apoptotic cells. Approximate visual readback of the representative plot is 5.8 ng/mL for RosaTlr9 CpG, 2.2 and 2.5 ng/mL for WT and RosaTlr9 LPS, respectively, and near the plot baseline for unstimulated and apoptotic-cell wells and WT CpG. The secreted cytokine methods specify a 7-hour supernatant. The panel is representative of at least three independent experiments. Per-experiment TNF values, animal nesting and variability are unavailable in the panel. S2C shows similar cargo-reporter flow distributions for overnight-cultured WT and RosaTlr9 preparations at 1:2, 1:5 and 1:10 macrophage-to-AC ratios, gated on CD45.1-negative cells. The methods specify a 60-minute uptake assay. This panel represents two experiments and supplies no individual fractions. Thus the uptake control supports the ligand/cargo dissociation. [Roberts et al., Fig. 2F and STAR Methods](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/9n2952w6)

The [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE93820) contains TXT and HDF5 quantifications. TXT header fields distinguish `est_counts` and `tpm`. The initial stable-ID qualification used release 79. Exact target IDs and lengths establish release 84 for the compatible deposit. The final descriptive replay sums transcript TPM by gene on release 84, with raw-read recovery for experiment 1. Expression below 1 TPM is flagged and the log contrast uses a 1 TPM offset. [Reference audit](REFERENCE_AMENDMENT.md) No inference is based on individual libraries as mice.

The SOFT map names GSM2462968–70 as experiment 1 and GSM2462971–73 as experiment 2. Each has a 0 h baseline and two 60 h libraries. Animal, pool and library nesting are unresolved. GEO titles and treatment fields identify the programmed minus/plus AC, deprogrammed plus AC and AC-only libraries. The Fig. 3G legend describes one experiment and averaged technical duplicates. Neither the SOFT nor the manuscript identifies a specific 60 h unstimulated GSM pair for that challenge. Its fold change is held. The programmed fold is computed from group-mean TPM, with AC-only mean TPM compared against the programmed unstimulated reference and an alternative flag against stimulated TPM to expose denominator sensitivity. A flag is a contamination screen, with no cell-of-origin assignment.
