## Question, premise and alternative

Does IFNα priming change the B3-versus-WT IL-22 contrast for a specified
STAT1-associated epithelial response? Bachmann et al. (2013; online 2012),
[primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23153456/), already reports
priming-dependent signaling/targets. Full methods remain inaccessible in this
pass (publisher 403); do not invent its priming dose or schedule. Saxton (2021),
Figs. 4–7, supplies the engineered ligand premise. Cineus/Luo et al. (2025),
Figs. 3–4/6, supplies an OSMR inflammatory context, not a B3/priming experiment.

**Hypothesis:** priming changes the B3–WT response contrast. **Alternative:**
general attenuation, effective exposure, survival or state mixture changes both
outputs without specific STAT bias. A priming response alone does not test B3.

## Required biological comparison

One defined epithelial model/preparation, split across unprimed/IFNα-primed and
PBS/WT IL-22/B3. Priming-only controls are the primed PBS arm. Candidate human
colon organoids require source/model and ligand-compatibility qualification;
donors are biological units, wells are nested. Estimate the priming-induced
change in B3–WT response, keeping priming and ligand increments visible.

Measure pSTAT1 and pSTAT3 separately with within-analyte calibration over an
appropriate early time course; do not divide uncalibrated antibody channels.
CXCL10 protein output is a proposed downstream endpoint. A separately validated
barrier function assay is needed before claiming protection; its specification
and laboratory feasibility are still open. Dose, priming/withdrawal schedule,
collection times, precision and meaningful effect are not fabricated here.

Loss of separation identifies a context boundary; retained separation extends
the tested context; attenuation of both responses or viability loss narrows
interpretation. Neither outcome automatically establishes repair. OSMR/OSM
challenge is conditional on new receptor/response evidence, not an automatic
extension to rescue the negative acute result. No memory claim without verified
cessation and a linked later endpoint.
