## What the completed analysis changes

The selected IFN-versus-barrier panel distinction persists descriptively, but the
broader IFN memberships show much smaller contrasts. The barrier panel also
decreases directly, the source's anti-inflammatory panel has a negative amplitude
residual, and the three-target subset is omission-sensitive. A useful extension
therefore asks how cellular context and response history determine particular
outputs; it should not treat the source's gene categories as complete functional
programs or repeat a generic STAT3-protective/STAT1-inflammatory premise.

## Alternative biological framing: exposure changes recipient competence

A provisional umbrella question is: **Does inflammatory history change a
recipient epithelial cell's response to a later signal after the original input
has ended, and does that difference reach mature repair output?** This needs to
be split into a specified history/recipient-response comparison and a separately
qualified functional comparison. It is not one already executable joint model.

The relevant alternatives are continued niche input, transient feedback, changed
cell composition/survival, and a persistent within-state change. A continuing-input
explanation and a persistent cellular change can coexist. A joint dataset must
identify tissue/state, exposure and cessation, comparison, actual biological unit,
RNA/chromatin timing and the measured later outcome. Matched state labels or
treatment names cannot join separate animals or independent studies.

Saxton Figure 6B explicitly notes Socs1 as an exception to its anti-inflammatory
category. This makes feedback and response kinetics a reasonable question to
qualify, not evidence that altered feedback causes persistence. Its single RNA
time point cannot distinguish these possibilities. [Saxton primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8054646/),
Figures 5-7 and Discussion, remains the published premise.

## Closest primary findings and contrary contexts

- [Cineus, Luo et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12133592/),
  Figures 3-4, 6 and Supplementary Figure 5e: IL-22-dependent OSMR induction
  provides a published example of changing epithelial responsiveness; STAT3
  participates in the inflammatory circuit. Reported Osmr decline after IL-22
  removal argues against assuming autonomous persistence. This axis is already
  published, not a new mechanism proposed here. Existing X3 source limits remain.
- [Lindemans et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16460) reports intestinal
  stem-cell regeneration. In contrast, [Zha et al., 2019, online 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6352747/)
  reports transit-amplifying expansion with Lgr5-positive stem-cell depletion.
  These context-dependent findings motivate separate expansion and reserve
  outcomes. They do not establish the same effect in alveolar epithelium.
- [Arozamena et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-79014-6), version of
  record 27 October: indexed Abstract, Results/Figure 2 and Figure 7 discussion
  report post-injury intestinal molecular memory and altered later regeneration.
  This is a close epithelial multiomics precedent. It does not establish an IL-22
  mechanism, a lung result, or an analysis-ready linked cohort for this repository.
