## What this question could add

The contribution is **exposed same-source reproduction and robustness**, not
novel discovery of cytokine pleiotropy. A direct condition contrast and a fixed
independent-membership check can establish whether an observed program pattern
extends beyond the particular plotted genes. An amplitude reference and bounded
library sensitivity can show whether that interpretation is fragile. Neither
task proves causality or supplies a population-level test from unknown units.

## Current hypothesis and rival

Question: in these six mouse-colon libraries, is interferon-associated expression
more attenuated than the selected barrier-associated response, or is a largely
shared reduction in response amplitude sufficient to describe the pattern?
Comparison tuple: bulk colon; source PBS/WT IL-22/22-B3 groups; six-hour RNA
sampling; two deposited libraries per group; normalized gene-row expression and
fixed-membership summaries; animal/preparation identities unverified.

The published-pattern prediction is differential attenuation. The main rival is
nonspecific response weakening plus gene-panel selection. Library variation,
cell-composition changes and unrecorded batches may coexist with either pattern.
Counts do not separate within-cell regulation from composition. Shared PBS
controls also couple the two vehicle-relative axes, restricting interpretation
of a correlation or fitted amplitude line.
