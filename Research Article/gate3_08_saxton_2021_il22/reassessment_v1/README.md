## Biological question and useful outcome

**Within the six deposited colon RNA libraries, is the reported difference
between interferon-associated and epithelial barrier-associated transcription
consistent with selective response attenuation, or mainly with a weaker overall
response, selected gene panels or individual-library sensitivity?**

This is an exposed, shared-source replication and robustness question. It asks
how far the actual expression measurements support the paper's interpretation.
It does not test a new ligand, optimize a therapeutic, identify causal STAT
targets, establish cell-intrinsic regulation or measure repair or memory.

The comparison is PBS, WT IL-22 and the published 22-B3 condition in mouse colon
at the source's six-hour sampling point. The deposit has two library records per
group and the paper reports independent biological RNA replicates. Separate
animal/preparation identities and batch/allocation keys remain unverified.
The permissible descriptive unit is therefore the deposited library, with gene
rows nested in the same six records; genes are not biological replicates.

The main endpoint is a source-level normalized expression contrast, including
the direct 22-B3 versus WT comparison, and fixed-panel/set summaries of those
same contrasts. Source counts and original FPKM values are different scales.
Published panels are outcome-selected reproduction targets. Independently
curated membership is an alternative gene-selection source, not independent
biological validation or a STAT-specific activity measurement.

## Hypothesis, alternatives and interpretation

The published-pattern prediction is that the interferon-associated response is
more attenuated than the selected barrier-associated response. The principal
alternative is broadly reduced response amplitude, with apparent separation
amplified by gene selection or processing. Whole-colon cell composition,
unmeasured batches and individual-library variation remain unresolved alternatives.

| Observed outcome | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---|
| Fixed panels and prespecified external IFN memberships show a coherent distinction across the bounded sensitivity checks | Supports that descriptive pattern in this deposit; does not prove STAT causality, preservation/equivalence of function or population generalizability |
| Pattern depends on the published panel or a particular library/normalization | Narrows the robustness of the reproduction; retain the unfavorable sensitivity result |
| Most changes resemble a common response-amplitude scaling | A simple global attenuation description remains compatible; no evidence that a mechanistic partition was independently established |
| Results are mixed, sparse or method-sensitive | Report partial reproduction or an unresolved comparison; do not seek another cohort or gene set to obtain a favorable result |

Any amplitude fit is a descriptive reference to the observed expression vectors.
Both PBS contrasts share controls, and the estimated axes contain noise. Fit
residuals, gene-wise correlations or a collection of genes do not supply an
independent biological test or exclude cell-composition explanations.
