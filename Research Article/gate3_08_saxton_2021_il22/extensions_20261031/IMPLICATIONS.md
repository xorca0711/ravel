# What this work contributes

31 October 2026. This note separates the paper's findings, the repository's
completed review and conditional research directions. It does not adopt a new
canonical RQ or certify novelty, causality or experimental readiness.

## Confirmed repository result

The recovered RNA inputs are technically coherent: six libraries and 35,275
deposited gene records, with intact source and run hashes. Repeated gene symbols
require identifier care; source-reported replication does not substitute for
verified animal identity. The independent [R1 review](../R1_REVIEW.md) establishes
these QC facts. It provides **no treatment-effect estimate**. The source review
also corrects figure locators and prevents receptor RNA, signaling readouts,
viability and mature tissue function from being treated as interchangeable.

This is a useful readiness and interpretation result. It is not evidence that
the repository has reproduced the published separation of inflammatory and
protective outcomes. The completed [source-readiness jobs](RESULTS.md) characterize
the symbol-to-ID ambiguities, reconcile six public metadata chains and inspect
the companion withdrawal source. They recover no separate animal key or qualified
link to later function. These results remain source-readiness findings.

## Biological implication of the paper

[Saxton et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2021.03.008) is relevant beyond its
structural method. Its main Figures 3–7 connect receptor context, signaling and
selected tissue outcomes. The transferable idea is that a cytokine-associated
response acquires its biological meaning in a particular recipient and endpoint.
The paper does not establish a universal protective transcript or signaling
score, chronic safety, lung lineage recovery or chromatin memory. The documented
Figure 5/7 label conflicts require care when tracing a claim to its measurement;
see the [source correction](../SOURCE_CORRECTION_2026-10-31.md).

The closest contrary context already read is [Cineus, Luo et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-025-02149-z),
whose main Fig. 4 links epithelial receptor-transcript regulation with an
inflammatory setting. Its withdrawal passage, citing Supplementary Fig. 5e,
motivates source qualification of reversibility, not a claim of durable recovery.
[Bachmann et al. 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23153456/) provides an older
recipient-priming precedent at abstract-read coverage. Thus generic context
dependence is already published; it should be treated as a premise.

## Plausible scientific branches, conditional on qualifying evidence

| Theme | Remaining question and useful comparison | Principal alternative / evidence limit | Repository fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recipient context | Is variation in an observed response associated with recipient state within a defined biological population? A state-resolved comparison needs animal/preparation identities and a measured response. | Whole-tissue composition, shared preparations and technical processing can produce an apparent state relationship. Receptor RNA alone is neither receptor abundance nor response. | Supports the existing recipient-context discussion; X2 qualifies identities only. |
| Persistence after cessation | Does a measured state remain after the initiating input has demonstrably ended, or track ongoing input? A linked history/cessation/later-readout record would distinguish these possibilities. | Residual stimulation, selective survival and changing composition can imitate memory. A declining receptor transcript addresses that transcript's reversibility, not mature recovery. | Conceptual relevance to [A14](../../../docs/research_dossiers/A14.md); X3 tests source linkage, without substituting IL-22 for A14's established IL-1 question. |
| Acute response versus later function | Does an early response correspond to later mature output and retained regenerative capacity in the same qualified biological units? | Survival, expansion, marker expression and mature function are distinct endpoints. Separate figures or matching treatment names do not establish a longitudinal join. | Conceptual relevance to [A19](../../../docs/research_dossiers/A19.md), whose Fzd/AT2 question remains unchanged. The current Saxton RNA samples do not supply that joint outcome. |

These are possible descriptive or model-discrimination directions, not an
engineering program or new accepted mechanisms. Their contribution would depend
on the exact population, timing, comparator, endpoint and source relationship.
Unfavorable or null findings would remain informative. The bounded source audits
are executable now; the biological comparisons remain unexecuted under the
documented policy and data/design limits.

## Literature refresh and stopping point

The [plan](README.md#source-and-literature-coverage) records the actual 31 October
searches and earlier primary reads. A further exact-title search on that date
located [Larsen et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34320411/) and
[Jiang et al., published 28 October 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11181-4).
The returned Nature indexed text included its abstract, Figure 4 legend and
selected Results/Discussion; these describe skin inflammatory recall and delimit
the lung observation as acute. Direct full-page access failed. The PubMed open
did not return the Larsen abstract. Neither is treated as a completed
full-figure/methods review, an eligible extension dataset or support for an IL-22
mechanism. They remain literature leads for a future precisely framed question.
No exhaustive forward/backward citation tracing or novelty clearance was done.
