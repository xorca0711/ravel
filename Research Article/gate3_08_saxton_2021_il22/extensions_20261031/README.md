## Why these extensions

The [reviewed results](../RESULTS.md) establish intact source files and a
reproducible QC record. They do not estimate treatment effects or establish
protective function, inflammatory suppression, chromatin memory or durable repair.
The paper's published results remain published premises, not independently
replicated repository findings. See [source corrections](../SOURCE_CORRECTION_2026-10-31.md)
and [current source coverage](../SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md).

The useful scientific implications are questions about recipient context and
endpoint meaning: whether a response depends on the current recipient state;
whether an observed transcript response persists after input cessation; and
whether an acute response is linked to later function. These are conditional
themes relevant to existing A14/A19 and their companions, not adopted hypothesis
changes or new mechanisms. The original colon evidence does not establish a lung
repair mechanism. No structure-based molecule engineering is proposed here.

The new work addresses three concrete gaps left by recovery. It is deliberately
limited to source identity, annotation and endpoint linkage. These audits can
support a feasibility decision even when an effect analysis cannot proceed.
