The first endpoint remains whether established lineage-identified Tregs contribute intact extracellular IGFBP4. A negative or fragment-only result is informative and must not trigger an automatic blockade experiment. Resolve whether the RNA signal corresponds to intact Treg-derived protein under a defined context. If it does, a target-specific reduction under native-IGF exposure can test receptor-response modulation. Neither lower signaling nor an intervention effect determines whether suppression is improved, impaired or unchanged; that functional question remains separate.

Ezh2-deficient and matched-control lineage-identified Tregs under common activation, separating locus-specific RNA, cell-associated protein and intact supernatant protein. If source and assay qualify, compare Treg-restricted IGFBP4 reduction with matched control under defined native-IGF and IL-2 exposure.

## Stages

| Stage | Required evidence or comparison | Stop, qualify or narrow when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Resolve source and molecular form | Lineage-identified Treg RNA, cell-associated protein, intact supernatant protein and fragments with medium/purity controls and linked recovery. | Adequately sensitive source-negative evidence restricts the secreted-mediator account. An assay below its validated range is unresolved, not proof of absence. |
| 2. Qualify native-IGF response | Defined medium, IL-2 and processing context; receptor-proximal readout, ligand identity and viable recovery. | Use native ligand for buffering. A reduced-binding analogue may qualify receptor competence but is not the sole buffering assay. |
| 3. Fix conditional target comparison | Treg-restricted IGFBP4 reduction versus control with verified target engagement; retain both genotypes if estimating contribution to their response difference. | Freeze the perturbation estimand, units and precision. RNA reduction alone does not establish depletion of intact extracellular protein; reduction need not be beneficial. |
| 4. Decide processing or functional extension | Use source-qualified discordance to justify a processing/availability comparison; justify suppression separately. | Do not assign ADAM9 from the Th17 precedent or infer suppression from phosphorylation/Foxp3. Preserve null and opposite effects. |

These are design decisions, not a compulsory sequence of separate studies. Source and assay qualification may be embedded in the same experiment. A genotype effect can be informative without an extra rescue; additional perturbations are justified by a target-attribution claim.

## Model choice and linked measurements

Specify whether the comparison concerns longstanding genetic loss or acute perturbation of established Tregs. Record starting tissue/state, driver or exposure timing, the interval before activation and readout, and actual control background. Either can support its stated comparison; neither automatically estimates the other. Verify the intended perturbation using the appropriate allele/recombination and molecular evidence. A claim about catalytic inhibition requires a qualified target-engagement readout; Ezh2 gene-level RNA alone does not provide it. Wang Figure S4D supplies an inducible-loss/H3K27me3 precedent in already sorted Tregs; Peeters Figure 4 supplies a separate inducible-gain precedent. Neither qualifies the actual response preparation or turns existing constitutive deposits into an acute experiment. Exact implementation and timing remain unresolved. Direct-target chromatin assays are not prerequisites for the present functional question.

If interpreting RNA–protein–response discordance, link the measurements to the same biological preparation, assigned condition and sampling window through nested aliquot IDs. Different studies or unlinked preparations cannot establish post-transcriptional regulation. This linkage is required for that interpretation, not for a standalone qualified response comparison. Preserve recovery and state composition as measured outcomes; conditioning on recovered cells does not recover a direct genotype effect.

The [neighbor-paper review](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/RQ_FOLLOWUP_REVIEW_20261112.md#neighboring-article-flow-and-revisited-gaps) explains why these specifications matter and which requirements were already present.

## Biological unit and readout

Independent mouse or independently identified source pool is the intended biological unit. Repeated preparations from the same animal, aliquots, wells and cells are nested; they do not add independent animals. Pooling and pairing must be recorded explicitly. Fix the actual genetic control, common exposure and preparation/accessory-source structure; allocate aliquots and blind readouts where feasible.

Intact source-attributed IGFBP4 first; receptor-proximal IGF response and absolute recovery next. AKT is supportive but not IGF-specific. Suppression at matched viable input is a later distinct endpoint, not inferred from Foxp3 frequency or phosphorylation.

Source tissue, age/sex, allele/control, timing, assay range, biological-unit variance and meaningful precision remain unresolved. Do not substitute an arbitrary minimum sample count for a power or precision rationale.

Measure intact protein and fragments with validated specificity/range; a total immunoreactivity assay alone may not distinguish them. Record medium blanks, source attribution and viable input/recovery. Preserve total recovered extracellular protein and conditional per-recovered-cell quantities separately. Acute receptor-proximal response is distinct from later division, recovery and suppression; measure each only for its stated question.

The initial responding population is the lineage-identified Treg population under native-IGF exposure. Treg source plus Treg population response does not establish same-cell autocrine action versus Treg-to-Treg paracrine action. A conditioned-medium assay on another recipient needs a separately named recipient and response comparison. Record medium volume and collection time: recovered intact protein is net accumulation after secretion, cleavage, uptake and loss, not a secretion rate.

## Informative outcomes

RNA without intact Treg-derived protein restricts the proposed secreted-mediator account. Qualified reduction changing receptor response supports IGF modulation, not beneficial function. Improved, worsened or unchanged later suppression distinguish harmful contribution, possible compensation or lack of necessity only within the qualified context. An imprecise null remains unresolved.

State composition, non-Treg source, serum carryover and proteolysis can dissociate RNA from intact extracellular protein. An effect on recipient cells or general viability can mimic a Treg-autonomous functional effect.

## Conditional extension

**Does processing separate RNA abundance from extracellular IGF modulation?** Karino (2025), Figure 3G–I, already supplies polarized-CD4 source and Th17-associated cleavage precedents. It does not establish the relevant protease or intact Treg-derived protein in this Ezh2 setting. The extension is model discrimination in established Tregs, conditional on source qualification.

If intact protein is detected but its abundance or IGF response does not follow RNA, compare intact protein and fragments alongside a validated native-IGF receptor response in matched preparation aliquots. A qualified reduced-binding-ligand response can help distinguish ligand-availability constraints from receptor-response competence; the analogue and native ligand require separate response calibration and cannot be treated as equipotent by assumption. A target-restricted IGFBP4 comparison with measured protein engagement is needed to assign a contribution to IGFBP4 rather than the rest of the medium.

Predominantly cleaved protein would motivate a specific processing question only if fragment identity and source are established. An effect restricted to native ligand with retained analogue responsiveness would be consistent with binding-mediated availability, not proof of the entire mechanism. No effect after verified protein reduction and adequate precision limits necessity in that context; an unresolved source stops the secreted-protein extension. ADAM9, suppression, recipient repair and benefit from blockade remain separate hypotheses.
