    <h2 style="color: #1F77B4; font-weight: 600; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Abstract</h2>
    <p style="text-align: justify; line-height: 1.8;">
      Aging impairs the regenerative capacity of mammalian organs and is a major risk factor for chronic lung disease. The causalities for persistent fibrosis after lung injury in old individuals have been unclear. We used longitudinal single-cell RNA-seq after lung injury and dissected aging effects computationally and experimentally at baseline and during repair. In old mice, sustained fibroblast activation in the resolution phase of repair was associated with prolonged cellular senescence and persistent epithelial-mesenchymal crosstalk. Single-cell interpretable tensor decomposition analysis identified the strongest link of aging with T/B-lymphocytes and macrophages. A Gzmk-high CD8-T cell state was unique to aged mice, co-localized with progenitors, and its co-culture or Gzmk treatments in lung organoids reduced progenitor function by induction of stem cell senescence. In summary, our study highlights effects of immune aging on progenitor function and provides a high resolution map of a lung regeneration time course in the context of aging.
    </p>
    
    <h2 style="color: #1F77B4; font-weight: 600; margin-top: 2rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">Dataset Description</h2>
    <p style="text-align: justify; line-height: 1.8;">
      This dataset was generated using longitudinal single-cell RNA sequencing to study lung injury and regeneration in young versus aged mice. The experimental design included:
    </p>
    <ul style="margin-left: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Animal Model:</strong> Young (12-weeks-old) and aged (18-months-old) female and male C57BL/6J mice</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Injury Model:</strong> Oropharyngeal instillation of bleomycin sulphate (2U/kg) to induce lung injury and fibrosis</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Time Course:</strong> Longitudinal sampling at days 0, 3, 10, 20, 30, and 37 post-bleomycin injury</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Methodology:</strong> Drop-seq based single-cell RNA sequencing with microfluidic droplet encapsulation</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Cell Processing:</strong> Single-cell suspensions generated using enzymatic digestion with dispase, collagenase, elastase, and DNase</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Data Processing:</strong> Quality control, doublet removal, ambient RNA correction, and hierarchical cell type annotation</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong style="color: #1F77B4;">Cell Types:</strong> Comprehensive annotation including epithelial, stromal, endothelial, and immune cell populations</li>
    </ul>
    <p style="text-align: justify; line-height: 1.8;">
      The dataset provides a high-resolution map of cellular dynamics during lung regeneration, enabling detailed comparison of repair processes between young and aged lungs across multiple timepoints.
    </p>
