# A5: developmental-gene recruitment in adult repair

<a id="organizing-biological-question"></a>

## Organizing Biological Q

A5 asks whether adult alveolar repair reuses part of a developmental epithelial
programme, and what that recurrence means. The fixed developmental RNA component
is higher in repair intermediates in the original 24 mice. Inspecting its members
has made the interpretation more specific: **junction/polarity-associated RNA with
selected matrix support**, alongside heterogeneous signaling and lower original
transport-gene means. This nominates a process to investigate; productive repair
versus prolonged transition remains unresolved.

## Literature context & visual hypothesis

Guo's postnatal-day-1 AT1/AT2 epithelial population supplies a defined developmental
comparison. Adult injury-associated Krt8+ transitional cells provide a setting in
which some of those genes might recur. Birth stress, ordinary alveolar identity
and injury-associated plasticity are alternatives to functional developmental
reuse. Strunz also reported poor overall correspondence between developmental
and injury signatures. A positive *subset* can coexist with that observation;
A5 does not require the two populations to have the same global identity.

## Evidence, analyses and next steps

The comparison uses author-defined **Krt8+ ADI minus activated AT2** cells within
24 original Strunz mice sampled on days 2-21. Each mouse had at least 30 cells per
arm after the fixed raw-depth floor of 500 UMI. The readout is expected gene
detection at a common 500-UMI sampling depth, expressed as a paired difference in
percentage points and averaged equally across mice. It measures RNA detection,
not the fraction of cells changing fate. Cells are nested within animals, and
sampling-day columns are cross-sectional observations.

The 99-gene primary already retains the fixed operational/labeling exclusions;
the 57/53-gene forms add the specified identity and control-list exclusions.

The [original fixed-component result](../A5_A11_shared_component_contract/reports/REVISED_TEST_RESULTS.md#a5-partial-developmental-signature-recruitment)
was positive in all 24 mice: mean **+0.735 pp**, 95% CI **0.579-0.892**. The
57-gene identity-excluded and 53-gene identity/control-excluded forms remained
positive (+0.557 and +0.602 pp). These exclusions weaken the specific nominated
identity/stress explanations; they do not exhaust plasticity, annotation or
composition effects. Independent sourcing of the gene list is distinct from
replicating the observation in another injury study.

The [member-attribution result](reports/COMPONENT_ATTRIBUTION_V1_RESULTS.md) then
showed why the positive aggregate should not be called uniform regulatory
activation. The original six gene-expression-regulation members contribute a
net **-0.125824 pp** to the full 99-gene component, and the four original transport
members contribute **-0.093947 pp**. These are module-denominated contributions,
not gene-level means; overlapping role rows must not be summed. The small positive
regulator contributions in the 57/53 forms concern four retained members and do
not reverse the full-component finding.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

The fixed-component contrast is supported in the original 24 mice. The newer
junction/polarity interpretation is an **exploratory process-context lead** from
an outcome-exposed examination of those same measurements. Functional annotations
help describe the members; they are not independent evidence that the selected
process is active or unusually coherent relative to an appropriate alternative.
The complete member display, mixed matrix/signaling findings and negative
original transport and regulator contributions restrict a uniform programme claim.

This warrants a more specific question about where that RNA context occurs. It
does not yet choose junction assembly, beneficial repair or prolonged transition
as the explanation, nor justify calling the pattern random noise. No automatic
target priority follows from the annotation label or the selected display.

## What remains open, and why it follows

The current interpretation is a **junction/polarity-associated RNA context with
selected matrix support**, rather than a uniformly recruited developmental
programme. A consequential alternative is a heterogeneous, selected transitional
state: broad or cross-tissue annotations and the mixture of sampled days may make
RNA differences appear to describe an assembled process. Persistent remodeling
and productive repair could both produce parts of this pattern. The existing
cross-sectional comparison cannot distinguish them.

The next proposed biological discriminator is therefore an independent
localization/state comparison: do the nominated junction proteins and
laminin/Col4a5 material assemble in transitional epithelial regions, and does
that context accompany productive or persistent transition? Localized structure
would support an architecture-associated state; absent or mislocalized protein
would weaken that reading. Persistence without maturation would weaken the
productive-repair interpretation. An RNA-only result would leave those links
unresolved. No protein assembly, maturation trajectory, lineage or repair function
has been measured here. The [reviewed proposal and literature record](../../docs/research_reviews/2026-11-03-tool-assisted-screening-review/prerequisites/A5_CANDIDATE_PROCESS_COMPARISON_V1.md)
explain this discriminator; it is not a newly authorized experiment or analysis.

**External replication is a separate branch.** Testing the unchanged component
in GSE303646 still requires the authentic barcode/library/mouse/author-state map,
pool/split information, reconciliation of 56 libraries with the 55-mouse report,
and raw-count alignment. The [latest source qualification](../../docs/audits/2026-11-03-rq-evidence-trajectory/A5_TECHNICAL_RESULT.md)
is held before scoring, not a negative replication result or proof that the map
is globally unavailable. Resolving that input would test generality of the fixed
RNA measurement; it would not itself answer the localization/function question.
No further annotation sweep follows automatically from the completed display.

## Organizing Biological Q

Adult alveolar repair recruits the exact independently sourced developmental epithelial component beyond the identity, stress and labeling genes excluded by the frozen shared-component contract. Its association in 24 mice motivates independent replication of that same component. Whether it performs a developmental function during repair is a separate, untested biological question.

**What is already known, and what remains:** Developmental-programme reuse is established broadly. The current contribution is reproducibility of a fixed measurement; replication is useful without being a new mechanism. [Primary-source comparison](../../docs/history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a5).

**What the measurements would decide:** Reproduction in independent, source-qualified animals supports transport of the fixed component. Failure or loss after valid state/identity comparison weakens that generalization. Functional reuse requires a separately justified endpoint and intervention; gene overlap alone cannot supply it.

**Current disposition:** Fixed-component replication; mapping held. This revision specifies proposed work; scientific acceptance, model access and assay qualification remain pending.
