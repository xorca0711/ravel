## Purpose, comparison and alternatives

The organizing endpoint is later surviving birthdated neuronal output after a defined transient exposure in aged SVZ–olfactory-bulb lineage. Compare D25K directly with WT IL-10 and vehicle, retaining early progenitor output as a distinct measurement. The prediction is a later D25K advantage; common ligand benefit, an early-only response, different survival, direct progenitor effects and heterogeneous response remain credible. Existing direct null comparisons do not establish equivalence.

For each IFN marker, decompose each pooled mean difference across the three original lineage-associated bins symmetrically: representation contribution = sum[(p_left−p_right)(mean_left+mean_right)/2]; within-bin contribution = sum[(mean_left−mean_right)(p_left+p_right)/2]. Opposing contributions must remain visible; do not label a component 'dominant' using a post hoc threshold. Oligodendrocytes stay a separate descriptive compartment.
