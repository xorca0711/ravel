# A34 development plan

**Status — 8 November 2026:** proposed response comparison, unexecuted. The source RNA screen and candidate continuation are completed, outcome-exposed evidence. Read the [current synthesis](README.md), [context](LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md) and [dossier](../../docs/research_dossiers/A34.md). This plan is not an executable numerical contract or a laboratory protocol.

## Decision and comparison

First determine which qualified responses differ: directed movement, inflammatory-context CD25/IL-2 response, viable recovery, both branches, or neither. This distinguishes phenotypes before assigning them to CCR2. Actual Il2ra/background status and target-restricted specificity become essential when making a CCR2-mediated claim; a simple genotype comparison can report a bounded phenotype.

Matched activated Ezh2-deficient and genetic-control Treg preparations in a qualified CCR2-ligand gradient, with no-gradient/equal-concentration controls. In separate aliquots, assess a qualified inflammatory accessory-cell condition and downstream IL-2 response. Do not assume purified-cell CCL2 alone induces CD25.

## Stages

| Stage | Required evidence or comparison | Stop, qualify or narrow when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish source and background | Lineage identity, CCR2 protein, actual Ezh2 comparator and allele/background information. | For the CD25 branch, qualify Il2ra status rather than assuming the CCR2 explanation. |
| 2. Qualify separate assays | Ligand gradient with no-gradient/equal-concentration controls; separate responsive inflammatory accessory-cell condition. | A purified-cell CCL2/CD25 null is not a test of the closest published mixed-cell account. |
| 3. Fix response comparisons | Movement, CD25/pSTAT5 and recovery retain separate endpoints/windows and preparation-level pairing. | Choose primary endpoint priority and precision; cell/well counts are not independent mice. |
| 4. Assess contribution | Report the genotype phenotypes before attributing them to CCR2. | A mixed-cell antagonist cannot identify a Treg-intrinsic effect; in-vivo homing needs separate tracking. |

These are design decisions, not a compulsory sequence of separate studies. Source and assay qualification may be embedded in the same experiment. A genotype effect can be informative without an extra rescue; additional perturbations are justified by a target-attribution claim.

## Biological unit and readout

Independent mouse or independently identified source pool is the intended biological unit. Repeated preparations from the same animal, aliquots, wells and cells are nested; they do not add independent animals. Pooling and pairing must be recorded explicitly. Fix the actual genetic control, common exposure and preparation/accessory-source structure; allocate aliquots and blind readouts where feasible.

Absolute viable cells traversing the assay and fraction of viable input; separately CD25 protein, IL-2-induced pSTAT5 and absolute recovery. Proliferation requires a division assay. None of these is a direct in-vivo homing measurement.

Source tissue, age/sex, allele/control, timing, assay range, biological-unit variance and meaningful precision remain unresolved. Do not substitute an arbitrary minimum sample count for a power or precision rationale.

## Informative outcomes

Movement-only loss supports a movement phenotype; signaling/recovery-only loss supports a separate fitness phenotype; both allow shared state effects or joint contributions. Neither weakens these endpoint-specific hypotheses only if assays and precision are adequate. A CD25 difference attributable to Il2ra/background cannot be assigned to CCR2.

General activation/viability differences, accessory-cell effects, or an independent Il2ra/background defect can mimic a CCR2-linked fitness result. The source RNA comparison cannot distinguish them.

## Dry-analysis opportunities

Another source is useful only if its measured endpoint or source/assay qualification can change the stated decision. A new expression atlas alone cannot supply the proposed response or target-mediated effect. Any new numerical execution needs its own source qualification, explicit exposure record and prospective contract; no historical plan restarts automatically.
