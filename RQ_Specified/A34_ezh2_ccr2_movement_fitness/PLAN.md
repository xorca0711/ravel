## Decision and comparison

Wang Ccr2 is positive at the point estimate and crosses zero under omission, while DuPage is negative. The immediate task is context qualification, not confirmation of a universal receptor reduction. An absent reduction does not block a useful genotype movement comparison, but changes its interpretation. [Wang interpretation](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/BIOLOGICAL_INTERPRETATION_V1.md) · [Literature and extension review](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/RQ_FOLLOWUP_REVIEW_20261112.md).

First determine which qualified responses differ: directed movement, inflammatory-context CD25/IL-2 response, viable recovery, both branches, or neither. This distinguishes phenotypes before assigning them to CCR2. Actual Il2ra/background status and target-restricted specificity become essential when making a CCR2-mediated claim; a simple genotype comparison can report a bounded phenotype.

Matched activated Ezh2-deficient and genetic-control Treg preparations in a qualified CCR2-ligand gradient, with no-gradient/equal-concentration controls. In separate aliquots, assess a qualified inflammatory accessory-cell condition and downstream IL-2 response. Do not assume purified-cell CCL2 alone induces CD25.

## Stages

| Stage | Required evidence or comparison | Stop, qualify or narrow when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish source and receptor context | Lineage identity, actual Ezh2 control/background and CCR2 protein in the chosen activation context. | If protein is unchanged or higher, record that boundary; do not label a subsequent phenotype as receptor loss. Qualify Il2ra independently for a CD25 claim. |
| 2. Qualify separate assays | A responsive ligand gradient with no-gradient/equal-concentration controls; separate inflammatory accessory-cell condition if pursuing fitness. | A purified-cell CCL2/CD25 null does not test the closest published mixed-cell account. Retain input and recovered-cell viability. |
| 3. Fix response comparisons | Genotype differences in absolute viable traversal and viable-input fraction; separately CD25, IL-2-induced pSTAT5 and recovery. | Freeze endpoint priority, windows and precision. Report increased, unchanged or decreased responses; do not select only the predicted deficit. |
| 4. Assess target contribution and boundary | Consider a Treg-restricted target comparison only after a qualified phenotype; assess abundance/response discordance if present. | A mixed-cell antagonist does not identify a Treg-intrinsic contribution; in-vivo homing/retention requires separate tracking. |

These are design decisions, not a compulsory sequence of separate studies. Source and assay qualification may be embedded in the same experiment. A genotype effect can be informative without an extra rescue; additional perturbations are justified by a target-attribution claim.

## Public-source checkpoint and revised sequence

Loyher reports specific fluorescent CCL2 binding despite an antibody stain that did not detect Treg CCR2. Bonfiglio reports impaired movement of bulk Ezh2-deficient CD4 cells to CCL19/CCL22; this is a general movement precedent, not a CCR2-dependent Treg result. Validate CCR2 detection with a qualified specificity control; an antibody-negative result alone does not establish receptor absence. Specific ligand binding is an alternative measurement, not automatically an absolute protein count. Retain the CCR2-ligand gradient and no/equal-gradient controls, with viable input/recovery. For CCR2 attribution, assess a justified control for broader movement competence and verify its responsiveness; this is not a prerequisite for reporting a bounded genotype effect. Keep CD25/IL-2 signaling separate.

## Model choice and linked measurements

Specify whether the comparison concerns longstanding genetic loss or acute perturbation of established Tregs. Record starting tissue/state, driver or exposure timing, the interval before activation and readout, and actual control background. Either can support its stated comparison; neither automatically estimates the other. Verify the intended perturbation using the appropriate allele/recombination and molecular evidence. A claim about catalytic inhibition requires a qualified target-engagement readout; Ezh2 gene-level RNA alone does not provide it. Wang Figure S4D supplies an inducible-loss/H3K27me3 precedent in already sorted Tregs; Peeters Figure 4 supplies a separate inducible-gain precedent. Neither qualifies the actual response preparation or turns existing constitutive deposits into an acute experiment. Exact implementation and timing remain unresolved. Direct-target chromatin assays are not prerequisites for the present functional question.

If interpreting RNA–protein–response discordance, link the measurements to the same biological preparation, assigned condition and sampling window through nested aliquot IDs. Different studies or unlinked preparations cannot establish post-transcriptional regulation. This linkage is required for that interpretation, not for a standalone qualified response comparison. Preserve recovery and state composition as measured outcomes; conditioning on recovered cells does not recover a direct genotype effect.

The [neighbor-paper review](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/RQ_FOLLOWUP_REVIEW_20261112.md#neighboring-article-flow-and-revisited-gaps) explains why these specifications matter and which requirements were already present.

## Biological unit and readout

Independent mouse or independently identified source pool is the intended biological unit. Repeated preparations from the same animal, aliquots, wells and cells are nested; they do not add independent animals. Pooling and pairing must be recorded explicitly. Fix the actual genetic control, common exposure and preparation/accessory-source structure; allocate aliquots and blind readouts where feasible.

Absolute viable cells traversing the assay and fraction of viable input; separately CD25 protein, IL-2-induced pSTAT5 and absolute recovery. Proliferation requires a division assay. None of these is a direct in-vivo homing measurement.

Source tissue, age/sex, allele/control, timing, assay range, biological-unit variance and meaningful precision remain unresolved. Do not substitute an arbitrary minimum sample count for a power or precision rationale.

Retain input, traversing and non-traversing viable counts and available recovery losses. A traversing-cell percentage without its viable-input denominator can confound movement and attrition. No-gradient/equal-concentration conditions address directed response versus undirected movement; they do not by themselves prove receptor specificity. Surface-protein strata are post-perturbation observations, not new independent units.

## Informative outcomes

Movement-only loss supports a movement phenotype; signaling/recovery-only loss supports a separate fitness phenotype; both allow shared state effects or joint contributions. No resolved difference weakens a deficit prediction only when assays and precision are adequate; an increased response is a contrary result to retain. A CD25 difference attributable to Il2ra/background cannot be assigned to CCR2.

General activation/viability differences, accessory-cell effects, or an independent Il2ra/background defect can mimic a CCR2-linked fitness result. The source RNA comparison cannot distinguish them.

The neighboring DuPage/Peeters/Zhang studies do not supply a matched recruitment-versus-retention estimate for this model. The present assay measures directed movement; any later tissue-access claim needs explicit entry/retention and recovery measurements, rather than extrapolation from a transwell fraction. No additional spatial screen is currently required.

## Conditional extension

**When does CCR2 abundance predict directed movement?** The proposed increment is a context boundary in Ezh2-perturbed Tregs, not the general discovery that CCR2 can support Treg trafficking. Watanabe (2024), Figure 5, already links tissue context to receptor profiles and chemokine responses; the tumor-trafficking precedent is older still. Neither establishes the present genotype effect.

If CCR2 protein and movement disagree in a qualified assay, select one justified activation context or ligand-exposure contrast and retain matched genotype preparations, surface protein, viable input and gradient controls. Estimate genotype movement responses in each condition, with independent mice or disjoint source pools as biological units and preparations/aliquots nested. Differences restricted to one context would narrow transportability; preserved receptor abundance with impaired movement would motivate response-competence or general motility explanations. Loss of viable recovery can coexist with either pattern.

Protein-matched or receptor-positive subsets are descriptive conditional comparisons, not proof of a direct genotype effect. Target attribution requires a restricted perturbation with specificity verified in the qualified context. If no movement difference is resolved with adequate precision, do not add homing experiments merely to obtain a positive result. The CD25/accessory-cell branch remains a separate, optional comparison; its absence need not prevent a movement study.
