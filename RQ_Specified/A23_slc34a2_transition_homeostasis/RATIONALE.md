# A23 rationale: homeostatic stress versus lineage erasure

This question was derived on 1 October 2026 after inspecting Nb3 and its
follow-up. It is a biological hypothesis, not a name for a marker-score check.

## Biological context and inference

Epithelial Npt2b/Slc34a2 deletion causes an alveolar microlithiasis phenotype in
a mouse model, establishing a role for this transporter in lung homeostasis
([Saito et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aac8577)).
Independent repair studies identify Krt8-associated transitional epithelium,
with persistence in fibrotic lungs
([Strunz et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17358-3)).
Those observations make a homeostasis-to-state connection plausible, but do
not establish that phosphate imbalance drives the Nb3 transcriptional pattern.

The proposed mechanism is therefore explicit and falsifiable: loss of
SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis promotes a transition-associated
stress response while substantial alveolar identity remains. Transport-linked
restoration should reduce that response if homeostasis is the relevant input.
Whether this response is reversible, precedes calcification, or predisposes to
failed maturation is a separate temporal and functional question.

## Evidence and alternatives

Nb3's transition panel rises by +0.524. Omitting Krt8, Sprr1a or Clu one at a
time retains effects of +0.437 to +0.568, and both reference types retain a
positive contrast. All three individual markers rise. Targeted Slc34a2 RNA
decreases and is not included in either identity or transition scoring.
Eight wells on one plate do not establish eight independent preparations.

The AT2 and AT1 baseline panels are near zero compared with NKX21's broad
identity phenotype. Yet paired-depth adjustment shifts AT2 to −0.219 in the
seven-well paired subset (rather than the eight-well mouse-QC baseline), so the
hypothesis cannot rest on an assertion of intact identity. The defensible
contrast is smaller identity attenuation with a coherent transition-associated
signal. Wider Hallmark changes are correlation-sensitive and do not identify
the mediator.

Rivals include altered proportions of pre-existing epithelial states,
generic culture stress, changes in proliferation, perturbation off-target
effects, and indirect injury caused by extracellular mineral/inflammatory
changes. A bulk average cannot decide among them. Even a measured transport
defect and transition signal occurring together would not by themselves prove
that one mediates the other.

## Relationship to other questions

A1 concerns regulatory distinctions between RNA-similar transitional states;
A8 concerns maturation information beyond transition. A23 proposes a specific
homeostatic input to state entry and its reversibility. It should share their
state/fate standards rather than claim another trajectory from this bulk screen.
The [plan](PLAN.md) names the observations needed to distinguish the proposition
from cell-mixture and secondary-injury explanations.
