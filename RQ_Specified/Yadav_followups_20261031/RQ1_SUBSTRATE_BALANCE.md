# RQ1 — source ARG1 and the balance of arginine consumption and ornithine delivery

**Owner: existing Wg-P06.** This operationalizes the [current P06 card](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W1_wagner_th17_autoimmunity/rq_derivation/P06.md)
without changing its hypothesis or adopting scientific acceptance.

## Finding → gap → purpose

Yadav provides intervention/rescue evidence for a profibrotic immune–fibroblast
circuit. The reviewed reconstruction retains those selected directions, but does
not jointly measure source-conditioned arginine consumption, ornithine delivery
and collagen in qualified paired biological units. Hamanaka provides a recipient
arginine-requirement premise under specific media/TGF-beta conditions. Source-cell
metabolism and recipient-cell metabolism are distinct measurements.

**Question:** in human peripheral-blood neutrophil–primary lung fibroblast
crosstalk, does recipient arginine sufficiency modify the net collagen response
to source ARG1 activity? This is model discrimination within an actual measured
exposure range, not discovery of ARG1 or an assumed crossover.

**Hypothesis:** arginine preservation partly offsets lost ornithine contribution
when recipient arginine is limiting. **Strongest rival:** ornithine supply dominates
throughout the source-conditioned range. Cytokines, source/recipient survival,
glutamine/proline supply and translation stress remain consequential alternatives.

## Comparison, units and measured endpoints

- Model: nominated human blood-neutrophil/primary lung-fibroblast context, with
  TGF-beta input declared. Disease status is not a subgroup to select after seeing
  outcomes. Blood-source findings do not automatically describe IPF tissue cells.
- Primary comparison: the effect of validated source ARG1 restriction on newly
  produced/deposited collagen across independently characterized recipient
  arginine/ornithine exposure conditions. A recipient-only comparison can qualify
  nutrient response but cannot identify source-cell causality.
- Independent units: actual source and recipient donors with declared crossing;
  preparations, wells and repeated measurements are nested. Shared donors across
  pairs induce dependence. No pairing is inferred from row order or labels.
- Required measurements: extracellular arginine and ornithine over exposure,
  source perturbation validity, collagen as absolute output and relative to
  independently measured viable recipient exposure. Viability-normalized output
  supplements absolute output; it is not by itself a mediation analysis.
- Companion measurements: cytokine context, source/recipient survival and
  translation-stress evidence needed to assess the specified rivals. Collagen
  RNA, alpha-SMA and proliferation remain separate endpoints.

## Informative outcomes and stopping conditions

| Outcome | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Arginine preservation offsets lost ornithine contribution within observed exposures | Supports the context boundary; quantify both contributions without assuming universality |
| Collagen tracks ornithine despite independent arginine control | Favors supply dominance in that context |
| Arginine supports collagen, but source activity does not shift it enough | Recipient requirement stands; source-level compensation remains unsupported |
| Cytokine, viability or signaling changes explain the response without route evidence | Retain that explanation; no metabolic-transfer label |
| Only complete artificial deprivation yields the pattern | Does not establish relevance to the nominated source–recipient system |

Stop population inference for unidentified units; stop the joint mechanism test
without linked exposures and output. An inaccessible source holds that job, not
the independent RQ2/RQ3 qualification. All earlier outcome exposure is retained.
Actual model access, donor crossing, assay performance, meaningful effect and
precision are unresolved. No bench operations are authorized by this plan.
