## Finding → gap → purpose

The Yadav source reconstruction preserves ornithine rescue and OAT perturbation
panels; Fig. 3K records relative proline/hydroxyproline abundance, not a reproduced
source-to-recipient tracer flux. Lee's OAT/PRODH/ROS/TGF-beta evidence is a specific
alternative to attributing every OAT effect to collagen precursor supply.

**Question:** how much of the collagen response is attributable to recipient
incorporation of delivered ornithine, to signaling, or to both? The intended work
first qualifies what published assays actually measure; a joint mechanistic
effect cannot be inferred from separate source studies.

**Working prediction:** if precursor provision contributes to the response,
qualified source/recipient tracing should demonstrate incorporation into recipient
proline and collagen alongside a collagen response under interpretable exposure
and signaling conditions. **Rival:** ROS/TGF-beta, polyamine routing, alternative
substrate supply or cell loss accounts for the functional response. Incorporation
alone establishes a route, not its necessity or quantitative contribution.

## Comparison, units and endpoints

- Use the qualified RQ1 source/recipient model if empirical follow-up becomes
  available; do not substitute Lee's recipient-only OAT model for source ARG1.
- Units are identified donor/preparation pairs, with tracer aliquots, injections
  and wells nested. Separate experiments do not become paired through a common
  condition label.
- Measurands: precursor enrichment and time, corrected isotopologue fractions,
  labeled recipient proline and collagen, absolute/relative product pools and
  total collagen as provided by the actual assay. Polyamine pools remain separate.
- ROS, TGF-beta activity, glutamine/proline availability and viable source/
  recipient amount are companions required by the intended interpretation.
  A fractional label at one time is not an absolute flux or synthesis rate.
- Source-restricted and recipient perturbations must have demonstrated validity;
  OAT inhibition alone is not a unique route discriminator. Declare potential
  off-target or shared-compartment effects rather than invent specificity.

## Informative outcomes

Label in recipient collagen establishes transfer in the tested context; it does
not establish necessity. A collagen response tracking measured incorporation
under valid comparisons supports a precursor contribution. A response tracking
ROS/TGF-beta with limited or unchanged qualified incorporation favors signaling,
subject to tracer sensitivity. Both can contribute. No detected label is not
biological absence unless assay sensitivity and labeling validity support that
interpretation. Pool abundance without label/time cannot answer the route question.

No raw-MS processing or flux estimation is authorized at T0. Unknown useful
effect, assay sensitivity, donor variance and host capability remain explicit.
The purpose is model discrimination; novelty and scientific acceptance remain
unassessed. Do not create an independent second thesis solely by relabeling RQ1.
