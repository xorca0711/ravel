## Finding → gap → purpose

Yadav source interventions affect matrix-associated endpoints. The reviewed
extension does not measure mature epithelial contribution. Existing A20 evidence
shows support-ligand and ECM RNA can coexist; A13's predictive proxy failed and
strict linked-triad coverage is insufficient. Those outcomes remain preserved.
The Atg5 2026 study provides a separate niche/repair precedent, but its perturbation
and reported organoid/marker endpoints cannot establish an ARG1-specific effect
or automatically qualify absolute mature function.

**Question:** does reducing myeloid ARG1 activity alter mature epithelial recovery
through the fibroblast niche, alongside its effect on matrix production?
This is a perturbation-effect question with no justified directional claim for
epithelial function yet. The leading threat is direct intervention effects,
source-cell depletion, changed inflammation or epithelial selection being
mistaken for restored fibroblast support or maturation.

## Comparison, units and endpoints

- A future qualified model links defined myeloid source, primary lung fibroblast
  recipient/niche and alveolar epithelium. Selection of species/model requires
  actual availability and biological suitability; no blood-to-IPF equivalence.
- Primary epithelial contrast: source ARG1-restricted minus matched source-active
  context in absolute lineage-linked mature AT1 output per starting epithelial
  input, assessed after a defined recovery period. The exact mature functional
  assay and timing remain to be qualified, not inferred from a marker score.
- Matrix companion: newly produced/deposited collagen, separately measured in
  the same qualified biological design. Report each endpoint; do not invent a
  composite score or a universal matrix-to-repair correlation.
- Units: actual myeloid, fibroblast and epithelial donors/animals with declared
  crossing/nesting; shared donors induce dependence. Images, organoids and wells
  are nested observations, not independent donors.
- Separate early AT2 survival/expansion from later lineage-linked maturation and
  retained functional AT2 reserve. Organoid count/diameter, AT1 RNA/proportion and
  loss of a transitional score cannot replace the primary endpoint.
- Source/recipient attribution requires appropriate direct-exposure controls,
  viable fibroblast and epithelial tracking, intervention engagement and declared
  inflammatory context. Equal starting input or adjustment for post-treatment
  survival alone does not prove a support-activity or maturation mechanism.

## Informative outcomes and interpretation

| Joint result, if measured with adequate validity/precision | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Lower collagen and improved mature epithelial output | Supports a favorable joint response; mediator and indirect route still need attribution |
| Lower collagen but no resolved epithelial benefit | Antifibrotic effect does not establish regeneration; imprecision is retained |
| Lower collagen and poorer mature output | Indicates a possible tradeoff requiring source-activity/viability attribution |
| Epithelial changes follow direct exposure or fibroblast loss | Does not establish repaired niche support |
| Only organoid size, RNA or cell fractions change | Context/support evidence, not the proposed mature-function result |
