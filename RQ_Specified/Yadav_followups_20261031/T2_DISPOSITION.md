## RQ1 / Wg-P06: substrate balance

**Purpose/comparison:** estimate the collagen consequence of validated source
ARG1 restriction versus its appropriate control in an actual accessible
source–primary-lung-fibroblast system. The hypothesis remains that recipient
arginine preservation may offset lost ornithine contribution; the net direction
and any crossover are not assumed. Source and recipient donors, preparations
and actual crossing define biological units; wells and repeated measurements
are nested observations.

**Required measured endpoints:** calibrated extracellular arginine/ornithine
exposure over the relevant window, qualified new/deposited collagen output,
and viable source/recipient measurements kept distinct from collagen. Recipe
concentrations and intracellular peak areas do not supply those exposures.
Effect modification by recipient arginine context requires that context to be
independently characterized, with an identifiable comparison across it; a
single source-control contrast estimates only its particular context's effect.
Do not assume a starvation threshold, factorial arm or numerical margin.

**Consequential alternatives:** source loss or inflammatory changes, recipient
loss, direct intervention exposure and other nutrient changes may explain an
observed collagen change. A source-control comparison can estimate the bounded
effect once allocation, unit identity and endpoint validity are adequate; it
does not alone attribute that effect to arginine preservation or ornithine
delivery. Add rescue or orthogonal measures only for the stronger intended claim.

**Disposition:** design requirements are specified; no eligible source-linked
exposure/collagen dataset or confirmed laboratory model is available here.
Reopen with actual source/recipient access and identity/crossing, perturbation
and assay validation, temporal linkage, variance/precision and oversight.
Higher, lower or imprecise unchanged collagen are all informative; none
establishes mature epithelial repair.

## RQ2 / Wg-P06 arm: recipient routing and signaling

**Purpose/comparison:** distinguish the contribution of recipient precursor
incorporation from signaling in the same qualified source-control context.
The working prediction is that a precursor contribution should be accompanied
by demonstrated label in recipient products/collagen under interpretable
exposure; an OAT-associated collagen change alone leaves signaling, viability
and other substrate effects unresolved. This remains an arm of Wg-P06.

**Required endpoint/unit linkage:** independently identified source and
recipient preparations, precursor identity/enrichment and timing, calibrated
extracellular exposure, qualified recipient product and collagen-label
measurements if claiming transfer, and collagen output. Instrument access,
standards, sensitivity, isotope correction and contamination/carryover
discrimination must be established for the actual model. A kinetic flux claim
also needs its own identifiable dynamic measurements/model; static processed
fractions do not provide it. ROS/PRODH or other signaling measurements and
perturbation specificity are required to the extent that the intended claim
attributes a contribution to that route, not as obligatory extra arms for
every simpler effect question.

**Disposition:** Hamanaka describes recipient intracellular channels; Lee
provides separate signaling precedent. Neither establishes source-delivered
ornithine incorporation into collagen or a numerical fraction of the ARG1
effect mediated by either route. T1-A supplies bounded source descriptions;
R2 design is eligible for review. Raw-MS, flux, collagen/Lee numerical and
source-transfer estimates remain held. Reopen empirical work only with the
linked actual model, assays, preparation identities and prospective claim scope.

## RQ3 / proposed Yd1-P02: matrix and mature epithelial output

**Purpose/comparison:** estimate the joint consequence of source ARG1
restriction versus control in a qualified source–fibroblast–epithelial system.
No justified direction for mature repair is assumed. The biological unit is
the actual source/fibroblast/epithelial donor-preparation crossing, not organoid,
image, cell or well count. Matched parallel aliquots may support destructive
assays if their preparation identity is explicit; they are not new replication.

**Joint endpoints:** matrix output and **absolute lineage-linked mature
epithelial output per starting epithelial input**, with a model-valid mature
functional assay. Record early AT2 survival/expansion separately from later
mature output, and retained functional AT2 reserve when that is part of the
claim. Actual identity/function criteria, collection times and assays remain
unknown until a model is qualified. Relative marker RNA, organoid formation,
size and staining ratios are separate measured endpoints.

**Consequential alternatives:** altered survival/selection/expansion can change
marker fractions without conversion or functional maturation; direct epithelial
exposure and niche-cell loss can mimic altered fibroblast support. Measure the
relevant exposure/viability and timing before attributing a route. Adjustment
for a post-treatment cell count does not establish mediation. A bounded joint
effect comparison need not prove the whole pathway.

**Disposition:** there is no eligible linked ARG1/matrix/mature-function
numerical source here. Atg5 is a distinct intervention; Fig. 6L/M measures
SPC+/DAPI+ and PDPN+/SPC+ ratios, without the required absolute lineage/function
endpoint or matrix-preparation join. Do not repeat its source search or fit a
new proxy/classifier. Lower matrix with better or worse mature output is
informative. An imprecise null cannot establish preservation; that claim needs
a justified margin and precision plan on actual independent units.

Reopen only with actual compatible model/data access, donor/preparation linkage,
validated perturbation, epithelial lineage/functional readout and starting-input
denominator, linked matrix endpoints, timing, variance and oversight. Preserve
[A13](../../docs/research_dossiers/A13.md)'s negative proxy and coverage limits,
[A20](../../docs/research_dossiers/A20.md)'s Fzd2/Fzd1 question and separate
cohorts, [A8](../../docs/research_dossiers/A8.md)'s early/late linkage gap,
[A19](../../docs/research_dossiers/A19.md)'s marker/selection distinction and
[A22](../../docs/research_dossiers/A22.md)'s failed transport/generalization.
None becomes an ARG1 mediator or a substitute mature-output test.
