# A8: conditional maturation candidates from later article analyses

Reviewed against the [current A8 workspace on main](https://github.com/xorca0711/scRNA_seq/blob/f61343cc16c83e979b071393adccdcf8084ea294/RQ_Specified/A8_maturation_component_at1_contribution/README.md)
on 1 October 2026. The main workspace is not otherwise restored into this older
checkout. This addendum adds candidates, not an A8 endpoint analysis.

**Nb3 E2.** IFN-dominant versus hypoxia-dominant Krt8-associated responses may
differ in maturation competence. Test whether independently defined programmes
add information beyond shared transition about mature AT1 output. Time, injury
burden, mixture and proliferation are rivals. The bulk responses are not two
demonstrated trajectories, and neither is assigned a productive or arrested fate.

**Nb3 E3.** ELOVL1-linked lipid homeostasis and ATP6V0E-linked acidification
could alter the capacity to mature, separately from growth and survival. Lower
AT1-marker scores motivate the candidate, but Wnt independence is unestablished.
Functional perturbation and an independent maturation endpoint are required.
[Nb3 evidence and strict review](../../Research%20Article/gate2_N2_nabhan_2026/reports/RQ_INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md).

**England E-N5.** Mutant mixed-identity cells may acquire early AT1 features
without late maturation. Gate dependence and in-sample calibration leave
classification effects as a rival; use independent state assignment and late
protein, morphology or traced output rather than the predictor genes as the
outcome. [England review](../../Research%20Article/gate2_C2_england_2025/RQ_RETROSPECTIVE_REVIEW.md#a8-maturation).

The frozen shared/maturation component and biological-unit linkage still need
an eligible dataset. Organoid coverage, an RNA marker mean or a transfer label
cannot replace mature AT1 contribution. These are conditional candidates under
[A8](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a8), with no new grade or global identifier.
