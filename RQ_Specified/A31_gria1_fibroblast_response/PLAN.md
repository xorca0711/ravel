# A31 development plan

**Status:** proposed, unexecuted functional comparison. Outcome exposure applies
to all atlas evidence. This document is not an executable analysis contract.

## Decision and comparison

Determine whether adult alveolar-fibroblast glutamate responsiveness has a
GRIA1-dependent component. The conventional baseline is matched control versus
GRIA1 perturbation under the same qualified challenge. Define response as
challenge minus matched vehicle within each arm, then compare these responses.
This separates evoked response from basal signal. A pure genotype effect
does not require a rescue or a second mechanistic intervention merely to satisfy
a template. Additional arms depend on the claim being attempted.

## Biological unit and readout

Donor is the primary biological unit. Repeated isolations, cultures, preparations,
well measurements and cells are nested within donor. Use donor-matched
preparations when feasible and record any unpaired structure; repeated cultures
do not increase the number of independent donors. No arbitrary donor
minimum is a power calculation. Selection, blinding/allocation, background and
control validity must match the eventual system.

An acute receptor-proximal current or other validated response is a candidate
assay class, not a selected protocol. GRIA1 RNA, calcium elevation alone and
long-term growth are not interchangeable with receptor activity. Assay,
exposure, timing, effect margin, variance and sample size are unresolved.

## Informative outcomes

- Response plus a qualified perturbation effect supports a GRIA1 contribution
  under the chosen conditions; sufficiency and sole mediation remain untested.
- Response without a resolved perturbation effect leaves dependence unsupported
  or imprecise; it does not prove another receptor's mechanism.
- No response under a calibrated assay limits the atlas-to-function inference
  for that preparation; it does not erase the RNA result.
- Reduced viability, identity drift or inadequate engagement makes an apparent
  effect ambiguous and requires source/assay qualification rather than relabelling.

## Dry-analysis opportunities

Prioritize a source with matched perturbation, acute functional response,
identity/viability and independent preparation metadata. A new cell atlas can
qualify expression or anatomical transport only. A fibroblast label selected
using GRIA1 cannot provide independent identity validation. A later epithelial
co-culture readout is a separate repair question requiring its own rationale;
it is not automatically appended to obtain a positive phenotype.
