# A15 interpretation and gate clarification, 27 September 2026

This review changes current interpretation and future eligibility handling only.
It preserves the original question contract, v1/v2 withdrawals, v3 specification,
all numbers and the historical side-branch decision label. Registration is still
pending owner decision. See the [repository audit](../../../docs/audits/2026-09-27-rq-rationale/REPORT.md).

## Rival-2 result

The whole-lung programme shift and the epithelial-panel intervals are valid
reported measurements under their stated assumptions. The marker audit supports
**epithelial enrichment with no flagged large arm difference in the measured
markers**. It does not prove a pure compartment, equivalent purity or unchanged
epithelial subtype composition. A 1-log2-unit diagnostic threshold cannot exclude
smaller consequential differences, especially with four mice per arm.

The epithelial effects are inconclusive. Their wide intervals leave substantial
shifts compatible with the data; the omnibus result does not rescue equivalence.
A combined Sftpc/Ager identity score can mask opposite changes in AT2 and AT1
identity. A day-7 epithelial translatome also cannot exclude an earlier transient,
regional, post-translational or compositional change. Thus the historical phrase
`weak bound on rival 2` must not be used to say that an epithelial-state mediator
has been ruled out. Only the reported endpoint intervals bound effects in this
experiment. An outcome change plus failure to detect a mediator is not a mediation
test. Whole-lung collagen change demonstrates a downstream response, not integrin
occupancy or an isolated fibroblast response.

## Resolve the parent gate's contradiction before any future fit

Condition 2 requires three **verified independent** units per arm. Condition 6
allows a descriptive fallback when independence is unknown. Those cannot both
pass under that situation. For the parent hypothesis, unknown independence is
**unresolved eligibility and stops a confirmatory test**. A separately scoped
descriptive study can run as a different task, just as rival 2 did; it does not
pass the parent gate. This clarification tightens no numerical threshold and
retroactively licenses no analysis.

The parent contrast also needs an actual epithelial ligand perturbation with
measured engagement, not just elimination of the recipient's ligand. An AREG arm
does not remove every EGFR ligand. Compare integrin and ligand arms within the
same recipient-ligand condition, preferably with a factorial recipient condition
to quantify compensation. Removing recipient ligand changes the biological
system; it does not by itself answer the contrast in an autocrine-competent system.
Both routes can contribute, so their interaction must be possible in the design.

Recipient TGF-beta receptor blockade alone is insufficient to establish mediation:
it can create an outcome floor. Require target engagement, viability/dynamic range,
an active-versus-total ligand distinction, and an appropriate rescue or bypass
comparison where feasible. Receptor-proximal signalling demonstrates response;
attributing that response to source-specific latent-ligand activation needs the
corresponding controls. Exact margins and multiplicity belong in the future
candidate-specific specification.

## Search and evidence status

No eligible deposit was found in the recorded search. That is not proof that no
public deposit exists. The current data do not settle the parent mechanism. The
A2 observation is relevant, confounded motivation; a new question must not inherit
its grade or count it as independent confirmation. Evidence relevance is distinct
from evidence sufficiency and ownership of a claim ID.
