## Independent reference universe and explicit scale

For each Ensembl row in the complete 42,548-row deposited count matrix, retain it for normalization if its count is strictly positive in each of the eight epithelial libraries and its ID does not map to any of the seven scored panel genes or Itgb6. This whole-matrix reference is defined independently of treatment effects and panel direction, without a significance, variability, or outcome-based filter. It excludes the scored endpoints themselves and the nominated perturbation target; it does not claim that every remaining gene is biologically invariant. Record the eligible IDs, exclusions and reference count. At least 1,000 eligible rows are required; otherwise stop rather than fall back to a panel-based estimator.

For reference gene g and library j, define q_g = exp(mean_j(log(count_gj))). Define the uncentered factor r_j as the **arithmetic median of the positive ratios** count_gj/q_g; the median of an even number is the arithmetic average of the middle two. Recenter s_j = r_j / exp(mean_j(log(r_j))), so the eight factors have geometric mean 1. The normalized count is count_gj/s_j. There is no further division by that library's total.

Each corrected panel score is the equal-weight arithmetic mean of log2(count_gj/s_j + 1) over the unchanged measurable panel members. This is an **unstandardized log2 normalized-count score**, with a +1 normalized-count pseudocount, at the cohort's reference effective depth. It is not log2 CPM, and its magnitudes are not interchangeable with the frozen standardized CPM primary. The scale is fixed here rather than selected to obtain agreement. Pooled size factors are estimated without arm labels; the exact test conditions on the resulting scores.

This choice assumes the broad reference has a stable typical relative abundance (or balanced changes). A global shift in all reference genes, compositional changes, and the absence of absolute RNA controls remain limitations. Genes with any zero are excluded to make the geometric-mean ratio estimator well-defined. The exclusion can favor broadly detected genes and is disclosed rather than hidden.
