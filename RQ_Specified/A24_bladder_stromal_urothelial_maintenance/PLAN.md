# A24 development plan

Draft after outcome exposure; no executable contract is frozen.

## Biological decision

Does the aged bladder stromal compartment contribute to loss of normal urothelial barrier maintenance independently of epithelial-intrinsic ageing and changing epithelial differentiation?

## Unit and endpoint

At the conceptual level, separate stromal context from epithelial age while measuring barrier integrity, cell composition and survival independently. Animal or independently prepared biological material is the unit; cells and imaging fields are nested. Exact assay, meaningful effect, variance, resources and sample size remain unknown.

A separately measured barrier difference attributable to stromal context at comparable epithelial starting condition would support the stromal contribution. A difference confined to epithelial age or differentiation would favor the rival. Marker positivity, tissue-average RNA or a ligand-receptor score alone cannot discriminate them.

## Evidence qualification before computation

Qualify normal-bladder stromal subpopulations and epithelial identities in source-independent material, then determine whether a valid barrier endpoint and independent animals are available. Keep cancer-selected fibroblasts and constitutive umbrella-cell phenotypes separate. Do not fit a senescence score as a substitute for the missing function.

## Existing questions and supporting branches

A13 and A20 concern specified lung fibroblast-to-epithelial recovery or receptor contexts; A22 concerns the reverse epithelial-to-fibroblast output direction. A24 concerns normal bladder stroma and urothelial barrier maintenance. Shared niche biology and decomposition methods do not make those targets or endpoints interchangeable.

Nb5-P05 supports marker/endpoint qualification; P07/P08 may supply explicitly scoped sex/age sensitivities. No supporting branch establishes the stromal mechanism.

## Stop and interpretation rules

RNA detection and captured fractions do not establish protein output, secretion, senescence, barrier failure or stromal causation. The normal bladder question is not inferred from the published tumour niche, and no therapeutic direction is assigned.

If required population/animal/endpoint support is absent, report a held design,
not a null or a failed biological hypothesis. If outcomes are imprecise, retain
the uncertainty; no equivalence margin is supplied here. Freeze one justified
primary comparison, source/code hashes, biological units, scale, exclusions,
multiplicity and exposure before substantive execution. Use a new registered
contract and the research runner, preserving all earlier outcomes. Do not
invent a sample size, mediator or independent cohort to satisfy a template.

Actual resources, assay validity and precision remain unqualified. Human
scientific acceptance and experimental readiness are separate future decisions.
