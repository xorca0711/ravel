# A21: refined biological hypotheses and discriminating outcomes

**Priority 3 is a specified functional test, not an executed result.**
No inspected source supplies the required linked adult alveolar repair evidence.
The biological purpose remains the mapping of Fzd receptor identity, receiving
cell context and subsequent repair output.

## Focused hypothesis and alternatives

**H-maintenance.** Fzd4-dependent signaling sustains or restores capillary vascular
integrity during adult alveolar repair, and this competence permits later gCap
renewal and aerocyte descendant production.

**H-entry.** Fzd4 has a selective role in regenerative entry beyond its effect on
vascular integrity or survival.

**H-fate.** Fzd4 has unequal effects on gCap self-renewal and aerocyte production;
aerocyte output can be selectively impaired while gCap renewal is retained.

These remain alternatives. The current source reconstruction grounds structural
maintenance plausibility; it does not establish adult lung function. The family
screen provides no robust alternate receptor nominee. State-dependent receptor
requirements remain possible without a proposed replacement receptor.

## Required data and primary estimands

A qualifying source must connect receptor intervention and engagement,
independently defined initial gCap identity, independent animals/preparations,
initial viable labelled population, subsequent lineage identity and vascular
performance in a defined adult repair setting. Initial population measurement
must precede the intervention for the intended baseline-normalized estimand;
otherwise redefine the estimand before analysis rather than conditioning on a
post-treatment denominator. Match injury severity and observation context through
the study design. Keep homeostasis and repair separate before an interaction.

Retain two primary outcomes: absolute traced gCap descendant yield and absolute
traced aerocyte descendant yield, each relative to the initial viable labelled
population. Report absolute counts alongside normalized yields. Fractions among
survivors cannot substitute for these outcomes. Track lineage-label fidelity
and starting identity independently of Fzd4 and response genes.

Vascular performance, endothelial viability/absolute abundance, receptor
engagement and early regenerative-state entry are distinct secondary outcomes.
Junction RNA or basement-membrane coverage alone does not measure perfusion or
barrier function. Sampled scRNA cell frequencies are not absolute tissue counts.

## Discriminating biological outcomes

| Result with valid engagement and adequate precision | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Early integrity impairment, then reduced gCap and aerocyte output | Compatible with H-maintenance; further independent mechanistic evidence is needed for mediation |
| Maintained vascular function and viable pool, but lower early entry and both lineage outputs | Favors H-entry |
| Preserved gCap renewal and maintenance, but lower aerocyte yield | Favors H-fate |
| Altered integrity with both lineage outcomes preserved within predefined margins | Supports vascular maintenance without the proposed renewal consequence |
| Neither functional nor lineage effects of useful size under valid perturbation | Weakens the corresponding directional hypothesis within that context |
| Only transcript changes, unresolved lineage, poor engagement or imprecise outcomes | Inconclusive; does not select a mechanism |

Estimate total receptor-intervention effects first. Post-treatment survival
adjustment, mediation across unlinked panels, and merging tumor with normal-lung
cohorts cannot identify a direct regenerative effect. Temporal ordering alone
also cannot prove mediation. A design with independently discriminating mechanism
measurements or rescue would be needed to separate causal routes.
