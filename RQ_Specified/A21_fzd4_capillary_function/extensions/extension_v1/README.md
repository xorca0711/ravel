# A21 extension: vascular maintenance and Fzd receptor context

**Priorities 1 and 2 analysed; priority 3 specified but functionally untested.**
Executed 30 September 2026. This is exploratory source reanalysis and hypothesis
refinement, not biological acceptance or a new claim grade.

[Results](RESULTS.md) · [Three scientific figures](FIGURES.md) ·
[Methods and reproduction](METHODS.md) · [Sources](SOURCES.md) ·
[Functional hypotheses and outcomes](FUNCTIONAL_DESIGN.md) · [Parent A21](../../README.md).

The recovered Bian source workbooks support FZD4-associated restoration of
canonical signaling and basement-membrane coverage in tumor endothelium.
The perfusion comparison involves upstream Foxf1 loss, not Fzd4 rescue.
The all-ten-Fzd analysis of the existing 12-animal atlas nominates no alternative
receptor under the frozen criteria. Foxf1, Fzd4 and Lrp6 are lower in every
primary eligible transitional-versus-major gCap pair.

The focused proposal is that **Fzd4 supports vascular integrity during repair,
and this competence permits subsequent capillary repopulation**. Direct effects
on regenerative entry or aerocyte production remain alternatives. Adult lung
receptor-dependent lineage output, functional rescue and mediation are untested.
The original gCap renewal and aerocyte descendant endpoints remain separate.
