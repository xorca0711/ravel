# A21 investigation plan

**Organizing question:** Does Fzd4-dependent vascular integrity support gCap self-renewal and aerocyte production during alveolar repair?

**Working hypothesis (untested):** Fzd4 preserves capillary vascular integrity, enabling subsequent gCap self-renewal and aerocyte production after injury.

**Stage 0 completed, 30 September 2026; functional stages remain unexecuted.**
[Executed results and refined decisions](RESULTS.md) · [Figures](FIGURES.md).

The proposed biological sequence is Fzd4-dependent recruitment of injured gCap
cells into a regenerative state, followed by gCap renewal and aerocyte descendant
production. Early state/activity and later lineage output must be linked to test
that sequence. General endothelial maintenance is the competing mechanism; the
expression-replication stage only checks how much support the existing lead adds.

## Stage 0: resolve the expression lead

Recover unit identity, round, tracing window, genotype, sex, injury context,
sampling time and capillary-substate definitions. CAP1/gCap and CAP2/aerocyte
compatibility is a crosswalk, not lineage assignment. Test finer mapping with
information independent of Fzd4 and the cycling genes used in the association.

Eight source samples cannot separate all entangled covariates; a saturated
regression is not a solution. Within-round groups of four cannot supply strong
replication. Seek an independent resolved cohort for the within-state relationship;
predefine confounders, coverage, useful effect and uncertainty before inspection.
If the association fails or depends on substate composition, retire the pooled
lead rather than repeatedly trying scores or cell floors.

## Stage 1: distinct functional estimand

Test the total perturbation effect on traced capillary descendant output per
initial viable labeled population in a defined injury or homeostatic context.
Keep gCap self-renewal and aerocyte contribution distinct. Verify engagement and
selectivity, defining intervention timing relative to phase before new outcomes.
This plan selects no operational perturbation or exposure schedule.

Animals/preparations, not cells/images, provide replication. Link initial population,
descendants, viability, absolute endothelial count and vascular performance within
units. Evaluate injury and homeostasis separately before estimating an interaction.
General vascular controls need comparable observation and engagement information.

## Renewal versus maintenance

A selective lineage-output effect with preserved or independently characterized
survival/performance favors renewal specificity. A pattern confined to maintenance
favors the alternative. These interpretations require a design that distinguishes
the pathways, such as ordered independent measurements or a separate mechanistic
rescue. Conditioning on post-treatment survival in regression does not identify
a direct renewal effect and may introduce selection bias.

Freeze outcomes, useful effect margins, precision, multiplicity and independent
validation before confirmatory scoring. Precise absence of a useful renewal-specific
effect weakens the hypothesis; failed engagement or sparse outcomes are inconclusive.
Failed expression replication removes the correlation as support, without establishing
no receptor function.

First deliverable: cohort/substate/lineage evidence inventory with access status.
The current atlas is descriptive context. More pooled correlation, pseudotime or
marker scores cannot replace receptor-dependency and functional measurements.
