# A27 development plan

Draft after outcome exposure; no executable contract is frozen.

## Biological decision

In spleen CD8 cells, does the older repertoire concentration primarily reflect redistribution among differentiation states, or a change in concentration within comparable states under qualified receptor ascertainment?

## Unit and endpoint

A later association with an independently measured repertoire-function endpoint could extend this question, but response breadth, antigen specificity and immune impairment are not measured here. Animal is the biological unit; source clones and cell resamples do not add animals.

Estimate state-specific repeated-clone-cell fractions on a common sampling scale and a declared fixed-composition contrast, retaining the aggregate result. If the standardized contrast attenuates and state weights explain the pattern, redistribution is favored. Persistent supported state contrasts motivate quantitative replication; uncertain sparse results remain inconclusive.

## Evidence qualification before computation

Qualify finer labels without using clone size or age-selected genes, retain all CD8 and receptor-recovered denominators, and identify independent animals in an external source. Fix clone definition, state set, standardization and sampling support in a new contract; do not relax failed strata after seeing effects.

## Existing questions and supporting branches

A6 concerns IPF macrophage expression; A17 concerns persistent founder effects on mutant epithelial clone growth. A27 concerns TCR-defined spleen CD8 repertoires during ageing. Common mixture reasoning or the word clone cannot collapse distinct cells, clone identities and biological endpoints.

A26 asks the separate tissue-context question; P07/P08 retain sex and age-window limits. No inference for other T-cell subsets is universalized from CD8.

## Stop and interpretation rules

RNA state, clone repetition and diversity are distinct from immune function, proliferation rate or disease risk. Existing evidence and internal deletions are not independent validation. Literature precedent prevents marketing a familiar within-state finding as a new mechanism.

If required population/animal/endpoint support is absent, report a held design,
not a null or a failed biological hypothesis. If outcomes are imprecise, retain
the uncertainty; no equivalence margin is supplied here. Freeze one justified
primary comparison, source/code hashes, biological units, scale, exclusions,
multiplicity and exposure before substantive execution. Use a new registered
contract and the research runner, preserving all earlier outcomes. Do not
invent a sample size, mediator or independent cohort to satisfy a template.

Actual resources, assay validity and precision remain unqualified. Human
scientific acceptance and experimental readiness are separate future decisions.
