# A20. Do subtype-specific Fzd signals maintain a fibroblast niche that enables alveolar maturation?

**Proposed biological hypothesis; 29 September 2026; untested.**
[Canonical card](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a20) ·
[Nb2 derivation](../../Research%20Article/gate2_N1_nabhan_2023/RQ_DERIVATION.md) ·
[Investigation plan](PLAN.md).

## Working hypothesis and biological logic

Fzd2-dependent signaling helps maintain an AF1-like trophic state whose secreted
activity enables AT2 descendants to mature. Fzd1-rich fibroblast contexts provide
a different response state and are not automatically interchangeable sources of
that support. Thus the epithelial consequence of Fzd stimulation depends on the
receiving fibroblast state, not simply fibroblast expansion or receptor abundance.
The Fzd2-to-trophic-state-to-maturation chain is a proposed mechanism to test.

This explains why epithelial support and stromal remodeling may coexist without
being the same output. A fibroblast can supply a repair niche while also producing
matrix; whether that matrix is restorative or persistent requires its own endpoint.
No beneficial-versus-harmful AF1/AF2 dichotomy is assumed.

## Biological predictions and rivals

1. In a verified AF1-like starting context, comparable loss of Fzd2 produces a
   larger decrement in trophic activity and mature epithelial output than Fzd1
   loss. This direction concerns loss, not an assumed inverse agonist response.
2. The relative receptor effects differ across initial fibroblast states. A
   within-subtype comparison should therefore explain more than changing the
   proportion of AF1/AF2 cells in an untreated atlas.
3. Epithelial effects track a functional fibroblast-derived support activity;
   source/recipient separation and a mechanistic restoration comparison would
   distinguish that activity from direct epithelial drug exposure or cell depletion.

A precise absence of the predicted AF1 dependence weakens the primary hypothesis;
a reverse effect contradicts its direction. Equivalent receptor effects across
subtypes weaken the context extension. An interaction alone does not establish
a secreted mediator. General fibroblast growth, subtype replacement, mechanics,
ECM-mediated support and direct epithelial reception remain biological rivals.

## Evidence and the remaining inferential step

In five paired day42 samples, AF2 has more Fzd1 while AF1 has more Fzd2, selected
support-ligand RNA and ECM RNA. Within-AF1 Fzd1–support correlation changes sign
under omission; no pairs survive the 100-cell floor.
[These observations](../../Research%20Article/gate2_N1_nabhan_2023/branch_analysis/RESULTS.md#n6-fibroblast-subtype-changes-the-fzd1-interpretation)
do not establish receptor dependency or secreted niche activity.

The [published Fzd2 study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12964-025-02501-8)
provides mesenchymal perturbation motivation. Fzd2 involvement and the AF1/AF2
framework are prior art. The proposed extension asks about a receptor-specific,
state-dependent trophic contribution to **mature epithelial output**, keeping
growth and actual matrix remodeling separate. Specific trophic mediators remain
unnominated; ligand RNA alone is insufficient to nominate a causal one.

First build a source/assay/subtype crosswalk and inventory functional perturbation
records. [Draft scope](config/question_contract.json) remains unexecuted. A13 owns
added predictive information beyond IL1B; A15 owns epithelial integrin/TGF-beta
input. A20 concerns control of the fibroblast support state by Fzd reception.
