# A20 extension: receptor context, support activity and matrix function

**Executed 30 September 2026.** [Results](RESULTS.md) · [Three figures](FIGURES.md) ·
[Methods](METHODS.md) · [Functional evidence audit](SOURCES.md) · [Parent A20](../../README.md).

The three priorities were advanced through a functional-source audit and a new
four-donor factorial RNA analysis. Direct Fzd1/Fzd2 functional tests remain
unidentified in the inspected sources.

| Priority | Work executed | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Niche persistence versus support activity | Resolve source/recipient design, replication and endpoints | Published co-culture evidence separates growth support from differentiation questions; no matched Fzd-specific mature-output join |
| 2. Support versus matrix | Compare fixed genes in 16 donor-matched libraries; examine functional matrix context | Support and collagen RNA respond differently; deposited matrix and functional mediation are unmeasured in this RNA cohort |
| 3. Context dependence | Direct TGF-beta x CHIR interaction within four donors; Jones GEO/BioSample audit | Concurrent-input RNA interaction is executable; initial-subtype Fzd interaction and Fzd2–Notch epistasis remain untested |

The counts come from primary human fibroblasts in Ng-Blichfeldt et al. 2019,
adding a perturbation context distinct from the prior mouse atlas. CHIR acts
downstream of FZD, and the RNA treatments were concurrent rather than sequential
state conditioning. This does not replicate a Fzd-specific functional effect.

Canonical-response RNA increases while the selected support-ligand average
decreases after CHIR in every donor. TGF-beta alone raises FZD2 RNA while lowering
the support-panel average. Receptor expression and canonical-response RNA cannot
substitute for measured niche function.
