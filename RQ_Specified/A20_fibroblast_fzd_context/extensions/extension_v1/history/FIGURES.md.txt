# A20 figure gallery

Three figures, each exported as 300-dpi PNG, vector PDF and editable SVG.
These are observed source-sample results; no simulated biological outcomes are shown.
[Results](RESULTS.md) · [Methods](METHODS.md) · [Source eligibility](SOURCES.md).

## F1. Identity probes and receptor context

![A20 F1](figures/A20_F1_identity_receptors.png)

[PDF](figures/A20_F1_identity_receptors.pdf) · [SVG](figures/A20_F1_identity_receptors.svg).
Five paired day-42 source samples, with predicted doublets removed and at least
50 cells in each author-labelled subtype. Lines join the same sample; colors
identify samples and the legend records experimental round. Pdgfra/Pdgfrb and
Col13a1 support a local identity crosswalk; Col14a1 is mixed. Fzd1 and Fzd2 have
opposite subtype rankings. Each axis is log2(CPM+1), independently scaled.
Identity agreement and receptor RNA do not establish lineage, activity or function.
Source: GSE262927, reused from Nb2; [paired data](tables/paired_differences.tsv).

## F2. Selective support RNA and broad matrix RNA

![A20 F2](figures/A20_F2_support_matrix.png)

[PDF](figures/A20_F2_support_matrix.pdf) · [SVG](figures/A20_F2_support_matrix.svg).
The same five pairs as F1, retaining its sample colors. Each point is an observed
AF1-minus-AF2 difference in log2(CPM+1); black ticks are medians and grey lines are
observed ranges, not confidence intervals. Candidate support genes have different
directions, while every prespecified collagen gene is higher in AF1. Hgf differences
are small and Lrrc15 is often undetected. Gene-level RNA is not secreted protein,
functional trophic activity, deposited matrix or epithelial maturation.

## S1. Fixed eligibility and panel sensitivity

![A20 S1](figures/A20_S1_coverage_sensitivity.png)

[PDF](figures/A20_S1_coverage_sensitivity.pdf) · [SVG](figures/A20_S1_coverage_sensitivity.svg).
Paired medians and full observed ranges for all six predefined doublet/floor
combinations. The 20/50/100-cell floors retain 8/5/0 pairs. No eligible pairs at
100 cells is an explicit limitation, not an estimate of zero effect. The four-gene
support, four-gene earlier ECM and eight-gene collagen panels have separate gene
membership; their magnitudes do not quantify relative amounts of biological
function. [Eligibility](tables/eligibility.tsv) · [Estimates](tables/paired_summary.tsv).
