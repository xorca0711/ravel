# A20. Do subtype-specific Fzd signals maintain a fibroblast niche that enables alveolar maturation?

**Atlas context and priorities 1–3 extension completed, 30 September 2026.
Direct functional H1/H2 remain untested.**

[Extension results and refined hypotheses](extensions/extension_v1/RESULTS.md) ·
[Three new figures](extensions/extension_v1/FIGURES.md) ·
[Priority decisions](extensions/extension_v1/metadata/priority_decisions.tsv).

The extension adds four paired human fibroblast donors across concurrent
TGF/CHIR inputs. Canonical-response RNA rises while selected support-ligand RNA
falls; higher FZD2 RNA after TGF does not imply greater functional support.
CHIR acts downstream of FZD, so receptor-specific causation remains unresolved.
The original atlas analysis below and its scientific outputs are preserved.
[Results](RESULTS.md) · [Three figures](FIGURES.md) · [Sources and eligibility](SOURCES.md) ·
[Methods](METHODS.md) · [Investigation plan](PLAN.md) · [Canonical question](../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md#a20).

The present evidence narrows the question to whether Fzd2 maintains a selective
fibroblast support state, and whether its epithelial benefit comes from preserving
an expandable AT2 pool or from instructing later maturation. The proposed
Fzd2-to-secreted-support-to-mature-output chain is not an established mechanism.

In five paired day-42 source samples, AF1 has higher Fzd2, Wnt2, Fgf7/Fgf10 and
collagen RNA than AF2; Hgf is lower. Pdgfra/Pdgfrb identity probes support the
within-source comparison. The profile is not a universal increase in trophic
activity, and matrix expression does not classify a state as beneficial or harmful.
Eight pairs qualify at 20 cells per subtype, five at 50, and none at 100.

The analysis reuses the Niethamer atlas already examined in
[Nb2-N6](../../Research%20Article/gate2_N1_nabhan_2023/branch_analysis/candidate_questions/Nb2-N6.md).
It adds independent identity probes and individual-gene/collagen-panel comparisons;
it is not independent replication. Published Fzd2 perturbation and Notch/lineage
studies supply complementary evidence, but neither provides the registered
Fzd2-versus-Fzd1, subtype-specific mature-output comparison.

The [refined hypotheses](RESULTS.md#hypothesis-refinement-and-discriminating-biological-outcomes)
retain receptor specificity, fibroblast abundance, secreted support, mechanics and
epithelial maturation as distinguishable biological explanations. A13 and A15 retain
their existing scopes. No claim grade or biological acceptance has changed.
