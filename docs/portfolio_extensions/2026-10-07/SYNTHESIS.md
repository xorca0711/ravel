## Joint reasoning across groups

These results share a measurement problem: a population label, mixture or
aggregate score can diverge from the biological quantity of interest. Their
units and endpoints remain distinct. They are not combined into a meta-analysis
or a universal biological mechanism.

- A11 and A30 both narrow RNA associations, but one is paired tissue prediction
  and the other a compartment/support estimand. Their gene scores are not joined
  as an independent causal signature.
- A17 and A25 demonstrate denominator/representation distinctions. A mathematical
  clone distribution is not an animal replicate of microglial ageing.
- Nb4 supports a separate response experiment. A9/A20/A22 supply related
  source/recipient reasoning, not independent GRIA1 functional evidence.
- Niethamer reuse across Nb1/Nb2/A3/A20/A21, Wp/A30 reuse of GSE138266, and atlas
  component reuse remain single source lineages. More reports do not add animals.

## New-question decision

Register **[A31](../../../RQ_Specified/A31_gria1_fibroblast_response/README.md)**
as a proposed adult alveolar-fibroblast GRIA1 acute-response question. Existing
Nb4-P09 owns the exposed expression premise. Closest published expression and
nerve/fibroblast precedents limit novelty; the planned increment is a specific
functional endpoint in a qualified context.

The canonical card, dossier, development plan, source context and versioned
qualitative schematic are registered together. The baseline is donor-matched
GRIA1 perturbation/control under the same challenge, comparing
challenge-minus-vehicle response. Donor is the primary unit; repeated cultures,
wells and cells are nested. Viability/identity are qualification and outcome
measurements, not automatic post-treatment adjustments or survivor gates.
Assay, engagement, meaningful effect, variance and sample size remain unknown.

The main agent reviewed source/result/overlap evidence and incorporated an
independent G3 scientific review of A31. That review clarified units and evoked
response; it did not supply human acceptance. The rendered schematic was
visually inspected. No mechanism, laboratory readiness or claim grade is promoted.

A11/A17/A25/A30 extensions keep their existing owners. G1's England E-N6 and
G3's Wg functional companions remain article-local candidates with their source
and endpoint holds. Creating another A ID would not solve those gaps. All
earlier questions remain available for development; eligibility, not a portfolio
ranking, determined the runs in this pass.
