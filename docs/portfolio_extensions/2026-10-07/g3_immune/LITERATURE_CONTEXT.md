# G3 A30 common-support extension: literature context

## Published starting point

[Schafflick et al., Nature Communications, 14 January 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-14118-w), Results “CSF leukocytes exhibit a specific composition and transcriptome,” Figure 3c–g, Methods “Doublet detection” and “T cell clustering analysis”: compartment and CD4 subcluster differences are established premises. Their Scrublet/cluster exclusions differ from this repository's operational lineage gate. This source owns GSE138266; its publication and every Wp/A30 reanalysis are one evidence lineage.

[Van Puijfelik et al., EBioMedicine 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42250325/), Figure 2B–F legend and indexed Results: CCR5-high Th17.1 enrichment in paired CSF provides a concrete selective-trafficking/subset rival. Its natalizumab comparison is in blood, not longitudinal CSF. Existing A30 context supplies the prior source read; this pass inspected PubMed/PMC indexed text and Figure 2 legend, not the supplementary gating plots.

[Hayashi, Mittl et al., Nature Immunology, version of record 5 February 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41590-025-02412-3), Figure 1e–f, Extended Data Figure 2a, Results “Identification of T cell subsets by single-cell sequencing”: paired compartments have distinct expression, including different immediate-early patterns between CD4 and CD8 populations. This limits treating one activation panel as a complete biological activation measurement. Methods/Results say the cohort RNA was previously published; no independent-validation claim is made here.

## What this question could add

A bounded context/sensitivity extension of A30, not a new canonical question: estimate the RNA association in states actually represented within both samples of a donor, report excluded coverage, and inspect measured continuous activation. This does not calibrate the gene null, certify CD4 purity or establish local induction.

## Current hypothesis and rival

The existing A30 hypothesis asks whether inflammatory-associated RNA remains higher in CSF among comparable putative CD4 states. The principal threat here is missing cross-compartment support; residual activation and selective trafficking remain biological explanations. Tuple: paired human CSF/PBMC; deposited cross-sectional sampling; ten donors; same exposed retained cells and de novo states; inflammatory/regulatory RNA means; no intervention or clinical effect.

## What the comparison would teach

Persistence on substantial observed support supports a residual association in that restricted population. Attenuation, sign changes or poor coverage limit transport to all retained cells. Linear activation standardization is a sensitivity under separate within-compartment slopes, not a causal adjustment. Original pooled, common-state, activation-overlap and standardized estimands remain separately labelled. No regulatory equivalence is inferred.
