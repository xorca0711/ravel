### All nine Nb4 proposal families

P01 species transfer and P02 evolutionary source redistribution need animal/pool,
orthology and label qualification; P02 also requires a justified third-species
context. P03 disease decomposition needs harmonized diagnosis/donor/state inputs;
P07 regional diseased epithelium needs independently measured anatomy/pathology.
P04 tissue association has two source lung/blood donor pairs; independent paired
units are missing. P05 myeloid identity/state needs a fixed crosswalk and
independent coverage. P06 AT2-s fails the source matched-coverage gate. P08
pericyte TBX5 has descriptive contrasts already, but vessel-bed identity and
contractility are not measured. P09 is the bounded new run here, because its
first expression/specificity/transport stage was explicitly unexecuted and its
source plus external neural/fibroblast observations are available. This does not
retire or rank the other eight proposals. [Master plans](../../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/README.md).
