## Current hypothesis and rival

Do SCN7A and GRIA1 have reproducible, same-cell expression in source-labelled
alveolar fibroblasts across donors, studies and assays? The consequential
alternative is that a two-gene sensory interpretation conflates separate
expressing cells, neural contamination, anatomical sampling or detection depth.
This is a measurement/transport question; sensory function remains unmeasured.

Comparison tuple: normal adult human lung; author-labelled fibroblast subtypes
and available neural/mural/other source populations; no intervention; concurrent
RNA counts; matched alveolar versus adventitial subtype; donor as biological
unit, with assay, protocol and anatomical region nested rather than replicated.

## Published finding to repository observation to next comparison

Published atlases nominate fibroblast expression, while the independent atlas
also identifies neural SCN7A. The repository P09 plan remains unexecuted; its
existing C3/chemokine/MYRF results do not answer this two-gene question. The
new comparison therefore estimates each gene and their intersection within
donor/assay/region strata, transports fixed genes to the separate Madissoon
study, and retains neural controls and depth diagnostics.

Joint detection with both genes individually transported would support a
narrow expression phenotype worth localization. Discordant genes, sparse joint
detection, assay reversals or predominantly neural expression would narrow the
candidate. Neither result establishes sodium sensing, glutamatergic activity,
membrane localization, excitability or support of alveolar repair.

## Evidence limits and wet baseline

Published labels were not recomputed using the two genes, but their original
clustering/annotation may have involved either gene; label independence is not
established. A fixed SOX10 plus S100B-or-PLP1 flag is a sensitivity analysis,
not validated doublet removal. Raw droplets and independent protein/localization
data are absent. Full measured-gene library denominators prevent a panel-only
normalization artifact; assay effects are still not randomized.

A later wet baseline would first establish cell-resolved RNA/protein
localization in independently identified adult alveolar fibroblasts and glial
controls. A qualified direct response to a specified stimulus, with an
appropriate receptor/channel perturbation and viability control, would then
address function. Stimulus, model access, assay validity, meaningful effect
margin and biological-unit variance are presently unknown. No bench readiness
or new global question ID follows automatically from this analysis.
