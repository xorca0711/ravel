## Summary

This proposal combines documentation depth, biological rationale, scope narrowing
and the consequences of completed analyses. It covers A0–A18, A12-S1 and the
shared A5/A11 workspace. Priority means value for further work in this repository,
not a new literature-wide novelty assessment.

A12/A13 need the most local rationale development. A16 needs conceptual correction;
A0/A1/A11 need sharper boundaries between their ambitions and supported decisions.
A5 is comparatively well narrowed, and A10 has a clear measured endpoint.
Missing evidence cannot be supplied by more prose or repeated scoring.

The [canonical register](../../../RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md) retains current hypotheses;
the [execution ledger](../../roadmap_runs/2026-09-28-gap-fill/RESULTS.md) records
completed work and remaining gates. Candidate wording below does not change
stable IDs, claim grades, owner retain/reject decisions or frozen contracts.

## Plans for each question

The proposed framing states the next scientific decision, not an established
answer. “Now” identifies work possible from existing evidence. The gate identifies
what must change before another analysis is useful. No empty folders are needed
solely to make the question series look uniform.

current result; a conserved-process or causal-fate hypothesis remains separate.

**Now:** add a scope/decision summary connecting discovery, mature-endpoint

2. **Consolidate the better-defined branches:** A5/shared ownership, A10 timing
   and units, and A2/A9/A15's separate claims. Draft A17's model amendment from
   verified inputs before marking any refit runnable.
3. **Keep evidence gates explicit:** A3/A4/A7/A8/A14/A18 and A12-S1 require their
   named linked inputs. A6 remains low priority. No prior failed gate is silently
   relaxed, and the bounded searches do not prove that suitable data cannot exist.
4. **Synchronize only adopted scope changes:** update the canonical card and
   workspace introduction together, identify dated amendments and retain historical
   evidence. Grouping A8/A9 is a proposal, not deletion. A15–A18 retain/reject
   decisions remain open.

For every revised question, a reader should be able to identify the biological
premise, main rival, population and outcome, narrow comparison, independent unit,
possible interpretations and exact next evidence gate. Add a rationale or plan
file only where existing documents cannot supply that account clearly. Completion
is defined by those decisions, not a target word count.

This proposal does not execute analyses, mark the computational queue runnable,
conduct a new literature/data search, contact authors or change registered grades.
