## Current source authority

[docs/DATASETS.md](../../DATASETS.md) is the single repository-wide public-source
list linked from the main README. It incorporates the previous inventory and
November supplement, additional comparison-specific candidate decisions, pending
sources, recorded screens and source-project/alias locators. Local article/RQ
registers remain supporting evidence for their source versions and comparisons.

Coverage includes all 544 identifiers from the preceding bounded census, the
114-row Wp screen (64 recorded rule failures, 50 requiring record inspection),
102 register-search question/source rows and 16 G0 feasibility records. Merged Saxton work adds 19 identifiers and
18 comparison-specific interaction-screen rows: 563 source/project identifiers
are represented in the master list. These are
not counts of utilized or independent datasets. Used, inspected, rejected for a
specific comparison, pending, search-only and alias roles remain distinct. The
master list also identifies non-GEO author products and resources; sample/file
inventories remain in the owning evidence records. A missing explicit source
identity remains unknown rather than being invented.

The original storage inventories and v1/v2 accession CSVs are preserved as dated
artifacts. Their 7 November counts are not presented as a live storage scan.
Historical candidate assessments retain their dates and comparison scope;
later recorded use is stated separately. Search hits do not become individually
rejected studies without a supporting decision.

[Move manifest](document_moves.json) records 20 relocated documents, original

- `docs/history/reviews/`: completed framing, alignment and presentation reviews.
- `docs/history/source-inventories/`: the incorporated November supplement.
- `docs/methods/tools/`, `background/` and `workflows/`: reference/teaching material.

The previous documentation index is retained in the review-history folder.
Current indexes identify 36 RQs, 39 article-local candidates and 20 paper packages.
Twelve old paths retain small compatibility locators for recorded machine or
frozen-document consumers. Their original section anchors are retained.

Generated claim summaries, the claim register, original pipeline/rationale
records, all registered scientific contracts/receipts, numerical outputs and
hash-bound Markdown remain unchanged. Audits, historical runs and source
qualification collections already have purpose-specific folders and retain their
stable evidence paths. No worktree, raw cache, question, adverse result or evidence
folder was deleted as unused. No validator or governance rule was weakened.
