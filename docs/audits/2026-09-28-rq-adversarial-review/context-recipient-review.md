## A3: separate injury association, origin, niche and memory

**Verdict:** biologically credible, locally unidentified. Existing signals concern population composition and confounded time comparisons. `mouse_design_audit.csv` gives G1's age-inclusive model seven columns but rank six. A covariate cannot separate effects the design does not identify. N4 has no relevant cross-round comparison at late time points. Late sampling also lacks an independently established recovery endpoint.

**Biological attack:** recruitment and lasting niche change are explanations of interest, not merely technical nuisances to regress away. Aegerter's primary study distinguishes recruited macrophages from resident cells and shows persistence of origin need not imply persistence of the functional profile. [Aegerter et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41590-019-0568-x). An aging study independently implicates the surrounding lung environment. [McQuattie-Pimentel et al.](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/140299). Therefore “injury versus aging” is too broad to establish an intrinsic memory mechanism even if its observational contrast becomes identifiable.

**Gap-fill:** first nominate one macrophage programme and a useful effect margin. Require independent mice with matched age/harvest, injured and sham groups represented within processing strata, and programme-independent state annotation. Estimate an injury-history effect on both abundance and within-state expression; retain origin as a distinct stratification when available. If origin cannot be measured, name the result population-associated. For a memory claim require linked ancestry and a later measured function or a design separating cell-intrinsic persistence from niche influence. Stop if time/condition remain aliased; do not replace missing controls with an integration method. A precise interval within the margin can retire the specified programme prediction; imprecision cannot.

## A4: a sharper question than whether two markers overlap

**Verdict:** sequential Wnt history and IL-1 responsiveness remain untested. Neither chromatin co-accessibility nor depth-matched co-detection is a substitute. The Nb1 ligand differences survive a relevant measurement control, but source RNA and receiver response are separate observations.

**Biological attack:** Wnt-supported identity, proliferative competence, actual proliferation and differentiation are different endpoints. Nabhan reports niche-associated maintenance and context-dependent autocrine Wnt, so a binary Wnt-maintenance versus IL-1-transition story is unnecessarily rigid. [Nabhan et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29420258/). Sequential and simultaneous activity can coexist, and a selected lineage can be enriched before a response without Wnt causing it.

**Gap-fill:** define the quantity as later response/fate conditional on independently verified prior pathway history, compared with suitable history-negative cells in the same biological units. Require present activity measured separately from persistent reporter label, known recorder specificity/decay, linked descendants and a viability denominator. Freeze an endpoint and a useful contrast before comparing groups. Restrict any RNA-only follow-up to an expression-replication question. Do not reopen route B with another favorable control. The newer England deposit comparison described in the main report is relevant to state entry but still supplies no Wnt-history link.

## A6: useful decomposition, weak standalone mechanism

**Verdict:** keep conditional and low priority; respect the owner's rejection of A6 as the next analysis in `DEVELOPMENT.md:824–847`. That rejects its prioritization, not all possible future macrophage biology.

**Biological attack:** “within-state” depends on annotation resolution. Coarse states hide disease-induced mixture; fine states can define away the change being tested. Disease may itself determine entry into a state, so conditioning on that state can also select different populations. Harmonization cannot turn these into the same causal cell population. Proliferation RNA and more proliferating cells are distinct quantities; neither explains fibrosis by itself. Primary work already establishes a role for macrophage origin in fibrosis, raising the bar for a new biological contribution. [Misharin et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5551573/).

**Gap-fill:** only reopen around a named effector programme, origin or tissue outcome. Use fixed, independently supported common states and donor units; audit common support across diagnoses/cohorts before fitting. Predefine a composition-standardized contrast and a separate within-state contrast; report the omitted/unmatched population. Check a small prespecified annotation-resolution sensitivity. The existing validation proliferating subset has 3 IPF/1 control; it cannot carry replication. Require independent cohort transport and meaningful precision. A narrow within-state interval near zero bounds that programme under that state definition; it does not establish that all IPF biology is composition-only.

## A11: lesion association survives; specificity and added information do not yet follow

**Verdict:** eight positive Kim pairs support a lesion-associated RNA shift. The stronger predefined result remains unresolved (`q=0.0547`, relative-score interval spanning zero). This is neither a passing result “almost significant” nor evidence that no addition exists. The source-list partition contains pairwise sharing and no universal three-context core.

**Biological attack:** tumour epithelium versus normal AT2 compares different identity/composition distributions. List-exclusive genes need not be lesion-exclusive, and subtracting the shared score assigns a fixed one-to-one tradeoff between instruments without establishing conditional independence. The acute comparator tests general inducibility in a small, selected explant population; it cannot settle cancer specificity in either direction. Gene-set background displacement does not make the original pragmatic 0.10 score margin invariant across normalizations. A new margin would require a newly declared instrument, not retroactive replacement of the original decision.

**Gap-fill:** preserve both completed tests. Clarify whether the next claim is lesion enrichment, conditional prediction beyond a shared score, or specificity relative to non-neoplastic repair. These need different endpoints. Require independent, comparable epithelial identities and donor-level contrasts across the relevant contexts; retain malignant/normal identity uncertainty rather than treating histology or reference labels as proof. Freeze normalization, gene membership, comparators and useful margin. For predictive addition fit a training-only shared-component baseline and a nested extension evaluated on unseen donors/studies. For specificity require the relevant independent comparator and a direct contrast of effects; two separate significance calls do not test specificity. A future precise bound can reject the nominated specificity prediction; another underpowered negative cannot. No gene trimming to rescue the failed relative criterion.

## A12 and A12-S1: predictive context is promising but source and direction remain conditional

**Verdict:** the epithelial pilot earns external validation. Reaggregation of saved predictions reproduces RMSE 0.42714 to 0.32437, beating the training-mean baseline 0.37628 (`Q2=0.25688`). This is a stronger statement than improvement over an already poor source model alone. The fibroblast result worsens and is unstable; it should not inherit the epithelial interpretation. See [independent arithmetic evidence](evidence.json).

**Biological attack:** the signed equal-weight RNA index is a declared measurement, not calibrated receptor availability. Its inhibitory terms can represent a responding/compensating state; the same endpoint may influence predictor RNA. Gene disjointness removes direct gene reuse but not reverse direction, correlated inflammation, compositional change or shared library denominators. The additive model tests conditional information; it does not test that recipients modify a ligand's effect. Adding TNF is a useful rival but does not exhaust alternative inflammatory inputs. Twelve patients, five predictors, fixed ridge and reused cohorts warrant restraint even with honest folds.

**Source identity:** P4 shows macrophage-majority attribution is sensitive to unknown assignment in 62/70 patient-histology units, and still 55/70 under the alternate threshold. Those units come from 23 patients. They are not 70 independent replications. Label confidence thresholds do not identify the unknown cells. This uncertainty does not prohibit a clearly conditional assigned-macrophage prediction; it prohibits calling that source dominant across tissue or secreted protein.

**Gap-fill:** freeze the current epithelial instrument and prediction procedure for an independent cohort, including normal/lesion pairing, state rules, TNF comparator, baseline prediction, normalization and primary loss. Determine a worthwhile held-out improvement and sample precision before seeing validation outcomes. Report calibration, absolute prediction performance and donor influence as well as relative loss. Resolve unknown-cell identity with independent annotation/morphology/protein evidence and relevant contamination assessment before elevating tissue-wide source attribution. Keep accounting bounds visible until then. Only after transport should a separately specified temporal or selective-response design test receptor competence and signalling direction. Retire the nominated predictor if external data precisely exclude useful improvement; do not retune it on that validation set. A12-S1 closes only with identity evidence, not a different classifier cutoff.

## A13: a completed negative predictive pilot, not a blocked task

**Verdict:** preserve the original failed coverage gates and the later amended test. Arithmetic reaggregation confirms RMSE 0.22828 to 0.23726 and joint `Q2=-0.19837`; all eligible sensitivities worsen aggregate loss. Eight individually improved predictions do not overcome the prespecified aggregate deterioration. The candidate fibroblast programme has no current predictive case for expansion on these patients.

**Biological attack:** a fibroblast TGF-beta hallmark is not active TGF-beta, and an epithelial HPCS-related RNA score is not plasticity capacity or future fate. Both can reflect disease severity and selection of recovered cells. The model neither tests mediation nor feedback. Excluding TGIF1 prevents literal gene overlap; it cannot supply these missing causal links. A failed predictor is compatible with a fibroblast effect acting through another route, time or measurement.

**Gap-fill:** close this computational pilot. Reopen only with an independently motivated recipient endpoint or fibroblast signal and an independent population with genuinely matched triads. Require a common fibroblast state definition rather than choosing the largest label per patient, sufficient independent patient support based on precision, and identical held-out evaluation of source/TNF baseline versus extension. Declare any changed compartment rule as a new estimand. For feedback require temporal ordering or a selective intervention with a distinct downstream readout. Do not lower cell floors or cycle through modules to find gain. The broad biological hypothesis stays open; this nominated predictor is deprioritized.

## A14: strongest route to an outcome, still a conditional design

**Verdict:** the existing P5 design is a useful next causal/outcome direction. Its advantage is the separation of initial state entry from later recovery, not the mere collection of more marker panels. The source study already reports withdrawal-related maturation markers, so repeating that observation alone is replication. [Choi et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32750316/).

**Biological attack:** a withdrawal-versus-continuous comparison changes recent exposure, cumulative exposure and schedule together. Call this a schedule effect unless those dimensions are independently identified. A recipient perturbation may alter baseline support or survival. Transitional-score loss can mean maturation, reversion, death or replacement. A fraction mature among survivors can rise while total mature yield falls.

**Gap-fill:** retain the proposed independent-preparation blocks, verified post-entry recipient intervention, matched handling, exposure/engagement checks, separate viability and lineage denominators, and independent mature protein/morphology endpoint. Primary estimands are the preparation-level recovery-schedule contrast and its recipient-status interaction, tested directly with multiplicity specified. Mature yield per starting lineage-labelled input is preferable to fractions alone. Add a separate validated function only if the claim is functional repair. Estimate variance of those preparation-level contrasts to plan precision; the existing standardized-effect scenarios are not a chosen sample size. Failed specificity, engagement, withdrawal verification or survival makes a result inconclusive. A precise absence can retire each hypothesis separately. No experiment was performed in this review.
