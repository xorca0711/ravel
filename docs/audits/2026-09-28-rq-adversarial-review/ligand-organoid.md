## Confirmed implementation defect: A15 normalization sensitivity

In [02_rival2_execute.py:551](../../../RQ_Specified/A15_epithelial_integrin_tgfb_activation/scripts/02_rival2_execute.py), `size_factors()` estimates each library's median ratio against geometric means **from raw counts**. At lines 565–566 the score divides a raw count by `sf[c] * totals[c] / 1e6`. Those factors already include a library-depth component. Multiplying by the full library total removes that component again. This is not the stated median-of-ratios alternative to CPM.

An exact counterexample makes the defect independent of biological assumptions: two libraries with identical proportions and counts `(100,200,300)` and `(200,400,600)` have size factors `1/sqrt(2)` and `sqrt(2)`. Correct count/size-factor normalization gives identical values. The implemented denominator gives library B **half** of A's normalized value, approximately a −1 log2 score difference for sufficiently expressed genes, despite identical composition. [Executable counterexample](verify_review.py) and [saved evidence](evidence.json).

The factors also use only positive genes in `targeted` (line 552), which is a targeted panel universe rather than an independently justified broad reference. That is a second assumption requiring examination; it is not needed to prove the double-normalization defect.

**Impact:** withdraw confidence in the alternative-normalization check and the statement that both declared sensitivities agreed. The saved primary CPM calculation is separate, and this finding alone does not change its numerical value or resolve the biological question. Existing output-verification success did not protect against this methodological error.

**Gap-fill correction:** append a dated erratum, retain original files, define a genuine count-normalization alternative with a justified reference universe and explicit score scale, add proportional-library invariance as an independent test, and regenerate only the affected sensitivity into a new output directory. Recompute its effect, interval and gate consequences, then reconcile the current interpretation. No result is predicted in advance. This is the highest-priority computational repair in the reviewed snapshot.

## Confirmed interpretation defect: C49's bound

[CLAIMS.md:119](../../../CLAIMS.md) describes the 22-donor E6 null as bounding correlations relative to rho 0.43. But [e6_donor_level_axis_coupling.py:180–184](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_05_cardoso_2026/trials/e6_donor_level_axis_coupling.py) returns `1.96/sqrt(n-1)`: an approximate significance threshold. It is neither a confidence limit on the observed association nor a detectable effect with specified power. With rho 0.348 and p 0.112, absence/equivalence does not follow.

Propose correcting the explanatory wording through the claim owner's process while preserving the existing grade and numerical result. If an inferential bound is needed, specify the association model, a meaningful margin and valid donor-level uncertainty. C36's “no analysis; CellChat unavailable” is also a historical status statement that later expression/LR work has superseded; the biological claim of reception nevertheless remains unestablished. These are distinctions between stale justification and a changed evidence grade.

## A2: delivery is plausible, but failure of an abundance screen does not establish it

**Verdict:** the source-ranking work and screen lead justify a question, not a delivery mechanism. The two legs do not manipulate delivery relative to abundance. The title's forced alternative should become a quantitative question about their contributions under a specified recipient context.

**Biological attack:** the cited macrophage–pericyte mechanism is a particular cellular system. It cannot be silently transported to epithelial source–fibroblast cultures. [Minutti et al.](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/181058/). Fibroblast-produced amphiregulin is a relevant competing source in the other cited mechanism. [Zhou et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23086930/). The recovered EGF medium, partial source perturbation and unchanged recipient ligand provide several ways a source-specific decrement can be small. RNA abundance is not the delivered protein quantity; neither RNA source ranking nor compartment CPM ratios resolve compensation. Conversely, a null donor correlation does not positively support short-range delivery.

**Gap-fill:** first resolve the exact biological system and outcome: source contribution to a defined recipient response, rather than “the AREG mechanism” across all tissues. Require independent source/recipient preparations and source/guide/position crossing, known medium context, direct source/recipient ligand evidence and a recipient engagement readout. Prespecify the contrasts for changing source supply, changing presentation, and their dependence on recipient state. Let both routes contribute. Use viable recipient output and culture composition as separate endpoints; “not detectably changed” is not an equivalence control. A source-specific null is informative only with adequate engagement, range and precision. Preserve the current screen and depth-pass decisions; another source-RNA ranking or depth-budget sweep does not fill this gap.

## A9: receptor RNA/resource coverage is a prerequisite, not evidence for competence

**Verdict:** mechanistically plausible but weakly motivated by the current differential coverage alone. A 7/22 EGFR_ERBB2 coverage result versus an 11-donor rule describes an assay/resource gate. It does not establish which receptor complexes dominate biologically.

**Biological attack:** AREG binds EGFR; other ERBB expression and resource representation can influence inference without changing AREG's primary binding identity. Ligand-specific receptor use and TGF-beta/ERBB coupling have direct precedents. [Primary ERBB study](https://aacrjournals.org/cancerres/article/70/19/7421/559654/ERBB-Receptor-Activation-Is-Required-for). Resource versions are alternative representations of prior knowledge, not independent experiments. Constitutive receptor RNA can differ from surface receptor, receptor activation or an inducible response.

**Gap-fill:** retain a within-state RNA coverage screen only as eligibility work. Test a fixed ligand response against directly measured receptor availability/activation, with independent preparations and validated receptor-specific contrasts. Define whether the aim is response prediction, effect modification or complex identity; the last requires direct complex evidence. Predefine useful changes and controls for basal survival/support. No additional database voting can replace those measurements. A precise receptor-specific effect supports the nominated competence component; failed engagement is inconclusive.

## A10: concurrent organoid size association is the supported endpoint

**Verdict:** growth-related epithelial RNA adds conditional information in the measured screen, including beyond the nominated E2F/G2M scores. It is not a validated forecast, independent-preparation result or repair measurement. Negative plate R-squared on three of four plates is a material transport failure even though relative loss improves on all four.

**Biological attack:** concurrent RNA can reflect the larger organoid rather than precede growth. Area can reflect architecture, lumen, segmentation and cell size as well as cell number; the recovered bounding-box method prevents treating an earlier arithmetic inconsistency as proof of incorrect area units. Epithelial RNA fraction is not a direct cell fraction. A new Hallmark partition is not proof of distinct mechanisms, and a fibroblast RNA increment near zero is not evidence against niche support. Four repeated wells from one mixture do not make population uncertainty identifiable.

**Gap-fill:** recover the preparation/source/lot-to-library map and imaging calibration/segmentation meaning before another fit. Choose the target explicitly: concurrent size association, future growth from an earlier measurement, or mature functional yield. For transport, use independent preparations and a common target/control design across plates; separate unseen-target from unseen-preparation evaluation. Freeze the transformations and train-only prediction procedure, and compare with a usable baseline. Require worthwhile absolute performance/calibration as well as relative gain. Functional repair additionally needs independent maturity/viability/function. If the design map or temporal ordering cannot be established, close the concurrent-association result rather than promising a stronger prediction from additional scores.

## A15: the parent contrast is still blocked, and its two routes can cooperate

**Verdict:** retain as a proposed mechanistic direction pending the owner's registration decision. Itgb6-associated screen change is a lead shared with A2; the antibody study is contextual evidence, not an independent test of the same epithelial-source/recipient contrast.

**Biological attack:** epithelial activation of a latent ligand and supply of an EGFR ligand are not exclusive possibilities. Fibroblast-side amplification, matrix availability, epithelial state change and recipient baseline support remain live rivals. The antibody side-branch reads whole lung and an enriched epithelial translatome, not isolated fibroblast reception or active ligand delivery. The epithelial intervals permit large effects. Its source paper also contains an epithelial TGF-beta response measurement, so a null in two selected panels and an omnibus cannot support a claim that no epithelial response exists. [Auyeung et al., Figure 4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35170357/). That precedent does not prove mediation either.

**Gap-fill:** repair the sensitivity first. For the parent test, require verified independent units, a bounded epithelial ligand arm alongside the integrin arm, comparable recipient conditions, measured active-versus-total ligand and a recipient-proximal response. Separate direct receptor engagement from downstream fibrosis. Preserve outcome dynamic range and viability when using route-discriminating controls; an outcome floor after receptor blockade is not proof of mediation. Compare source and recipient contributions without deleting the very recipient compensation the question purports to study, or explicitly declare that change of system. Unknown preparation identity cannot pass the parent gate via its descriptive fallback. Stop when route-specific engagement or identifiability fails. A positive RNA change alone should never advance this mechanism to a repair or therapeutic claim.
