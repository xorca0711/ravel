### A0 — conserved epithelial transition programme — L

**Start:** different epithelial transitions might reuse a programme that could
eventually modulate fate. **Now:** the frozen 50-gene lung programme passed its
discovery comparison but failed intestinal intermediate-versus-both-endpoints
transfer in three mice; only one had the intended positive intermediate-minus-
mature direction. The current scope correctly contracts to state correspondence
and construct validity. This is a useful failed operational generalization,
not a new conserved mechanism and not a refutation of all shared repair biology.
The next decision is whether independent biological evidence justifies the
source/target mapping; retuning genes on the failed target would answer a new,
exposed question. [Result](../../../RQ_Specified/A0_conserved_epithelial_transition_program/reports/PILOT_V1_RESULTS.md)

## What qualifies as noise or drift in this record

The problem is not the presence of hypotheses or unfavorable outcomes. A useful
hypothesis can remain untested. The problematic steps are identifiable:

| Pattern | Concrete record | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| A technical result becomes a mechanism | Original A1 silenced/closed story; A9 database absence; A29 raw Compass sign and claim that an expression-matched null absorbed TPM composition; A30 activation-excluded wording | These readings were withdrawn or corrected. Carry the correction, not the earlier favorable headline. |
| A failed proxy acquires a new biological label | A22 general predictor → focal protein extension; A23 marker screen → entry/residence; A3 persistence → memory | The extensions can be reasonable, but they are new comparisons. They do not rescue the old screen or inherit its validation. |
| A source-nominated target is presented as a screen discovery | Fzd2/Fzd4, PGAM, GRIA1, ARG1 | The repository can refine context, replication or a missing endpoint. It has not discovered these molecules or their published pathways. |
| A plausible story is written as if a rival had lost | Early A18 two-channel mechanism; A17 predicted loss of founder-model support; A20's subsequently corrected inversion | Hypotheses need neutral alternatives and an actual discriminator. The current restored scopes are more defensible. |
| Supporting work proliferates without a new decision | A1's 27 branches; A1/A8/A14's common missing outcome linkage; repeated proposal/context/figure layers | Real preparatory work exists, but another document or score does not reduce the same biological uncertainty. |
| A small numerical gain is promoted | A11's regularized prediction; A10's relative gain despite poor absolute transport | A11's duplicate-feature control and A10's plate failures materially limit the affirmative story. |

Assistant provenance is explicit in several original cards, including A15–A17;
the report does not infer authorship of every sentence. A generated explanation
can be correct, and a human-written one can overreach. The operational criterion
here is whether the statement follows from the actual units, endpoints and
comparison—not who wrote it.

The current dossiers usually state their limits candidly. The remaining risk is
**maturity inflation through presentation**: equal-sized folders and polished
schematics can make a proposal look equivalent to a measured result. A5's
component, A31's expression contrast, A17's mathematical diagnostic and A32's
unexecuted joint-effect proposal should not be presented as interchangeable
achievements. Also read the latest linked report: A11, A25 and A30 have important
later qualifications beyond their compact dossier evidence paragraphs.

## Implication for the next research decisions

1. **Preserve the strongest narrow claims.** A5 offers a fixed component for
   independent measurement replication; A31 offers a defined receptor/population
   premise. State exactly those claims before proposing a broader mechanism.
2. **Use context screens for the decisions they support.** A12 points toward
   epithelial validation, A21 weakens a simple cycling rationale, A25 requires
   regional/sex scope, A27 narrows broad lineage context, A28 separates score
   arms, and A30 defines a restricted supported population.
3. **Keep failed screens closed under their original definitions.** A0, A10,
   A11, A13, A16, A22 and A23 need their unfavorable or attenuated findings in
   the main interpretation. A new biological reason may justify another study;
   a replacement score alone does not.
4. **Treat missing discriminators as specific project decisions.** A1/A8/A14
   need the linked measurements their current questions require. A2/A9/A15 need
   effective-input/recipient evidence. A17 can still advance through a qualified
   model comparison. A20/A32 are focused biological proposals, but current data
   do not estimate their principal effects. None of this requires a universal
   extra experimental arm or makes all descriptive analysis invalid.
5. **Do not equate portfolio size with discovery.** Several A IDs use the same
   animals, patients, screen wells or source articles. Their distinct questions
   are legitimate, but cross-links and repeated assays are not independent
   replication. No question is retired or promoted by this assessment.

The most defensible overall product so far is a set of bounded observations,
well-documented failed generalizations and sharper discriminating questions.
Some analyses have narrowed the target; many others have narrowed what can
honestly be claimed. The next meaningful advance for the proposal-heavy group
will come from the missing comparison or measurement, not more elaborate prose.
