7 October 2026. All current dossiers' hypothesis, evidence and interpretation
boundaries were reviewed against the registry and current linked reports.
This is a consistency and design review, not a fresh systematic literature
review or a complete independent reproduction of every historical analysis.
Existing primary-source scans remain linked in each question guide. No RQ is
ranked, retired, promoted or accepted by this ledger.
