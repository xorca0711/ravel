## 2. A3 overcompresses an RNA observation into an axis-level restriction

The [README rationale](../../../RQ_Specified/A3_macrophage_injury_history/README.md#what-the-analysis-changed)
and its context/dossier say that the heterogeneous panel restricts the
“uniform-axis story.” The [owning result](../../../RQ_Specified/A3_macrophage_injury_history/reports/RNA_CONTEXT_DISPLAY_V1_RESULTS.md#what-narrowed-and-what-remains-unmeasured)
supports a narrower statement: the five regulator **transcripts are not all
uniformly maintained** in these six libraries. It does not measure regulator
activity, and the mechanistic axis has not been specified to require concordant
contemporary transcript abundance. The [DY1 question](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_DY1_damani_yokota_2026/rq_discordance/README.md#working-hypothesis-and-alternatives)
already allows different regulatory and transcriptional phases.

The surrounding caveats prevent an explicit claim that the mechanism was refuted,
but this wording can still make a descriptive result look like mechanistic
discrimination. That is one of the earlier flaws this pass was meant to remove.

**Required correction:** name the restricted claim precisely—uniform maintenance
of the measured regulator RNAs—and leave sequential regulation, protein/activity
and the NAM mechanism unresolved. Keep Egr2/Irf4 as source-specific transcript
leads, not selected maintenance regulators. Preserve the source report and frozen
outputs. Also make the separate BMDM reference and the original NAM/DY1 question
easy to distinguish in the current argument; the latest available display should
not stand in for the whole parent question.
