1. A12: independent transport of the fixed epithelial prediction is a reasoned
   next decision. Receptor causality and active ligand source remain separate.
   The 23-patient intake is metadata/source work, not a completed rerun or
   independent validation cohort. The unchanged registered text concerns a
   still-unspecified mechanistic experiment.
2. A21: maintenance, survival or traced lineage output may each support a useful
   narrower study. All three are not mandatory for every question. Linking
   integrity to later output is a stronger claim with extra inferential needs;
   the current expression discrepancy does not establish that route.
3. A28: joint protein measurement can test marker-level patterns; suppressive or
   effector competence needs an appropriate functional readout. No equivalence
   margin, sample size, independent functional dataset or laboratory access is
   invented. The frozen two-competence text remains a proposal.
4. A31: localization/partner context is a useful optional discriminator. A
   qualified acute GRIA1 comparison can be informative directly. The RNA lead
   does not make localization a mandatory prerequisite or insert CNIH3 into the
   registered functional hypothesis as an accepted mediator.
