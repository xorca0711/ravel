## Purpose and standard of success

Ravel is an independent, paper-driven computational biology portfolio feasible
without laboratory affiliation. Reading and reproducing papers leads to justified
extensions and defensible biological questions that may eventually inform lab
work. A strong contribution can be description, replication, prediction, a model
diagnostic or a negative finding. No mandatory remarkable gene, new causal
mechanism or numerical funnel to one or two validated targets is imposed.

The problem to repair is the visibility and coherence of the scientific argument.
Technical safeguards, analyses and completion banners have sometimes obscured
what a comparison actually changed. Available datasets/scores can displace the
question; adjacent findings from different models can look like one established
mechanism; a distant functional hold can obscure useful intermediate decisions.
Changing headings alone is insufficient. Additional analyses merely to improve
the prose would repeat the problem.

For each owner, distinguish:

1. Broad biological purpose and the narrower current question/estimand.
2. Published premises and this repository's actual contribution.
3. Relevant alternative explanations or the principal validity problem,
   appropriate to descriptive, predictive, replication or hypothesis-driven work.
4. The performed comparison, units/readout, result and what became weaker,
   narrower, mixed or unestimated.
5. Current interpretation and the next discriminating decision, with informative
   outcomes and exact missing inputs where relevant. A justified stop is allowed.
6. Execution completion and biological resolution as separate statements.

Use natural scientific prose and an appropriate small table or figure rather
than forcing every question into identical boxes. Cardoso's intercellular cascade
is an example of progressive reasoning, not a compulsory molecular format.
If an informative comparison is absent, show the incomplete argument honestly.
Do not manufacture historical predictions or present exposed follow-ups as
prospectively unexposed. Missing function does not invalidate useful description.

## Rationale and apparent narrowing review

The owner additionally requested an explicit assessment of whether each current
rationale is accurate and whether apparent narrowing could instead reflect noise
or unsupported inference. This is part of every owner review, not only copyediting.
Check the published premise against the actual repository readout/unit, feature
selection and prior outcome exposure, evidence reuse, composition and technical
sensitivity, and the most consequential alternative. Failure of one explanation
does not establish its replacement. A named gene or an annotation category is not
automatically a narrowed biological explanation.

Record whether the contribution is supported narrowing, an exploratory context
lead, a useful negative/diagnostic boundary, or an incomplete/unsupported inference,
with the specific evidence and interpretation consequence. These descriptions can
overlap and are neither acceptance grades nor retain/reject decisions. Preserve
the result while correcting an unwarranted rationale; uncertainty alone is not
proof of random noise. Review targeted contradictions that could change the
conclusion. A genuinely new test or material reframe follows existing contracts
and literature review rather than being manufactured to obtain a positive story.
