## Finding to decision

IL-22: the published OSMR premise and bounded repository IFN/barrier distinction
motivate an acute Osmr RNA comparison. Composition and attenuation remain rivals.
Its result changes the rationale for a later epithelial response study, not a
functional claim grade. Healthy acute colon and chronic colitis differ.

IL-10: source gene-response heterogeneity motivates reproduction of attenuation
and composition, with donor/processing limitations. This is source verification,
not discovery of thresholds or synthetic selectivity. New functional/model claims
need separate evidence specified in the plan.
