  [HRA009633 study page](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/HRA009633)
  succeeded after the web tool failed. The study is released (6 April 2026),
  but sequencing files are **controlled access**, under DAC HDAC001467.
  Public release of metadata does not authorize downloading the controlled files.
  The page lists bulk RNA, bulk ATAC and single-cell RNA, with CHIR and NRG1
  comparisons. Preparation/donor mapping and exact Fig. 7 values remain unrecovered.
- **Speth access is unchanged:** a fresh direct
  [GSE328925 request](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE328925)
  confirms private status and a scheduled 1 May 2027 release. This does not
  establish that the authors cannot share processed data before that date.
- **A public alternative is nominated:**
  [GSE234197](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE234197)
  describes whole-lung scRNA-seq in young/old mice at days 14, 28 and 60 after
  bleomycin or PBS. It reports three replicates per condition/time/age combined
  using cell hashing. GEO lists an HTO assignment workbook, RAW archive and
  Seurat object. Their contents and usable cell-to-mouse assignments have not
  yet been examined. This is a concrete candidate, not an executed or fully
  qualified replacement. No existing reference to this accession was found in
  the inspected research documentation/registry.
