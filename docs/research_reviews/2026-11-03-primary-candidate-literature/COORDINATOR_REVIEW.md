scope. Specifications and scripts were reviewed before execution; complete
tables, reports and both rendered PNGs were reviewed afterward. No numerical
rerun or change to frozen outputs was needed. The other four jobs have specific
source or measurement limits rather than invented results or redundant reruns.

  were checked; positive/negative/zero counts partition every region total.

  limits. Irf4/Pparg co-increase in this BMDM IL4 reference does not reproduce or
  refute the distinct alveolar IL13 mechanism. No NAM intervention, occupancy,
  catalytic activity, memory or mediation conclusion follows.
- **A12/Lin:** the cached primary text was checked at the ERBB4/ERBB2 comparisons
  and HRA009633 data statement. The later full-text reading supersedes the initial
  abstract-only access limit. ERBB2 participation matters to the receptor context;
  the source does not support an ERBB4-only narrative. Missing preparation-level
  values/map prevent the proposed reproduction, not the literature interpretation.
- **A32/Speth:** the cached primary GEO response explicitly reports private
  status and a 1 May 2027 release date. It does not supply an executable expression
  comparison. This is not a negative ARG1 result; pooling and the distinction
  between RNA and mature epithelial function remain relevant after release.
- **A31 and Wg-P06:** existing nomination/source descriptions were reused.
  The bounded search found no new input for their direct functional or linked
  exposure/collagen comparisons. More co-detection or pathway RNA would not fill
  those particular gaps.

Both figures have readable labels, complete named-feature coverage and visible
