## Purpose and design

Narrow biological targets and competing explanations for the six previously
recommended candidates. Use current results first, then assess whether each
new literature-informed comparison adds information. Do not repeat a metadata
qualification, atlas screen or source-value extraction already completed on
unchanged inputs. Required source checks support the scientific comparison;
they are not the outcome this work is intended to optimize.

Work is exposed and exploratory unless explicitly reproducing a fixed published
comparison. The literature and prior outcomes are already known. Source-defined
gene sets must be recovered from the actual paper/supplement and locked before
new outcomes, with exact locators and species/identifier mappings. Named genes
below nominate questions; they are not invented regulons or weighted scores.
Do not select genes on the new outcome, relabel states, or claim that a later
freeze removes prior exposure.

Each executable job needs a versioned specification and the repository research
contract/runner: question ownership, purpose, biological unit, comparator,
measured endpoint, estimand, assumptions, input hashes, code freeze and receipt.
Use original donor/animal/preparation identities; cells/regions are not replicates.
Unknown pairing or dependency stays unknown. No fabricated effect margin, power,
source mapping or empirical result. Restrict inference to identifiable contrasts.

## Execution sequence and informative outcomes

### PS1 — A5: which developmental features point toward which destination?

**Starting evidence:** a fixed Guo component is elevated in Strunz repair
transitional versus activated AT2 cells across 24 primary mice. External fixed
replication remains held on its authentic cell/library/mouse/state map.

**Literature applied:** Dong's HIF/KLF4/5 airway-diversion result and Liu's
adaptive AT2-to-bronchiolar repair show why developmental reuse is not a single
direction. Klinkhammer supplies a separate mesenchymal-niche explanation.

**New question:** can a separately declared, source-defined epithelial feature
comparison distinguish expression associated with alveolar identity from airway/
basal diversion in existing adequately mapped repair observations?

**Permitted analysis:** first check whether the original qualified Strunz inputs
and original labels contain the required endpoints. If they do, freeze an
exploratory gene-level/source-module comparison using mouse-level summaries,
existing states and sampling times. Keep the original Guo component and its
exclusions intact; never substitute a new score for the held replication. Report
coverage and full named-feature results, including discordant directions.
Within-state/time comparisons must match actual support; no pseudotime-to-fate
claim or forced batch correction. Prespecify any inferential family and adjustment.

**Decision:** coherent association with one destination's measured RNA features
may narrow the next biological hypothesis. Mixed directions leave reuse broad.
Neither outcome demonstrates lineage, future mature output or developmental
function. If airway/terminal states are absent, do not call transitional versus
AT2 expression a successful fate discriminator. Record that particular limit.

### PS2 — A12: receptor-dependent identity stability as a concrete reference

**Starting evidence:** the closed 12-patient pilot improves epithelial RNA
prediction with recipient context; it does not establish IL-1 specificity.
Independent fixed-procedure replication and its failed source candidates stay
unchanged.

**Literature applied:** Lin's NRG1/ERBB4 result (Fig. 6–7, corrected Fig. 3m
record), with the already-reviewed IL-22/OSMR paper as a separate tissue precedent.

**New question:** what directly measured epithelial response and regulatory
features support receptor-context-dependent identity stability in the Lin source?

**Permitted analysis:** finish the relevant primary figure/methods read, then
reproduce a bounded published RNA/ATAC or functional source-panel comparison
only if its source values, conditions and actual biological preparations can be
identified. Prefer deposited processed counts/source data to raw-read processing.
Preserve secretory and AT2 origins and actual NRG1/receptor comparisons. A CHIR
contrast alone is not an ERBB4-specific perturbation. Separate source reproduction
from any explicitly justified later transfer to A12; do not reuse the closed A12
pilot for a receptor search or silently replace IL-1 with ERBB4.

**Decision:** a reproduced source effect can nominate a concrete receptor/identity
comparison, but does not explain the old pilot or establish mature alveolar
recovery. Missing source values permit a literature conclusion, not a synthetic
reproduction. Article reproduction belongs in an ungated article-local package
only if actual work exists; A12 owns the bounded connection and interpretation.

### PS3 — A3/DY1-P02: PPARgamma identity maintenance versus IRF4-associated loss

**Starting evidence:** existing DY1 reference work already describes global
RNA/ATAC relationships and conditional cofactor-genotype effects. The original
NAM unit/assay/temporal holds remain. The highlighted published discrepancy is
bulk RNA versus separate bulk ATAC; scRNA contributes state/composition context.

**Literature applied:** Verwaerde supplies an IL-13–IRF4/PPARgamma identity-loss
precedent in alveolar macrophages, alongside the established Daniel/Deochand/Dacic
premises. It does not establish that this switch occurs in NAMs.

**New question:** does a justified, prespecified target-level view of the
non-infectious reference distinguish preserved identity from suppressed identity
or nonparallel RNA/accessibility, beyond the completed aggregate summaries?

**Permitted analysis:** inspect completed reference outputs first. If a distinct
comparison is informative, use the qualified non-infectious macrophage reference
or its immutable per-feature estimates. Source-based targets may include IRF4,
PPARG/RXRA, STAT6/EGR2 and a separately interpreted ARG1 endpoint, without calling
this a verified regulon. Freeze exact targets, contrasts, mapping and denominators;
report all target/region results rather than selecting significant peaks. Existing
unknown dependence means conditional descriptions must not become population
inference. No RNA/ATAC scale subtraction, occupancy claim, trajectory or mediation.

**Decision:** the profile can distinguish interpretations in that reference,
not select a NAM intervention or prove memory. If it only duplicates an existing
panel, reuse that result and explain why another run adds nothing. Do not reroute
previously restricted work through this chat, extend to pathogen challenge or
relax a scientific/safety hold to obtain numerical output.

### PS4 — A31: preserve the direct GRIA1 question

**Starting evidence:** adult alveolar-biased GRIA1 RNA and external donor support
already nominate the population. Membrane receptor, acute response and GRIA1
contribution remain unmeasured.

**Literature applied:** Wang's cardiac glutamate comparison is receptor-family
context, with GRIA3 among reported targets; it does not supply adult lung GRIA1
function.

**Permitted next analysis:** only a newly identified, relevant response dataset
or genuinely distinct source-supported receptor comparison can add to the
completed atlas screen. Biological units would be independent adult lung donors/
preparations with a defined glutamate-response endpoint and receptor context.
Do not run another co-detection panel as a substitute for the functional question.
If no such input is found in this bounded work, report the existing narrowing
and the exact functional measurements missing. Do not fabricate a wet-lab run.

### PS5 — A32: matrix and epithelial recovery need not move together

**Starting evidence:** no repository comparison measures the joint effect of
source-restricted ARG1 on matrix and mature epithelial output.

**Literature applied:** Sun provides an ARG1-positive airway-repair context, not
isolated enzyme causality; Pantuck supplies an epithelial GLS1/differentiation
counterpoint; Speth supplies a new non-infectious fibrosis-resolution context.

**New screen if supported:** in Speth's deposited resolution data, describe
source-defined myeloid ARG1/state RNA together with fibroblast matrix and
epithelial identity endpoints over the actual sampled stages. Use identified
animal/preparation summaries and published/source-defined features, retaining
composition effects and endpoint absence. Do not construct an optimized net-repair
score, pretend stages are repeated measures in the same mouse, or turn inferred
HGF/MET/TWEAK/cAMP interactions into measured activity. If source pooling prevents
biological inference, only an explicitly informative source-level description
may proceed; do not report cell-level significance.

**Decision:** a temporal dissociation can nominate a more precise window/state
for the hypothesis; concordance does not establish ARG1 causality. This is a new
context screen, not the held joint-effect test. The Sun/Pantuck sources cannot be
pooled as if they measured two outcomes of one ARG1 comparison. Reuse prior
Yadav source work and preserve its negative/held results.

### PS6 — Wg-P06: do not replace substrate balance with pathway RNA

**Starting evidence:** Hamanaka source reconstructions and Yadav follow-up
descriptions are complete; the source–recipient balance effect is not measured.

**Literature applied:** retain the distinction between source neutrophil ARG1,
extracellular arginine/ornithine and recipient fibroblast metabolism. None of the
new papers supplies this human source–recipient comparison.

**Permitted next analysis:** only a new source with linked exposure, source/
recipient units and collagen readout can test the balance. If absent, keep the
existing conclusion and specify the missing joint measurement. Do not repeat
completed panel extraction, fit flux from abundance, infer physiological exposure
from recipes, or presume a compensation crossover. This is separately owned by
Wg-P06, not relabeled as A32.

- Maintain a compact job ledger: question, exact comparison, status, receipt,
  output, decision implication and specific hold. Distinguish failed access,

  stop at writing a plan when the authorized comparison is executable. Do not
  expand a failed branch solely to obtain a positive result.
- Put actual article reproduction/basic questions under their article package;
  existing RQ extensions under the owning RQ. Use immutable run storage for
  numerical deposits. Keep coordination here; do not add per-paper gallery links
  to the root README or create a new canonical RQ.
- Before each adopted extension runs, link this literature rationale in its
  contract and synchronize owning README/context/dossier with its exact scope.
  Preserve canonical hypotheses and registered illustrations unless a separately
  justified revision is needed; do not weaken governance or validation.
- Review numerical outputs, interpretation and any figures. Run targeted tests
  and required repo checks; reuse unchanged earlier checks. Report completed
  scientific comparisons separately from technical completion and unresolved
  functional questions. Scientific acceptance remains human.
- The review chat owns final integration, push and PR. The technical chat may
  make narrowly scoped pre-execution freeze commits required by the runner;
  do not push, open a competing PR or switch another checkout's branch.
