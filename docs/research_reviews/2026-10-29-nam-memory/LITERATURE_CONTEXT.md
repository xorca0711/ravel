## What this question could add

The proposed contribution is a reproduction followed by a qualified decomposition
of population and regulatory associations, then a context comparison of ARG1
output. It is not novelty clearance for STAT6-PPARγ signaling, macrophage memory,
or ARG1-dependent fibrosis. The exact increment depends on qualifying units,
common states, independent target definitions and distinct functional endpoints.

## Current hypothesis and rival

Persistent PPARγ/RXRA-associated regulatory changes may accompany a different NAM
response after prior type 2 inflammation. Alternatives include state redistribution,
cell origin, ongoing niche input and technical differences. Arg1 may be a downstream
functional contributor while also tracking population composition; these explanations
can coexist. The complete comparison tuples, units and limits appear in the
[stepwise dossier](README.md#stepwise-analysis-and-decisions).

## What the comparison would teach

Within-state RNA effects persisting across eligible units would support a response
difference beyond measured mixture, without proving a chromatin mechanism.
Predominantly compositional effects would revise the interpretation, not erase
the functional NAM result. Coherent external TF targets would strengthen candidate
prioritization. Instability across annotation, normalization or source maps would
hold mechanistic claims. ARG1 associations diverging across resolution and fibrosis
would motivate context-specific output measurement, not a universal repair score.
