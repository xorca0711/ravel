## The framing to retain and develop

At article level, ask how epithelial-state abundance, pathological location and
local cellular organization relate across the sampled lesion contexts. The
stronger question of why those states persist or have different later outcomes
remains with the relevant existing owners and requires linked evidence.

Within this purpose, distinguish three potentially coexisting explanations:
different populations are present; similar populations are arranged differently;
or comparable epithelial states have different measured responses. These are
biological possibilities, not three established mechanisms. Cross-sectional
differences do not identify the direction of influence.

A12 retains its narrower question about recipient context beyond its specified
ligand/mixture comparison. A1/A8/A14 retain the separate state/output/withdrawal
questions. This review does not merge them into a single untestable “repair versus
cancer” claim.

| Completed observation | What it changes | What it does not decide |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier epithelial prediction improves while fibroblast prediction worsens | Supports an exploratory epithelial predictive branch; preserves an unfavorable compartment result. | A causal receptor configuration or absence of fibroblast effects. |
| IL1RAP detection rises while positive-nucleus intensity falls | Separates a change in detection frequency from uniformly higher intensity among detecting nuclei. | Whether altered state abundance, within-state regulation or measurement explains the distributions. |
| Joint detection attenuates with depth standardization; IL1R1 direction changes with candidate selection | Makes a simple, stable receptor-configuration reading of aggregate RNA less defensible. | Refutation of receptor function or proof that the underlying biology is noise. |
| Adjacent-spot association is heterogeneous and changes with broad RNA covariates | Establishes a descriptive tissue-organization pattern whose biological carriers need qualification. | Cell identity, contact, direction of signaling, or a decomposition of the old predictive gain. |

The nearest comparison should therefore be chosen for a named biological
decision: localize which states/regions carry the pattern, distinguish abundance
from arrangement where qualified measurements permit it, then address a
within-state response if that is the estimand. No mandatory assay ladder or new
score sweep follows. The currently held author-endpoint jobs still need their
actual inputs; identifying a conceptual branch does not make it executable.
