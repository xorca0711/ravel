# RQ folder diagnosis under the owner's biological screening definition

3 November 2026. Review of all 33 registered README organizing-question sections
and heading structures, with detailed current-entrypoint checks for A3, A5, A9,
A12, A31 and A32, plus the current Cardoso/A2 evidence. This is an information
architecture and scientific-framing assessment, not a fresh audit of every
historical calculation or a visual review of all 33 schematics.

## Main finding

The directory ownership is largely coherent: all 33 IDs have registered homes,
local literature context and an illustration, while shared and article-owned
evidence remains at its source. The main weakness is how research progress is
represented inside those homes. Execution completion, interpretation limits and
future functional requirements are easier to find than the sequence in which
observations narrowed a biological explanation.

The coordinator's recent queue contributed to this problem by treating better
annotation, conditional detection and sampling diagnostics as the default next
steps. They need a named biological decision to justify their place in screening.
The [prospective amendment](BIOLOGICAL_SCREENING_AMENDMENT_V1.md) corrects that
emphasis without discarding useful work.

Some repairs are scientific, not merely editorial. A broad hypothesis followed
by adjacent RNA analyses and a held functional test can omit the intermediate
candidate-discrimination step entirely. Rearranging those paragraphs would not
fix that omission. The current explanatory alternatives and the comparison
that can narrow them need to be developed explicitly, without pretending an
entire mechanism has been demonstrated or changing an old frozen analysis.

The existing cut-or-fill review usefully removed overclaims and preserved
negative outcomes. Its emphasis on what cannot be concluded should now be
paired with what each result *did* narrow. Many source/functional limits are
correct; they are not evidence that all these RQs are artificial or worthless.

## Distinctions to retain

Several questions already have sharper biological contrasts: A15 connects the
repository's Itgb6-associated response to the published latent-TGF-beta activation
route; A23 separates state entry from prolonged residence; A31 names a receptor,
population and response question; A28/A29 distinguish functional arms or metabolic
explanations. These are examples to develop, not grounds to rank or retire other
questions. Descriptive, predictive and mechanistic questions can coexist.

Related IDs also need an explicit map of shared and distinct links. For example,
A2/A9 concern source availability and recipient context, while A15 concerns a
separate activation route emerging from A2's evidence. A1/A5/A8/A14/A19 concern
different parts of epithelial competence and maturation. A3/A6/A32/Wg-P06 connect
history, composition, metabolism and recipient consequences. These are conceptual
connections, not evidence that the IDs are duplicates or that their findings can
be combined into one causal chain. Preserve their distinct estimands and owners.

## Proposed entrypoint, preserving the existing architecture

Keep the existing question-first and literature/visual order. Within that
structure, make the current biological progression explicit:

1. **Organizing Biological Q:** the phenomenon to explain; current narrow
   hypothesis or estimand; leading alternative. Distinguish the broad aim from
   the currently executable subquestion.
2. **Literature context and visual hypothesis:** identify published premises and
   proposed links. Place a short link-by-link evidence map next to the existing
   schematic, so the illustration does not imply every arrow was tested here.
3. **What screening changed:** starting observation, comparison performed, actual
   finding, candidate/context narrowed or weakened, and remaining alternative.
   Include mixed and negative results. Report completeness separately.
4. **Next biological decision:** the next discriminating comparison, why it follows,
   what each informative outcome changes, and what the available measurement can
   resolve. Separate an executable partial screen from the missing stronger test.
5. **Evidence and execution history:** compact links to reports, contracts,
   scripts, receipts and archived plans. One current synthesis should direct
   reading rather than multiple equal-status current/old summaries.

This requires rewriting the current scientific synthesis, not moving every
file or adding another folder hierarchy. No bulk reorganization, question-ID
change, canonical hypothesis rewrite or schematic replacement was performed in
this diagnosis. Full evidence and frozen outputs remain preserved. The technical
chat is preparing the 22-owner biological-link mapping to ground any later
owner-specific edits.
