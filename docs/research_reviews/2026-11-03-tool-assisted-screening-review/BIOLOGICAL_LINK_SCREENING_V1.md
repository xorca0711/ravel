## Active biological decisions

### A5: annotate to nominate a process, not manufacture a positive module

The larger question is which development-associated process is recruited during adult repair and whether it relates to productive differentiation rather than persistent transition. The fixed component is one measured subquestion within that aim. The immediate executable step is source annotation of the *same* 99-member component. Source-supported examples already include gene-expression regulation (Cecr2, Esrp1, Eya2, Rbpjl, Tfcp2l1, Zfp462), receptor/ligand context (Areg, Egfr, Erbb3, Fzd5, Shh), extracellular structure (Col4a3/4/5), and transport (Aqp5, Scnn1b/g, Slc26a9). These annotations are neither measured activity nor causal assignments. The full 99/57/53 memberships, negative regulatory subset result and overlapping Eya2 roles remain.

New metadata maps all 99 original symbols while preserving their names: 86 exact reviewed primary mappings, six explicit reviewed synonyms, two NCBI/GeneID-confirmed aliases, and five genes with multiple unreviewed protein entries. Reviewed UniProt status does not imply experimental support; GO qualifiers/evidence/references and tissue/species context must remain explicit. These are annotation results, not new effect estimates. The next numerical decision is whether full evidence-coded role attribution reveals a distributed process worth a bounded independent-source comparison, or supports a mixture of unrelated/uncertain members. All signs and unresolved roles must be shown. Root reviews the biological decision and fixed rules before any recalculation; no selected replacement programme is authorized.

## Compact diagnosis of the RQ folders

The existing architecture supports progression: the registry binds each question to its card, dossier, local literature context and illustrated README, while current result reports and amendments preserve the actual comparisons and their limits. No new folder hierarchy or bulk rewrite is needed. The [coordinator diagnosis](RQ_FOLDER_DIAGNOSIS_V1.md) inspected all 33 homes; this map covers the 22 owners in the authorized screening sequence.

The difficulty is synthesis inside the landing pages. A5's fixed-component replication obscures the broader process-and-consequence question; A12's epithelial narrowing is not foregrounded alongside the unfavorable fibroblast branch; A9's marginal receptor/feedback RNA leaves availability, prior stimulation and starting state insufficiently separated; A3's cross-model reports do not themselves bridge induction, maintenance and effector output. A31/A32 correctly name missing functional outcomes, but those distant endpoints can obscure what the partial screens already narrowed.

Two distinctions should be visible in each existing evidence/current-status section. RNA co-detection or an RNA response score nominates context; it does not establish receptor assembly, active signaling or repair. Likewise, a completed execution or valid receipt resolves a calculation, not the biological hypothesis: A31 retains opposing donor/assay contexts, A32 retains unestimated epithelial comparisons, and A12 retains an unfavorable fibroblast result. Adding another completion paragraph above the organizing question would worsen this problem.

The bounded remedy is one current progression in the existing structure: organizing phenomenon and candidate/alternative; published premises; what the actual comparison changed; the nearest informative next comparison; linked execution history. The active A31 update follows that placement. Broader owner-specific edits require the coordinator's review of this map; canonical hypotheses, historical results and illustrations remain preserved.
