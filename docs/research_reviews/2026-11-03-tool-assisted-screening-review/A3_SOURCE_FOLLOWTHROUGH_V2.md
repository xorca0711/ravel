## Biological decision and exact remaining uncertainty

The positive intake result is a real RNA count product with source-supported condition columns and an identified STAT6 context. It makes a bounded RNA-only candidate question more concrete than the initial accession/title uncertainty. A useful first contrast would describe source-labeled post-IL4 RNA relative to source control, with the initial IL4 condition shown separately. Persistent candidate RNA would nominate a context for further assessment; disappearance, reversal or heterogeneous members would weaken or narrow that nomination. Neither outcome identifies a lung NAM repair-memory axis.

Before a numerical contract, resolve whether control and pIL4 samples share harvest/culture duration and whether rep1/rep2 are distinct preparations or repeated libraries. The inspected header has no explicit matched48-hour continuous-IL4 condition, so pIL4-versus24-hour-IL4 cannot by itself isolate withdrawal from elapsed time. A direct continued-input-versus-cessation effect is therefore a stronger contrast than these labels currently justify. Unknown cross-assay pairing restricts joint RNA/ATAC inference, while a separately qualified RNA description remains possible. The current deliverable is source preparation; no new effectiveN, pairing or causal effect has been invented.
