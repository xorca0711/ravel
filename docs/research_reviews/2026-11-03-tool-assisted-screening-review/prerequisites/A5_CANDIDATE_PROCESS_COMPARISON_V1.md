## One biological branch point

**Does the fixed developmental component contain a distributed epithelial junction/basement-membrane remodeling pattern in Krt8-associated transitional cells relative to activated AT2 cells, with a different pattern in membrane transport features?** This is a candidate explanation of *what kind of RNA recruitment* the component measures. It is not a prediction that remodeling is beneficial or that mature AT1 output has been reached.

The alternatives may coexist: recruitment may mainly reflect changed receptor/ligand context; it may be a heterogeneous transition/identity mixture without a coherent architectural pattern; or architecture-associated RNA may mark persistent maladaptive remodeling rather than productive differentiation. The useful decision is whether to nominate epithelial architecture/localization for a subsequent independently qualified comparison, keep signaling context as a separate lead, or make **no process nomination**. Fourteen GO-root scores are not the objective.

## Executable descriptive contrast and informative outcomes

Use the original within-mouse transitional-minus-activated-AT2 comparison in the 24 eligible Strunz mice, with the same depth-adjusted expected detection endpoint, eligibility, source labels and sampling times. Reuse immutable member-level outputs if their lineage and denominators qualify. Display named-gene paired differences and the original module-denominated contributions without summing overlapping families. Preserve per-mouse/time heterogeneity, zeros and opposite signs. The 57/53 exclusions are sensitivities of fixed definitions; missing transport members there are not evidence of biological transport loss.

A distributed same-direction pattern among distinct junction and matrix candidates, alongside a different transport pattern, would nominate an architecture-associated RNA state for independent localization/context work. A pattern confined to receptor/ligand members would weaken that specific architectural explanation and motivate a separate recipient/input question. Sparse or opposing architecture members would yield no coherent architectural nomination; a positive aggregate cannot override them. Mixed families can coexist and need not be forced into a winner. No effect margin, significance cutoff or beneficial-repair decision is invented.

Existing cross-sectional RNA can distinguish these observed feature patterns; it cannot determine whether the same cells later mature, whether architecture changes precede signaling, or whether any of the features cause repair. Productive versus arrested remodeling requires independent state/time or functional evidence. This proposal therefore stops at a source-grounded nomination decision.
