# A5 complete-member annotation and decision rules — draft v2

3 November 2026. Source-only preparation under the owner's biological-screening amendment. This is not a numerical freeze, a new component or an accepted mechanism. The full original 99/57/53 memberships remain fixed. Earlier member outcomes were exposed; annotation extraction does not access contribution or expression files.

## Biological decision for review

Which development-associated process, if any, organizes recruitment of the existing component during adult repair, and therefore merits a specified regulator/effector or independent-source follow-up? The leading alternative is a mixture of state/identity, generic transition/stress and unrelated functional annotations. A positive aggregate alone does not discriminate these explanations. A process nomination would require interpretable contributions from multiple distinct members and retained opposing contributions; annotation multiplicity, a broad metabolic label or one highly contributing gene is not coherence evidence. No numerical coherence threshold or replacement programme has been invented.

The currently executable comparison remains source annotation of every fixed member. A subsequent contribution calculation is proposed only after the coordinator chooses the informative biological contrast and approves exact rules. Its biological unit remains the original mouse, with original eligibility/denominators; accessions, GO terms and repeated annotations are not independent replicates. The candidate process, functional consequence and independent follow-up eligibility remain to be reviewed. No contribution summary is produced here.

## Deterministic extraction and draft grouping

1. Join each QuickGO UniProtKB accession to its unique original gene using the retained identifier map. Preserve each distinct source annotation ID; do not count duplicated functions across isoforms as additional biological support.
2. Join each GO ID to the saved term/ancestor responses. The original ancestry cache plus a separate 72-term additions file covers the retrieved annotation set. Only `is_a` and `part_of` ancestry was requested; `regulates` and `has_part` were not traversed. Source API responses, request URLs, hashes and headers are retained; no explicit ontology release identifier was returned.
3. Preserve `NOT` qualifications and exclude them, ND records and obsolete terms from positive annotation assignments. Do not infer an inverse biological effect from NOT. Split direct `enables`/`involved_in` annotations from `acts_upstream_of*`, `contributes_to` and localization/complex context. Upstream-or-within association is not direct activity or direct participation.
4. Draft categories are membership in one of the 14 saved [GO roots](A5_GO_ROLE_ROOTS_V2.json), including descendants through the two allowed relations: gene-expression regulation, receptor activity, receptor-ligand activity, intracellular signal transduction, adhesion, junction organization, extracellular structural constituent, cytoskeleton organization, transport, lipid metabolism, small-molecule metabolism, protein metabolism, cell-cycle process and catalytic activity. Categories overlap and are provisional broad associations. They are not disjoint fractions, activated pathways or a frozen biological model.
5. Preserve evidence families separately: experimental codes; phylogenetic inference; sequence/computational inference; electronic annotation; author statements; curator inference; and ND. Experimental includes genetic and expression evidence and does not automatically mean direct molecular action. Keep exact codes and references rather than ranking genes by annotation count.
6. At the original-gene level, union eligible associations across mapped entries. Preserve experimental associations, activity/involvement associations and upstream-only associations separately. Nineteen members lack a positive association to these particular 14 roots; this means unclassified by the draft categories, not biologically unannotated or inactive. No contribution data were loaded.

The [manifest](A5_ANNOTATION_MANIFEST_V2.json) binds all six source/mapping inputs and both output tables by SHA-256. It records coverage and excluded-record counts. Mechanical checks confirmed 99 unique member rows, 4,344 unique source annotation IDs, complete accession/term joins, and exclusion of every NOT/ND record from positive assignment.

## Remaining review and interpretation limits

The coordinator must decide which process-level comparison could change the next biological nomination, whether these draft categories are fit for that comparison, and how overlapping/uncertain associations will be displayed without adding their contributions twice. Broad metabolism and generic signaling categories may require narrower source-grounded subquestions; no new categories should be chosen because their effects look favorable. Preserve the original negative regulatory-subset result and every member sign. A later exposed shortlist needs its own rationale/version, not a replacement of the original component.

Annotation can identify plausible regulators and effectors; it cannot show their activation, causal position, repair function or a transition-to-mature fate. Independent source transport and direct consequence evidence are separate next steps. UniProt and QuickGO provide public source annotations, not experimental validation by this repository. Primary guidance: [GO evidence codes](https://geneontology.org/docs/guide-go-evidence-codes/), [GO relations](https://geneontology.org/docs/ontology-relations/), and [QuickGO API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/QuickGO/api/index.html). Direct GO ontology download returned HTTP403; the retained QuickGO responses supplied the bounded term/ancestor information without an installation.
