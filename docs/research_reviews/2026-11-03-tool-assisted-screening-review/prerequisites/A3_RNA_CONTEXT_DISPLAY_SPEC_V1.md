## Biological question and decision

The prior fixed-target repeat-stimulation view was heterogeneous and did not measure maintenance. Does the independently deposited source show RNA context compatible with retained abundance after IL-4 exposure, compared with its source control and initial-IL4 libraries? The exact fixed regulators are Stat6, Egr2, Pparg, Rxra and Irf4; Arg1 remains a separate effector. Higher post-context values could nominate a narrower persistence comparison; return toward control, reversal or library disagreement would weaken or narrow that nomination. No expanded positive target set will follow from an unfavorable result.

The comparison is descriptive and cross-sectional. Unresolved untreated harvest alignment means a post-versus-control difference cannot isolate stimulation history from elapsed culture time; initial-versus-post likewise cannot isolate withdrawal. The primary units displayed are six measured libraries. The paper reports two biological replicates per condition, but individual preparation IDs, shared preparations and batch mapping remain unknown. Equal suffixes do not establish paired preparations. No population estimate, DE test, withdrawal effect, NAM/infection memory or chromatin/repair mechanism is targeted.

## Normalization and numerical outputs

For library j, S_j is the sum of **all 24,411 deposited gene values**, with no expression filtering or target-only denominator. For target g, reported-count CPM is 1,000,000 * y_gj / S_j. This normalizes the scale of this deposited matrix, not total sequenced reads or all cellular molecules. S_j must be finite and positive. Preserve noninteger input values if present in a future separately qualified source; never round for compatibility with a downstream package.

Output the 36 target/library rows in fixed gene then library order: exact gene/source row, GSM, source column, condition, library label, source-reported value, S_j, CPM, log2(CPM+1), presence status and role. An observed zero stays zero, including its transformed display value. A missing gene is not a zero; duplicate/absent targets or mismatched hashes/columns stop this exact run rather than silently dropping or remapping them. Output all 52 library totals unchanged for provenance, plus 18 target/condition rows with the arithmetic mean, minimum and maximum of the two library CPM values. A condition mean is a descriptive library mean; the two points and their range remain visible. No fold-change, effect threshold, p-value, confidence interval, pooled cross-source statistic or target ranking is added.

CPM is compositional and inherits transcript-length/isoform and quantification biases. Within-gene comparisons do not remove changes in effective length, global RNA composition or unknown batch. No TMM/DESeq size-factor correction or length correction is silently substituted. These restrictions are appropriate to this bounded descriptive display and do not claim an optimal population-level RNA model.
