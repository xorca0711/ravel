3 November 2026. Metadata recovery only. Question: which observations can separate induction, continued input, persistence after cessation and later effector association in a macrophage reference? This does not transport the entire STAT6–EGR2–PPARgamma/RXRA–ARG1 sequence into lung NAMs or establish repair memory. Biological units must be identified preparations/animals, not assay files or shared replicate labels. No new numerical analysis or contract is proposed as qualified.

## Disposition and exact next step

No new source is executable under a frozen comparison yet. The useful partial question is persistence after cessation versus continued-input association, with RNA and accessibility endpoints kept distinct. A compatible RNA-only source could nominate a persistence-sensitive candidate without ATAC; it could not establish chromatin maintenance. A contrary or absent persistent RNA pattern would weaken that candidate in its reference model, not disprove lung injury memory. Persistent accessibility without the relevant expression/response also leaves the effector bridge unresolved.

If this branch is reopened for execution, first obtain accessible GSE322520 sample/count metadata or reconcile GSE247943 sample identities and count columns with GSE247938 preparation/timing. Qualify independent units and whether a usable peak-count product exists before proposing a count-based cross-assay analysis. Raw-read reprocessing, installation and a wider search are not authorized by this intake. Missing identity/count products are the current hold, not evidence against the biological hypothesis.
