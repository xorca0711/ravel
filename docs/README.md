## What changed in this organization

The former source supplement is incorporated into `DATASETS.md` and archived.
Twenty existing documents now live under planning, reviews, source-inventory
history, method tools, background or workflows. Compatibility locators preserve
paths required by frozen evidence, contracts and generated reports. Existing
numerical evidence, current RQ dossiers and registered specifications stay in place.

[Consolidation and move audit](audits/2026-11-08-source-consolidation/README.md)
records coverage, destinations and link/preservation checks. The
[previous documentation index](history/reviews/DOCUMENTATION_INDEX_2026-11-08.md)
preserves the former detailed review list.

Downloaded full-text papers and publisher supplements remain outside Git.
Generated research-figure PDFs may be tracked with their provenance. The
[bibliography](../REFERENCES.md) and current article reports retain source citations.
