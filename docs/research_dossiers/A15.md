Does the epithelial input to fibroblast activation run through the integrin or through the ligand?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

In the relevant epithelial–fibroblast context, does epithelial integrin-dependent activation explain the response beyond total TGF ligand supply?

## Established knowledge

Integrin alpha-v-beta-6-mediated activation of latent TGF-beta is established biology, including Munger 1999. The contribution must concern context dependence, not rediscovery of activation.

## Repository evidence

The Itgb6 side branch is observational and technically confounded by split wells and position. The 28 September normalization correction supersedes the earlier double-depth-normalized result; the parent mechanism remains blocked.

## Unresolved gap

Active ligand, total ligand, epithelial integrin context and fibroblast response have not been measured in an identifiable comparison.

## Working hypothesis

Epithelial ITGB6-containing integrin activity may change fibroblast response by activating latent TGF-beta, rather than by changing total ligand supply. That pathway is already established. The repository-specific idea is whether the epithelial Itgb6-associated fibroblast response in the A2 context is explained by this activation route; neither active TGF-beta nor a decisive fibroblast functional endpoint was measured there.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Context-specific endpoint required. Integrin-mediated latent TGF-beta activation and profibrotic epithelial-fibroblast signaling are published. The source-specific attribution and meaningful recipient endpoint still need definition; this is not a new pathway claim. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a15). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Differences reflect total ligand, another activator, matrix context, recipient composition or technical depth/preparation effects.

## Discriminating outcomes

A source-attributable change in active TGF-beta and the nominated recipient response at comparable latent supply would favor activation. Supply-only change or a response independent of the activation contrast favors rivals. Itgb6 RNA, total TGF-beta and a fibroblast RNA score cannot substitute for active ligand and function.

## Next investigation

Use the corrected evidence and qualify an assay separating active from total ligand. Do not promote the Itgb6 side branch to a direct activation test.

## Experimental bridge

Compare activation and supply contributions with independently measured ligand activity and fibroblast function in a suitable matrix context. Separate common preparations from technical replicate wells.

## Interpretation boundary

Itgb6 RNA, ligand RNA and a TGF response score do not individually identify the activation mechanism.

## Decision and maturity

Context-specific mechanism proposal; direct measurement is missing and established literature limits the novelty claim.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
