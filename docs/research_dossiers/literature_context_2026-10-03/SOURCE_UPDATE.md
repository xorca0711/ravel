## Additional close precedents from the wider topic search

**A25 — [Shea and Villeda, eLife 97671](https://elifesciences.org/articles/97671)**
([primary index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40298588/),
DOI [10.7554/eLife.97671](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.97671)).
The publication reports hippocampal intermediate ageing states and functional
investigations; the prior wider pass accessed publisher-indexed Results and
PubMed. Its reported scRNA age samples pool animals, which must not be treated
as independent animal libraries. Reopening the publisher page in this application
returned a browser challenge; full primary figures/supplements were not newly
inspected. Version/date reconciliation and exact external-unit qualification
remain part of the next full source audit.

This is closer than a generic lifespan atlas. The useful prospective A25 question
is whether intermediate RNA organization needs an added component beyond flexible
mixture/continuous rivals and transports across qualified animals/contexts.
The exact proposed comparison is not declared resolved or unprecedented.
[Li 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-023-00479-x), sex-dependent ageing,
remains a separate close precedent; reuse of either source is not new replication.

**A26 — [Mark et al. 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2022.939394/full)**,
DOI [10.3389/fimmu.2022.939394](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2022.939394).
Publisher full text was accessible again. The uninfected comparison includes
CD4 and CD8 differentiation subsets, spleen and marrow, and young/adult mice.
Its repertoire-structure comparisons are therefore closer than the previously
emphasized CD4-only precedent. The earlier read recorded 3/12-month ages; A26's
proposed 3/24-month common-state concentration contrast differs, but a different
age window or estimator alone does not establish scientific value. The precise
paired-unit/endpoint comparison remains to be qualified. Infection experiments
are outside this question's current scope.
