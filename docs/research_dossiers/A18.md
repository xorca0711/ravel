Are WT expansion and loss of AT2 identity regulated differently near mutant clones?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Do neighboring wild-type lineage expansion and loss of alveolar identity represent separable responses to a mutant epithelial neighborhood?

## Established knowledge

Growth and identity change are distinct outcomes. Spatial proximity and ligand-receptor nomination do not identify a causal signal.

## Repository evidence

The exposed pooled-pair analysis measures WT-neighbor size and the pro-Sftpc-negative fraction, an identity-loss proxy. Size-gradient separation from homeostatic references holds at one and two weeks under both original and dense-bin estimators; four-week separation depends on the estimator. Identity-loss distance slopes have inconsistent signs, which does not demonstrate distance independence. Log-size/logit-fraction sensitivity and declining distal occupancy remain relevant, but the two transformed slopes are not biologically interchangeable. Spatial rows lack verified mouse/clone/pair linkage and may repeat neighbors; separate mouse-indexed arrays cannot be joined to these rows by assumption.

## Unresolved gap

Qualified spatial units and linked growth/identity measurements remain missing for the joint biological question. Pooled neighborhoods are not independent animals, and a marker fraction is not a directly traced fate change.

## Working hypothesis

The local mutant epithelial context may alter absolute expansion of neighboring WT AT2 lineages separately from loss of WT alveolar identity. The immediate biological question is whether those outcomes dissociate after survival and starting state are measured. SPP1 and DLK1 remain possible source candidates; no two molecular signaling channels have been established.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Conditional outcome dissociation; molecular routes unassigned. Altered epithelial niches are published. A mapped WT lineage/context comparison could establish a useful dissociation, but dissociation alone is not proof of separate pathways. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a18). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Survival, division, starting composition, repeated neighbors and spatial sampling can alter size or identity fraction. Distance patterns alone do not identify signaling range or separate molecular pathways.

## Discriminating outcomes

Dissociation of growth and identity within qualified linked units would support studying separate response explanations. Concordant changes or a pattern explained by repeated sampling would restrict that interpretation. Neither pattern alone identifies a molecular channel or signaling range.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Retain the descriptive distinction without assigning channels. A unit-qualified joint comparison could show whether growth and identity changes dissociate; concordance or dissociation would then constrain which explanation merits study. A molecular candidate needs its own published or independently measured rationale; the shape of these pooled spatial plots alone does not select one.

## Experimental bridge

A compartment-resolved comparison would measure WT expansion, survival and AT2 identity independently while identifying source clones and biological units.

## Interpretation boundary

A useful separation of descriptive readouts and correction of an earlier mechanistic story. Different size and identity patterns do not establish two independent channels, identify a mediator or estimate an animal-level distance interaction.
