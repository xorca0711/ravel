# A20: research development dossier

Does Fzd2 sustain AF1 fibroblast support of an AT2 pool capable of mature alveolar repair more strongly than Fzd1?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does Fzd2-dependent AF1 support improve the yield of mature epithelial descendants beyond maintaining a larger early AT2 pool?

## Established knowledge

Fibroblast support can act through survival, matrix, early expansion or maturation. A receptor comparison alone does not identify the support mechanism.

## Repository evidence

The refined Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 workspace distinguishes AF1 support from a generic fibroblast effect. Existing source and extension analyses do not identify the exact mediator or establish mature repair capacity.

## Unresolved gap

Support of early pool size has not been separated from effects on subsequent maturation per surviving starting cell.

## Working hypothesis

Fzd2-dependent AF1 context changes productive mature descendant yield beyond its effect on early epithelial survival and expansion.

## Competing explanations

A larger early pool, altered matrix or differential survival accounts for all apparent mature-output differences.

## Discriminating outcomes

An effect on mature yield after accounting for the supported starting pool favors a later contribution; proportional output explained by pool size favors early support alone.

## Next investigation

Qualify source specificity and denominators for early pool and later mature output. Nominate a mediator only when direct evidence supports it.

## Experimental bridge

Compare fibroblast contexts with separate epithelial survival, early pool, mature yield and matrix measurements across independent preparations.

## Interpretation boundary

Fibroblast receptor RNA and epithelial marker scores cannot establish a specific trophic mediator or repair function.

## Decision and maturity

Keep the narrowed support question. Mechanistic mediation and mature-outcome evidence remain to be developed.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
