## Biological problem

Does Fzd2 sustain AF1 fibroblast support of an AT2 pool capable of mature alveolar repair more strongly than Fzd1?

## Established knowledge

Fibroblast support can act through survival, matrix, early expansion or maturation. A receptor comparison alone does not identify the support mechanism.

## Repository evidence

The owner-requested narrowing specifies the Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 contrast in an AF1-like context, absolute mature epithelial output and a pool-maintenance mechanism. Existing RNA results do not establish receptor dependence or identify a mediator. The narrowed hypothesis takes precedence over the first dossier's inverted mechanism.

## Unresolved gap

There is no qualified matched functional Fzd1/Fzd2 comparison establishing receptor-selective support of an early AT2 pool and its later mature descendants.

## Working hypothesis

In adult AF1-like fibroblasts, comparable Fzd2 loss reduces support of a maturation-competent AT2 pool and subsequent absolute mature epithelial output more than comparable Fzd1 loss. Pool maintenance is the proposed explanation, distinct from a later maturation-specific defect, general fibroblast loss or matrix effects. This restores the existing narrowed hypothesis and does not claim discovery of Fzd2-dependent repair.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Restored receptor contrast; novelty provisional. Zhou already reports mesenchymal Fzd2, AF1 identity and repair involvement. The possible increment is the matched Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 comparison and early-pool versus later-output distinction; its independent value remains provisional. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a20). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

A maturation-specific support defect occurs despite a preserved early pool; fibroblast depletion or matrix changes alter support; perturbation validity differs between receptors.

## Discriminating outcomes

A smaller early competent pool followed by reduced mature output is compatible with pool maintenance. Preserved early pool with reduced later output favors maturation-specific support. Unequal receptor engagement or fibroblast survival makes the receptor comparison ambiguous. Do not adjust away the post-treatment pool deficit or call timing alone mediation.

## Next investigation

Apply the existing functional eligibility gate: matched receptor contrasts, verified engagement, source/recipient identity, independent units, source viability and linked mature output. Hold new fits if eligibility fails; do not repeat atlas correlations as a substitute.

## Experimental bridge

The conceptual comparison requires valid receptor contrasts in the same AF1-like setting and separate trajectories of source viability, epithelial survival/expansion and lineage-linked mature function. The early pool is a possible mediator; do not condition it away in the primary total-effect contrast.

## Interpretation boundary

Receptor or support-ligand RNA rank is not functional dependence. Dividing by post-treatment survivors or adjusting for the early pool does not by itself identify a maturation mechanism.
