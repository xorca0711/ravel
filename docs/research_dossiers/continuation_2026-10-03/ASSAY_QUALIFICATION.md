conditional designs, not operational bench protocols or confirmed local assays.

For every chosen assay, record the model/species/context, valid signal range,
specificity, reproducibility, interference and viability behavior using local
evidence. A null is interpretable only if engagement and measurement are valid.
Where a package has not nominated a particular consequence, record the candidate
evidence and the decision needed; do not fill the blank with an attractive gene.

The resource record must distinguish an available line/model, permission and
material access, an assay that works locally, independent biological supply,
and supervision/time. Organoids represent their actual compartments; cell lines
can qualify a reduced response; slices/images/wells remain nested within donors.
Neither model simplicity nor publication use establishes fitness for a lineage,
reserve, vascular-function or tissue-repair endpoint.

Meaningful effect must be set on the selected endpoint's biological scale.
Between-animal/donor variation, repeated preparations, pairing, attrition and
missingness then determine precision. No default sample count or universal
effect threshold is justified by these packages. A feasibility or assay pilot
would need its own explicit decision, outcome, biological units and contract;
it cannot be retrospectively labelled confirmatory.
