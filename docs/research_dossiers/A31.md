# A31: research development dossier

Current synthesis: 4 November 2026. Existing evidence and registered hypotheses
are preserved. This is an argument-development record, not human scientific
acceptance, a claim-grade change or a newly authorized experiment.

## Biological problem

Does GRIA1 contribute to an acute glutamate response in independently identified
adult alveolar fibroblasts? Published expression and the local atlas contrasts
motivate testing this question without establishing functional competence. The
completed screens have narrowed the premise: GRIA1 marginal
enrichment persists, but its partner context is heterogeneous. **CNIH3 is a
conditional RNA lead**, with opposing donor/assay contexts, not a universal
receptor configuration.

The question can advance through a useful localization/context comparison or a
suitably qualified acute functional comparison. A separate localization study is
not an obligatory first step. Neither function nor its absence has been
demonstrated by the current RNA evidence.

## Established knowledge

Published lung atlases already describe GRIA1-enriched fibroblasts. Neural
SCN7A and related nerve/fibroblast or cardiac glutamate-response findings limit
specificity and novelty claims without demonstrating the proposed adult-lung
response. [Exact primary locators and access limits](../../RQ_Specified/A31_gria1_fibroblast_response/LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md#published-starting-point).
These are separate biological contexts, not one established causal route.

## Repository evidence

The [Nb4-P09 expression result](../../docs/portfolio_extensions/2026-10-07/g2_niche/RESULTS.md)
finds separate GRIA1 and SCN7A enrichment in eligible matched alveolar-versus-
adventitial fibroblast donor/region/assay contrasts. Source SS2/10x assays repeat
donors, and external cell/nucleus donor sets overlap. SCN7A is context, not a
required double-positive gate or a neuronal identity claim.

The [first partner screen](../../RQ_Specified/A31_gria1_fibroblast_response/reports/PARTNER_CONTEXT_V1_RESULTS.md)
preserves GRIA1 enrichment but finds heterogeneous partners: GRIA3 usually favors
adventitial fibroblasts, CNIH3 varies, and several targets are sparse or
assay-specific. Ten donor/assay contexts are not ten independent people.
This challenged the inference from one enriched receptor transcript to a
coordinated partner configuration.

The [newest shared-depth analysis](../../RQ_Specified/A31_gria1_fibroblast_response/reports/DEPTH_CONDITIONED_PARTNER_V1_RESULTS.md)
then asks whether joint detection exceeds what the marginal detection rates
predict within shared library-depth bins. It compares
observed-minus-marginal-expected joint detection between alveolar and adventitial
labels, using identical overlap weights. Fourteen primary strata qualify:
**112 estimated and 120 held partner-stratum rows**, summarized in **nine
donor/assay contexts** that still reuse people. The estimate describes selected
common-depth overlap cells, not every fibroblast.

| Conditional partner finding | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| CNIH3: 4 positive, 2 negative and 3 exact-zero contexts; range -0.322493 to +2.631652 pp | An exploratory source-dependent lead. P3 has opposite directions in SS2 and 10x. |
| Other seven partners: sparse, opposing, zero or single-nucleus-donor patterns | No uniformly coordinated partner configuration; all results remain in the display. |
| Exact-zero residuals | Arithmetic zeros can follow from absent marginal detection. They establish neither biological independence nor absent receptor machinery. |

Shared state, annotation, ambient RNA and residual depth within bins remain
alternatives. Conditioning the same sources is not independent replication of
GRIA1 enrichment, and assay-specific read/UMI protocols remain distinct.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

**Rationale verdict: exploratory context lead and a useful diagnostic boundary;
a stable complex is unsupported.** Published and local GRIA1 enrichment motivate
the acute-response question. The newer endpoint is conditional joint RNA
detection in a selected common-depth population. CNIH3's four positive, two
negative and three zero donor/assay contexts, including an opposing P3 assay
contrast, warrant a conditional lead rather than a broadly conserved partner
claim. Nine contexts reuse people; they are not independent replication.

The [conditional specification](../../docs/research_reviews/2026-11-03-tool-assisted-screening-review/prerequisites/A31_CONDITIONING_SPEC_V1.md)
was prospective for its new endpoint on an already exposed source. All eight
fixed partners remain reported; highlighting CNIH3 after seeing these results
does not validate its selection. Depth restriction addresses one technical
explanation while changing the target population; state/annotation, ambient RNA
and remaining depth or assay effects persist. Weakening a uniform partner
account does not support a special CNIH3 mechanism or functional absence. A
nonoverlapping context, protein localization or a qualified direct functional
comparison could each be informative. The existing evidence cannot decide
whether the opposing contexts reflect biological heterogeneity or technical/
sampling variation.

## Unresolved gap

The conditional RNA lead has not been connected to same-cell protein
localization, a direct acute response or GRIA1 dependence in adult alveolar
fibroblasts. These are unmeasured links, not demonstrated absence. A qualified
functional comparison can itself be informative without a separate localization
study; its endpoint and attribution must still be credible.

## Working hypothesis

Independently identified adult human alveolar fibroblasts exhibit an acute glutamate-evoked response with a GRIA1-dependent component: qualified GRIA1 perturbation reduces the challenge-minus-vehicle response relative to matched control preparations under the same exposure.

## Competing explanations

Annotation/state selection, ambient RNA, neural admixture and shared depth can
produce RNA association; common-bin restriction addresses part of depth while
selecting the overlap population. A measured glutamate response could arise by
other receptors or transport/metabolic routes. Perturbation-associated changes
could reflect identity or viability. CNIH3's opposing assay/donor directions
restrict generality, and exact-zero residuals do not establish biological
independence. SCN7A is neither a gate nor a required mediator.

## Discriminating outcomes

Compatible independent-donor RNA evidence would support transport of the
conditional context; discordance would narrow it. Localization would strengthen
the cellular interpretation but not prove activity. A qualified acute
challenge-minus-vehicle response reduced by GRIA1 perturbation would support a
contribution in that model. A calibrated nonresponse constrains that preparation;
an imprecise perturbation effect or identity/viability change remains ambiguous.
Neither a null result nor RNA non-detection establishes universal absence.

## Next investigation

A compatible, nonoverlapping donor source could test whether the RNA context
transports. Same-cell protein localization could test whether the nominated
transcripts correspond to relevant protein context. Either could narrow the
interpretation, but neither must precede a directly informative acute functional
comparison if that comparison has adequate identity, assay and perturbation
qualification.

For the registered functional question, a measured challenge-minus-vehicle
response that changes under qualified GRIA1 perturbation would support a
contribution in that preparation. A response without a resolved perturbation
effect leaves GRIA1 dependence unsupported at the achieved precision. A
calibrated nonresponse would constrain that context; an identity/viability change
would make attribution ambiguous. None proves universal functional absence.
The current RNA screens supply no acute response, target-engagement or repair
measurement and authorize no new source or intervention.

## Experimental bridge

A donor-matched control/GRIA1-perturbation comparison under the same qualified
acute exposure can address the registered effect. Donor is the biological unit;
preparations, wells and cells are nested. Challenge-minus-vehicle defines the
evoked endpoint, and identity/viability require explicit interpretation rather
than automatic post-treatment adjustment or survivor gates. A localization study
is an optional useful discriminator, not an obligatory prior experiment. The
acute assay, timing, meaningful effect, precision and source access remain
unqualified; no dose, model or replicate count is selected here.

## Interpretation boundary

The existing findings are exposed RNA expression/context analyses, not
receptor assembly, target engagement or function. A future qualified effect
would apply to its preparation and endpoint; it would not establish neuronal
excitability, SCN7A sodium sensing, a lung-wide sensory system or epithelial
repair. Repeated assays and donors cannot be counted as independent people.

## Decision and maturity

The newest conditional screen is complete and narrows the interpretation to
heterogeneous partner context with a CNIH3 lead that retains contrary results.
The acute functional hypothesis is still proposed and unexecuted. A useful
intermediate context result and an unmeasured functional question can coexist;
neither human acceptance nor a claim-grade change is assigned.
