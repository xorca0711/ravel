## Biological problem

Within a specified brain region and sex, do middle-age microglia exhibit reproducible RNA organization that requires an additional configuration beyond redistribution of young/old populations or a continuous age response?

## Established knowledge

Li et al. 2023 (doi:10.1038/s43587-023-00479-x) already report sex-dependent microglial ageing and a female middle-age pattern. Hammond et al. 2019 establish lifespan-related microglial diversity. The organizing question is model discrimination and transport in defined contexts, not discovery of generic ageing states.

## Repository evidence

Nb5 recovered count-valued official expression, four region labels and exact identities for 13,130 source-labelled microglia. The source has 6/4/4 mice at 3/18/24 months; all 24-month mice are male. No state-versus-mixture model has run. Source clusters and trajectories are outcome-exposed observations, not longitudinal transitions.

## Unresolved gap

The actual configuration, feature/model specification and supported region/sex population must be fixed. External datasets differ in age schedules, sex and regional sampling; independently mapped animal units and compatible endpoints remain unqualified.

## Working hypothesis

A region- and sex-qualified middle-age microglial distribution contains coherent structure that generalizes across animals and is inadequately represented by endpoint mixtures or a smooth age-response baseline. This is an RNA-organization hypothesis; a discrete cell state or functional role requires additional evidence.

## Competing explanations

Changing proportions of existing microglial populations, continuous or nonlinear regulation, regional sampling, processing and sex imbalance explain the apparent intermediate configuration. A mean-profile residual can arise under these alternatives without a new state.

## Discriminating outcomes

Compare animal-held-out predictive adequacy of prespecified endpoint-mixture, continuous and additional-component models with complexity control. Support for an added component must accompany reproducible cell-level coherence. Equivalent or unstable prediction leaves the models unresolved; training fit and embedding separation cannot decide.

## Next investigation

Turn the existing Nb5 microglial design brief into a complete exploratory contract, with population/sex scope, feature set, observation scale, baseline flexibility and animal-level splits fixed before execution. Source availability is resolved; model choices and identifiability are unfinished. Qualify an external study separately.

## Experimental bridge

Independent region- and sex-resolved biological material would test a frozen transferable configuration. Direct state/function or longitudinal linkage would be a later question. Internal animal holdout is useful exposed exploration, not independent-study confirmation or a disease-mechanism experiment.

## Interpretation boundary

No RNA configuration implies a cell transition, reversibility, beneficial state, Alzheimer mechanism or treatment target. A25 preserves the owner's disease-related motivation without turning it into a measured result. Old female absence cannot be repaired by regression.
