## Biological problem

Does a source-restricted change in myeloid ARG1 have different consequences for
matrix production and mature epithelial recovery in a qualified lung repair
context? Reduced fibrosis-related measurements do not by themselves establish
regeneration. A32 asks for those two effects in the same interpretable design,
with fibroblast involvement assessed rather than assumed.

## Established knowledge

[Yadav 2025](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188734), especially Fig. 3, supplies the
myeloid ARG1/ornithine–collagen premise. [Hamanaka 2025](https://doi.org/10.1042/BCJ20253033),
Figs. 3–6, makes nutrient context and recipient signaling relevant alternatives.
[Li 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2026.102979), Fig. 6H–M, provides a
different perturbation's organoid and differentiation evidence, not an ARG1
effect or a qualified absolute mature-function outcome. Exact reading coverage,
contrary findings and source versions are in the context note.

## Unresolved gap

The missing information is the paired biological-unit effect on newly produced
or deposited matrix and absolute lineage-derived mature epithelial output under
qualified myeloid-source ARG1 restriction. Neither an unlinked atlas nor a
matrix/RNA score supplies that joint effect. Source identity, intervention
specificity, mature endpoint validity, model access and precision are unresolved.

## Working hypothesis

In a qualified non-infectious lung-repair system, estimate how source-restricted myeloid ARG1 reduction versus matched source-active control changes absolute lineage-derived mature AT1 output per starting epithelial input and newly produced or deposited collagen, reported separately in the same biological design. No direction of the epithelial effect is prespecified from collagen alone. The principal validity threat is that source-cell loss, changed inflammation, direct recipient exposure or epithelial/fibroblast selection is mistaken for restored niche support or maturation.

## Competing explanations

Matrix reduction and epithelial benefit may coincide, diverge or remain
imprecise. Effects may arise through altered myeloid abundance, recipient
nutrient availability, inflammation or viability rather than a fibroblast-mediated
repair route. Early epithelial survival/expansion may change absolute later
output without changing maturation probability. These explanations can coexist;
the primary effect comparison does not claim to identify every mediator.

## Discriminating outcomes

Lower collagen with higher mature output supports a favorable joint response
in the tested system, without proving fibroblast mediation. Lower collagen with
lower mature output identifies a possible tradeoff. An imprecise or unresolved
epithelial effect does not imply preserved function. RNA, organoid size or
cell fractions alone cannot decide the primary mature-output question. Changes
dominated by source/recipient loss retain an attribution limit.

## Experimental bridge

The fit-for-purpose comparison is a qualified source-restricted ARG1 change
against matched source-active control, retaining the linked matrix and epithelial
outcomes. Actual source, fibroblast and epithelial donors/animals and their
crossing/nesting define biological units; wells, organoids and images are nested.
Track source/recipient identity, target engagement, viable amount and inflammatory
context. Distinguish early survival/expansion from later lineage-derived output.
Direct-exposure controls or additional compartment tests are needed only for
the stronger attribution being claimed; rescue and factorial designs are not
automatic prerequisites for estimating a bounded intervention effect.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a perturbation-effect and joint-outcome question, not a claim that ARG1
is universally beneficial or harmful. The source's macrophage/neutrophil
differences prevent automatic species transfer. Collagen is not lung function;
AT1 markers are not a calibrated mature-function assay. No drug, dose, schedule,
effect margin, sample size, cell source or laboratory availability is invented.
No infection experiment or pathogen outcome is part of the proposed comparison.
