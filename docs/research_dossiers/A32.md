## Biological problem

Does reducing source-restricted myeloid ARG1 change matrix production and mature epithelial recovery in the same repair context? Less collagen does not determine the direction of the epithelial effect. The completed public RNA screen supplies early source/context observations, while the registered joint functional comparison remains unmeasured.

## Established knowledge

Yadav's ARG1-related fibrosis result supplies a matrix premise; Hamanaka's fibroblast arginine work supplies a recipient-context alternative. Li's Atg5 study measures distinct organoid and differentiation endpoints under another perturbation, and the older myeloid Arg1-loss study cautions against universal effects. The [dated primary-source review](../../RQ_Specified/A32_myeloid_arg1_matrix_epithelial_recovery/LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md#published-starting-point) keeps those findings separate. Truchi's public resolution atlas motivates the completed RNA context screen; it is not an ARG1 intervention or independent replication of its own publication.

Days are cross-sectional. Source-defined mouse/state log2(CPM+1) RNA is compared as equal-mouse mean BLM minus PBS; cells and repeated genes do not add biological units.

These are RNA observations, not ARG1 protein activity, deposited collagen or epithelial output.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

**Rationale verdict: an exploratory source-population lead and a useful support boundary; the joint functional inference is incomplete.** The completion count is not biological narrowing. AM2/AM3 are candidates for observed Arg1-bearing populations under early injury, not validated source-specific ARG1 induction or a source–matrix circuit. Early AM1/fibroblast co-occurrence cannot assign direction or mediation. Sparse late counts cannot establish persistence.

Published narratives and prior outcomes were exposed; the panel and support rule preceded new expression estimates. Highlighting a source label after inspecting the fixed panel does not establish target priority. Shared injury severity, capture, relative recovery, within-label composition and background RNA remain alternatives. Raw age/day differences are capture-confounded, and the same deposited mice do not become independent validation. Nothing here selects a favorable epithelial response or demonstrates random noise.

## Unresolved gap

Linked source ARG1 exposure, matrix production/deposition and mature epithelial output are missing. The public dataset cannot estimate the intended epithelial comparison and supplies no ARG1 perturbation. AM2/AM3 labels are not automatically another paper's myeloid subtype.

The registered text below remains unchanged as a proposal; current findings do not
make its predicted mechanism or effect an accepted explanation.

## Working hypothesis

In a qualified non-infectious lung-repair system, estimate how source-restricted myeloid ARG1 reduction versus matched source-active control changes absolute lineage-derived mature AT1 output per starting epithelial input and newly produced or deposited collagen, reported separately in the same biological design. No direction of the epithelial effect is prespecified from collagen alone. The principal validity threat is that source-cell loss, changed inflammation, direct recipient exposure or epithelial/fibroblast selection is mistaken for restored niche support or maturation.

## Competing explanations

Source/recipient loss, shared injury severity, inflammation, direct recipient exposure, capture and cell selection can mimic coupled changes. A large pooled macrophage signal can reflect state abundance rather than within-state induction.

## Discriminating outcomes

A qualified source-restricted ARG1 contrast could yield less matrix with higher mature output, or less matrix with poorer output; both are informative joint effects. An imprecise epithelial result leaves uncertainty, not preserved function. Estimating those effects does not automatically identify fibroblast mediation. A narrower source/context description can also be useful without claiming the joint function.

## Next investigation

Retain the completed RNA context result and the separate access hold. Reopening the registered comparison requires a source with linked biological units, credible source restriction and suitable matrix/mature-output measurements. Further expression summaries in the same sparsely epithelial atlas cannot supply that missing joint endpoint. A new source or intervention needs its own specific rationale; no old plan restarts automatically.

## Experimental bridge

Actual source, fibroblast and epithelial donors/animals and their crossing define units; wells and cells are nested. The registered question compares two separate outcomes in one qualified design. Additional compartment, rescue or factorial tests are justified by a stronger attribution claim, not mandatory for every bounded effect estimate.

## Interpretation boundary

RNA abundance is not ARG1 activity, collagen deposition, lineage-derived mature function or repair. The epithelial direction remains unspecified. Source restrictions, species/context and actual assay access are unresolved; no intervention setting or effect margin is selected.

## Biological problem

Does a source-restricted change in myeloid ARG1 have different consequences for
matrix production and mature epithelial recovery in a qualified lung repair
context? Reduced fibrosis-related measurements do not by themselves establish
regeneration. A32 asks for those two effects in the same interpretable design,
with fibroblast involvement assessed rather than assumed.

## Established knowledge

[Yadav 2025](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188734), especially Fig. 3, supplies the
myeloid ARG1/ornithine–collagen premise. [Hamanaka 2025](https://doi.org/10.1042/BCJ20253033),
Figs. 3–6, makes nutrient context and recipient signaling relevant alternatives.
[Li 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2026.102979), Fig. 6H–M, provides a
different perturbation's organoid and differentiation evidence, not an ARG1
effect or a qualified absolute mature-function outcome. Exact reading coverage,
contrary findings and source versions are in the context note.

## Repository evidence

The [Yd1-P02 plan](../../RQ_Specified/Yadav_followups_20261031/RQ3_MATRIX_AND_REPAIR.md)
already specified separate epithelial and matrix outcomes. The reviewed
[T0 endpoint map](../../RQ_Specified/Yadav_followups_20261031/T0_RESULTS.md) and
[T2 disposition](../../RQ_Specified/Yadav_followups_20261031/T2_DISPOSITION.md)
found no qualified joint source-ARG1/matrix/mature-output dataset or confirmed
host model. Hamanaka-derived T1-B measurements belong to Wg-P06 and remain
shared-source substrate descriptions. They supply neither the ARG1 effect nor
epithelial recovery. A13's unfavorable proxy result and other RQs' holds persist.

## Unresolved gap

The missing information is the paired biological-unit effect on newly produced
or deposited matrix and absolute lineage-derived mature epithelial output under
qualified myeloid-source ARG1 restriction. Neither an unlinked atlas nor a
matrix/RNA score supplies that joint effect. Source identity, intervention
specificity, mature endpoint validity, model access and precision are unresolved.

## Competing explanations

Matrix reduction and epithelial benefit may coincide, diverge or remain
imprecise. Effects may arise through altered myeloid abundance, recipient
nutrient availability, inflammation or viability rather than a fibroblast-mediated
repair route. Early epithelial survival/expansion may change absolute later
output without changing maturation probability. These explanations can coexist;
the primary effect comparison does not claim to identify every mediator.

## Discriminating outcomes

Lower collagen with higher mature output supports a favorable joint response
in the tested system, without proving fibroblast mediation. Lower collagen with
lower mature output identifies a possible tradeoff. An imprecise or unresolved
epithelial effect does not imply preserved function. RNA, organoid size or
cell fractions alone cannot decide the primary mature-output question. Changes
dominated by source/recipient loss retain an attribution limit.

## Next investigation

Follow the [development plan](../../RQ_Specified/A32_myeloid_arg1_matrix_epithelial_recovery/PLAN.md).
Qualify an accessible non-infectious source–fibroblast–alveolar epithelial model
or genuinely linked dataset, with separate source identity, intervention,
matrix and lineage/function measurements. No new biological numerical analysis
is authorized by this registration. Numerical work requires a new frozen
question-specific contract with prior exposure, actual units and precision.

## Experimental bridge

The fit-for-purpose comparison is a qualified source-restricted ARG1 change
against matched source-active control, retaining the linked matrix and epithelial
outcomes. Actual source, fibroblast and epithelial donors/animals and their
crossing/nesting define biological units; wells, organoids and images are nested.
Track source/recipient identity, target engagement, viable amount and inflammatory
context. Distinguish early survival/expansion from later lineage-derived output.
Direct-exposure controls or additional compartment tests are needed only for
the stronger attribution being claimed; rescue and factorial designs are not
automatic prerequisites for estimating a bounded intervention effect.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a perturbation-effect and joint-outcome question, not a claim that ARG1
is universally beneficial or harmful. The source's macrophage/neutrophil
differences prevent automatic species transfer. Collagen is not lung function;
AT1 markers are not a calibrated mature-function assay. No drug, dose, schedule,
effect margin, sample size, cell source or laboratory availability is invented.
No infection experiment or pathogen outcome is part of the proposed comparison.
