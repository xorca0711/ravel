# Nb4-P01–P09 — conditional supplements in the article namespace

3 October 2026. These supplements complete the design/disposition fields alongside
the [existing plans](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/README.md)
and [article source audit](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/SOURCES.md).
Those plans retain their identifiers, estimands, exploratory multiplicity rules
and prior exposure. This document creates no global RQ, promotion or priority.
[Common terms](README.md) apply throughout: independent-unit uncertainty,
unknown meaningful effects/sample sizes, unconfirmed actual access and a
committed contract before any numerical stage.

The nearest common precedent is [Travaglini 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2922-4):
its identities, regional observations and species comparisons are already
published. [Madissoon 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01243-4)
and [HLCA 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02327-2) expand context but
can reuse donors and annotations. Spatial localization is not lineage/function.
None of these supplements declares a new matrix qualified.

## Nb4-P01

[Existing transfer plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P01_model_transfer.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** a broader frozen ortholog representation improves
mouse-to-human AT1/AT2 classification over the fixed marker model.
**Strongest rival:** label circularity or assay/age/region mismatch.

- **Population/unit/time:** adult AT1/AT2, with mesothelium as a specificity
  challenge; mouse animal/pool and human donor are distinct units. This is
  cross-sectional transfer, not a lineage sequence.
- **Primary/denominator:** paired difference in balanced classification accuracy
  per eligible human donor, equal donor weighting. Without independent labels,
  call the endpoint author-label agreement.
- **Controls/validity:** mouse holdout, marker-omission labels, depth/region/assay
  restrictions, training-only scaling and feature selection, calibrated rejection.
  Missing orthologs stay missing.
- **Results/action:** replicated gain changing an annotation decision supports
  utility; loss under valid labels favors circularity. Species-study confounding
  limits attribution to cross-dataset transfer.
- **Novelty/precision:** the increment is annotation consequence beyond the
  atlas's species-marker observations. A meaningful accuracy gain and donor
  variance must be justified; no new cutoff is set.
- **Hold/access:** need PRJNA632939 animal/pool, age, region and orthology audit,
  then nonoverlapping human reference labels. A later protein/morphology
  validation needs independently characterized tissue, not a second RNA score.

## Nb4-P02

[Existing redistribution plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P02_evolutionary_redistribution.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** similar standardized expression can coexist with changed
allocation among matched cell classes. **Rival:** class mismatch or capture,
assay, orthology and normalization differences.

- **Population/unit/time:** qualified matched classes within human donors and
  mouse animal/pools; third-species observations remain separate. No time or
  ancestral direction is inferred.
- **Primary/denominator:** per-unit equal-class standardized expression and
  allocation-profile divergence; allocation denominator is summed expression
  over the frozen class universe. It is not whole-lung abundance.
- **Controls/validity:** reconstruct HHIP/SERPINA1 as known controls, independent
  class alignment, donor omission, depth and orthology sensitivity; no missing
  class is zero-filled.
- **Results/action:** robust allocation difference supports a comparative
  cellular-context result; disappearance on class matching supports the rival.
  No abundance difference with wide uncertainty is not conservation.
- **Novelty/precision:** compare the exact discriminator with the
  [2025 lemur atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09114-8).
  A meaningful expression-equivalence margin is unknown; use the continuous
  two-axis description until justified. Broad evolutionary adaptation is not
  identifiable from two species.
- **Hold/access:** inherit P01's unit/orthology hold; qualify third-species lung
  units and source reuse. Tissue weighting or protein/function would be separate
  evidence, with unconfirmed access. P02 does not require a positive P01 result.

## Nb4-P03

[Existing disease-context plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P03_disease_context.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** a fixed disease panel changes within comparable
alveolar/adventitial fibroblast states in IPF. **Rival:** captured mixture,
unmatched disease states or preparation differences.

- **Population/unit/time:** source-defined IPF/control donors in Adams and
  Habermann, analyzed within study at observed sampling; pieces/regions nested.
- **Primary/denominator:** donor-state IPF–control expression contrast with full
  measured-gene libraries. Fixed-mixture versus captured-mixture decomposition
  is secondary descriptive accounting, not tissue abundance or mediation.
- **Controls/validity:** diagnosis/region checks, disease panel from independent
  evidence, state labels excluding tested genes, alternative decomposition
  order and independently assessed pathology; retain unmatched states.
- **Results/action:** transported within-state change supports a disease-context
  association; mixture-only or label-dependent effects narrow the question.
  Missing shared states cannot be fixed by adding zeros.
- **Novelty/precision:** localization alone is already known. The increment
  requires replicated attribution beyond mixture, with justified effect scale
  and between-donor variance; gene families retain the existing FDR rule.
- **Hold/access:** requalify raw layers, diagnosis, donor joins, panel provenance
  and overlap before fitting. Independent pathology-labelled tissue is
  unconfirmed. An epithelial-input/chemokine mechanism belongs to A22/A13.

## Nb4-P04

[Existing tissue-association plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P04_immune_tissue_association.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** a lung-associated programme extends across eligible
immune lineages after subtype comparison. **Rival:** activation, recruited-cell
mixture, blood contamination or processing.

- **Population/unit/time:** paired donor lung/blood subtypes, beginning with
  eligible T-cell populations and transferring a frozen programme to B/NK
  populations. Macrophage-versus-monocyte is a different contrast.
- **Primary/denominator:** paired donor lung-minus-blood programme contrast
  and prespecified cross-lineage concordance. The source has two paired lung
  donors; report both effects without population-level significance.
- **Controls/validity:** subtype labels excluding programme genes, independent
  activation/stress panels, depth/processing and contamination checks.
  Bulk reference populations add annotation evidence, not matched donors.
- **Results/action:** independent paired replication supports shared tissue
  association; lineage- or activation-restricted evidence narrows it. Spatial
  confirmation establishes location, not residence duration.
- **Novelty/precision:** durable residency is outside this endpoint. Meaningful
  tissue contrast and paired donor variance are unknown; a small descriptive
  display needs a contract but no invented power claim.
- **Hold/access:** no qualified independent matched lung–blood accession.
  New lung-only data cannot close this gap. Matched specimens and longitudinal
  residence measurements have no confirmed access.

## Nb4-P05

[Existing identity/state plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P05_immune_identity_state.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** the IGSF21/EREG/TREM2-labelled populations have
reproducible multigene identity beyond recurring state. **Rival:** activation
crossing identities, batch, doublets and sparse capture.

- **Population/unit/time:** independently mapped myeloid lineages in
  donor-resolved cross-sectional cohorts; source-era dendritic names are
  hypotheses to reconcile, not guaranteed ontogenetic assignments.
- **Primary/denominator:** held-out donor/study population-assignment agreement
  with uncertainty and eligible-population coverage per donor.
- **Controls/validity:** sentinel and tested-programme omission from labels,
  orthogonal protein/lineage references, activation represented separately,
  doublet/ambient and assay controls.
- **Results/action:** multigene transfer supports a reproducible molecular
  population; continuous cross-lineage variation supports a state model.
  Mixed results permit identity-by-state structure.
- **Novelty/precision:** repeating the three atlas labels adds little. New
  primary reuse identified in [source review](SOURCES.md) is not independent
  human validation. Define meaningful reproducibility and donor variance before
  testing; do not tune clusters for apparent separation.
- **Hold/access:** full-compartment crosswalk, donor coverage and nonoverlapping
  validation are unqualified. Independent protein/ontogeny evidence is
  unconfirmed; stable transcription alone cannot establish a new lineage.

## Nb4-P06

[Existing AT2 heterogeneity plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P06_at2_heterogeneity.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** AT2-s is a transferable subdivision rather than a
continuous transcriptional axis. **Rival:** depth, region, damage, cycle or
the marker used to create its label.

- **Population/unit/time:** independently identified human AT2 across donors
  and assays; cross-sectional RNA only. Species comparison uses P01's audit.
- **Primary/denominator:** donor-held-out predictive support for declared
  discrete versus continuous models at comparable complexity; donor-specific
  distributions and qualified rare-state contrasts.
- **Controls/validity:** marker-independent labels, technical depth/assay
  sensitivity and frozen model comparison. Subsampling is technical stability,
  not extra donors.
- **Results/action:** transferable subdivision supports a population question;
  continuous support narrows to a state axis. Failed coverage leaves the
  subdivision unresolved rather than absent.
- **Novelty/precision:** AT2 heterogeneity is established. Its reproducible
  geometry is the increment; no Wnt activity, quiescence or stemness follows.
  Meaningful predictive gain and donor variance remain unknown.
- **Hold/access:** retain the earlier rare-state coverage floor; do not lower
  it to call the same test passed. A separate all-AT2 estimand needs an amendment.
  Linked activity, time and mature output require unconfirmed functional
  material and retain A4/A7/A8/A19 ownership.

## Nb4-P07

[Existing regional-identity plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P07_regional_epithelial_identity.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** independently located distal IPF epithelium carries a
normal regional basal programme beyond generic injury. **Rival:** airway
admixture, pre-existing airway expansion or basal-subtype mixture.

- **Population/unit/time:** donor × measured region × independently labelled
  epithelial subtype, with normal and disease sources kept distinct in time
  and pathology.
- **Primary/denominator:** donor-level regional-programme contrast beyond a
  separately specified injury component; full measured-gene libraries.
- **Controls/validity:** region from sampling/pathology rather than RNA;
  independent label/programme provenance and complete overlap report;
  airway/admixture controls and blinded localization.
- **Results/action:** transferred normal-region pattern in verified distal
  disease cells supports molecular redeployment. Injury-only or admixture
  explanation narrows the question; neither proves cell migration.
- **Novelty/precision:** regional atlases already exist. The exact beyond-injury
  discriminator is the possible increment; define meaningful contrast and
  donor/region covariance before inference.
- **Hold/access:** measured-region joins, raw layers and independent pathology
  are unqualified. Spatial images illustrate location, not ancestry.
  Actual tissue access is unconfirmed. If the endpoint reduces to programme
  reuse or lesion addition, retain A0/A5/A11 ownership.

## Nb4-P08

[Existing mural-specialization plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P08_vascular_mural_specialization.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** a TBX5-associated pericyte pattern reflects vessel
context beyond shared contractile state. **Rival:** mixed mural identity,
capture, doublets or contractile variation.

- **Population/unit/time:** pericytes, vascular smooth muscle and qualified
  neighboring classes in donor-resolved tissue with independently annotated
  vessel beds; a cross-sectional comparison.
- **Primary/denominator:** donor-level TBX5/pericyte-programme contrast by
  independently established identity and vessel bed. Fields/cells nest in donors.
- **Controls/validity:** labels excluding TBX5 and tested contractile genes,
  independent anatomical imaging, depth/doublet controls and separate
  MYH11/CNN1/TAGLN programme provenance.
- **Results/action:** concordant molecular/anatomical replication supports
  regional specialization; within-identity contractile tracking favors state.
  Missing anatomy limits interpretation to cell-class expression.
- **Novelty/precision:** the atlas expression contrast is a reproduction
  control. A meaningful context effect and between-donor variance are unknown.
  Hormone-receptor RNA does not show hormone responsiveness.
- **Hold/access:** vessel-bed labels and RNA/image donor pairing are missing.
  Functional contraction/perfusion evidence and compatible tissue are
  unconfirmed; an Fzd4/endothelial-function extension belongs to A21.

## Nb4-P09

[Existing fibroblast-sensory plan](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/P09_fibroblast_sensory.md).

**Hypothesis/decision:** SCN7A and GRIA1 have reproducible fibroblast-specific
expression warranting a later response question. **Rival:** incidental sparse
RNA, ambient signal, doublets or mixed identities.

- **Population/unit/time:** independently labelled alveolar/adventitial and
  other fibroblasts, with neural/glial and mural comparators from full
  donor-resolved sources. Cross-sectional RNA precedes any functional claim.
- **Primary/denominator:** separate donor-level detection fractions and
  expression for each gene, plus joint within-cell detection. Denominators
  retain eligible cells and full measured libraries; assay modalities stay separate.
- **Controls/validity:** labels exclude both genes, gene-ID check, depth,
  ambient/doublet sensitivity, independent spatial/protein localization.
- **Results/action:** reproducible specificity supports an expression phenotype;
  one-gene evidence narrows the joint proposal. Failure of specificity stops
  the sensory interpretation, not every fibroblast question.
- **Novelty/precision:** the [2024 age atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-06111-x),
  Fig. 4, repeats the marker context; its sensory interpretation is not a
  direct response measurement. Meaningful detection/expression differences
  and donor variance are unknown.
- **Hold/access:** qualify full-compartment counts, donor overlap and protein
  localization. A defined stimulus, direct cellular response and specific
  causal comparison remain unselected/unavailable. No electrophysiology or
  sensory mechanism is inferred from a channel name.
