## Compact resource list for later owner/host confirmation

1. **Material and identity:** available animal lines/primary cultures/specimens,
   exact background/age/sex/pathology, current colonies or donor supply, and
   stable donor/animal/pool/preparation identifiers.
2. **Measured endpoint:** which lineage, mature epithelial function/reserve,
   vascular function, protein output or phosphate-handling assay already works
   locally, with calibration and viability controls.
3. **Contrast:** actual receptor/input/compartment comparisons, their engagement
   validity, co-interventions and independent preparation capacity.
4. **Linkage:** starting-state, assay/preparation, time and later outcome joins;
   public-source owners may need to supply these. No author contact was sent.
5. **Precision:** meaningful effect on the chosen biological scale, pilot
   between-unit variance, nesting and attrition; cells/wells cannot replace units.
6. **Practical access:** supervision, equipment/material rights, applicable
   oversight and available time. A funded aim or coauthored method confirms none
   of these. No experimental completion date can be inferred yet.
