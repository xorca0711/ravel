## Common design terms

These proposed requirements apply to every package below; they do not change
[research governance](../../RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE.md) or historical gates.

- **Model/access:** published platforms are feasibility precedents. Exact
  genotype, lineage specificity, species, age, sex, pathology, preparation,
  passage and relevant culture inputs must be recorded when a model is chosen.
  Reporter lines qualify signaling; organoids qualify represented compartments;
  slices retain architecture but nest within donors; vascular/lineage claims
  need the appropriate tissue or animal evidence.
- **Unit:** animals or donors are biological units. Repeated preparations from
  one donor, pools, wells, cells, sections and images retain their parent
  relationships. A pool is one observed pool unless individual outcomes exist.
  Randomization, blinding and pairing apply at the design-supported level.
  Splitting cells never supplies independent biological replication.
- **Timing:** t0 precedes intervention/predictor, t1 is an early response and
  t2 the later outcome. These specify ordering, not prescribed durations.
  Source-guided assay qualification must set actual times before outcome review.
  Destructive assays need verified sister preparations or another justified
  linkage; they do not reobserve one cell.
- **Mature output (M):** absolute lineage-linked viable descendants per recorded
  starting AT2 population, with independently validated AT1 protein identity and
  morphology. A separate model-appropriate function, such as validated barrier
  permeability in a suitable epithelial preparation, is required for functional
  maturation. Barrier behavior alone is not AT1-specific or gas exchange.
  Report survival, total descendant yield and survivor-conditioned fractions
  separately; MYRF alone and RNA scores do not define M.
- **Communication (C):** measure source output, recipient engagement and
  recipient consequence separately. Total output and output per viable source
  cell are distinct. Protein concentration is not secretion rate without
  collection/recovery calibration; RNA is neither.
- **Controls/validity:** package-specific controls supplement untreated or
  vehicle/sham references where relevant, assay-positive and assay-negative
  references, identity/contamination checks, viability, dynamic range and
  blinded scoring. Failed engagement or validity makes a null uninterpretable;
  no numerical control threshold is invented.
- **Precision/multiplicity:** meaningful effect margins and sample sizes are
  **unknown**. Establish the decision-relevant scale, between-unit variance,
  pairing, clustering and missingness before a prospective precision calculation.
  Sparse-unit displays stay descriptive; nonsignificance is not equivalence.
  Declare the complete testing family before execution; secondary outcomes
  cannot replace the primary. Nb4 retains its existing exploratory family rules.
- **Exposure/stop:** repository results and nominated features are already
  exposed. A future freeze records that exposure. Stop the specified inference
  for failed linkage, nonidentifiable contrasts or invalid assays, while stating
  which narrower descriptions remain possible. Retain contradictory and
  precise negative results. Changed endpoints/features require an amendment.

For pathogen-related source material, these packages concern host measurements
and interpretation only; no infection or vector-construction procedure is proposed.

receipts and failed runs remain intact. Each package names the enabling artifact
or measurement decision; access alone would not satisfy those conditions.

existing committed, hash-bound contract and runner.
