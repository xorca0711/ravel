# A20 — Fzd2 versus Fzd1 and the competent early pool

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A20.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Nabhan 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.05.022),
[Zhou 2025, Figs. 5–7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-025-02501-8) and
[Jones 2024, Figs. 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ado5561)
already motivate receptor-dependent stromal support.
**Repository:** the owner-requested [narrowed hypothesis](../../../RQ_Specified/A20_fibroblast_fzd_context/NARROWED_HYPOTHESIS.md)
takes precedence; [source audit](../../../RQ_Specified/A20_fibroblast_fzd_context/SOURCES.md)
records Zhou's direction-wording inconsistency and absence of a matched Fzd1
comparison. No direct Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 functional join exists.

**Hypothesis/decision:** comparable Fzd2 loss in a defined AF1-like context
reduces support and absolute mature AT2-descendant output more than Fzd1 loss,
through maintenance of a pool capable of later maturation.
Rival: maturation-specific support, source depletion/matrix effects or unequal
perturbation validity.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Prospectively identified AF1-like fibroblasts and matched starting AT2 populations; independent source/recipient units with technical nesting. Pdgfra labeling alone is not AF1 purity. |
| Contrast/timing | Matched Fzd2-loss, Fzd1-loss and reference contexts; qualify perturbation and viable source state first, early epithelial competent pool at t1, M at t2. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Total Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 contrast in absolute M per t0 AT2 input. Early viable competent pool and source support are secondary mechanistic measurements, not automatic adjustment covariates. |
| Controls/validity | Comparable receptor perturbation/engagement, source identity and viability, matrix/context, recipient-only and source-support references; early pool competence needs a calibrated functional discriminator beyond RNA. |
| Competing results/action | Reduced early competent pool with correspondingly reduced M is compatible with pool maintenance. Preserved pool but reduced M favors maturation-specific support. Source depletion or unequal perturbation leaves the receptor contrast unresolved; none of these patterns alone proves mediation. |
| Stop/limit | Hold without matched receptor contrast and early/later unit linkage. Never normalize away post-treatment pool depletion and call the remaining effect the total repair effect. |

**Novelty/precision:** generic Fzd2 repair involvement is published. The exact
matched receptor comparison and early-pool/late-output discrimination are the
possible increment. Meaningful M contrast, early-pool assay validity and
between-unit covariance are unknown; one total-effect contrast is primary.
**Access:** AF1-like preparations, comparable receptor contrasts and M assays
are unconfirmed. No secreted mediator or Fzd2–Notch route is selected.
