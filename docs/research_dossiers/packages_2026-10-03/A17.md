# A17 — founder persistence versus switching and observation

Proposed conditional modeling package. [Dossier](../A17.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [England 2025, clonal figures and Methods S1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2025.01.011)
provides real clonal evidence.
**Repository:** [current article record](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_C2_england_2025/README.md)
and [source accounting](../../roadmap_runs/2026-09-28-gap-fill/a17_source_accounting/REPORT.md)
recover 58 rows and 11 source-indexed mice. The deposited simulator's
unreachable slow-loss branch does not refute the source experiments.

**Hypothesis/decision:** a persistent founder component improves predictive
adequacy beyond a switching family of comparable complexity. Rival:
switching, loss, clone merger, detection or censoring explains size heterogeneity.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Source-defined clones at documented observation times, nested within mice. Terminal cohorts at different times are not repeated observations of the same clone. |
| Contrast/timing | Compare founder-only, switching-only and combined model families, each coupled to an explicit observation process. Use the actual cross-sectional time design, not invented longitudinal trajectories. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Held-out mouse predictive log score for the clone-size/count distribution at its observed time. Mice, not 58 rows or individual clones, supply validation units. Inadequate split support means descriptive model compatibility only. |
| Controls/validity | Simulation recovery and identifiability checks under each declared family; matching effective flexibility; detection, merger, labeling and censoring sensitivity; probability/branch invariants. These are model checks, not biological replication. |
| Competing results/action | Stable gain under plausible observation models supports a founder component within those families. Equal adequacy or parameter nonidentifiability leaves it unresolved. Observation-model dependence redirects effort to measurement evidence. |
| Stop/limit | Hold substantive comparison until model/observation assumptions, folds and unit accounting are frozen with corrected versioned code. Preserve historical simulator and outputs. A latent class is not a molecular cell type. |

**Novelty/precision:** distinguish a computational defect from a scientific
alternative. A meaningful predictive gain, feasible mouse-level uncertainty and
parameter recoverability must be justified before execution. No new biological
data or corrected fit is produced here. **Access:** source code/count records
exist; a newly hash-bound entrypoint and defensible observation contract do not.
Additional longitudinal/tracing material is unconfirmed and is needed only if
the existing design cannot distinguish the models.
