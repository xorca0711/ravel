# A1 — early regulation beyond early RNA

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A1.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Hassan 2024, Figs. 3/5/6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48632-3)
and [Sawhney 2025, Fig. 7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64224-1)
establish regulatory and response-state precedents.
**Repository:** [comparison matrix](../../../RQ_Specified/A1_transitional_epithelial_state_distinction/COMPARISON_MATRIX.md)
and [outcome audit](../followup_2026-10-03/OUTCOMES.md) lack the required early
regulatory/RNA/later-outcome join. Existing mature labelled endpoints are real.

**Hypothesis/decision:** RNA-similar transitional alveolar epithelial cells may differ in regulatory competence for later AT1 maturation. The remaining hypothesis would require an early regulatory feature to distinguish later lineage-derived mature AT1 output beyond the starting RNA state. No particular regulatory feature has yet been nominated for that prospective contrast; a generic chromatin assay does not complete it.

**Current novelty boundary:** Regulatory heterogeneity and functional transitional states are published. Exact-feature novelty cannot be judged until the regulatory feature and mature endpoint are fixed. [Source comparison](../NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a1). Existing design rows are conditional; the specificity holds above must be resolved before treating them as an experiment specification.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Independently labelled AT2-derived transitional populations; mouse/donor is the biological unit, assay preparations and descendants nested. Define populations without the tested regulatory/RNA feature. |
| Contrast/timing | Acquire a nominated regulatory feature and RNA comparator at t1, before M at t2. Choose the feature from mechanistic/source evidence and assay reliability, not its association with t2. A destructive measurement needs a justified sister-preparation design. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Improvement in held-out unit-level prediction error for absolute mature descendants per t0 AT2 input, adding regulation to the same frozen RNA/baseline model. M includes separate identity, morphology and functional qualification. |
| Controls/validity | Comparable early state, viability, assay batch and dynamic range; assay-positive/negative references; label definition excluding candidate features. Train-only transformations and whole-unit splits. Unlinked assay cohorts are not paired controls. |
| Competing results/action | Reproducible increment supports further prospective validation. No increment with adequate precision narrows the utility claim; increment lost after valid state/timing checks supports the rival. No prediction result identifies a causal regulator. |
| Stop/limit | Hold for missing early/late unit linkage, predictor leakage or unvalidated M. A current chromatin-versus-RNA difference remains descriptive. |

**Novelty:** the exact incremental, temporally ordered prediction differs from
showing regulatory state differences. A matched primary study already testing
that increment would make simple repetition redundant.
**Precision/access:** decision-relevant error improvement and between-unit
outcome variance are unknown. Primary lineage-compatible cultures/tracing and
early regulatory assays are published platforms, not available resources.
One frozen feature/model increment is primary; additional features require
declared multiplicity or a separate exploratory stage.
