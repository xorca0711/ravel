# A8 — early RNA component and later mature output

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A8.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** transitional differentiation is established in
[Strunz 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17358-3) and
[Choi 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2020.06.020).
**Repository:** [current workspace](../../../RQ_Specified/A8_maturation_component_at1_contribution/README.md)
and [outcome inventory](../../../RQ_Specified/A1_transitional_epithelial_state_distinction/reports/A1_A8_A14_OUTCOME_INVENTORY.md)
separate the 119-gene overlap diagnostic from a missing outcome-linked test.
MYRF is not AT1-specific.

**Hypothesis/decision:** the frozen candidate RNA component improves prediction
of later M beyond a fixed early shared-transition comparator. Rival: endpoint
leakage, contemporaneous identity, generic stress or composition.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Comparable lineage-defined starting AT2-derived populations in independent animal/donor preparations, with valid linkage between early assay and later descendants. |
| Contrast/timing | At t1, measure fixed component and comparator before t2 outcome. Compare the same baseline model with versus without the component; preprocessing and feature choices stay within training units. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Held-out unit prediction-error improvement for M per t0 input. Report absolute mature yield, survival and total descendant yield, not just a fraction of surviving cells. A concurrent comparison is an association-only alternative with a separate contract. |
| Controls/validity | Outcome identity/morphology/function independent of candidate genes; starting-state and input accounting; leakage check; whole-unit and later independent-study validation; assay-positive/negative references. |
| Competing results/action | Stable incremental information supports predictive utility. No increment with adequate precision narrows utility; collapse on unit/study transfer favors the rival. A positive result does not show that changing the score changes fate. |
| Stop/limit | Hold for missing t1→t2 linkage, unverifiable preparation nesting or RNA-only outcome. Do not turn A0's failed cross-tissue signature into an A8 rescue. |

**Novelty:** a linked incremental test, not another maturation score or a
known AT1-like culture result. New Rochelle 2026 evidence in [source intake](SOURCES.md)
adds an endpoint precedent but is not yet the required early-feature/M join.
**Precision/access:** useful error improvement, between-unit M variance and
functional assay validity are unknown. Lineage-compatible primary models
are published; local access is unconfirmed. One model increment is primary.
