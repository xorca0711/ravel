# A12 — specific reception versus general inflammation

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A12.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Choi 2020, IL-1 recipient experiments](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2020.06.020)
provides the lung precedent. Cell-specific signaling in
[Saxton 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abc8433) is a methodological
comparison, not a validated lung platform.
**Repository:** [current recipient-context evidence](../../../RQ_Specified/A12_recipient_context/README.md)
reports epithelial prediction gain in twelve patients and unstable fibroblast
results; large unassigned IL1B contributions prevent macrophage-only attribution.

**Hypothesis/decision:** recipient competence modifies an IL-1-dependent
response beyond effective input and nonspecific inflammatory state.
Rival: the index reports general inflammation or cell mixture.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Independently defined epithelial and fibroblast recipients, analyzed as separate compartments in identified donor/preparation units. Source identity remains explicit, including unassigned sources. |
| Contrast/timing | Measure competence at t0, recipient engagement at t1 and consequence at t2 under an interpretable IL-1-dependent contrast plus a distinct inflammatory comparator. Source/input history is measured rather than inferred from RNA. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Difference in IL-1-dependent recipient response by baseline competence per t0 viable recipient input. Nominate the consequence assay before execution; the existing endpoint is inflammatory RNA, not demonstrated activity. |
| Controls/validity | Untreated/vehicle, validated engagement reference, receptor-dependence, alternate-inflammatory-input, recipient identity and viability controls. Source output, input availability and response are separate C measurements. |
| Competing results/action | A competence interaction specific to the IL-1-dependent contrast supports reception. The same relationship without that dependence favors a general inflammatory marker. Epithelial success cannot substitute for failed fibroblast transport. |
| Stop/limit | Hold source-specific causal attribution without protein/input and receptor evidence. NF-kB-associated RNA does not identify the ligand or source. |

**Novelty/precision:** reception biology is established; the exact specificity
contrast is the increment. Define a meaningful response interaction and
between-unit variance before sizing; epithelial and fibroblast hypotheses form
separate declared tests. **Access:** primary recipients and validated engagement
and consequence assays are unconfirmed. A reduced signaling model tests
specificity only, not mature tissue repair.
