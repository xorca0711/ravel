## Fresh targeted checks

Queries included receptor withdrawal with mature AT1/reserve, Fzd2 versus Fzd1
fibroblast support, AREG delivery/EGFR context, CD177/clone switching,
SLC34A2 handling/transition, SCN7A/GRIA1 fibroblasts, TBX5 vessel context and
IGSF21/EREG/TREM2 identity. Primary records were used for conclusions; secondary
search hits were only leads. No repeated bulk GEO/BioSample audit was performed.

- [Sawhney 2025 publisher full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64224-1)
  confirms both regulatory-circuit and distinct response-state precedents.
  This is a recheck of an existing source, not a newly discovered study.
- The Fzd2 search returned [Zhou 2025](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-025-02501-8).
  Repository search showed it was already audited in
  [A20 SOURCES](../../../RQ_Specified/A20_fibroblast_fzd_context/SOURCES.md).
  Its inconsistent transition-direction wording is retained; no novelty claim
  is made for rediscovering the Fzd2 repair result.
- [Rochelle 2026](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/188701), Figs. 6–8 and
  data availability, is an additional human AT2 expansion/differentiation
  precedent. Fig. 7 distinguishes donor-coded molecular and secreted-output
  assays. The article does not supply the complete A19 receptor/reserve contrast.
  Its stated sequencing accessions are GSE306194 and GSE306714.
- [2024 age atlas](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06111-x), Fig. 4 and
  data availability, repeats the SCN7A/GRIA1 fibroblast context and cites the
  2020 atlas for the sensory interpretation. That passage is not a direct
  sensory-response experiment. GSE260769 is a new candidate source for
  qualification, not an executed dataset here.
- [Complement C1q study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10923196/),
  indexed primary comparison text, integrates human atlas myeloid cells with
  mouse cells. It therefore cannot be counted as independent human replication
  of the original IGSF21/EREG/TREM2 labels. Full methods were not newly audited.
- The TBX5 search found [fetal-lung ChIP GSE167207](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE167207).
  A fetal whole-context binding dataset is not qualified adult pericyte
  anatomy/function evidence. No new P08 endpoint was established.

The primary JCI/publisher pages were accessible. Some direct PMC retrievals
returned browser checks; indexed text and already-read local source audits
are labelled accordingly. The web tool could not open the two new GEO accession
pages. An inaccessible page is not evidence that its data do not exist.
