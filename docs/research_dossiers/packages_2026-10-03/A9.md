# A9 — recipient competence under comparable AREG input

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A9.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Kaiser 2023](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.20221462) and
[Cardoso 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10399-6) establish relevant
mesenchymal EGFR response; general receptor involvement is not new.
**Repository:** [corrected ligand report](../../../analysis/corrections/ligand/RESULTS.md)
has only seven of 22 donors meeting EGFR_ERBB2 coverage, below its 11-donor
floor. The Nb3 EGF context is adjacent evidence, not an AREG competence assay.

**Hypothesis/decision:** measured recipient competence modifies fibroblast
response to comparable effective AREG input. Rival: input availability, other
ligands, recipient mixture or generalized activation.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Independently defined fibroblast recipient states from identified donors/preparations; shared donors and split wells remain nested. |
| Contrast/timing | Characterize receptor protein/functional competence before input; compare recipient states at matched effective input with a receptor-dependent contrast. Measure engagement at t1 and a validated recipient consequence at t2. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Between-state difference in the receptor-dependent recipient response per t0 recipient input. The concrete consequence assay must be selected and validated before freezing; current RNA does not nominate its scale. |
| Controls/validity | Protein/surface specificity, engagement reference, untreated/vehicle and receptor-dependence controls, AREG versus other documented medium inputs, viability and recipient identity. Do not assume an EGFR homodimer or EGFR–ERBB2 complex from database membership. |
| Competing results/action | Competence-linked, receptor-dependent response at comparable input supports the hypothesis. Difference explained by effective input or other ligands favors those rivals. A null with failed engagement is uninterpretable. |
| Stop/limit | Hold without ligand/recipient measurements and MesSTIM preparation map. RNA coverage alone cannot license a receptor mechanism. |

**Novelty/precision:** source effects are A2; this comparison concerns recipient
effect modification. The interaction, meaningful response scale and between-unit
variance require specification; primary interaction and any receptor panel have
declared multiplicity. **Access:** characterized recipients and C assays are
unconfirmed. A reporter can qualify engagement, not replace primary fibroblast
consequences.
