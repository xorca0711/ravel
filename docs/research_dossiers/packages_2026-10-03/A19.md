# A19 — defined withdrawal, mature output and retained reserve

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A19.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Nabhan 2023, Figs. 4–7](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.05.022)
provides receptor-selective context; [Jacob 2017, Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5755620/)
concerns AT2 maturation. [Rochelle 2026, Figs. 6–8](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI188701)
adds human expansion/differentiation evidence, not the complete receptor-specific
withdrawal/reserve comparison.
**Repository:** [current results](../../../RQ_Specified/A19_fzd_response_reversibility/RESULTS.md)
and [external review](../../../RQ_Specified/A19_fzd_response_reversibility/EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE.md)
retain missing functional linkage and a candidate, not causal, pre-withdrawal state.

**Hypothesis/decision:** a defined pre-withdrawal state permits mature output
after a specified input is removed while retaining a responsive AT2 reserve.
Rival: duration, mixture, survival or generic downstream pathway withdrawal.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Lineage-defined primary AT2-derived preparations with measured starting/pre-withdrawal state; donor/animal is the unit, wells/lineages nested. |
| Contrast/timing | Compare continued versus withdrawn **specified receptor input**, documenting other medium/mechanical changes. Measure state before withdrawal, engagement cessation early, and M plus reserve later. CHIR and Fzd-selective inputs are distinct contrasts. |
| Primary outcomes/denominators | Joint profile: M per t0 AT2 input and retained viable AT2 reserve per the same input. Reserve requires independent AT2 identity plus demonstrated later responsive/self-renewing capacity; a survivor fraction alone is insufficient. |
| Controls/validity | Continuing-input, no-input and qualified differentiation references; effective input/cessation, baseline composition, survival, total yield and lineage specificity. Functional M and reserve assays must be independently calibrated. |
| Competing results/action | Increased M with retained functional reserve supports productive recovery in the defined context. M accompanied by reserve depletion is a tradeoff; area/marker-only change favors simpler alternatives. A state interaction needs its own supported contrast. |
| Stop/limit | Hold the joint claim when either mature function, reserve or unit linkage is missing. Multi-factor medium changes do not isolate receptor-specific withdrawal. |

**Novelty/precision:** complete input/state/M/reserve linkage is the increment.
Define a biologically meaningful joint success/tradeoff region and between-unit
covariance before sizing; no reserve noninferiority margin is invented.
**Access:** primary lineage material, receptor inputs and validated M/reserve
assays remain unconfirmed. Published human engineered tissue does not establish
local inventory or equivalence to the mouse system.
