# A21 — adult vascular function linked to endothelial lineage

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A21.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Gillich 2020, capillary lineage experiments](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2822-7)
establishes capillary roles. Bian's tumor-context Fzd4 evidence is mapped in the
[extension source audit](../../../RQ_Specified/A21_fzd4_capillary_function/extensions/extension_v1/SOURCES.md);
it is not normal adult repair.
**Repository:** [current results](../../../RQ_Specified/A21_fzd4_capillary_function/RESULTS.md)
measure Fzd4 RNA. Recovered Bian figure rows lack stable cross-panel animal IDs;
their existence supersedes an older access failure.

**Hypothesis/decision:** Fzd4-dependent capillary integrity may permit gCap self-renewal and subsequent aerocyte production during normal adult alveolar repair. The proposed route links vascular function to lineage output; it must be distinguished from direct regenerative entry, endothelial survival and parallel injury responses. Tumor-vessel rescue is a precedent, not evidence that this complete adult repair route has been demonstrated.

**Current novelty boundary:** Capillary lineage specialization and Fzd4 vascular effects are established. The candidate increment is adult repair function/lineage linkage; co-occurrence alone does not establish integrity-mediated renewal. [Source comparison](../NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a21). Existing design rows are conditional; the specificity holds above must be resolved before treating them as an experiment specification.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Validated starting adult gCap lineages in a defined repair context. Animals are units, clones/vascular fields nested; baseline labeling efficiency and specificity are measured. |
| Contrast/timing | An interpretable Fzd4 contrast with baseline lineage/vascular state, early integrity/function and later lineage output in a linked design. Terminal separate cohorts cannot provide within-animal early/late correlation. |
| Primary outcomes/denominators | New lineage-labelled gCap progeny with division evidence and retained gCap identity, plus aerocyte descendants, per t0 labelled population, alongside a separately validated vascular-function endpoint (permeability or perfusion, selected before execution). Survival and vascular area are separate. |
| Controls/validity | Reference/vehicle context, receptor engagement, lineage specificity, vascular assay calibration and injury/viability controls; anatomy and cross-panel animal IDs. A tumor rescue is a context control, not replication. |
| Competing results/action | Linked output/function differences surviving mortality accounting support coupling in the specified design. Output loss explained by cell loss or RNA-only changes favors the rival. Temporal association still does not by itself prove mediation. |
| Stop/limit | Hold without function and lineage in the appropriate adult context. Do not attribute every Foxf1-loss perfusion result to an Fzd4-restoration experiment. |

**Novelty/precision:** normal-repair linkage is the increment, not capillary
subtypes or tumor-vessel rescue. Function selection, meaningful lineage/function
effects and unit variance are unknown; declare the joint family before testing.
**Access:** intact vascular/lineage models and validated functional assays are
unconfirmed. A minimal epithelial organoid cannot replace vascular function.
