# A7 — broad identity attenuation and baseline-state interaction

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A7.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Hassan 2024, Figs. 3/5/6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48632-3)
and [Sawhney 2025, Fig. 7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64224-1)
already connect CEBPA with identity/plasticity and distinct response states.
**Repository:** [ES1 result](../../../Research%20Article/epithelial_state_specificity/results/SUMMARY.md)
is conditional on labels; [source qualification](../followup_2026-10-03/METADATA.md)
separates six CEBPA contexts from four AP-1 contexts and preserves pool limits.

**Hypothesis/decision:** Cebpa loss broadly attenuates AT2 identity, with a
possible additional dependence on starting state. Rival: defining states after
genotype effects, selection/survival or depth creates the apparent interaction.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Prospectively defined or independently validated baseline AT2 states within replicated genotype groups. Animals/preparations are units; joint RNA/ATAC measurements from one pool are not replicates. |
| Contrast/timing | Establish state at t0, genotype/input contrast, then assess identity at t1. Developmental and adult contexts remain separate. An endpoint-only state label permits conditional description, not baseline-state interaction. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Average change in independently measured AT2 identity across starting states; separately estimate the genotype-by-starting-state interaction on the same scale. Report absolute viable AT2-identity yield per t0 input and survivor-conditioned identity separately. |
| Controls/validity | Matched genotype/control preparations, direct perturbation validity, baseline-label exclusion of outcome genes, viability, depth and protein-level identity checks. AP-1 is a separate study/contrast, not extra CEBPA replication. |
| Competing results/action | Broad effect with a reproducible interaction supports both components. Broad effect without a precise interaction supports only the broad conclusion; a nonsignificant interaction is not equivalence. Post-treatment relabeling favors the selection rival. |
| Stop/limit | Hold replicated interaction until library→pool→animal/preparation and baseline-state evidence exist. Identity loss is not mature fate or a specific repair mechanism. |

**Novelty/precision:** the increment is the exact additional baseline-state
interaction, not rediscovery of CEBPA function. Define meaningful identity and
interaction scales and between-unit variance before sample size; primary and
interaction tests form a declared family. **Access:** appropriate primary
genotype/state material and independent identity measurements are unconfirmed.
Pathogen-related source experiments supply context only.
