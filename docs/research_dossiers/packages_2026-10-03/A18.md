# A18 — separate neighboring WT growth, identity and survival

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A18.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [England 2025, Figs. 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2025.01.011)
and [Cardoso 2026, niche comparisons](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10399-6)
already describe mutant-context effects; [Nabhan 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2606113123)
adds epithelial-identity/stromal context.
**Repository:** [current England workspace](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_C2_england_2025/README.md)
does not map pooled spatial observations to the required WT clones/animals.
SPP1/DLK1 remain nominations, not established separate channels.

**Hypothesis/decision:** The local mutant epithelial context may alter absolute expansion of neighboring WT AT2 lineages separately from loss of WT alveolar identity. The immediate biological question is whether those outcomes dissociate after survival and starting state are measured. SPP1 and DLK1 remain possible source candidates; no two molecular signaling channels have been established.

**Current novelty boundary:** Altered epithelial niches are published. A mapped WT lineage/context comparison could establish a useful dissociation, but dissociation alone is not proof of separate pathways. [Source comparison](../NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a18). Existing design rows are conditional; the specificity holds above must be resolved before treating them as an experiment specification.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Independently identified WT AT2-derived lineages near and away from mapped mutant context, with genotype and spatial records. Clones/fields nest in animals; cocultures nest in donor/preparation. |
| Contrast/timing | Establish t0 WT input and context, early WT engagement/state at t1, then lineage-resolved growth, identity and survival at t2. Distance is observational unless the context contrast is experimentally justified. |
| Primary outcomes/denominators | Absolute WT descendant yield per t0 WT lineage/input and independently measured WT identity, with survival separate. Treat growth and identity as a declared joint family; specify the meaningful dissociation region before testing. |
| Controls/validity | WT-only/reference context, verified genotype/labeling, spatial detection/clone boundaries, equal starting input, independent identity panel and viability. Known source amount and recipient exposure constrain any niche attribution. |
| Competing results/action | Reproducible dissociation beyond measurement uncertainty supports separable outcomes. Coupling explained by survival/mixture favors the rival. Dissociation alone does not establish two molecular pathways. |
| Stop/limit | Hold independent-unit comparison without clone/animal/time maps. A proportion of WT cells is not absolute growth; identity markers are not survival. |

**Novelty/precision:** the proposed increment is a mapped dissociation, not the
existence of a mutant niche. Meaningful growth/identity effects, covariance and
between-animal variance are unknown. **Access:** mapped tracing or compatible
primary coculture and independently validated WT outcomes are unconfirmed.
Minimal cultures may test a bounded recipient effect but omit immune and
vascular feedback.
