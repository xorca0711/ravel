# A14 — exposure history and fibroblast reception are separate tests

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A14.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Choi 2020, Fig. 7](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2020.06.020)
already demonstrates withdrawal-associated recovery. [Ciminieri 2023, Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12042164/) also provides the fibroblast-history/organoid-growth precedent; the present candidate requires the later mature-output distinction.
**Repository:** [current question](../../../RQ_Specified/A14_withdrawal_recovery_and_reception/README.md)
retains two hypotheses; [outcome audit](../followup_2026-10-03/OUTCOMES.md)
finds no qualified compartment/input-history-to-mature-output export.

**Hypothesis/decision:** H1: longer IL-1-beta exposure reduces subsequent lineage-derived mature AT1 recovery after input cessation, with comparable recovery intervals and culture age. H2, assessed separately: fibroblast IL-1 reception modifies that mature-output recovery beyond direct epithelial reception. H2 has no justified direction yet. The proposed increment concerns post-cessation maturation, not the already published effects of withdrawal or fibroblast pre-exposure on organoid formation.

**Current novelty boundary:** Choi establishes withdrawal recovery; Ciminieri already tests fibroblast IL-1 pre-exposure and organoid growth. The candidate increment is linked mature AT1 recovery after verified cessation with compartment attribution, not generic history or niche involvement. [Source comparison](../NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a14). Existing design rows are conditional; the specificity holds above must be resolved before treating them as an experiment specification.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Lineage-defined AT2 epithelium with independently characterized fibroblasts in identified primary preparations; wells/lineages nested in donor/animal. |
| Contrast/timing | At t0 document starting state; vary exposure history and fibroblast reception in separately identifiable contrasts, or a justified factorial design. At withdrawal distinguish nominal removal from measured signal cessation; observe early recovery and later M at fixed source-qualified times. |
| Primary outcomes/denominators | H1: difference in M per t0 AT2 input between histories after cessation. H2: difference in the same outcome by fibroblast reception at matched epithelial input. Treat both as a declared primary family; interaction is separate unless powered prospectively. |
| Controls/validity | Never-exposed, continuing-input and withdrawal references; compartment-specific engagement, residual-input measurement, equivalent handling, viable source/recipient counts and starting composition. Signal kinetics must be calibrated. |
| Competing results/action | History effect after verified cessation supports persistence in this system; it does not establish intrinsic memory. A reception contrast with valid compartment specificity supports stromal dependence. Elimination by residual-input or survival accounting favors those rivals. |
| Stop/limit | Hold autonomous-memory or irreversible-fate claims without the discriminating evidence. Inseparable exposure and reception changes cannot answer either hypothesis. |

**Novelty/precision:** compartment attribution and persistence beyond measured
cessation are the increment; generic reversibility is redundant. Actual time
windows, meaningful M differences and between-unit variance remain unknown.
**Access:** published primary organoids/tracing demonstrate platforms, not
available materials. A reporter-only model can qualify cessation/engagement
but cannot answer the mature-output questions.
