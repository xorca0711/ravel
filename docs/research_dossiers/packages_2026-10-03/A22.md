# A22 — chemokine output beyond source amount and selection

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A22.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Nabhan 2026, identity/chemokine screen](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2606113123)
and [Cardoso 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10399-6)
already establish epithelial/stromal context.
**Repository:** [current extension](../../../RQ_Specified/A22_epithelial_identity_niche_response/reports/identity_amount_v2/RESULTS.md)
retains weak prediction, target-omission sensitivity and failed whole-plate
transport; the [source map audit](../followup_2026-10-03/METADATA.md)
does not resolve preparation/donor independence. C3 is not a stable output proxy.

**Hypothesis/decision:** Loss of NKX2-1-dependent AT2 identity may lower actual fibroblast chemokine output within comparable fibroblast states beyond a change in viable epithelial source amount. The RNA direction is already published. The proposed extension asks whether it reaches extracellular protein output; the primary chemokine protein and collection/recovery definition have not been nominated, and broad AT2-score generalization failed.

**Current novelty boundary:** Nabhan already reports NKX2-1-dependent fibroblast chemokine transcripts and niche changes. The possible contribution is source-amount/selection-qualified protein output, not rediscovery of the transcript effect or an assumed immune-recruitment function. [Source comparison](../NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a22). Existing design rows are conditional; the specificity holds above must be resolved before treating them as an experiment specification.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Defined primary epithelial sources and independently identified fibroblast states from mapped donors/preparations. Common mixtures and split wells stay nested. |
| Contrast/timing | Record epithelial identity and amount before recipient output, with an amount comparison distinguishable from identity change. Receptor/input and fibroblast state at baseline and follow-up constrain induction-versus-selection. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Total extracellular output of one prospectively selected source-supported chemokine per recorded starting recipient population. Report output per viable fibroblast and epithelial amount separately; collection/recovery validation is needed for secretion-rate language. |
| Controls/validity | Source-free/recipient-only, identity-preserving amount reference, vehicle and assay-positive controls; independent identity markers, viable source/recipient counts, fibroblast-state abundance, protein recovery and dynamic range. Additional identity perturbations test generality without replacing a failed primary. |
| Competing results/action | Within-state output change at comparable source amount supports induction. Amount-only tracking or altered fibroblast mixture favors those rivals; an effect restricted to NKX21 stays target-specific. |
| Stop/limit | Hold secretion/recruitment claims without protein output or function. Spatial sample blocks need animal/region/treatment joins; screen/spatial treatment discrepancies remain unresolved. |

**Novelty/precision:** general identity-to-niche communication is published;
actual output attribution and independent preparation transport are the increment.
The chemokine, meaningful output scale and unit variance are not yet qualified;
freeze one primary and control any panel. **Access:** primary paired preparations,
quantitative protein assays and independent counts are unconfirmed.
