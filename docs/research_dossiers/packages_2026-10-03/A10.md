# A10 — independent preparation transport of growth information

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A10.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Nabhan 2026, screen and SI methods](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2606113123)
measures organoid growth and concurrent expression.
**Repository:** [follow-up result](../../../RQ_Specified/A10_organoid_growth_outcome/reports/FOLLOWUP_RESULTS.md)
retains negative absolute held-out R-squared on three of four plates.
The [newer methods review](../capabilities_2026-10-03/METHODS.md) supersedes its
historical supplement-access limit but does not supply preparation identities.

**Hypothesis/decision:** an early epithelial programme adds transportable
information about later area beyond initial size in independent preparations.
Rival: concurrent growth state, target/plate confounding or segmentation artifacts.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Primary AT2–fibroblast organoids from identified epithelial preparations and fibroblast donors; wells and images nested. Targets require supported overlap between preparations. |
| Contrast/timing | For forecasting, measure predictor before the future growth interval. Existing day-14 RNA/area is concurrent. Day-7 area is post-perturbation: useful as a forecasting baseline, not automatically a pretreatment causal confounder. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Improvement over the fixed initial-size model in held-out preparation error for later measured area/growth on a frozen scale. Also report absolute error and R-squared, not relative improvement alone. Images/wells are not the biological sample denominator. |
| Controls/validity | Blinded segmentation/calibration, size reference, independent cell counts and viability, known target/plate/preparation map, train-only transformations and target-aware whole-preparation splits. Include declared source controls without treating them as independent donors. |
| Competing results/action | Stable absolute and incremental performance supports prediction within the tested transport domain. Failure across preparations or targets favors the rival. A descriptive fit does not establish programme causality. |
| Stop/limit | Hold forecasting without early predictor acquisition and preparation joins; retain the valid concurrent result. Area is not mature output, epithelial number or repair function. |

**Novelty/precision:** the increment is robust transport, not reanalysis of a
published growth screen. Define useful prediction error, between-preparation
variance and validation-unit needs before sample size. One frozen programme
block versus baseline is primary; component searches are secondary.
**Access:** source maps or a compatible independently replicated primary platform
are unconfirmed.
