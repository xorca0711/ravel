# A15 — activation of latent ligand versus supply

Proposed conditional package. [Dossier](../A15.md) · [Common terms](README.md).

**Published:** [Munger 1999](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0092-8674(00)80545-0)
establishes integrin-mediated latent TGF-beta activation.
**Repository:** read the [interpretation correction](../../../RQ_Specified/A15_epithelial_integrin_tgfb_activation/reports/INTERPRETATION_AUDIT_2026-09-27.md)
and [normalization erratum](../../../RQ_Specified/A15_epithelial_integrin_tgfb_activation/reports/NORMALIZATION_ERRATUM_2026-09-28.md).
The parent test remains blocked; epithelial enrichment is not purity and
nonseparating panels do not exclude epithelial mediation.

**Hypothesis/decision:** epithelial activation context changes active TGF-beta
and fibroblast consequence at comparable latent supply. Rival: supply, matrix,
another activator or a parallel EGFR-ligand route accounts for the response.

| Design element | Conditional specification |
|---|---|
| Population/unit | Defined epithelial source and fibroblast recipient preparations with recorded matrix/input context and independent donor/animal parents. |
| Contrast/timing | Compare epithelial activation context with a separately interpretable ligand-supply contrast. Measure latent/active ligand before downstream recipient consequence; establish starting epithelial state before perturbation. |
| Primary outcome/denominator | Change in active-ligand-dependent recipient response per t0 recipient input at comparable latent supply. Active and total ligand are separately calibrated measurements, not two interchangeable RNA panels. |
| Controls/validity | Validated active-ligand positive and latent/no-activation references, source-free/recipient-only, vehicle and route-specific engagement controls; matrix, EGFR-input and viability accounting. An active-ligand bypass is informative about the route only if recipient competence is verified. |
| Competing results/action | Altered active ligand plus attributable recipient consequence supports context-specific activation. Supply-only tracking favors supply; parallel activation/EGFR effects permit mixed routes. A null epithelial RNA panel cannot decide mediation. |
| Stop/limit | Hold without functional ligand measurement, preparation independence and a distinguishable route contrast. Whole-lung collagen response is not integrin occupancy or isolated fibroblast activity. |

**Novelty/precision:** the context-specific attribution is the increment, not
rediscovery of alpha-v-beta-6 biology. Meaningful active-ligand/recipient scale
and unit variance remain unknown. Freeze one primary route contrast and a
declared secondary family; do not turn exploratory controls into a positive
primary. **Access:** compatible source, matrix and validated active/latent
assays are unconfirmed; RNA-only models cannot substitute.
