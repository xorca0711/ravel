positive developmental-component association to junction/polarity-associated RNA

argument and next decision, not human acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does adult alveolar repair reuse part of a developmental epithelial programme,
and what does the observed recurrence mean? The fixed component first makes
that broad purpose measurable. Its positive aggregate then raises a descriptive
process question: is recruitment distributed across junction/polarity and matrix
features, concentrated in signaling context, or heterogeneous across members?
These patterns may coexist. Productive repair versus prolonged transition is the
next biological uncertainty, not an outcome already supplied by the RNA score.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

The fixed-component contrast is supported in the original 24 mice. The newer
junction/polarity interpretation is an **exploratory process-context lead** from
an outcome-exposed examination of those same measurements. Functional annotations
help describe the members; they are not independent evidence that the selected
process is active or unusually coherent relative to an appropriate alternative.
The complete member display, mixed matrix/signaling findings and negative
original transport and regulator contributions restrict a uniform programme claim.

This warrants a more specific question about where that RNA context occurs. It
does not yet choose junction assembly, beneficial repair or prolonged transition
as the explanation, nor justify calling the pattern random noise. No automatic
target priority follows from the annotation label or the selected display.

## Unresolved gap

## Working hypothesis

Adult alveolar repair recruits the exact independently sourced developmental epithelial component beyond the identity, stress and labeling genes excluded by the frozen shared-component contract. Its association in 24 mice motivates independent replication of that same component. Whether it performs a developmental function during repair is a separate, untested biological question.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Fixed-component replication; mapping held. Developmental-programme reuse is established broadly. The current contribution is reproducibility of a fixed measurement; replication is useful without being a new mechanism. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a5). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Shared plasticity, composition or annotation choices can produce overlap without
a functionally reused developmental module. For the current descriptive result,
cross-sectional state selection and sampled-day mixture are especially relevant;
broad or cross-tissue functional annotations can make heterogeneous RNA changes
look like an assembled process. Fixed identity/control exclusions weaken the
specified gene-list explanations but do not exclude unlisted stress programmes,
ambient RNA or label/composition effects.

Architecture-associated RNA and signaling context can coexist. Even correctly
localized architecture could accompany prolonged or maladaptive remodeling;
beneficial repair is not the default explanation for positive RNA recruitment.

## Discriminating outcomes

For the proposed process comparison, concordant junction-protein localization and
laminin/Col4a5 material in transitional epithelial regions would support an
architecture-associated state. Absent or mislocalized protein would weaken that
interpretation. Persistence without maturation would weaken a productive-repair
reading. An RNA-only difference leaves those links unresolved, and localization
alone would not establish a causal repair effect.

For the separate replication branch, agreement in independently source-qualified
mice supports transport of the unchanged component; reversal or loss under the
fixed comparison weakens it, and inadequate precision remains unresolved. Missing
source mapping prevents a test and is not a negative biological replication.
No retuned component, inferred animal identity or relaxed threshold follows.

## Next investigation

The existing RNA branch has reached a source-grounded nomination decision.
All-member annotation is complete; another annotation or score sweep is not an
automatic next step. The proposed independent localization/state comparison now
has a reason: it can test the unmeasured link from nominated RNA features to
structure and distinguish productive from persistent-transition context. Its
source/model, independent units, localization endpoint and state/outcome linkage
must be qualified before any new analysis or experiment. This pilot does not
launch that work.

## Experimental bridge

An independently qualified localization/state comparison could address the
nominated architecture-associated context without first claiming repair function.
Testing a causal role in productive repair would require a separately justified
functional endpoint and intervention that distinguish the intended effect from
survival or population selection. No intervention, effect margin, sample size or
laboratory-ready protocol is selected here. Historical conditional packages are
planning records, not automatically active commitments.

## Interpretation boundary

The measured endpoint is RNA detection. It establishes neither protein assembly,
deposited matrix, pathway or transport activity, developmental ancestry, a
maturation trajectory nor beneficial repair. The original fixed effect, exposed
member interpretation, external replication proposal and functional proposal have
different evidential scopes. Reused source data never become independent animals.

## Decision and maturity

The completed and reviewed descriptive evidence narrows interpretation while
retaining negative regulator/transport findings and mixed matrix/signaling
members. It supports a next scientific question, not a human retain/reject decision
or claim promotion. Independent injury replication remains mapping-held; the
localization/state discriminator is proposed and unexecuted. The registered
hypothesis and illustration are preserved.

Novelty is not certified by a changed label or a bounded search. Before a new
confirmatory comparison, establish the relevant primary precedent, endpoint
validity, biological-unit variance, meaningful effect and model feasibility.
Current computational feasibility does not imply laboratory access.
