Does adult alveolar repair reuse part of a developmental epithelial programme?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which developmental epithelial component is recruited during adult repair, and what function does that recruitment explain?

## Established knowledge

Developmental and injury-associated programmes can share genes without implying developmental ancestry or successful regeneration.

## Repository evidence

The external Guo developmental signature was positive across 24 mice and survived identity/stress exclusions. GSE303646 contains 56 libraries from 55 mice, but annotation remains unresolved and it is not completed replication.

## Unresolved gap

The observed partial recruitment has not been connected to an independent repair function or shown to exceed generic plasticity under a qualified replication design.

## Working hypothesis

Adult alveolar repair recruits the exact independently sourced developmental epithelial component beyond the identity, stress and labeling genes excluded by the frozen shared-component contract. Its association in 24 mice motivates independent replication of that same component. Whether it performs a developmental function during repair is a separate, untested biological question.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Fixed-component replication; mapping held. Developmental-programme reuse is established broadly. The current contribution is reproducibility of a fixed measurement; replication is useful without being a new mechanism. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a5). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Overlap reflects shared plasticity, composition or annotation choices rather than a functionally reused developmental module.

## Discriminating outcomes

Reproduction in independent, source-qualified animals supports transport of the fixed component. Failure or loss after valid state/identity comparison weakens that generalization. Functional reuse requires a separately justified endpoint and intervention; gene overlap alone cannot supply it.

## Next investigation

Resolve the replication annotation gate and document sample overlap. Keep the frozen component; select any future functional endpoint before inspecting its relationship.

## Experimental bridge

Compare component-positive and comparable component-negative cells using lineage and function appropriate to the nominated process. Any causal manipulation must distinguish survival from the specific repair function.

## Interpretation boundary

Transcriptional reuse establishes neither developmental ancestry nor a beneficial role.

## Decision and maturity

A bounded observational result exists. Functional meaning and independent replication remain development tasks, not automatic consequences of the positive score.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
