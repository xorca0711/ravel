# A28: Separately regulated regulatory and effector competence in Th17 cells

Registered 5 October 2026 as **proposed**, derived from the executed Wp
reproduction ladder, branch cards, metadata phenotypes and extension analyses
of Wang et al. 2025. The derivation supplied a bounded scientific review; human
retain/reject and scientific acceptance remain unrecorded. Registration is not
a claim of novelty, functional validation or permission to execute an
unfinished numerical design.

[Workspace](../../RQ_Specified/A28_th17_arm_asymmetry/README.md) · [Plan](../../RQ_Specified/A28_th17_arm_asymmetry/PLAN.md) · [Derivation](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/rq_derivation/Q1_arm_asymmetry.md).

## Biological problem

The Th17 pathogenicity score in wide use is a difference: a pro-inflammatory
gene module minus a pro-regulatory one. When that difference moves, it is
reported as a change in pathogenicity. But a difference can move by either
term, and whether a cell that lost its regulatory programme is the same cell as
one that gained an effector programme is a question about biology, not about
arithmetic. If the two competences are separately regulated, perturbations that
produce identical score changes can produce functionally different cells.

## Established knowledge

The modules are Gaublomme et al. 2015, *Cell* 163:1400
(doi:10.1016/j.cell.2015.11.009), which derived them from single-cell profiling
of Th17 cells in vitro and in EAE. Th17-lineage plasticity in both directions
is established: helminth exposure shifts IL-17-fate-reporter cells toward
Tr1/Treg-like function (doi:10.3389/fimmu.2026.1767639), and Notch3-DLL1
signalling subverts Treg cells into Th17 cells in MS and EAE
(doi:10.1016/j.immuni.2025.09.007). That Th17 cells occupy a spectrum between
regulatory and inflammatory function is therefore a **premise**, not a finding
claimed here. What is not established is whether the two arms of this
particular score move independently under different perturbation classes.

## Repository evidence

Three executed Wp results, all with verified receipts. At 1 mM versus 25 mM
glucose the score rises in 4 of 4 animal-paired comparisons (+0.133 and +0.109
in Th17n) and does so **through the pro-regulatory arm falling**; the
pro-inflammatory arm does not consistently rise across the two animals. Across 15,830 cells the two arms are
anti-correlated at Spearman −0.389 to −0.503, and scoring the pro-inflammatory
arm alone makes the glucose effect sign-inconsistent across the two animals
(−0.053 and +0.020). In ten paired human donors, corrected R4 v3 gives an inflammatory CSF-minus-blood median of +0.061825 (10/10 positive) and a regulatory median of +0.006024 (6/10 positive; nominal paired p=0.232422). M3 v2 uses a slightly different finite-gene universe: its inflammatory median is +0.060759 (3/1,000 null draws as extreme), while regulatory authors/all-gene null fractions are 0.292/0.884. These restricted size-only nulls do not establish genome-wide specificity or regulatory equivalence.

## Unresolved gap

The two best-supported arm asymmetries in the package point in opposite arms,
under a nutrient perturbation in mouse culture and a compartment contrast in
human disease. Deposited RNA cannot distinguish separately regulated
competences from one latent axis, because both arms are computed from the same
transcriptome and the modules were built to be anti-correlated. The mouse
evidence rests on two animals; no independent nutrient dataset qualified.

## Working hypothesis

Regulatory and effector competence are separately regulated within the same Th17 cell rather than two ends of one axis, so a perturbation can remove one without adding the other. Nutrient restriction is predicted to lower per-cell Foxp3 and CTLA4 protein with IL-17 unchanged, and a pathogenic cytokine condition to raise IL-17 with Foxp3 and CTLA4 unchanged. A single latent axis predicts that both perturbations move both proteins reciprocally. No causal claim about which competence is upstream is implied.

## Competing explanations

One latent axis, where the one-sided move is a property of gene lists built
from opposing polarisation conditions rather than of cells. Composition rather
than state: the Wp glucose decomposition found the composition term positive in
both animals under all four labelling and stratification combinations while the
within-state term disagreed in sign and its stop rule fired as inconclusive.
Proliferation, since low glucose slows division and cycle phase was regressed
out of the published latent space. For the human leg, local activation, which
exceeds its own matched null in the same direction and correlates with every
module at Spearman −0.61 to −0.87 in blood; and a CSF-enriched Th17-lineage
subset (doi:10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106324), which would raise an effector module
through composition.

## Discriminating outcomes

A per-cell joint protein readout in Th17n culture under 1 mM against 25 mM
glucose and under pathogenic cytokines, co-staining Foxp3, CTLA4, IL-17A and
IL-17F with a division tracker, mouse as unit. The endpoint is the joint
distribution, not two marginal means: whether the cells losing Foxp3 are the
cells gaining IL-17. Foxp3 and CTLA4 falling with IL-17 flat under restriction,
and the converse under pathogenic cytokines, supports separate regulation.
Reciprocal movement under both supports one axis. Disjoint cell populations
indicate composition. Loss of the effects under matched division indicates the
proliferation confound.

## Next investigation

No further analysis of the deposited RNA is warranted, because every available
readout is another summary of the transcriptome in which the arms are
anti-correlated by construction. The useful next step is the protein
experiment. An interim option is to score the arms separately in an independent
Th17 nutrient dataset; the Wp external screen found 50 candidates deferred to
record inspection and none admissible, so that route is open but unavailable.

## Experimental bridge

The named culture experiment with intracellular protein staining, a division
tracker and the mouse as unit. An IL-17 fate reporter would strengthen it by
identifying cells that previously transcribed IL-17, which is how Th17-lineage
plasticity is demonstrated in the current literature. Nothing in the score, in
either arm, is secretion, suppressive function or fate; those require
functional assays this question does not substitute for.

## Interpretation boundary

RNA module scores are not protein, not cytokine secretion and not regulatory
function. The anti-correlation of the two arms is partly a property of how the
modules were derived, so no analysis of the same transcriptome can adjudicate
this. The mouse evidence is two animals and the human evidence ten donors; both
are precision-limited, and a failure to separate the hypotheses would be
uninformative rather than evidence for one axis. Reanalysis of the source
deposits is one evidence lineage with the source paper, never independent
replication.

## Decision and maturity

Registered as a proposed model-discrimination question whose decisive
measurement is a wet experiment this project does not currently hold. Human
acceptance, an effect margin and any functional claim remain pending. No effect
margin is nominated, because the package holds two mice and no independent
dataset from which one could honestly be derived.
