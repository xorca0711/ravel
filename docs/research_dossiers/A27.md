## Biological problem

In spleen CD8 cells, does the older repertoire concentration primarily reflect redistribution among differentiation states, or a change in concentration within comparable states under qualified receptor ascertainment?

## Established knowledge

Ahmed et al. 2009 (doi:10.4049/jimmunol.182.2.784) show age-associated clonal changes within naive CD8 cells; Mogilenko et al. 2021 establish clonal GZMK-positive ageing-associated CD8 populations. The broad within-state phenomenon is not novel. A27 is a bounded quantitative attribution/replication question in specified state and tissue contexts.

## Repository evidence

The Nb5 broad CD8 restriction retains a higher older spleen mean at a conditional depth of only five reconstructed cells per mouse, with 4/3/4 mice at 3/18/24 months. CD4 has no 24-versus-3 difference; its 18-month result depends on one mouse. Neither outcome resolves finer states or receptor recovery.

## Unresolved gap

Fine differentiation states, their independent definition, receptor recovery denominators and adequate mouse-level support remain unqualified. The common-depth eligibility minimum is not a power calculation. GSE145562 pools mice and cannot supply individual replicates without recovered identity.

## Working hypothesis

For specified CD8 states, a measurable component of the spleen age contrast remains within states after composition and receptor ascertainment are accounted for; an alternative is that redistribution accounts for most of the observed difference. Both may contribute. No causal percentage of ageing explained is implied.

## Competing explanations

Age-dependent abundance of already concentrated states, annotation shifts, differential receptor reconstruction and sparse sampling produce the apparent within-CD8 contrast without a reproducible within-state change.

## Discriminating outcomes

Estimate state-specific repeated-clone-cell fractions on a common sampling scale and a declared fixed-composition contrast, retaining the aggregate result. If the standardized contrast attenuates and state weights explain the pattern, redistribution is favored. Persistent supported state contrasts motivate quantitative replication; uncertain sparse results remain inconclusive.

## Next investigation

Qualify finer labels without using clone size or age-selected genes, retain all CD8 and receptor-recovered denominators, and identify independent animals in an external source. Fix clone definition, state set, standardization and sampling support in a new contract; do not relax failed strata after seeing effects.

## Experimental bridge

A later association with an independently measured repertoire-function endpoint could extend this question, but response breadth, antigen specificity and immune impairment are not measured here. Animal is the biological unit; source clones and cell resamples do not add animals.

## Interpretation boundary

RNA state, clone repetition and diversity are distinct from immune function, proliferation rate or disease risk. Existing evidence and internal deletions are not independent validation. Literature precedent prevents marketing a familiar within-state finding as a new mechanism.

## Decision and maturity

Registered as a proposed descriptive attribution/replication question with source and state-definition work remaining. Scientific value can include reproducible measurement without mechanistic novelty. Human acceptance, precision and functional claims remain pending.
