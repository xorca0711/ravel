# A0: research development dossier

Is a conserved programme reused across epithelial transitions, and could it modulate fate?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which part of the lung injury-associated programme distinguishes later productive epithelial maturation from a persistent intermediate state?

## Established knowledge

Injury-associated transcription can recur across contexts without implying a shared eventual fate. The canonical card records the source studies and the fixed discovery signature.

## Repository evidence

The frozen 50-gene lung discovery programme did not transfer as a mature intestinal endpoint. That stop is evidence against the tested universal interpretation, not a reason to refit the signature.

## Unresolved gap

There is no eligible linked early-state and later mature-outcome comparison establishing which component forecasts productive lung repair.

## Working hypothesis

A lung-context component, separable from generic stress and transition, is associated with later mature epithelial output.

## Competing explanations

The programme reflects injury severity or cell mixture; its apparent maturation association follows sampling time rather than regenerative competence.

## Discriminating outcomes

A prospective early measurement should distinguish later mature output within comparable starting populations. An association disappearing after baseline state and injury burden are accounted for favors the rivals.

## Next investigation

Qualify a source with linked independent biological units, early measurements and later outcome. Stop at a feasibility report if the linkage is absent; do not retrain on the failed transfer endpoint.

## Experimental bridge

Compare matched starting populations across independent preparations and track mature descendant yield and retained progenitors separately from survival and growth. The lab must establish a valid lineage and functional endpoint.

## Interpretation boundary

Cross-context score recurrence is neither a universal regeneration programme nor a causal repair mechanism.

## Decision and maturity

Development candidate; the original transfer claim remains stopped. Source qualification precedes new prediction or experimental commitment.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
