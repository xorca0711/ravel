# A0: research development dossier

Is a conserved programme reused across epithelial transitions, and could it modulate fate?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

What boundary does the failed lung-to-intestine transfer place on the proposed conserved epithelial transition programme?

## Established knowledge

Published lung injury studies establish transitional populations preceding mature recovery in that setting. Shared transcription across tissues does not establish a conserved fate-modulating mechanism.

## Repository evidence

The frozen 50-gene lung programme failed the specified intestinal intermediate-minus-mature contrast: the three-mouse median was negative and only one mouse was positive. This is a failed operational transfer, not a direct experiment on conserved function. The original stop remains in force.

## Unresolved gap

The existing transfer test cannot distinguish absence of a conserved function from an invalid cross-tissue state or endpoint correspondence. Neither possibility licenses post hoc signature rescue.

## Working hypothesis

The frozen programme has a restricted context of expression rather than the prespecified cross-tissue intermediate-state ordering. This is an interpretation to examine, not a newly confirmed mechanism.

## Competing explanations

The target populations are not comparable biological transitions; sampling time or coverage obscures the intended contrast; alternatively the proposed cross-tissue specificity is absent.

## Discriminating outcomes

Independent evidence establishing the source and target state correspondence could determine whether the negative test addresses the intended conservation question. Without that correspondence, the defensible conclusion remains failure of this operational transfer. A different signature or endpoint requires an explicit new hypothesis and amendment.

## Next investigation

Audit the biological mapping and endpoint validity in the existing source-recovery record. Record whether any independent rationale warrants reopening; do not refit the failed target or automatically restart analysis. Lung early-state-to-later-output prediction belongs under A8.

## Experimental bridge

A future conservation claim would require comparable, independently defined transitions in both contexts and a shared functional perturbation outcome. No shared causal component or feasible paired model is established here, so a wet experiment is not yet justified by this pilot.

## Interpretation boundary

The negative transfer restricts this signature and contrast; it neither proves absence of all conserved epithelial biology nor supplies a lung maturation predictor.

## Decision and maturity

Original transfer branch stopped; question retained for source and construct review. Reopening requires independent biological justification, a distinct decision and a prospective amendment. No new signature, result or claim grade is accepted.
