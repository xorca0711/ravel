Is a conserved programme reused across epithelial transitions, and could it modulate fate?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

What can be shared across epithelial transitions, and what boundary does the failed frozen lung-to-intestine transfer place on that proposal?

## Established knowledge

Published lung injury studies establish transitional populations preceding mature recovery in that setting. Shared transcription across tissues does not establish a conserved fate-modulating mechanism.

## Repository evidence

The frozen 50-gene lung programme passed its discovery comparison but failed the specified intestinal intermediate-versus-mature ordering in three mice; only one mouse had the intended positive direction. The intermediate-versus-stem result does not rescue the failed mature-endpoint contrast. The prespecified stop pruned later specificity tests.

Discovery used nine injured-lung mice and eleven early human-airway donors; its favorable contrasts are selected estimates, not independent confirmation. Prior Strunz/A5 outcomes were exposed. The 50-gene membership was fixed before scoring the three intestinal mice. The endpoint is a within-cell percentile-rank RNA score averaged by biological unit/state, not fate or function; species, assay, state mapping and untested lung specificity remain limits.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A0_conserved_epithelial_transition_program/reports/PILOT_V1_RESULTS.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

The failed RNA ordering does not distinguish a tissue-specific signature from noncomparable states, timing, species or assay effects; conserved function was not measured. None of these explanations is selected by the negative result.

## Working hypothesis

Biological aim: adult epithelial repair recruits part of a transition programme also used in other epithelial contexts. The frozen 50-gene lung-to-intestine test failed its intended ordering; the remaining task is to determine whether independently justified source and target states are comparable. No replacement signature or conserved fate-control mechanism is proposed by that failure.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Stopped transfer; biological mechanism unspecified. Conserved transition biology is published. This remains construct qualification and a failed-transfer boundary, not a new mechanism or a positive conservation result. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a0). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Noncomparable transitions, sampling/coverage and absence of the proposed specificity remain alternatives. Positive lung discovery does not independently validate intestinal state mapping.

## Discriminating outcomes

Independent support for a comparable source/target transition would justify a newly specified mapping question. Lack of that support leaves the original transfer stopped. Post hoc gene or label changes cannot convert the failed frozen ordering into successful transfer.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Retain the stopped transfer. Review the source-recovery evidence for an independent biological rationale for a different mapping question. Without one, do not tune the signature again. With one, specify a new exposed comparison and informative outcomes separately; the original failure remains unchanged.

## Experimental bridge

A future RNA-conservation comparison needs comparable, independently defined transitions and an appropriate fixed RNA endpoint. Causal fate modulation is a separate, stronger question requiring relevant perturbation/function evidence. A functional experiment is not a prerequisite for an informative RNA comparison, and this failed pilot does not qualify a causal component or paired functional model.

## Interpretation boundary

Informative failed generalization. The result restricts this signature, population mapping and endpoint contrast, not all conserved repair biology. Selecting a replacement gene set using the failed target would create a new exposed question rather than rescue the frozen transfer.
