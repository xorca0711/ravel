Does CD177 identify a priming phenotype within comparable mutant cells?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does CD177 identify a useful prospective epithelial response context, or merely a concurrent transcriptional neighborhood?

## Established knowledge

A marker-associated mutant state may reflect current state, contamination or composition. Priming requires a subsequent response, not only a baseline association.

## Repository evidence

Matching attenuates the fixed four-gene mean log1p(full-library CP10k) score contrast from 1.256 to 0.410 and from 1.332 to 0.315 in the two libraries, while substantial imbalance remains. Independent animal identities, a linked later response and complete exclusion of contamination are not available. This was an amended computation on fully exposed data. Matching uses concurrent RNA coordinates, not an independently measured pre-intervention starting state; the registered hypothesis's historical phrase "starting-state matching" must be read with that limit. Excluding the 660 direct grouping/outcome genes does not eliminate correlated-feature overadjustment, shared normalization or detection-dependent selection. Matching does not convert these libraries into independent animal-level evidence.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A16_cd177_state_attribution/correction_20260928/reports/CORRECTED_C1_REPORT.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

Source/protein attribution, qualified biological units and a specified later response remain unlinked. Current marker-positive RNA cannot establish prospective competence.

## Working hypothesis

Epithelial CD177 protein may identify a priming phenotype within comparable mutant transitional cells that differs in a later response. It is a candidate marker, not a proposed causal fate regulator. Source/protein attribution and one specific later response still need qualification; the corrected RNA comparison attenuated after improved starting-state matching.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Protein attribution and later endpoint required. State markers and mutant epithelial heterogeneity are precedents. A prospective, source-validated marker contribution is possible, but the missing response prevents a complete novelty judgment. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a16). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Cell-state composition, ambient/doublet contamination, remaining imbalance and concurrent expression can explain the neighborhood pattern without intrinsic priming.

## Discriminating outcomes

Independently attributed marker status associated with a specified later response could support a prospective marker. Loss of that relation with source/context qualification would limit it. Neither result requires treating the marker as causal or establishes a fate mechanism.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Keep the matched and unmatched observations together. Independently attributed CD177 status linked to a later response could support a prospective marker claim; loss of that relationship under source/context qualification would restrict it. A causal perturbation claim is a different question and is not required merely to evaluate a marker.

## Experimental bridge

A future prospective-marker comparison should link baseline marker attribution to a specified later response across independent preparations and account for survival. Descendant fate is a separate endpoint only if that is the intended claim.

## Interpretation boundary

A diagnostic restriction on the marker interpretation. The attenuation shows sensitivity to the measured context but neither proves all of the signal is confounded nor establishes an intrinsic programme. No priming mechanism or later fate prediction has been selected.
