Every package must state: precise hypothesis; strongest rival; contribution over
the nearest primary precedent; population and starting state; intervention or
observation contrast; primary outcome and denominator; expected outcomes under
both explanations; positive/negative/vehicle and viability controls as relevant;
biological unit and nesting; timing and lineage linkage; assay validity;
multiplicity; effect/precision rationale; stop/amendment rule; inference limit;
published platform reference and actual-access status. A direction-neutral
unknown is preferable to a made-up threshold, mediator or sample size.

For mature AT1 output, specify lineage-linked identity, morphology and the actual
validated functional endpoint separately. Absolute output per starting population
and survivor-conditioned efficiency estimate different things; report survival
instead of automatically adjusting away treatment-mediated depletion. A19 also
needs a distinct retained-reserve measure. For cell communication, specify
source output, recipient engagement and recipient consequence separately. For
metabolism, distinguish RNA prediction, metabolite abundance and flux.

Meaningful effects are set from the biological decision and measurement scale,
not the strongest observed result. Precision requires variance among actual
animals/donors/preparations and the intended pairing/nesting; adding cells or
technical wells does not provide that variance. No numeric effect margin or
sample size has been supplied for these unqualified experiments.
