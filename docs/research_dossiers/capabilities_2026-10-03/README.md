## What the new reading changes

1. Receptor reporters, organoid growth and lineage-resolved mature output answer
   different questions. A19 needs mature AT1 output and retained AT2 reserve;
   A20 needs support of a competent early pool and subsequent output. Neither
   endpoint is supplied by an area measurement or Wnt reporter alone.
2. Cardoso's mutant epithelial–fibroblast–immune circuit and England's mutant
   clone/reprogramming results are substantive precedents. A2/A13/A18/A22 must
   identify what their proposed discriminator adds to those published circuits.
   The existing negative A13 proxy result still nominates no mediator.
3. Yadav supplies direct metabolic and tissue assays, while its mouse/human
   source-cell difference argues against casually transferring a macrophage
   mechanism to human IPF. Wagner's documented contribution to that paper was
   spatial analysis; the collaborator's wet platforms are not his lab's inventory.
4. Developmental, inflammatory and metabolic expression programmes remain
   measurements to qualify. Saxton's cell-line signaling and intestinal organoids,
   DuPage's Treg/tumor models, and cross-field regeneration studies illustrate
   useful discriminators without automatically validating them in adult lung.
