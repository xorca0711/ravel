## Biological problem

Which account can explain the source's PGAM-related phenotype: a PEP-dependent route, altered biosynthetic demand, stress, or a combination? The local analyses describe relative RNA and correct a computational association; they have not selected one mechanism. The strongly directional registered hypothesis remains a proposal, not the conclusion of the current screen.

## Established knowledge

The [local primary-source review](../../RQ_Specified/A29_pgam_effector_mechanism/LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md#published-starting-point) records published PEP/JunB-related, stress and metabolic-context mechanisms, together with contrary findings in other T-cell settings. These are distinct premises, not a single established pathway in the source's preparation. The source's Fig. 1E isotope-label measurement is a labeling fraction, not an absolute metabolite pool; its lack of a detected PEP change is not equivalence or a calibrated pool result.

## Repository evidence

The [7 October correction](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/CORRECTIONS_2026-10-07.md) controls the Compass reading. Corrected forward PGAM consistency has a **positive association, rho +0.291248 and BH q 0.180810** under substituted inputs. It does not reproduce the paper's negative direction or identify PGAM as distinctive; the micropools reuse source animals and are not independent biological replicates. The old negative-penalty interpretation is historical.

The [bulk reproduction](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/R3_RESULTS.md) uses deposited TPM with **library as the only verifiable unit**: animal/culture identities are absent. The focal Th17n/EGCG division-1 contrast has five libraries per arm. Gates and drug/cell contexts are not interchangeable. The [E3 result](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/E3_RESULTS.md) shows relative effector-set enrichment (+0.544) and lower ISR-set transcripts (-0.491) against an expression-decile-matched null; zero of 1,000 draws as extreme is not zero probability or animal-level inference.

The two-gene-overlap sensitivity retains the ISR RNA direction. It does not measure stress activity, ATF4 protein/translation or absolute per-cell effector output. Expression matching does not remove TPM composition ambiguity. Published isotope fractions do not provide pools or metabolic flux, and the source's phenotype is not independently validated by reanalysis of its own libraries.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

**Rationale verdict: descriptive relative-RNA context and a corrected negative/diagnostic boundary; PEP-mechanism selection is unsupported.** Lower biosynthetic/ISR transcripts with higher relative effector RNA does not exclude stress or establish demand relief. Correcting the Compass sign removes a previously claimed corroboration rather than selecting its opposite mechanism. A new name for the same RNA pattern adds no causal evidence.

Question derivation followed exposed outcomes; the reduced-overlap sensitivity was frozen before its new estimates while reusing exposed full-set values. Declared gene sets, division gates, library-only units, finite null draws, drug specificity and unknown absolute RNA content constrain interpretation. A PEP pool decrease would address one prediction but cannot uniquely distinguish all routes, and a supplementation response would not be unique proof. None of the uncertain alternatives is demonstrated to be noise.

## Unresolved gap

Absolute pool abundance, discriminating protein/signaling activity and independently qualified biological units are missing. The current data do not resolve stress, demand, drug-specific effects and RNA-content composition or establish a complete feasible comparison.

The registered text below remains unchanged as a proposal; current findings do not
make its predicted mechanism or effect an accepted explanation.

## Working hypothesis

PGAM restriction raises Th17 effector output by lowering the phosphoenolpyruvate pool and relieving PEP-mediated inhibition of the JunB/BATF/IRF4 complex, rather than by inducing a stress response; relief of biosynthetic and proliferative demand is a third, separable possibility. The PEP route predicts that the PEP pool falls under PGAM inhibition and that PEP supplementation abolishes the effector gain; the demand route predicts that matched growth slowing without PGAM inhibition reproduces it. No mechanism here is proposed as new, and a transcript-level observation cannot establish any of them.

## Competing explanations

Stress outside the scored transcript set, biosynthetic demand, division-related dilution, RNA composition and drug effects remain alternatives. A stable bulk pool, if precisely established in the relevant context, would challenge that bulk-pool prediction without excluding transient or compartment-specific changes.

## Discriminating outcomes

A calibrated pool decrease would support one prediction of the proposed PEP account, without selecting its causal contribution. Appropriate independent evidence could favor stress or demand, possibly together. A non-significant label-fraction contrast or an RNA module cannot establish equivalent pool size or absent activity. The comparison must be chosen for the inference it can actually resolve.

## Next investigation

Retain the corrected Compass and relative-RNA results, adverse directions and composition limits. Qualify evidence that separates the intended candidate accounts before any new calculation or experiment. The current synthesis selects no target priority or preferred route; another score would need a distinct informative comparison.

## Experimental bridge

Actual model and preparation identities determine biological units and whether observations are matched. A qualified comparison needs interpretable perturbations and endpoints for its chosen claim; no full factorial design, common culture, laboratory access or cost assumption is established here.

## Interpretation boundary

Library-level TPM and gene-set null draws are not independent animals, secretion or function. Isotope-label fractions are not pools or flux; ISR RNA is not stress activity. The published mechanisms remain competing premises, and the current evidence selects none.

## Biological problem

Inhibiting one step of glycolysis — the phosphoglycerate mutase reaction, 3PG
to 2PG — increases Th17 effector output, while inhibiting neighbouring steps
decreases it. Why a single reaction behaves oppositely to the pathway it
belongs to is unexplained, and the answer determines whether PGAM is a
plausible target at all: a metabolite-signalling route, an energetic route and
a stress route imply different companion targets, different dose behaviour and
different off-target risks.

## Established knowledge

Three published mechanisms could produce this phenotype, and none is proposed
here as new. Cellular stress supports Th17 differentiation **in the absence of**
TGF-β, with 2-DG and 3-bromopyruvate enhancing polarisation through sustained
cytoplasmic calcium and partial XBP1 (Brucklacher-Waldert et al. 2017, *Cell
Reports* 19:2357, doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2017.05.052; full text read).
Phosphoenolpyruvate — two steps downstream of PGAM's substrate — is a negative
regulator of Th17 differentiation, binding JunB and blocking DNA binding of the
JunB/BATF/IRF4 complex, reportedly without changing glycolysis, proliferation
or survival (Ishikawa and colleagues 2023, *Cell Reports* 42:112205,
doi:10.1016/j.celrep.2023.112205; full text read). Competition between
biosynthetic growth and effector output is a general premise. Two boundary
findings constrain all three: complete T-cell *Pgam1* deletion **attenuates**
CD4 and CD8 responses (Toriyama et al. 2020, *Commun Biol* 3:394,
doi:10.1038/s42003-020-01122-w), so dose matters; and 2-DG **reduces** IL-17A
in lung Th17 tissue-resident memory cells (doi:10.1016/j.isci.2025.114051), so
the direction is context-dependent rather than general.

## Repository evidence

Against a null matched on expression decile — addressing baseline-expression-dependent
null behavior without resolving TPM compositional ambiguity — EGCG in Th17n division-1 libraries raises the Th17
effector programme (+0.544, 0/1,000 null draws as extreme; not an exact zero p value) while histones (−0.465), serine and
one-carbon (−0.299), cell cycle (−0.260) and ribosomal proteins (−0.205) all
fall, and the integrated stress response is the most downward-shifted set
(−0.491, 0/1,000 null draws as extreme; not an exact zero p value). The UPR set containing *XBP1* (+0.063, p 0.121), NRF2
(−0.069, p 0.419) and heat shock (+0.025, p 0.372) show no detected set-specific departure;
this is not evidence of equivalence. Th17n EGCG is
the only one of four drug-by-cell-type arms where the effector programme rises;
in the other three it falls by 0.73 to 1.18. A frozen sensitivity established
that the stress result is not an artefact of the two genes shared with the
serine set: removing *MTHFD2* and *SHMT2* leaves the shift at −0.491, unchanged
to four decimals. At gene level ATF4 output falls (*CHAC1* −2.03, *TRIB3*
−1.75, *NUPR1* −1.28, *DDIT3* −1.11, *ASNS* −0.87) while *ATF4* itself does not
move (−0.19, adjusted p 0.77) and *SESN2* rises (+1.53, adjusted 0/1,000 null draws as extreme; not an exact zero p value4).

## Unresolved gap

Co-occurrence in five bulk libraries per arm is not a mechanism, and no
transcript measures stress-response activity, which is set by eIF2α
phosphorylation and ATF4 translation. The PEP route predicts the phenotype
directly but the source's own ¹³C tracing reports PEP unchanged under EGCG — a
15-minute label ratio, not a pool size, in one condition. The source cites the
PEP work as reference 7, in the Introduction's opening citation bundle only; its
Discussion proposes stress and serine, closes by stating the mechanism needs
further study, and does not return to the PEP mechanism despite its Compass
analysis singling out the 3PG-to-PEP segment.

## Competing explanations

Absolute PEP might remain stable, which would challenge the proposed bulk-pool
route; the isotope-label ratio does not establish absolute pool stability. ISR-independent stress through
sustained cytoplasmic calcium, which no transcript module can see. A
compositional effect in a different form: cells with fewer ribosomal and
histone transcripts have proportionally more of everything else, so the
effector gain may be per-cell-RNA-content rather than increased output per
cell. Division-rate dilution of accumulated cytokine transcript, which the
division-1 gate does not control, since both inhibitors restructure rather than
remove that gate's signature. Off-target action of EGCG, a promiscuous
polyphenol, for which the Wp Compass sensitivity gives no reassurance: the
forward PGAM consistency association is +0.291, rank 73/83 in ascending signed rho, and fails nominal pool-level BH.

## Discriminating outcomes

An absolute pool decrease would support one prediction of the proposed
bulk-pool route, without identifying its causal contribution. A precisely
bounded absence of that decrease would weigh against that route, while leaving
transient or compartment-specific alternatives open. Appropriate independent
evidence could support stress or demand explanations, potentially in combination.
Neither a pool association nor a supplementation response uniquely selects a
mechanism. The current relative-RNA and label-fraction measurements do not
decide among these routes; a complete comparison and its feasibility remain
unqualified.

## Next investigation

No further analysis of the deposited transcriptome is warranted; it has been
read three ways (transcript modules, Compass reactions, expression-matched
programme nulls) and all three are summaries of the same libraries. The useful
next step is to qualify the missing discriminating measurements and an actual
comparison. Absolute abundance answers the pool-decrease prediction that a
label ratio cannot answer; it does not by itself distinguish all candidates.

## Experimental bridge

The existing Wp-P02 and A29 proposals share a scientific context, but do not
establish a complete factorial design, actual shared cultures, host access or
the cost of adding measurements. The independent biological unit and any
matched preparation relationship must follow the actual qualified model;
wells or cells do not create additional replication. Comparison scope,
perturbation validity, functional endpoints and precision remain unresolved.
Additional arms are justified by the intended inference, not automatically
required by this conceptual bridge.

## Interpretation boundary

Library-level bulk TPM with no animal field supports no animal-level or causal
inference. Programme membership is a declared gene list, not measured pathway
activity, and ISR target transcripts are a downstream proxy for a response set
by eIF2α phosphorylation and ATF4 translation. The expression-matched null
does not identify absolute transcript abundance or remove compositional
confounding; a genuine global response remains possible. Five libraries per arm; empirical p values
describe the gene-set draw, not sampling of mice. A negative PEP result would
constrain the mechanism, not establish any alternative.
