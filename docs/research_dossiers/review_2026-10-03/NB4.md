# Nb4 article-local candidate review

The [nine existing plans](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/README.md) remain Nb4-P01–P09. Their frozen planning assets and original queue are preserved; this review does not reprioritize the portfolio, execute a branch or promote any candidate to a global RQ. The source paper is an atlas precedent ([S14](SOURCES.md#s14)), with disease/reference and regional comparators [S18](SOURCES.md#s18) and [S19](SOURCES.md#s19). Existing source and eligibility records remain [article-local](../../../Research%20Article/gate2_N3_travaglini_nabhan_lung_atlas_2020/reports/proposals/SOURCES.md).

| Candidate | Bounded contribution and decisive qualification | Hold on interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| P01, cross-species marker transfer | Determine whether source-defined markers transfer after qualifying orthology, tissue/state alignment and independent animal/donor units. | Failure can reflect measurement or sampling; human/mouse expression difference is not evolutionary adaptation. Pooled mouse identity remains a source-unit issue. |
| P02, cellular expression redistribution | Compare which cell populations contribute expression after P01's unit/orthology audit; a third species could constrain an evolutionary account. | Similar tissue aggregates do not imply conserved cell-level function. Two species alone cannot establish the direction or cause of evolutionary change. |
| P03, disease versus mixture | Qualify donor-level disease and common-state comparisons with discovery/reference contributor overlap removed or disclosed. | Integrated-atlas mapping is not independent replication when source individuals recur; within-state and compositional estimands differ. |
| P04, lung–blood immune relationships | Recover genuinely paired donor compartments and sampling context before within-person comparisons. | The source ledger has no qualified paired validation accession. Similarity does not establish migration, recruitment or residency. |
| P05, immune identity versus state | Test reproducibility of IGSF21/EREG/TREM2-associated populations across independently eligible cohorts using a noncircular identity crosswalk. | Recurrent labels or marker clusters do not establish stable lineage identity; A3/A6 remain adjacent owners of their existing questions. |
| P06, AT2-s subdivision versus continuum | Qualify rare-population coverage, measurement sensitivity and between-donor consistency before comparing discrete and continuous explanations. | Prior matched source coverage failed. Do not lower eligibility to rescue a rare label or equate a cluster with distinct function. |
| P07, regional epithelial identity | Link measured region and independent pathology to the proposed normal-regional programme before testing disease redeployment. | Spatial position and shared expression do not establish ancestry. Shared-plasticity findings return to A0/A5/A11 without duplicating those questions. |
| P08, vascular/mural specialization | Recover vessel-bed identity and adequate mural-cell donor coverage for TBX5-region versus response comparisons. | Pericyte transcription is not contractile function, and this is not A21's endothelial lineage endpoint. A functional branch needs a separate justified assay. |
| P09, fibroblast sensory candidate | First establish SCN7A/GRIA1 attribution using specificity, contamination checks and independent spatial coexpression. | RNA coexpression does not establish ion conductance, sensing or a downstream repair role. The ledger contains no qualified functional validation source. |

These plans are legitimate discovery or measurement questions when their decision value is explicit. They do not all need an immediate wet-lab hypothesis. Metadata qualification can end in a documented hold; numerical continuation needs the prospective contract and the plan's biological-unit and endpoint requirements.
