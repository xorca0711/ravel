# Primary-source ledger

Reviewed 3 October 2026. The scope is a targeted check of proposed discriminators. Access levels below are deliberate: an abstract or indexed excerpt is not a full methods/supplement audit. Repository evidence remains in the linked question dossiers; these sources provide published precedents, not independent replication of repository calculations. No absence of prior work is established by this search.

## S01

[Choi et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487779/). **Access:** Primary abstract/indexed text; withdrawal figure attribution checked against the existing article-local source synthesis, not a new supplement audit.

**Locator:** Abstract; Figure 7A–H as recorded in the local Choi README.

**Established result and consequence:** Inflammatory input and its persistence affect transition and maturation. Withdrawal-associated recovery is already a published result; generic reversibility is insufficient novelty for A14.

## S02

[Hassan and Chen 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-48632-3). **Access:** Publisher full text.

**Locator:** Abstract; Results and Figures 3, 5 and 6.

**Established result and consequence:** CEBPA supports AT2 identity, with context-dependent progenitor programmes after its loss. A7 must extend beyond identity loss; pooled source samples do not create animal-level replication.

## S03

[Sawhney et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64224-1). **Access:** Publisher full text.

**Locator:** Abstract; Figure 7 and Discussion.

**Established result and consequence:** Defensive AT2 and reparative programmes are distinguished. A1/A7/A8 need a precise added discriminator; a combined transition score cannot automatically identify either function.

## S04

[Guo et al. 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-07770-1). **Access:** Publisher full text.

**Locator:** Abstract; neonatal cell atlas and epithelial subtype results.

**Established result and consequence:** Birth-associated transcriptional states motivate A5. Their recurrence in adult data is an expression comparison, not a traced developmental function in repair.

## S05

[Strunz et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-17358-3). **Access:** Indexed primary abstract and data-availability record; direct publisher retrieval failed.

**Locator:** Abstract; GSE141259 source identification.

**Established result and consequence:** A Krt8-associated transitional population precedes alveolar differentiation in the studied setting. This does not link every sequenced early cell to its later fate or establish cross-tissue transfer.

## S06

[Nabhan et al. 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29420258/). **Access:** Primary record and existing article-local source audit; no new full supplement audit.

**Locator:** Published Wnt niche result; repository Nb1 evidence verification.

**Established result and consequence:** The Wnt-responsive alveolar stem-cell niche is established background. A4 asks about within-population switching versus selection; the local acute-data coverage limit remains.

## S07

[Nabhan et al. 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37321220/). **Access:** Primary abstract; detailed figure claims retained from the existing local SOURCE_AUDIT and HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.

**Locator:** Abstract; local audit of Figures 1–2, 4B–D and 5A–E.

**Established result and consequence:** Compartment-specific Fzd dependencies are published. A19–A21 require distinct temporal or functional outcomes; the exact adult AF1 Fzd2-versus-Fzd1 comparison is not supplied by these broad precedents.

## S08

**Locator:** Abstract and publisher highlights; DOI 10.1016/j.stem.2025.01.011.

**Established result and consequence:** Published clonal evidence and mutant-state plasticity motivate A16–A18. A local model implementation error does not invalidate those experiments; CD177 function and separate WT outcomes need their own evidence.

## S10

[Cardoso et al. 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10399-6). **Access:** Indexed primary abstract.

**Locator:** Abstract, AREG–EGFR niche result.

**Established result and consequence:** Mutant epithelial AREG-associated niche remodeling is published. It motivates A2/A9 but does not identify delivery dependence in the repository's source design.

## S11

[Aegerter et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41590-019-0568-x). **Access:** Publisher abstract and figure headings; full text subscription-limited.

**Locator:** Abstract; Figures 5–7 headings.

**Established result and consequence:** Macrophage origin and prior tissue history can accompany persistent, changing functional states. This precedent makes lineage composition a serious rival to an intrinsic memory interpretation in A3.

## S12

[Munger et al. 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10025398/). **Access:** Indexed primary record/abstract.

**Locator:** Abstract; DOI 10.1016/S0092-8674(00)80545-0.

**Established result and consequence:** Integrin alpha-v-beta-6 activation of latent TGF-beta is established. A15 needs a context-specific attribution and direct activation measurement, not rediscovery through RNA association.

## S13

[Uehara et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9981730/). **Access:** Primary full text.

**Locator:** Abstract and phosphate homeostasis/microlith results; DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-36810-8.

**Established result and consequence:** SLC34A2/Npt2b deficiency has extracellular phosphate and tissue consequences. Alternative transport activity is not equivalent to increased transporter RNA; A23's compartment/timing question remains distinct.

## S14

[Travaglini et al. 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7704697/). **Access:** Primary full text.

**Locator:** Abstract; lung cell types, localization and cross-species comparisons; DOI 10.1038/s41586-020-2922-4.

**Established result and consequence:** The atlas establishes descriptive identities and expression patterns. Nb4 extensions require independent units and the specific endpoint; expression alone does not establish ancestry, evolution or sensory function.

## S15

[Kaiser et al. 2023 (online 2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9767680/). **Access:** Indexed primary full-text excerpts, PubMed abstract and publisher record.

**Locator:** Abstract and mesenchymal EGFR/AREG niche results; DOI 10.1084/jem.20221462.

**Established result and consequence:** AREG-responsive mesenchymal EGFR contributes to the regenerative niche. A2/A9/A13 need a more specific delivery, recipient or output contrast than the existence of mesenchymal support.

## S16

[Hoffman et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63735-1). **Access:** Publisher abstract and results text.

**Locator:** Abstract; epithelial intermediate and fibroblast niche results.

**Established result and consequence:** Reciprocal epithelial–fibroblast effects are established in the studied disease model. A13/A22 must name a direction, output and unresolved contrast rather than claim generic bidirectional signaling as new.

## S17

[Gillich et al. 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2822-7). **Access:** Indexed primary abstract.

**Locator:** Abstract; capillary subtype and renewal findings.

**Established result and consequence:** gCap and aerocyte functions and progenitor relationships are known. This supports the A21 lineage question but does not establish Fzd4-dependent production of aerocytes.

## S18

**Established result and consequence:** Integrated healthy/disease states provide a reference for A6 and Nb4. Contributor overlap must be checked before treating atlas mapping as external replication.

## S19

## S20

[GEO GSE169125 (MesSTIM)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE169125). **Access:** Indexed primary GEO study description only; no sample-level audit or download in this stage.
