## No automatic promotion

These statuses describe evidence eligibility, not owner retain/reject decisions. A new source can reopen a held comparison after an explicit qualification record. Preserve the old negative result and prior exposure; use a new contract for new numerical work. Neither a convenient dataset nor a named laboratory substitutes for the missing measurement.
