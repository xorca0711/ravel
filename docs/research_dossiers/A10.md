# A10: research development dossier

Do epithelial programmes add information about measured organoid growth?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Do epithelial programmes explain measured organoid growth beyond earlier organoid size in independent preparations?

## Established knowledge

Area growth, survival, cell number and mature alveolar function are different outcomes. Concurrent RNA may describe growth without forecasting it.

## Repository evidence

Residual-growth scores improved the descriptive baseline, but absolute predictive R-squared was negative on three of four plates. Target and plate are entangled and independent preparation identities remain unresolved.

## Unresolved gap

The result has not demonstrated transport to independent preparations, temporal prediction or a mechanistic contribution to growth.

## Working hypothesis

A defined epithelial programme adds stable information about later growth beyond initial size across independent preparations.

## Competing explanations

The association is concurrent growth state, plate/target confounding, area measurement or a preparation-specific effect.

## Discriminating outcomes

Improvement over the initial-size baseline in held-out independent preparations favors transport; collapse on whole-plate or preparation holdout favors the rival.

## Next investigation

Recover preparation and timing metadata before another model. Preserve failed plate performance and distinguish descriptive association from forecasting.

## Experimental bridge

Compare independent preparations with early measurements and later growth, recording survival and composition separately. A subsequent causal comparison needs a programme-specific mechanism rather than score manipulation.

## Interpretation boundary

Organoid area is not repair capacity, mature fate or a direct count of productive descendants.

## Decision and maturity

A descriptive growth result exists; robust prediction and functional interpretation remain unestablished.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.

## Primary-source and feasibility review

The existing screen already separates niche and epithelial responses. Concurrent RNA and later size association is not early prediction or transport across independent preparations.
