Does recipient receptor and inhibitor context explain responses beyond IL-1 ligand RNA?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does recipient context predict a response specifically attributable to IL-1 beyond ligand RNA and general inflammation?

## Established knowledge

Receptor, accessory protein and inhibitory context can modify cytokine response. General TNF/NFkB transcription is not uniquely attributable to IL-1.

## Repository evidence

The recipient index improved epithelial prediction in twelve patients while fibroblast results were unstable. Unassigned sources account for roughly 52–72% of IL1B, limiting a macrophage-only source interpretation.

Evidence locators (source citations and numerical details remain in these records):

- [RQ_Specified/A12_recipient_context/README.md](../../RQ_Specified/A12_recipient_context/README.md)

## Unresolved gap

The endpoint is insufficiently IL-1-specific, and source assignment is incomplete; ligand RNA versus recipient competence is not a causal decomposition.

## Working hypothesis

IL-1 receptor and inhibitory context in recipient epithelial cells or fibroblasts may explain an IL-1-dependent response beyond ligand RNA abundance. Those are separate recipient questions; the current portfolio has not fixed one compartment, receptor/inhibitory contrast and biological consequence for a mechanistic experiment. General NF-kB or inflammation scores cannot establish IL-1 specificity.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Recipient compartment/axis/endpoint required. IL-1-dependent epithelial and stromal effects are published, including recipient-history effects. Exact-compartment novelty is unresolved until the contrast and consequence are specified. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a12). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

The index tracks general inflammatory state, cell composition or another stimulus rather than specific IL-1 reception.

## Discriminating outcomes

A recipient-context effect under a qualified IL-1-dependent contrast supports specific reception. The same response without IL-1 dependence favors a general inflammatory marker. Source-attribution uncertainty and a generic pathway score cannot identify which cells delivered the active input.

## Next investigation

Keep the exposed pilot exploratory; qualify a specific endpoint and reconcile ligand source coverage before further prediction. Supporting A12-S1 remains source-assignment work, not a separate mechanism.

## Experimental bridge

Compare recipient contexts with an interpretable IL-1-dependent response and independent receptor/protein measurements, separating epithelial and fibroblast outcomes.

## Interpretation boundary

A transcript-based receptor index does not establish receptor activity, and NFkB-associated RNA cannot identify the responsible ligand.

## Decision and maturity

Exploratory recipient-context evidence exists. Specificity and functional competence are the missing discriminators.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
