Do regulatory programmes distinguish RNA-similar transitional states and their functional responses?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which regulatory or starting-state distinctions help explain why similar transitional RNA markers accompany different epithelial outcomes?

## Established knowledge

Transitional RNA resemblance can coexist with regulatory differences. Separate chromatin, methylation and lineage measurements answer different questions unless the source design links them.

## Repository evidence

Current source recovery maps all 22 retained HPCS aliases to named mice, resolving that identity hold while library/chase confounding remains. In the iATC-minus-iAT2 comparison at the checked ±1-kb promoter windows, CDKN1A H3K27ac/H3 reverses at an alternate start in one preparation, whereas H3K27me3/H3 is lower at both checked starts in both preparations. These preparations share one iPSC-line context. The [promoter/preparation sensitivity report](../../RQ_Specified/A1_transitional_epithelial_state_distinction/reports/ROBUSTNESS_REPORT.md) supplies the histone comparison. The separate AP-1 endpoint is HOPX acquisition among lineage-labelled cells, not completed functional AT1 differentiation. Opposite regional directions in three mice per genotype (interaction p=0.10) challenge a blanket benefit claim without establishing a replicated regional mechanism. The source-informed specification followed inspection of source layouts and values; these reanalyses add no independent replication.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A1_transitional_epithelial_state_distinction/reports/REGULATORY_FATE_REPORT.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

A primary regulatory feature and an eligible early-regulatory/later-output comparison have not been jointly qualified. Recovered mouse identities do not remove library/chase confounding, and separate preparations cannot be stitched into a same-cell regulatory mechanism.

## Working hypothesis

RNA-similar transitional alveolar epithelial cells may differ in regulatory competence for later AT1 maturation. The remaining hypothesis would require an early regulatory feature to distinguish later lineage-derived mature AT1 output beyond the starting RNA state. No particular regulatory feature has yet been nominated for that prospective contrast; a generic chromatin assay does not complete it.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Feature nomination required. Regulatory heterogeneity and functional transitional states are published. Exact-feature novelty cannot be judged until the regulatory feature and mature endpoint are fixed. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a1). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Starting identity, injury/model context, promoter choice, sampling, source/library structure and parallel responses can generate similar RNA with different chromatin or outcomes.

## Discriminating outcomes

An early regulatory feature adding information beyond RNA about linked later output would support a bounded predictor. Dependence on source, promoter definition or a lack of incremental information would restrict it. A descriptive regulatory contrast can remain useful without settling prediction, memory or causality.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Retain the descriptive evidence and choose one comparison that could change the biological interpretation before adding another branch. For the registered predictive question, a fixed early feature and linked later output must be available in appropriate units. Added information beyond RNA would support a bounded prediction; source/TSS dependence or no incremental information would limit it. A narrower descriptive regulatory comparison can still be useful without claiming prediction or function.

## Experimental bridge

A lineage-compatible system would compare early feature-defined populations and later mature output with matched viability and starting composition. A separately justified perturbation/control comparison could address a defined effect question; successful prediction is not a prerequisite, and neither comparison alone establishes regulatory mediation.

## Interpretation boundary

Useful source, regulatory-context and outcome distinctions, not a selected common epigenetic mechanism. Source-specific consistency can nominate a feature for a stated comparison, but these separate studies do not link that feature to later mature output beyond RNA. The chromatin interpretation remains measured histone signal, not proof of closed chromatin, absolute histone occupancy, memory or fate control.
