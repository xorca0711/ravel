## Biological problem

How much of the paired CSF–blood inflammatory RNA difference remains within the observed state and activation support, and what can that restricted comparison explain?

## Established knowledge

Compartmentalisation and the CCR5-high Th17.1 candidate are published premises. The latter is enriched in paired CSF, while its reported post-natalizumab reduction concerns blood. The [updated primary-source context](../../RQ_Specified/A30_csf_compartment_effector_state/LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md) distinguishes exact source locators, shared GSE138266 evidence, the separate 2026 CD8-focused RNA/TCR study and access limits.

## Repository evidence

The corrected all-cell paired inflammatory association is positive in all ten donors. Within observed shared-state and activation support, linear activation standardization retains seven donors, with median inflammatory RNA-score contrast +0.03810 and positive direction in six. Median retained coverage is 67.7% for CSF and 33.0% for blood, with only 3.9% CSF coverage for the least-covered retained donor; the floor-five sensitivity interval crosses zero. The standardized activation contrast is zero by construction, not evidence that biological activation has been controlled. The comparison changes the target population rather than explaining every cell in either compartment. Inputs and motivating outcomes were already exposed; a later frozen specification does not make this confirmatory.

## Unresolved gap

Independent lineage/activation qualification, clinical context and functional outcomes remain unresolved. Trimming to common support cannot identify unmeasured activation, trafficking or residency, nor demonstrate regulatory equivalence.

## Working hypothesis

In paired CSF and blood, the inflammatory RNA programme remains higher in CSF within comparable CD4 T-cell states after accounting for measured activation and composition. Regulatory-associated RNA is evaluated separately, without presuming unchanged regulatory function.

## Competing explanations

Selective trafficking, activation, clinical/source differences and remaining mixture can explain the paired pattern. A positive within-observed-support association is not proof that the compartment caused the expression state.

## Discriminating outcomes

Persistence after independent state/activation and clinical qualification would strengthen transport of the contextual association. Dependence on those definitions would restrict its population or interpretation. Neither establishes local induction, residency or regulatory equivalence.

## Experimental bridge

Conditional paired sampling with validated CD4/subset and activation protein measurements, clinical covariates and suitable functional endpoints could extend the RNA association. TCR adds shared-clone information, not residency. A later perturbation/control experiment is justified only for a defined causal question; no mutant, assay panel, sample size or laboratory feasibility is established here.

## Interpretation boundary

A bounded association survives a more restricted observed comparison. This is useful context narrowing, but it does not exclude biological activation or select local compartment induction. State and activation definitions share the transcriptome, and the sensitivity result limits a uniformly robust interpretation.
