## Biological problem

CSF and blood can contain different mixtures and states of CD4 T cells. A paired RNA difference may reflect activation, selective trafficking or context-associated expression. A30 asks whether a residual association is measurable within comparable states; it does not presume a compartment-induced mechanism.

## Established knowledge

Compartmentalisation and the CCR5-high Th17.1 candidate are published premises. The latter is enriched in paired CSF, while its reported post-natalizumab reduction concerns blood. The [updated primary-source context](../../RQ_Specified/A30_csf_compartment_effector_state/LITERATURE_CONTEXT.md) distinguishes exact source locators, shared GSE138266 evidence, the separate 2026 CD8-focused RNA/TCR study and access limits.

## Repository evidence

The [v3 erratum](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/A30_ERRATUM.md) corrects the scoring denominator. Pro-inflammatory paired medians are +0.0558/+0.0498/+0.0570 in 9/10/9 donors; empirical BH is 0.0321/0.0368/0.0321 in the six-test family. The [saved-artifact audit](../audits/2026-10-07-pr140/REPORT.md) verifies arithmetic, not raw-cell identity or scientific acceptance. Residual activation persists, the null matches size within loaded panel genes, and non-significant regulatory RNA is not equivalence. The earlier disease comparison failed to reproduce the published pattern under this implementation; it is not a refutation of the entire paper.

## Unresolved gap

Are the residual RNA differences robust to lineage/gating, depth, activation balance and state-support qualification? The source cannot establish local induction, residence or regulatory function. Ten paired donors and incomplete clinical covariates restrict precision and interpretation; descriptive states do not guarantee biologically comparable populations.

## Working hypothesis

In paired CSF and blood, the inflammatory RNA programme remains higher in CSF within comparable CD4 T-cell states after accounting for measured activation and composition. Regulatory-associated RNA is evaluated separately, without presuming unchanged regulatory function.

## Competing explanations

Residual activation, selective trafficking and subset or clonal composition may each contribute. Dropout-sensitive putative CD4 gating, depth, null construction and processing are measurement threats. These alternatives need assessment, not an invented mutually exclusive mechanism or compulsory extra experiment arm.

## Discriminating outcomes

Persistence after credible adjustment supports a residual compartment association; attenuation supports a contribution from the measured activation/composition features under that model. Mixed or imprecise outcomes remain informative. Protein/TCR can improve phenotype and clonal resolution but shared clones do not prove residence or induction. A causal claim requires a separately justified intervention/identification design. Regulatory function cannot be inferred from a non-significant RNA contrast.

## Experimental bridge

Conditional paired sampling with validated CD4/subset and activation protein measurements, clinical covariates and suitable functional endpoints could extend the RNA association. TCR adds shared-clone information, not residency. A later perturbation/control experiment is justified only for a defined causal question; no mutant, assay panel, sample size or laboratory feasibility is established here.

## Interpretation boundary

RNA programmes are not cytokine secretion, regulatory function, residence or PGAM flux. Adjustment may remove mediators as well as confounding. GSE138266 reanalysis shares evidence with Wang and Schafflick, not independent validation. The ratio-of-medians composition summary is descriptive and uses imputed means where a state is absent from one compartment. No causal fraction or equivalence is established.
