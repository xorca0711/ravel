# A30: Compartment-specific effector state versus local activation in CSF T cells

Registered 5 October 2026 as **proposed**, derived from the executed Wp
reproduction ladder, branch cards, metadata phenotypes and extension analyses
of Wang et al. 2025. The derivation supplied a bounded scientific review; human
retain/reject and scientific acceptance remain unrecorded. Registration is not
a claim of novelty, functional validation or permission to execute an
unfinished numerical design.

[Workspace](../../RQ_Specified/A30_csf_compartment_effector_state/README.md) · [Plan](../../RQ_Specified/A30_csf_compartment_effector_state/PLAN.md) · [Derivation](../../Research%20Article/gate2_W2_wagner_Th17_PGAM/rq_derivation/Q3_csf_compartment.md).

## Biological problem

T cells recovered from cerebrospinal fluid in CNS autoimmunity score higher on
inflammatory gene programmes than the same donor's blood T cells. Whether that
reflects a state the compartment imposes, or simply that cells in an inflamed
site are more activated, determines whether the compartment is a therapeutic
context or only a sampling location — and whether a module score measured
there means anything beyond activation.

## Established knowledge

Immune mechanisms in multiple sclerosis are compartmentalised: CSF leukocytes
have a location-associated composition and transcriptome, with MS increasing
cell-type diversity in CSF including cytotoxic T helper cells and follicular
helper cells (Schafflick et al. 2020, *Nat Commun* 11:247,
doi:10.1038/s41467-019-14118-w) — the deposit this question would reanalyse,
so the same evidence lineage rather than independent support. A specific
CCR5-high Th17.1 cluster enriched in CSF relative to paired blood, in a
pre-cytotoxic state and reduced after natalizumab, has since been described
(doi:10.1016/j.ebiom.2026.106324). Compartmentalisation is therefore a
**premise**; what is unresolved is whether a transported Th17 module's
one-sided elevation is distinguishable from activation.

## Repository evidence

The source's published disease comparison does not reproduce. Nothing
separates the disease groups in CSF at BH ≤ 0.05, the smallest adjusted value
anywhere in CSF being programme N3 at BH 0.147. In blood the modules do
separate the cohorts, but 1,000 matched-size random gene sets separate them as
well (observed +0.055 against a null spanning +0.004 to +0.070, p 0.29), and
the declared activation set separates them in the opposite direction (−0.177)
while correlating with every module at Spearman −0.61 to −0.87. The contrast
that does survive a matched null is the paired within-donor tissue difference
in ten donors: pro-inflammatory +0.061 in 10/10 donors, empirical p 0.003;
programme N3 +0.081, p 0.007; activation +0.109, p 0.013; while pro-regulatory
is +0.007, p 0.394 on the authors' HVG set and +0.022, p 0.931 on all 58
mapped genes, and the 805-gene Table S3 EGCG signature sits inside its null
(p 0.297).

## Unresolved gap

A paired donor-level contrast on ten donors cannot separate a
compartment-specific effector programme from local activation, because the
activation set exceeds its own matched null in the same direction. The deposit
carries no age, sex, treatment or disease-duration field, so nothing can be
adjusted for donor characteristics, and two deposit defects had to be handled
during reproduction: one CSF library deposited unfiltered at 737,280 barcodes,
and a hard-zero lineage gate leaving 1 to 124 cells in four blood libraries.

## Working hypothesis

The cerebrospinal fluid compartment imposes an effector state on CD4-lineage T cells that is not reducible to local activation, with regulatory competence unchanged rather than suppressed. The prediction is that within matched activation strata the paired CSF-minus-blood elevation of the pro-inflammatory module persists while the pro-regulatory arm still does not move; an activation-only explanation predicts the elevation collapses. Compartment association is not evidence that the compartment caused the state.

## Competing explanations

Local activation, the expected and measured competitor. Tissue residency or
recirculation, since CSF samples a migratory population and nothing in a paired
contrast shows that the same cell would have scored lower in blood.
Composition, now with a named candidate population in the CSF-enriched
CCR5-high Th17.1 cluster, which would raise an effector module through mixture
rather than through any cell's acquired state. Precision: ten donors with no
covariate fields, where a failure to separate strata would be uninformative.

## Discriminating outcomes

Two legs. The reanalysis leg repeats the paired contrast within activation
strata defined independently of the modules, reports both arms separately
against the same matched null, and decomposes the paired difference into
composition and within-state terms. Persistence of the elevation within strata,
with the pro-regulatory arm still flat, supports a compartment-specific state;
collapse supports activation; a dominant composition term indicates a mixture
shift. The measurement leg requires paired CSF and blood CD4 T cells from the
same donors sorted on activation markers with intracellular IL-17A and Foxp3
protein, plus TCR sequencing: cells of shared clonal origin scoring alike
across compartments would indicate residency rather than an acquired state,
and that is the only readout that addresses residency with the donor as unit.

## Next investigation

The reanalysis leg is executable in this package under a new prospective
contract, and is the only leg of the three Wp-derived questions for which that
is true. Its limits are known in advance: it cannot defeat residency, and with
ten donors it is precision-limited. It should be run with the stratification,
the arms and the decomposition fixed before any value is inspected.

## Experimental bridge

Paired CSF and blood sampling with protein and TCR readouts in a new MS cohort,
donor as unit, with age, sex and treatment recorded — the covariates the reused
deposit lacks. This project holds no clinical samples, so the bridge identifies
what would be needed rather than proposing that this project perform it.

## Interpretation boundary

Donor-level module scores are not protein, not cytokine secretion and not
pathogenic function; the modules are mouse-derived and transported by symbol
identity, mapping 57 of 63 and 24 of 30 genes. A compartment association is
not evidence that the compartment caused the state. Reanalysis of this deposit
shares its evidence lineage with both the source paper and Schafflick et al.
2020, so neither supplies independent validation. The reproduction found the
source's published human claim unsupported, and that negative result stands
whatever this question returns.

## Decision and maturity

Registered as a proposed measurement-validation and boundary-extension
question with one executable leg and one that needs samples this project does
not hold. Scientific value here can include a reproducible negative. Human
acceptance, precision and any functional claim remain pending; no effect margin
is nominated on ten donors.

## Relationship to existing questions

concentration during ageing, with the animal as unit; A30 contrasts CSF against
paired blood for human CD4 module expression in autoimmunity, with the donor as

neighbour overall, being exactly a redistribution-versus-within-state
