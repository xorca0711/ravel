## Biological problem

How does ending an inflammatory exposure affect later epithelial maturation, and what separate contribution could fibroblast reception make?

## Established knowledge

[Choi 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487779/) reports inflammatory transition and recovery after withdrawal. [Ciminieri 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12042164/), Fig. 1 and Results, already compares fibroblast pre-exposure and organoid treatment. Thus generic withdrawal, history or fibroblast involvement is not new; the remaining candidate concerns linked mature output after verified cessation.

## Repository evidence

Source intake identified Choi Figure 7E's twelve qPCR endpoints and the relevant withdrawal comparison. The legend reports four biological replicates from two experiments but does not map preparation identities or pairing. Preparation-linked numerical values were not found in the inspected attachments; the figure/methods Limch1 versus Limch2 discrepancy is retained. Continuous-input RNA and a separate metabolic intervention do not reproduce withdrawal.

[Current result, design and limits](../research_reviews/2026-11-03-full-priority-screening/A14_SOURCE_MANIFEST.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

Preparation-level values, their experimental map and a qualified relation to the intended mature-output endpoint remain missing. RNA marker recovery does not by itself establish mature lineage output or functional recovery.

## Working hypothesis

H1: longer IL-1-beta exposure reduces subsequent lineage-derived mature AT1 recovery after input cessation, with comparable recovery intervals and culture age. H2, assessed separately: fibroblast IL-1 reception modifies that mature-output recovery beyond direct epithelial reception. H2 has no justified direction yet. The proposed increment concerns post-cessation maturation, not the already published effects of withdrawal or fibroblast pre-exposure on organoid formation.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Narrowed post-cessation outcome; novelty provisional. Choi establishes withdrawal recovery; Ciminieri already tests fibroblast IL-1 pre-exposure and organoid growth. The candidate increment is linked mature AT1 recovery after verified cessation with compartment attribution, not generic history or niche involvement. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a14). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Continued input, initial state, composition and source/recipient effects can explain an apparent persistent transition. Persistence in an unmatched source is not automatically memory after verified cessation.

## Discriminating outcomes

Qualified post-cessation marker recovery could reproduce the source RNA comparison. A mature-output or fibroblast-reception effect is a separate endpoint and could differ from marker recovery. Continued-input data or unlinked preparations cannot decide those contrasts.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Use the identified source comparison to define what recoverable values could actually estimate. A qualified post-cessation contrast could inform marker recovery; the stronger mature-output and separate fibroblast-reception claims require their own linked endpoints. No direction for fibroblast reception or new interaction effect is invented, and continuous-input data do not substitute for the missing withdrawal contrast.

## Experimental bridge

Lineage-linked absolute AT1 yield and an independently qualified mature functional endpoint address recovery; engagement/cessation measurements address residual input; viable source and recipient counts address loss. Keep the history and fibroblast-reception comparisons separately interpretable, with comparable culture age and recovery intervals. Organoid-forming efficiency alone repeats a closer precedent and cannot answer mature recovery.

## Interpretation boundary

Completed source/endpoint clarification with an unestimated local withdrawal effect. Published plausibility does not mean the proposed mature-output or fibroblast-reception question has run. Failure to recover values from these attachments is not proof that no suitable data exist elsewhere.
