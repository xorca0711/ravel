# A14: research development dossier

Do exposure duration and fibroblast IL-1 reception separately determine recovery after withdrawal?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does input history alter recovery after withdrawal through the epithelial compartment, fibroblast IL-1 reception, or continued residual stimulation?

## Established knowledge

Choi 2020 already examined withdrawal and later AT1 markers. Simply repeating that relationship is not the new contribution; compartment and persistence explanations require distinct evidence.

## Repository evidence

The canonical question contains two separable hypotheses: exposure history and fibroblast IL-1 reception. Existing context does not establish an autonomous memory mechanism.

Evidence locators (source citations and numerical details remain in these records):

- [RQ_Specified/A14_withdrawal_recovery_and_reception/README.md](../../RQ_Specified/A14_withdrawal_recovery_and_reception/README.md)

## Unresolved gap

Recovery differences have not been separated into lasting cell-state change, residual input, recipient compartment and differential survival.

## Working hypothesis

Prior input history modifies subsequent epithelial recovery in a manner dependent on a defined recipient compartment.

## Competing explanations

Residual stimulation, different survival or starting composition explains apparent persistence after withdrawal.

## Discriminating outcomes

A history effect after verified input removal and survival accounting favors persistence; loss of the difference after those checks favors continued input or selection.

## Next investigation

Review the published withdrawal result and define the added compartment-level discriminator. Keep history and fibroblast reception as separate estimands within the card.

## Experimental bridge

A compartment-resolved recovery comparison should measure mature descendant yield, survival and remaining input independently. Its design must distinguish the two hypotheses without treating a marker rebound as full recovery.

## Interpretation boundary

A delayed RNA response after nominal withdrawal does not establish intrinsic memory or irreversible fate.

## Decision and maturity

Conceptually testable but novelty and compartment measurement remain to be qualified. No duration or laboratory operating conditions are selected here.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.

Published input-withdrawal recovery makes generic reversibility an insufficient novelty claim. Compartment attribution and persistence after verified withdrawal are the remaining discriminators.
