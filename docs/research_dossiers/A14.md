Do exposure duration and fibroblast IL-1 reception separately determine recovery after withdrawal?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does longer IL-1-beta exposure reduce later mature AT1 recovery after verified cessation, and does fibroblast reception modify that recovery separately from direct epithelial reception?

## Established knowledge

[Choi 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7487779/) reports inflammatory transition and recovery after withdrawal. [Ciminieri 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12042164/), Fig. 1 and Results, already compares fibroblast pre-exposure and organoid treatment. Thus generic withdrawal, history or fibroblast involvement is not new; the remaining candidate concerns linked mature output after verified cessation.

## Repository evidence

The canonical question contains two separable hypotheses: exposure history and fibroblast IL-1 reception. Existing context does not establish an autonomous memory mechanism.

## Unresolved gap

Recovery differences have not been separated into lasting cell-state change, residual input, recipient compartment and differential survival.

## Working hypothesis

H1: longer IL-1-beta exposure reduces subsequent lineage-derived mature AT1 recovery after input cessation, with comparable recovery intervals and culture age. H2, assessed separately: fibroblast IL-1 reception modifies that mature-output recovery beyond direct epithelial reception. H2 has no justified direction yet. The proposed increment concerns post-cessation maturation, not the already published effects of withdrawal or fibroblast pre-exposure on organoid formation.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Narrowed post-cessation outcome; novelty provisional. Choi establishes withdrawal recovery; Ciminieri already tests fibroblast IL-1 pre-exposure and organoid growth. The candidate increment is linked mature AT1 recovery after verified cessation with compartment attribution, not generic history or niche involvement. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a14). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Residual stimulation, different survival or starting composition explains apparent persistence after withdrawal.

## Discriminating outcomes

Lower absolute mature output after the longer history, despite verified cessation, favors H1; comparable recovery weakens it. A separate fibroblast-reception contrast at comparable epithelial input addresses H2. Record lineage yield, survival and mature function; organoid number or a falling transitional score cannot distinguish recovery, death and replacement.

## Next investigation

Review the published withdrawal result and define the added compartment-level discriminator. Keep history and fibroblast reception as separate estimands within the card.

## Experimental bridge

Lineage-linked absolute AT1 yield and an independently qualified mature functional endpoint address recovery; engagement/cessation measurements address residual input; viable source and recipient counts address loss. Keep the history and fibroblast-reception comparisons separately interpretable, with comparable culture age and recovery intervals. Organoid-forming efficiency alone repeats a closer precedent and cannot answer mature recovery.

## Interpretation boundary

A delayed RNA response after nominal withdrawal does not establish intrinsic memory or irreversible fate.

## Decision and maturity

Conceptually testable but novelty and compartment measurement remain to be qualified. No duration or laboratory operating conditions are selected here.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
