Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does ending Fzd-supported expansion increase mature AT1 output while preserving responsive AT2 descendants in a primary AT2 context retaining alveolar identity?

## Established knowledge

Maintaining an expandable population and producing mature descendants are different functions. CHIR exposure is not equivalent to receptor-specific Fzd input.

## Repository evidence

The Nb2-derived workspace and later extension narrow the question to withdrawal, mature output and reserve. Pre-withdrawal airway-like state is a candidate competence feature, not established causation.

## Unresolved gap

Starting-state competence, input duration and input identity have not been separated using mature descendant and reserve outcomes.

## Working hypothesis

In primary AT2-derived cultures retaining alveolar identity before the schedule comparison, ending Fzd-supported expansion may increase later absolute lineage-derived mature AT1 output while preserving AT2 descendants capable of renewed expansion. Continued receptor input is the comparator. Fzd5-selective stimulation is a source-supported candidate input, not a frozen reagent choice; schedule, starting-state competence and input identity retain separate decisions.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Conditional joint outcome; novelty provisional. Fzd-supported AT2 growth, Wnt withdrawal and differentiation are established. The possible increment is the joint receptor-specific AT1-output/functional-reserve tradeoff; existing CHIR withdrawal and expanded-cell differentiation do not establish it. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a19). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Observed differences follow survival, starting population mixture, expansion duration or a generic downstream pathway effect.

## Discriminating outcomes

More mature AT1 output with a retained responsive AT2 reserve supports productive withdrawal in that context. More AT1 output with reserve depletion is a tradeoff; more area alone tests neither. Lineage-linked absolute counts address yield, independent identity/function addresses maturity, and later response capacity addresses reserve. CHIR withdrawal does not isolate Fzd5 withdrawal.

## Next investigation

Separate the three estimands in the next contract and qualify lineage, mature endpoint and reserve measurements. Use the current extension boundaries rather than reverting to a generic Wnt question.

## Experimental bridge

Compare continued versus ended receptor input after a common expansion history. Assess alveolar identity before assigning the schedule comparison. Lineage-linked counts measure absolute descendants; orthogonal identity/function measures qualify AT1 maturation; renewed response capacity tests the retained AT2 reserve. An identity-marker or surviving-cell fraction alone does not test the joint claim. Fzd5 is a candidate model choice, and CHIR cessation is not equivalent to ending a receptor-specific input.

## Interpretation boundary

Marker changes or expansion after stimulation do not demonstrate productive mature repair with retained reserve.
