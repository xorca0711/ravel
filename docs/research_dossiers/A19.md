## Biological problem

Can ending a specified Fzd-supported expansion input increase mature AT1 output while retaining AT2 descendants able to respond again? Current culture-context results show why marker change alone cannot answer that joint question. Starting-state competence, schedule and input identity remain distinct decisions.

## Established knowledge

Compartment-specific Fzd growth and alveolar differentiation are published premises. The [2022 human organoid source](../../RQ_Specified/A19_fzd_response_reversibility/SOURCES.md) provides donor-coded qPCR and source-block RNA comparisons under CHIR media conditions. Nabhan and the later expansion/differentiation precedents motivate the receptor question; they do not turn the CHIR comparison into Fzd-selective cessation or a measured reserve.

## Repository evidence

The [completed result](../../RQ_Specified/A19_fzd_response_reversibility/RESULTS.md) reports CHIR-absent versus present qPCR in three matched HTII-280-positive-derived donor lines. SFTPC falls in all three, while FOXJ1, TP63 and SCGB1A1 rise; intervals are mostly wide. In separate airway-derived source lines, early SFTPC induction diminishes from passage 1 to 3 despite continued CHIR. Changing donor labels and non-detects in another passage series prevent treating it as another three-donor trajectory.

Eight bulk libraries in GSE197949 represent two source blocks; explicit donor mapping is unresolved and the knockdown hairpin differs by block. They are not eight donors. CHIR absence increases both AT1-associated and airway RNA panels, with gene-level exceptions. **Bulk SFTPC increases in both control blocks whereas the three qPCR effects are negative.** Different preparations and normalization prevent pooling these into a universal identity-loss estimate.

GSK3B knockdown lowers its RNA but yields nonuniform marker changes; it does not prove a CHIR off-target mechanism. The measurements are normalized qPCR expression or bulk RNA panels, not traced mature output, barrier function or response to a later bout. Separate source figures do not identify a treatment-by-starting-state interaction.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

**Rationale verdict: useful context diagnostics and an exploratory competence lead; the receptor-specific schedule/reserve inference is incomplete.** The mixed marker responses undermine a simple equation of input removal with productive alveolar maturation. They do not select an airway programme as the causal barrier, establish irreversible loss of competence, or identify Fzd5 as the operative input.

Source narratives and prior Nb2 outcomes were exposed; new workbook/count estimates followed a frozen exploratory plan. The competence refinement was post-analysis. Donor codes shared across figures do not create replication, and qPCR/bulk discordance remains part of the evidence. Selective growth/survival, changing populations, passage, missing cues and downstream GSK3 context remain alternatives. A mechanistic label cannot resolve that ambiguity or establish random noise.

## Unresolved gap

No qualified receptor-selective ended-versus-continued input comparison measures both lineage-derived mature output and later AT2 responsiveness. Pre-exposure state, acquired pre-cessation state and later outcome are not interchangeable.

The registered text below remains unchanged as a proposal; current findings do not
make its predicted mechanism or effect an accepted explanation.

## Working hypothesis

In primary AT2-derived cultures retaining alveolar identity before the schedule comparison, ending Fzd-supported expansion may increase later absolute lineage-derived mature AT1 output while preserving AT2 descendants capable of renewed expansion. Continued receptor input is the comparator. Fzd5-selective stimulation is a source-supported candidate input, not a frozen reagent choice; schedule, starting-state competence and input identity retain separate decisions.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Conditional joint outcome; novelty provisional. Fzd-supported AT2 growth, Wnt withdrawal and differentiation are established. The possible increment is the joint receptor-specific AT1-output/functional-reserve tradeoff; existing CHIR withdrawal and expanded-cell differentiation do not establish it. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a19). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Starting mixture, survival, expansion duration, passage selection and a generic downstream pathway effect can mimic a schedule response. An acquired state after schedule assignment may be a mediator rather than a baseline cause.

## Discriminating outcomes

Greater mature output with retained responsive AT2 reserve would support the joint benefit in that context; greater output with depleted reserve would reveal a tradeoff. Marker induction or area alone decides neither. A state-dependent schedule effect would refine the comparison but would not alone prove that acquiring that state caused reduced competence.

## Next investigation

Specify which input, schedule and outcome a qualified source can compare. A source-qualified narrower context question remains useful, but the registered joint benefit requires both mature output and reserve. Historical pipeline priorities are not automatically active; each further comparison needs a stated scientific purpose.

## Experimental bridge

The same qualified starting preparations and appropriate biological units must anchor a schedule comparison. Identity before assignment and subsequent lineage/output observations have different roles. Fzd5 remains a source-supported candidate input; CHIR absence is not a substitute for ending a receptor-specific stimulus.

## Interpretation boundary

RNA marker changes are not mature repair, functional reserve or an irreversible fate change. The current data do not establish receptor selectivity, a source-origin interaction or a causal state barrier.

## Biological problem

Does ending Fzd-supported expansion increase mature AT1 output while preserving responsive AT2 descendants in a primary AT2 context retaining alveolar identity?

## Established knowledge

Maintaining an expandable population and producing mature descendants are different functions. CHIR exposure is not equivalent to receptor-specific Fzd input.

## Repository evidence

The Nb2-derived workspace and later extension narrow the question to withdrawal, mature output and reserve. Pre-withdrawal airway-like state is a candidate competence feature, not established causation.

## Unresolved gap

Starting-state competence, input duration and input identity have not been separated using mature descendant and reserve outcomes.

## Competing explanations

Observed differences follow survival, starting population mixture, expansion duration or a generic downstream pathway effect.

## Discriminating outcomes

More mature AT1 output with a retained responsive AT2 reserve supports productive withdrawal in that context. More AT1 output with reserve depletion is a tradeoff; more area alone tests neither. Lineage-linked absolute counts address yield, independent identity/function addresses maturity, and later response capacity addresses reserve. CHIR withdrawal does not isolate Fzd5 withdrawal.

## Next investigation

Separate the three estimands in the next contract and qualify lineage, mature endpoint and reserve measurements. Use the current extension boundaries rather than reverting to a generic Wnt question.

## Experimental bridge

Compare continued versus ended receptor input after a common expansion history. Assess alveolar identity before assigning the schedule comparison. Lineage-linked counts measure absolute descendants; orthogonal identity/function measures qualify AT1 maturation; renewed response capacity tests the retained AT2 reserve. An identity-marker or surviving-cell fraction alone does not test the joint claim. Fzd5 is a candidate model choice, and CHIR cessation is not equivalent to ending a receptor-specific input.

## Interpretation boundary

Marker changes or expansion after stimulation do not demonstrate productive mature repair with retained reserve.

## Decision and maturity

A focused experimental concept exists; model, independent-unit variance and endpoint feasibility must be established before confirmation.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
