# A7: research development dossier

Does Cebpa loss attenuate AT2 identity across states or preferentially within a transitional state?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Is the effect of Cebpa loss on AT2 identity state-selective, or is there a broadly distributed identity change?

## Established knowledge

A change in the number of transitional cells can follow altered entry, survival or annotation. It does not by itself identify a state-specific genetic effect.

## Repository evidence

Existing single-well observations are descriptive. They cannot estimate a replicated genotype-by-state interaction or disentangle independent preparation from cell-level variation.

Evidence locators (source citations and numerical details remain in these records):

- [Research Article/gate2_C2_england_2025/README.md](../../Research%20Article/gate2_C2_england_2025/README.md)

## Unresolved gap

The interaction between genotype and independently defined starting state remains unmeasured at the biological-unit level.

## Working hypothesis

Cebpa loss changes AT2 identity across multiple starting states rather than exclusively within the transitional compartment.

## Competing explanations

A particular state is selectively affected, or differential survival and circular state labeling explain the apparent broad effect.

## Discriminating outcomes

Replicated within-state genotype effects with limited interaction favor a broad change; a reproducible interaction with survival accounted for favors state selectivity.

## Next investigation

Recover biological-unit and genotype labels, and define states independently of the identity endpoint. If replication is absent, report effect descriptions without population inference.

## Experimental bridge

A factorial genotype-by-starting-state comparison should measure identity, survival and descendant output separately across independent preparations.

## Interpretation boundary

More cells in one well cannot establish genotype replication; a reduced transitional fraction does not establish altered fate.

## Decision and maturity

Descriptive evidence only. A replicated interaction design is the next meaningful discriminator.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
