Does Cebpa loss attenuate AT2 identity across states or preferentially within a transitional state?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does CEBPA perturbation have a different consequence depending on the epithelial starting state, or does a changed reference state alter the apparent contrast?

## Established knowledge

Hassan and Chen (2024) establish CEBPA-dependent AT2 identity; Sawhney et al. (2025) further distinguish defensive and reparative programmes. The broad role of CEBPA is therefore established background, not this dossier's novelty claim.

## Repository evidence

ES1 measures context-dependent label-versus-reference RNA-panel detection at 2,000 UMIs per retained cell. The Cldn4/Krt8 call and Sftpc-conditioned reference are defined after treatment; the label-free ADI contrast differs across contexts, rather than testing an independently manipulated reference or starting state. Six CEBPA-condition wells and four separate AP-1 wells are pooled libraries, not a replicated starting-state interaction. Technical seeds and reusing these outcomes add no independent biological replication.

[Current result, design and limits](../../Research%20Article/epithelial_state_specificity/results/SUMMARY.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

Qualified pre-intervention state and independent preparation information remain missing for the interaction question. Separate perturbation series do not supply a factorial comparison by proximity.

## Working hypothesis

CEBPA loss attenuates alveolar identity across AT2 states; the additional candidate is that its effect differs between independently established starting states beyond this broad identity loss. Broad attenuation is the published premise, not the novel claim. The state interaction must be specified before genotype changes the state labels; CEBPA and AP-1 studies remain separate sources.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Published premise; baseline-state extension unresolved. CEBPA-dependent AT2 identity is established. Only a qualified baseline-state interaction and its consequence could extend that result; post-treatment state stratification does not isolate it. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a7). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Reference selection, treatment-induced identity change, composition and source/preparation differences can alter the apparent contrast without a baseline-state interaction.

## Discriminating outcomes

A reproducible effect difference across independently defined starting states would support an interaction. Dependence on the post-treatment reference would restrict the interaction interpretation; it would not establish that the reference change is purely technical, since it may also reflect biological identity change. Post-treatment labels alone cannot decide between those accounts.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Keep the conditional panel contrasts and reference-selection limitation as results. A future state-dependent effect comparison needs a defensible starting-state measurement and units appropriate to that question. A reproducible interaction would support state dependence; an effect explained by a changed reference would limit it. No additional post-treatment relabelling can recover the missing starting history.

## Experimental bridge

A future genotype-by-starting-state comparison needs independent preparations, a justified pre-intervention state definition, separate identity and survival outcomes, and later descendant output if a fate claim is intended.

## Interpretation boundary

Useful measurement and design correction. The evidence has not selected a selectively vulnerable starting population or demonstrated that initial competence determines response. The distinction is worth testing, but it was not established by the reference-dependent RNA pattern.
