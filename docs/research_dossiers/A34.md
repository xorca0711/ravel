## Biological problem

Which movement and fitness responses accompany Ezh2-associated CCR2 reduction in Tregs?

## Established knowledge

[Zhan et al. (online 2018; issue 2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41423-018-0187-8), Figures 5–6, reports an inflammatory accessory-dependent CD25 relationship. The prior review inspected indexed primary Results/legends, not the full supplement. [Chorro et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-07806-6), Figure 1 and Results, provides a consequential colony/Il2ra alternative detailed in the [shared review](../../Research%20Article/gate3_09_dupage_2015/rq_derivation/README.md#literature-correction). The two papers are not treated as interchangeable or as mutually invalidating.

[Watanabe et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12974-024-03144-1), Figure 5 and Migration assay Methods, adds a tissue-context receptor/chemokine-response precedent. It supports qualifying the context but supplies no Ezh2 effect or receptor-specific necessity estimate. The current literature note distinguishes this read from the limited-access older tumor-trafficking lead.

## Repository evidence

Wang adds a positive Ccr2 point contrast with a library-omission range crossing zero, unlike the DuPage decrease. A universal Ezh2-loss-to-CCR2-reduction account is therefore unsupported across these sources. The reduction named in this RQ is a context-qualified premise to verify in the chosen model, not an established property of all Ezh2-deficient Tregs. Increased, unchanged and decreased functional responses must all be retained. [Wang S4 evidence](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/RESULTS_V1.md#s4--five-exposed-genes-across-separate-activation-contexts) · [Interpretation](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/BIOLOGICAL_INTERPRETATION_V1.md).

Wang uses four deficient and five deposited-control libraries, with a four-library +/+ sensitivity. Mouse/pool and processing assignments remain unresolved, and the supplement disagrees with deposited control labels. The five-gene panel was outcome-exposed. Its different source scales permit descriptive direction checks, not a pooled effect, population inference or independent biological replication.

DuPage KO-low Ccr2 RNA is lower, while Il2ra lacks a corresponding stable deficit. Peeters Ccr2 gain-minus-WT RNA is positive fresh and negative after culture. These observations motivate a context-specific response comparison; they do not measure migration, retention, survival, or CCR2-dependent CD25 signaling.

The source studies differ in allele, control genotype, starting population and culture. DuPage uses KO versus HET in observed CD62L states; Peeters uses Y641F versus WT, fresh or four-day cultured. Their selected expression scales and unknown animal/pool mapping permit descriptive context checks, not a matched loss/gain causal test or independent replication. The coding top-ten lists were a reading budget; the three routes were chosen after inspecting outcomes and literature, not selected as statistically validated winners.

This is an adequate question-development rationale, not evidence that this route is superior to the rest of the coding frontier. The current [DuPage result](../../Research%20Article/gate3_09_dupage_2015/CANDIDATE_ANALYSIS_RESULT_V1.md) supplies the completed measurement; the proposed functional response remains unmeasured.

## Does the evidence warrant the narrowing?

**Rationale verdict: an exploratory receptor-context lead; neither movement loss nor a CCR2-to-CD25 mechanism is selected.** The source RNA supports a qualified movement/fitness comparison. The Il2ra/background precedent makes CD25 attribution a specific uncertainty rather than a universal CCR2 consequence. It does not demonstrate that Zhan used the affected colony or invalidate its inhibitor/chimera observations.

## Unresolved gap

Separate directed movement from receptor/signaling and recovery responses in the Ezh2 context. The CD25 branch is a conditional published account to reproduce and qualify, not an established universal CCR2 mechanism. A CCR2-independent CD25 result would also be informative.

Exact primary locators and access limits are retained in the local literature context. A26–A27 concern CD8 receptor repertoires, A28 Th17 outputs, and A30 paired CSF/blood RNA. None measures directed Treg movement or separates it from the specified recovery/signaling responses. Shared lymphocyte tags do not merge these estimands.

## Working hypothesis

After common activation, Ezh2-deficient murine Tregs may show a CCR2-related directed-movement deficit, an inflammatory-context fitness deficit, both, or neither. The first comparison estimates those separate genotype-associated responses; attribution to CCR2 requires a target-restricted test and qualified genetic background.

## Competing explanations

General activation/viability differences, accessory-cell effects, or an independent Il2ra/background defect can mimic a CCR2-linked fitness result. The source RNA comparison cannot distinguish them.

## Discriminating outcomes

Movement-only loss supports a movement phenotype; signaling/recovery-only loss supports a separate fitness phenotype; both allow shared state effects or joint contributions. No resolved difference weakens a deficit prediction only when assays and precision are adequate; an increased response is a contrary result to retain. A CD25 difference attributable to Il2ra/background cannot be assigned to CCR2.

## Public-source qualification update

Loyher reports specific fluorescent CCL2 binding despite an antibody stain that did not detect Treg CCR2. Bonfiglio reports impaired movement of bulk Ezh2-deficient CD4 cells to CCL19/CCL22; this is a general movement precedent, not a CCR2-dependent Treg result. Validate CCR2 detection with a qualified specificity control; an antibody-negative result alone does not establish receptor absence. Specific ligand binding is an alternative measurement, not automatically an absolute protein count. Retain the CCR2-ligand gradient and no/equal-gradient controls, with viable input/recovery. For CCR2 attribution, assess a justified control for broader movement competence and verify its responsiveness; this is not a prerequisite for reporting a bounded genotype effect. Keep CD25/IL-2 signaling separate. The [source audit](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/public_gap_followup_20261112/G1_SOURCE_AUDIT_V1.md) records exact locators and access limits; the [review](../../Research%20Article/gate3_10_wang_2018_ezh2/PUBLIC_GAP_REVIEW_20261112.md) separates resolved planning gaps from unexecuted comparisons. No new functional estimate, independent replication or hypothesis change is claimed.

## Experimental bridge

Matched activated Ezh2-deficient and genetic-control Treg preparations in a qualified CCR2-ligand gradient, with no-gradient/equal-concentration controls. In separate aliquots, assess a qualified inflammatory accessory-cell condition and downstream IL-2 response. Do not assume purified-cell CCL2 alone induces CD25.

Absolute viable cells traversing the assay and fraction of viable input; separately CD25 protein, IL-2-induced pSTAT5 and absolute recovery. Proliferation requires a division assay. None of these is a direct in-vivo homing measurement.
