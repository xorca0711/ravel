# A9: research development dossier

Does fibroblast receptor context determine the response to AREG?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does recipient receptor competence explain different fibroblast responses to comparable AREG input?

## Established knowledge

Receptor RNA, surface availability and signaling competence can differ. AREG and EGF share receptor context without being interchangeable experimental inputs.

## Repository evidence

Resource checks establish coverage of the specified receptor complex, not functional competence or a fibroblast mechanism. The canonical card preserves the original ligand-receptor question.

Evidence locators (source citations and numerical details remain in these records):

- [Research Article/gate2_N2_nabhan_2026/README.md](../../Research%20Article/gate2_N2_nabhan_2026/README.md)

## Unresolved gap

Receptor-dependent response has not been separated from ligand availability, other growth factors or recipient-state composition.

## Working hypothesis

A defined receptor-competence difference modifies fibroblast response when effective AREG input is comparable.

## Competing explanations

Recipient differences arise from other ligands, general activation, different fibroblast mixtures or ligand exposure rather than receptor competence.

## Discriminating outcomes

A response difference that tracks independently measured competence and a receptor-dependent contrast supports the hypothesis; RNA-only correlation without functional specificity does not.

## Next investigation

Establish the exact recipient population, receptor measurement and intended response endpoint. Check source medium and ligand context before selecting an interaction score.

## Experimental bridge

Compare recipient populations under interpretable ligand and receptor contrasts, with protein-level competence, viability and a defined functional output measured independently.

## Interpretation boundary

Ligand-receptor resource membership establishes neither binding in the experiment nor pathway activity or functional response.

## Decision and maturity

Mechanism nomination only. Functional receptor measurement and an interpretable input comparison are required before an experimental claim.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
