Does fibroblast receptor context determine the response to AREG?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

At comparable effective AREG input, which recipient properties explain a different response?

## Established knowledge

Receptor RNA, surface availability and signaling competence can differ. AREG and EGF share receptor context without being interchangeable experimental inputs.

## Repository evidence

The resource correction found AREG/HBEGF–EGFR_ERBB2 scoreable in only 7 of 22 donors, below the fixed 11-donor rule; the encoded complexes existed in the resource. The completed 3 November six-target screen then retained all 132 attempted within-donor recipient-label contrasts: 114 estimated in 19 paired donors and 18 unsupported. Mean per-cell log1p(full-library CP10k) EGFR and ERRFI1 are lower in Myofibroblast in every supported pair; ERBB2, ERBB3, ERBB4 and LRIG1 have mixed directions. These reused donors support an exploratory RNA-context lead, not independent validation or a response contrast at comparable effective input.

[Completed recipient RNA-context result](../../RQ_Specified/A9_areg_recipient_competence/reports/RECEPTOR_CONTEXT_V1_RESULTS.md) · [Earlier resource correction](../../analysis/corrections/ligand/RESULTS.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

A recipient definition, comparable effective exposure and a specific measured response are not jointly supplied by the current source. Receptor assembly/activity and downstream state remain unmeasured alternatives.

## Working hypothesis

Fibroblast competence to receive AREG may change its downstream response even at comparable effective AREG exposure. This asks about the recipient side of the AREG-EGFR axis, separately from A2 delivery. The relevant receptor/co-receptor or inhibitory contrast and functional response remain to be nominated; receptor RNA and database complex membership do not specify either.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Recipient contrast and functional endpoint required. Ligand-response and receptor-context biology are known. An actual competence-dependent functional contrast is required before novelty or assay readiness can be assessed. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a9). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Input availability, state mixture, resource/expression coverage and receptor or downstream regulation can produce different rankings or responses. Correcting one database interpretation does not choose among these biological explanations.

## Discriminating outcomes

Within-population receptor/feedback transcript context and segregation among recipient populations would motivate different descriptive explanations. Neither identifies receptor activity, assembly or response direction. In the separate functional question, different responses at comparable effective input could support recipient-context dependence; response differences explained by input availability would limit that interpretation.

## Next investigation

The [existing candidate comparison](../research_reviews/2026-11-03-tool-assisted-screening-review/BIOLOGICAL_LINK_SCREENING_V1.md#a9-specify-which-recipient-configuration-changes-the-hypothesis) asks whether EGFR and ERRFI1/LRIG1 transcript context occurs together within an independently identified fibroblast population or reflects different subpopulations. This follows from the lower EGFR and ERRFI1 marginal measurements without assigning a net response direction. A within-population pattern would motivate a receptor/feedback-context question; segregation among populations would shift the interpretation toward recipient composition. Neither outcome would turn RNA into surface receptor availability or effective inhibition. Feedback-associated RNA may also reflect earlier stimulation.

This is a proposed descriptive branch, not a completed co-occurrence result or a selected mechanism. It needs an interpretable population definition and transcript-detection comparison before any new run. The separate response question still asks whether recipient contexts differ at comparable effective input. A directly informative response comparison need not wait for the optional RNA branch. The 7/22 encoded-complex coverage failure and all six measured targets remain unchanged.

The concordant EGFR/ERRFI1 directions do not rank receptor activity or effective inhibition; annotation, depth and transcript detection remain alternatives. Exclusion by expression coverage does not establish homodimer signaling, receptor absence or functional competence, and correcting database absence does not select a causal configuration.
