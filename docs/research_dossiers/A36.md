## Biological problem

In the aged SVZ–olfactory-bulb lineage, does a defined transient D25K exposure increase later surviving neuronal output relative to WT IL-10 and vehicle, and how does that compare with the early progenitor response?

## Established knowledge

(2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.01.016), Fig. 5B–I, tests immune/IFN contributions; Fig. 6B–J measures broad pathways and neural-marker endpoints after one week of infusion. Fig. S6 reports clocks/PCA and DG STAT1, and Table S3 includes residual interferon-response enrichment under engineered IL-10 in combined astrocytes/qNSCs. These are published premises, not new discoveries here. The correction changes Fig. 2K/4J image presentation; quantitative analyses are reported unchanged.

Perez-Asensio 2013 (abstract) and Pereira 2015 (Figs. 4–7) support direct progenitor effects as an alternative to exclusively immune-mediated response. Sanchez-Molina 2022 (Fig. 1E–J, adult hippocampus under chronic astrocytic IL-10) separates early proliferation from reduced later newborn-cell survival. Nissen 2026 (Figs. 2–4/7) supplies a recipient IFNAR/DCX precedent in a fetal-derived human line, not aged donors or D25K. Exact DOI, access and version limits are in the linked context.

## Unresolved gap

The organizing gap is later surviving birthdated neuronal output after documented transient exposure. Existing one-week marker snapshots do not supply that comparison. An upstream broad-bin representation/within-bin RNA decomposition can refine population and measurement choice, but cannot identify purified NSCs or explain survival causally. Broad IFN enrichment is already reported and will not be rediscovered as a new mechanism.

## Working hypothesis

After a defined transient exposure in aged SVZ–olfactory-bulb lineage, D25K may increase later surviving birthdated neuronal output relative to WT IL-10 and vehicle in a way not adequately described by early progenitor abundance alone. Similar later output with both ligands, an early-only response, differential survival and direct progenitor effects remain alternatives; they can coexist.

## Competing explanations

Common ligand response, early expansion without persistence, altered labeling/capture and direct neural effects can all dissociate early response from later output. In RNA, library/pool variation and composition within broad annotations can mimic response differences. State representation is potentially meaningful, not automatically nuisance. None of these explanations demands an extra experimental group unless the intended inference requires it.

## Discriminating outcomes

A sustained direct D25K advantage supports the bounded later-output comparison without proving mediation. Similar later gains favor common ligand benefit. An early-only change redirects attention to persistence/maturation/exposure while retaining early biology. Mixed or imprecise outcomes remain visible. Upstream decomposition can narrow a compartment/readout or weaken a uniform recipient-response story; it cannot substitute for the later endpoint.

## Interpretation boundary

Current RNA estimates finite condition-pool measurements, without cell-resampled population P values or inferred animals. Within-bin does not mean within a purified state. RNA does not measure fate, secretion, IFN type, survival, maturation or repair. Age modification requires matched young/old treatment contrasts; young vehicle alone is insufficient. An aged-context treatment effect does not require a factorial age design. Physiological aging is not a measured lesion.
