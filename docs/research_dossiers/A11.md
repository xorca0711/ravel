Which lesion-associated programmes add to a shared epithelial plasticity component?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does the fixed lesion-associated RNA component add distinct information beyond specified shared developmental, injury and stress features?

## Established knowledge

Lesion-associated transcription can overlap ordinary injury and developmental plasticity. Association with a lesion does not establish malignancy or ancestry.

## Repository evidence

The eight-patient lesion association is supported, while the original beyond-shared criterion remains unresolved (q=0.0547). A later paired model lowers mean log loss from 0.226980 to 0.191987 nats after adding the fixed component. Duplicating an existing p53 feature lowers it to 0.190882 without adding information. That control is a model diagnostic, not another biological cohort.

The follow-up and capacity amendment are outcome-exposed reuse of the same eight patients. Training excludes the held-out patient, but this does not make the earlier feature-discovery history untouched. Both original models already order all eight pairs correctly: the gain concerns confidence/log loss, not newly correct pair ordering. Normal-AT2 versus author tumour-epithelial composition remains an alternative.

[Current result, design and limits](../portfolio_extensions/2026-10-07/g1_epithelial/RESULTS.md#a11-lower-log-loss-does-not-establish-distinct-residual-information). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

Distinct lesion-context information and a functional mediator remain unresolved. The fixed shared/stress comparisons do not exhaust all plasticity, composition or measurement explanations.

## Working hypothesis

The frozen lesion-associated residual component may contain reproducible lesion-context information beyond the specified shared developmental/injury and stress explanations. It is a set-defined measurement, not an identified cancer-specific state or mechanism. The paired-patient beyond-shared criterion remains unresolved, and no lesion-relevant functional mediator is nominated.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Measurement contribution unresolved. HPCS, regenerative overlap and stress-associated plasticity have close published precedents. A disjoint gene list alone is not novelty; the exact incremental information and biological value remain unresolved. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a11). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Shared stress/plasticity, composition and baseline/model-capacity effects can explain an apparent lesion-specific increment. A named component is not itself additional biological information.

## Discriminating outcomes

An increment that persists with a justified baseline and matched model capacity could support bounded additional information. Comparable improvement from duplicate-feature controls would restrict that interpretation. The current control exhibits the latter problem.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Keep the association and adverse capacity control together. A future information comparison needs a justified baseline and matched model capacity with outcome exposure retained. A gain persisting under that design could support bounded incremental information; gain reproduced by capacity controls would not. No new component or mechanism is selected here.

## Experimental bridge

A qualified independent lesion/non-lesion comparison with a frozen feature definition and justified model/control specification can address incremental information. A lesion-relevant functional claim is a separate question needing its own measured endpoint and composition/survival interpretation; functional evidence is not a prerequisite for the information comparison.

## Interpretation boundary

Supported association with an unfavorable information diagnostic. The apparent increment cannot establish a distinct lesion programme because altered regularization geometry can produce comparable improvement without new information. This corrects the narrowing rationale; it does not prove that p53 explains lesion biology.
