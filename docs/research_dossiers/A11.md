# A11: research development dossier

Which lesion-associated programmes add to a shared epithelial plasticity component?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which lesion-associated component exceeds shared epithelial plasticity and explains a specific lesion-relevant function?

## Established knowledge

Lesion-associated transcription can overlap ordinary injury and developmental plasticity. Association with a lesion does not establish malignancy or ancestry.

## Repository evidence

Lesion association replicated across eight patients, but the beyond-shared test remained unresolved at q = 0.0547. The three-donor acute-injury comparison did not establish injury specificity.

## Unresolved gap

The evidence does not yet identify a component with robust contextual specificity and an independently measured biological consequence.

## Working hypothesis

A bounded component adds lesion-context information beyond shared plasticity and relates to a specified function.

## Competing explanations

The signal is generic stress/plasticity, composition or uncertain baseline adjustment rather than a lesion-specific programme.

## Discriminating outcomes

Incremental information surviving an appropriate injury comparator favors specificity; equal recruitment in matched injury states favors shared plasticity.

## Next investigation

Preserve the unresolved adjusted test. Qualify a comparator and an independent endpoint before narrowing the component; do not tune away the near-threshold result.

## Experimental bridge

A future comparison should measure the nominated lesion-relevant function alongside matched non-lesion plasticity, with survival and starting-state composition accounted for.

## Interpretation boundary

A lesion score cannot establish malignant identity, transformation, lineage origin or clinical risk.

## Decision and maturity

Contextual association is supported more strongly than specificity. Functional development remains open without claiming the failed discriminator succeeded.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
