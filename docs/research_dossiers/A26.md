## Biological problem

Within comparable CD8 differentiation states and the same animals, does the age contrast in repertoire concentration differ between spleen and bone marrow, suggesting a tissue-context association beyond a shared systemic shift?

## Established knowledge

Organ-dependent immune ageing is established. Shifrut et al. 2013 (doi:10.3389/fimmu.2013.00379) already report spleen/marrow differences for CD4 repertoires, and Mogilenko et al. 2021 study multi-organ immune ageing. A26 asks about a specified CD8 context; CD4 findings are a precedent, not its answer.

A closer precedent is [Mark et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2022.939394): its uninfected comparison already includes CD8 differentiation subsets, spleen/marrow and age. The earlier CD4-only boundary is insufficient. The proposed older-age, common-state concentration estimand needs explicit comparison with that paper before any stronger contribution claim; see the [source update](literature_context_2026-10-03/SOURCE_UPDATE.md).

## Repository evidence

Nb5 broad T-cell results differ across spleen, marrow and thymus. Tissue-specific common depths preclude direct ranking. The later exact CD4/CD8 follow-up supports spleen only; absent exact marrow CD8 support remains a hold. This proposed spleen/marrow contrast was chosen after exposure to those results, not preregistered.

## Unresolved gap

Paired animals with comparable spleen/marrow CD8 states, receptor ascertainment and common observation support have not been qualified. The local failure of exact subtype support cannot be repaired by treating an absent label as zero or relaxing the historical gate.

## Working hypothesis

At a fixed, independently defined CD8 state and comparable sampling scale, the older-versus-younger change in local clone concentration differs between spleen and marrow. This is a tissue-context association hypothesis, not evidence that a particular local niche causes expansion.

## Competing explanations

Systemic age effects plus differing differentiation states, recirculating populations and receptor recovery explain the apparent organ difference. A few highly sampled clones or animal-selection differences can produce the same pattern.

## Discriminating outcomes

The target is an age contrast of within-animal spleen-minus-marrow differences at a common state and observation scale. A reproducible tissue-dependent contrast supports context dependence; compatible contrasts support a shared association only with useful precision. Cross-tissue receptor sharing alone does not prove movement or local proliferation.

## Next investigation

Qualify paired animal identities, common CD8 states and receptor denominators in a new source before any model. Treat 3-versus-24 months as the proposed initial contrast and the middle-age comparison as separately declared secondary work; this proposal remains exposed and can be revised prospectively before a frozen design.

## Experimental bridge

Independently sampled, paired-organ material with equivalent population definitions can distinguish tissue associations. A local-niche mechanism would require a separately justified functional comparison. Independent animals, not organs, sequences or cells, determine replication and precision.

## Interpretation boundary

No tissue-local concentration estimate establishes migration direction, growth rate, organ residency or a systemic/local causal mechanism. Missing exact marrow support remains explicit. Shared animals across organs are pairing, not extra biological replication.

## Decision and maturity

Registered as a proposed question with a held source-design gate. A tissue-context focus warrants separate tracking under the owner's heterogeneity instruction; its registration does not make the existing subtype data eligible or certify a novel mechanism.

## Relationship to existing questions

Nb4-P04 tests shared lung-versus-blood RNA programmes across immune lineages. A26 tests receptor-defined CD8 concentration across spleen/marrow with age. A27 instead asks whether within-spleen age changes reflect state redistribution or within-state concentration. Related targets can share evidence without universally covering one another.

P07/P08 are possible sex/age design modifiers. A26 shares source provenance with A27, so reuse must never be described as independent replication.
