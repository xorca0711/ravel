## Biological problem

Within the same animals and comparable CD8 states, does the age contrast in repertoire concentration differ between spleen and bone marrow?

## Established knowledge

Organ-dependent immune ageing is established. Shifrut et al. 2013 (doi:10.3389/fimmu.2013.00379) already report spleen/marrow differences for CD4 repertoires, and Mogilenko et al. 2021 study multi-organ immune ageing. A26 asks about a specified CD8 context; CD4 findings are a precedent, not its answer.

A closer precedent is [Mark et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2022.939394): its uninfected comparison already includes CD8 differentiation subsets, spleen/marrow and age. The earlier CD4-only boundary is insufficient. The proposed older-age, common-state concentration estimand needs explicit comparison with that paper before any stronger contribution claim; see the [source update](literature_context_2026-10-03/SOURCE_UPDATE.md).

## Repository evidence

Missing qualifying labels are not zero CD8 cells, and different tissue-specific rarefaction depths cannot be ranked as a tissue effect. ## Unresolved gap

Paired-organ animal identity, independently comparable state definition and receptor recovery support remain insufficient for the intended comparison.

## Working hypothesis

At a fixed, independently defined CD8 state and comparable sampling scale, the older-versus-younger change in local clone concentration differs between spleen and marrow. This is a tissue-context association hypothesis, not evidence that a particular local niche causes expansion.

## Competing explanations

State mixture, unequal recovery, technical depth and unpaired animal/cohort differences can mimic a tissue-context association. An absent label cannot be imputed as biological absence.

## Discriminating outcomes

An age contrast differing by tissue within the same animals and comparable state/receptor support would support a contextual association. Disappearance after those qualifications would restrict it. Missing labels and unequal technical depth cannot be interpreted as either outcome.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Reopen only for a source supporting the intended paired state/receptor comparison. A reproducible tissue difference at comparable ascertainment would support a contextual association; disappearance with state or recovery matching would restrict it. Lowering the old floor or borrowing A27's codes cannot create the missing pairing.

## Experimental bridge

Independently sampled, paired-organ material with equivalent population definitions can distinguish tissue associations. A local-niche mechanism would require a separately justified functional comparison. Independent animals, not organs, sequences or cells, determine replication and precision.

## Interpretation boundary

A well-defined but currently unestimated tissue comparison. Source ineligibility is an observation about the proposed analysis, not evidence of absent tissue-specific biology or a negative age interaction.
