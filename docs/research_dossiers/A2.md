## Biological problem

Does fibroblast response to AREG depend on delivery context, ligand availability, or recipient competence?

## Established knowledge

Ligand RNA, delivered ligand and receptor response are different measurements. Abundance and delivery mechanisms can operate together.

## Repository evidence

Read the 27 September interpretation audit before the earlier stage reports. The corrected RNA association is inconclusive, not evidence that AREG supply is absent or irrelevant. Source Itgb6 observations, EGF-containing medium and common-preparation split wells restrict attribution.

## Unresolved gap

The source data do not separate effective ligand exposure from recipient competence, common-preparation effects or other growth-factor input.

## Working hypothesis

Epithelial AREG delivery can change the effective ligand reaching fibroblasts and thereby their response, even when epithelial AREG RNA or source-cell abundance is similar. The biological contrast is delivery through effective availability versus a recipient-context effect. The source/presentation comparison and downstream fibroblast function remain unnominated, so this is a developing hypothesis, not a specified wet experiment.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Delivery contrast and functional endpoint required. AREG/EGFR support and recipient-dependent ligand responses are precedents. A new contribution requires a justified delivery contrast and an actual recipient consequence; another source-RNA association is redundant. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a2). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Differences arise from total ligand exposure, recipient composition, EGF-containing medium or shared preparation and split-well effects.

## Discriminating outcomes

Measure available AREG and the nominated fibroblast response separately. A response change following altered availability is compatible with delivery through supply; equalizing availability can remove that route. A remaining response difference points to recipient competence or other inputs, not automatically a delivery-specific mechanism.

## Next investigation

Document the source preparation hierarchy, medium and measured ligand availability. If these cannot be recovered, retain a descriptive result and define the missing comparison explicitly.

## Experimental bridge

Compare delivery contexts in a recipient model with independently measured ligand availability and a functional response. Count independent preparations separately from technical wells; protein and activity measurements are needed.

## Interpretation boundary

Areg RNA is not ligand delivery; an integrin association does not establish an AREG delivery mechanism.

## Decision and maturity

Reframe around separable supply, delivery and recipient explanations. Current analyses support nomination, not a mechanism or a preferred intervention.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
