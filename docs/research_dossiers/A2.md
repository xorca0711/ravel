Does the fibroblast response to AREG depend on delivery or on abundance?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which cells contribute AREG-related input, and does effective delivery to a recipient explain more than ligand RNA abundance alone?

## Established knowledge

Ligand RNA, delivered ligand and receptor response are different measurements. Abundance and delivery mechanisms can operate together.

## Repository evidence

Source and database audits corrected specific label, coverage and interpretation errors. The depth-standardized machinery/activation RNA association is positive but inconclusive across 22 donors (rho 0.2931, nominal p 0.185637); it neither establishes zero coupling nor favors delivery over abundance. Partial source perturbation, recipient ligand expression, supplemented EGF and split wells do not isolate effective delivery in the existing sources.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A2_areg_source_delivery/reports/INTERPRETATION_AUDIT_2026-09-27.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

The existing evidence lacks a qualified effective-input/recipient-response contrast. Source RNA, active ligand amount and receiver competence are different endpoints; the last is developed separately in A9.

## Working hypothesis

Epithelial AREG delivery can change the effective ligand reaching fibroblasts and thereby their response, even when epithelial AREG RNA or source-cell abundance is similar. The biological contrast is delivery through effective availability versus a recipient-context effect. The source/presentation comparison and downstream fibroblast function remain unnominated, so this is a developing hypothesis, not a specified wet experiment.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Delivery contrast and functional endpoint required. AREG/EGFR support and recipient-dependent ligand responses are precedents. A new contribution requires a justified delivery contrast and an actual recipient consequence; another source-RNA association is redundant. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a2). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Ligand availability, recipient competence, source mixture, culture supplementation and measurement coverage can alter the apparent association. Database coverage and expression eligibility must be distinguished from actual receptor usage.

## Discriminating outcomes

Responses tracking measured available ligand would support a delivery account in that context. Different recipient responses at comparable effective input would motivate receiver dependence. An inconclusive ligand-RNA association alone chooses neither explanation.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Use the existing source audit to specify what an informative delivery comparison would measure. Differences in response tracking available input would support that route in its context; comparable input with different receiver responses would point to a recipient question. Another ligand ranking would not supply either measurement. No delivery mechanism is selected from the current positive but inconclusive association.

## Experimental bridge

Compare delivery contexts in a recipient model with independently measured ligand availability and a functional response. Count independent preparations separately from technical wells; protein and activity measurements are needed.

## Interpretation boundary

Useful source/measurement correction; the delivery explanation remains untested. Failure to establish an RNA association does not select availability or delivery as the mechanism. This distinction prevents a plausible biological explanation from being presented as the winner of an inconclusive screen.
