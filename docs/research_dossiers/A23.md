Does SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis constrain entry into a transition-associated state?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does SLC34A2 impairment increase entry into a transition-associated state, or change persistence and exit of cells already in that state?

## Established knowledge

[Lv 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37139431/), primary full-text Results 3.2 and Fig. 3–4 captions, reports a SLC34A2/phosphate-linked repair mechanism. [Uehara 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36810-8) establishes phosphate/mineral consequences. Nabhan 2026, PDF p. 6 and Fig. S7F, already reports a Slc34a2-loss transitional module. The exact entry-versus-exit discriminator remains a provisional gap, not a demonstrated novelty result; Lv supplement and visual figure inspection were not performed in this bounded text review.

## Repository evidence

The external comparison of one PAM case with one adult control does not reproduce coordinated KRT8/SPRR1A/CLU elevation. Separate fractions of the PAM subject are not independent patients. SLC20A1/SLC20A2 RNA is lower across overlapping selections, so an increased-transporter-RNA explanation is unsupported; compensatory transport activity remains untested. Disease, donor, age and sampling are inseparable in the case comparison.

The original candidate markers were disjoint from the reported readouts; published-annotation and complete-cell-set sensitivities reuse the same case and can retain identity-selection bias. The alternative-transporter readouts were fixed before inspecting their values, after exposure to the preceding case results. These are overlapping sensitivity/extension observations, not independent confirmations. See the [transporter result](../../RQ_Specified/A23_slc34a2_transition_homeostasis/reports/transporter_context_v1/RESULTS.md).

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A23_slc34a2_transition_homeostasis/reports/external_pilot_v2/RESULTS.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

The current contrasts do not measure entry, residence time, exit or transport function in linked units. Relabelled scores and pseudotime cannot supply the missing dynamic endpoint.

## Working hypothesis

SLC34A2 transport impairment may increase acquisition of a transition-associated epithelial stress state before broad AT2 identity loss, rather than merely prolong an existing intermediate state by impairing exit to AT1 maturation. This entry-versus-residence distinction narrows the original proposal. Phosphate-dependent repair failure and the Slc34a2-loss transitional RNA module already have published precedents; the mechanism cannot be claimed new from their combination.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Narrowed entry-versus-exit question; novelty unresolved. Lv already links SLC34A2/intracellular phosphate to AT2 self-renewal and AT1 differentiation; Nabhan reports the transitional module, and Uehara establishes mineral-context biology. The inspected Lv endpoints do not resolve the proposed entry-versus-exit comparison; its novelty and biological value remain provisional. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a23). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Delayed maturation, selective survival/division, generalized injury and mineral context can alter occupancy without increased entry. One case/control comparison cannot establish population-level absence of the source effect.

## Discriminating outcomes

Increased entry with unchanged exit and prolonged residence without increased entry have different biological implications. Endpoint occupancy without unit-linked timing cannot distinguish them. A single external case/control marker comparison neither resolves timing nor establishes a population-level null.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Keep the external failure and compensation limit visible. A timing comparison would need unit-linked repeated or lineage-resolved measurements appropriate to entry versus exit. Increased entry with unchanged exit and prolonged residence without increased entry would have different implications; endpoint occupancy alone leaves the question open. No same-data retuning follows automatically.

## Experimental bridge

In a source-qualified primary AT2 context, link starting lineages to acquisition and exit from an independently qualified intermediate state. Identity measurements define the state; lineage/count observations distinguish entry, persistence, division and loss; compartment-specific handling measurements test transport, not transporter RNA. A separately qualified restoration comparison tests dependence on handling. None supplies a model, assay calibration, meaningful effect or independent-unit variance that is currently unknown.

## Interpretation boundary

Unfavorable external marker transfer and an unsupported RNA-surrogate compensation account. Entry versus delayed exit is a legitimate remaining distinction, not an explanation selected by static RNA observations. Endpoint fractions can change through survival, division and starting composition as well as transition timing.
