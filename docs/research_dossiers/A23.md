# A23: research development dossier

Does SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis constrain entry into a transition-associated state?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does SLC34A2-dependent phosphate handling affect transition entry directly, or through extracellular injury and loss of cell homeostasis?

## Established knowledge

SLC34A2 biology and pulmonary alveolar microlithiasis connect phosphate handling with tissue pathology, but chronic disease context does not isolate an acute cell-intrinsic transition mechanism.

## Repository evidence

The screen showed a +0.524 transition-marker contrast with three concordant markers. The external PAM case did not show coordinated marker increase; SLC20A1/2 RNA was lower, providing no transcriptional compensation evidence.

## Unresolved gap

Transport activity, timing and intracellular versus extracellular consequences remain unmeasured. The external negative result constrains transfer of the original interpretation.

## Working hypothesis

Altered SLC34A2-dependent homeostasis changes transition entry through a defined phosphate-handling process in the relevant epithelial context.

## Competing explanations

General injury, extracellular mineral context, survival or starting-state composition accounts for the marker change.

## Discriminating outcomes

A transition difference linked to independently measured handling and dissociated from general injury favors the hypothesis; an injury-only or context-specific effect favors the rival.

## Next investigation

Preserve the external mismatch and qualify activity and temporal measurements. Do not introduce unmeasured compensation as an automatic explanation for every negative result.

## Experimental bridge

A later comparison should distinguish transport/homeostasis, extracellular injury, survival and transition entry with independent endpoints; a restoration contrast is informative only if its biological specificity is validated.

## Interpretation boundary

Three markers are not a fate transition, RNA is not phosphate flux, and absence of a marker pattern is not absence of every identity change.

## Decision and maturity

A candidate screen observation with limited external transfer. Functional and temporal evidence must precede a causal experimental claim.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.

## Primary-source and feasibility review

Phosphate homeostasis and extracellular injury have published precedents. The negative external marker result stands; flux, timing and compartment attribution remain unresolved.
