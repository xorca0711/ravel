Does SLC34A2-dependent phosphate homeostasis constrain entry into a transition-associated state?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does SLC34A2 transport impairment increase acquisition of a transition-associated state, or increase its occupancy by delaying exit toward AT1 maturation?

## Established knowledge

[Lv 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37139431/), primary full-text Results 3.2 and Fig. 3–4 captions, reports a SLC34A2/phosphate-linked repair mechanism. [Uehara 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36810-8) establishes phosphate/mineral consequences. Nabhan 2026, PDF p. 6 and Fig. S7F, already reports a Slc34a2-loss transitional module. The exact entry-versus-exit discriminator remains a provisional gap, not a demonstrated novelty result; Lv supplement and visual figure inspection were not performed in this bounded text review.

## Repository evidence

The screen showed a +0.524 transition-marker contrast with three concordant markers. The external PAM case did not show coordinated marker increase; SLC20A1/2 RNA was lower, providing no transcriptional compensation evidence.

## Unresolved gap

The repository lacks linked state acquisition and exit observations with direct transport and compartment-specific phosphate measurements. Phosphate-linked repair failure is published; whether the proposed transition-step comparison adds a worthwhile contribution remains provisional. The external negative result constrains transfer.

## Working hypothesis

SLC34A2 transport impairment may increase acquisition of a transition-associated epithelial stress state before broad AT2 identity loss, rather than merely prolong an existing intermediate state by impairing exit to AT1 maturation. This entry-versus-residence distinction narrows the original proposal. Phosphate-dependent repair failure and the Slc34a2-loss transitional RNA module already have published precedents; the mechanism cannot be claimed new from their combination.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Narrowed entry-versus-exit question; novelty unresolved. Lv already links SLC34A2/intracellular phosphate to AT2 self-renewal and AT1 differentiation; Nabhan reports the transitional module, and Uehara establishes mineral-context biology. The inspected Lv endpoints do not resolve the proposed entry-versus-exit comparison; its novelty and biological value remain provisional. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a23). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

An existing intermediate state persists because exit/maturation is impaired; generalized injury, extracellular mineral context, survival, division or starting-state composition changes endpoint abundance without increased state entry.

## Discriminating outcomes

Lineage-linked acquisition and subsequent exit measured separately can distinguish increased entry from prolonged residence; endpoint state abundance cannot. Measure transport function, compartment-specific phosphate, injury and viability independently. A qualified restoration contrast can test transport dependence, but rescue of counts alone cannot identify the affected transition step. Preserve the external negative marker result.

## Next investigation

Preserve the external mismatch and qualify activity and temporal measurements. Do not introduce unmeasured compensation as an automatic explanation for every negative result.

## Experimental bridge

In a source-qualified primary AT2 context, link starting lineages to acquisition and exit from an independently qualified intermediate state. Identity measurements define the state; lineage/count observations distinguish entry, persistence, division and loss; compartment-specific handling measurements test transport, not transporter RNA. A separately qualified restoration comparison tests dependence on handling. None supplies a model, assay calibration, meaningful effect or independent-unit variance that is currently unknown.

## Interpretation boundary

Three markers are not a fate transition, RNA is not phosphate flux, and absence of a marker pattern is not absence of every identity change.

## Decision and maturity

A candidate screen observation with limited external transfer. Functional and temporal evidence must precede a causal experimental claim.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.
