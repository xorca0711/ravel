Does a maturation-specific programme add information about mature AT1 contribution beyond shared transition?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does an early maturation-related component predict later mature AT1 contribution beyond a generic transitional state?

## Established knowledge

Transition-associated and mature-state expression can overlap. Mature contribution requires an independent later endpoint rather than relabeling a contemporaneous score.

## Repository evidence

The 119-gene overlap analysis is a measurement diagnostic, not a fate test. Nb4 also shows that MYRF expression is not specific to AT1 because mesothelium expresses it.

Evidence locators (source citations and numerical details remain in these records):

- [RQ_Specified/A8_maturation_component_at1_contribution/README.md](../../RQ_Specified/A8_maturation_component_at1_contribution/README.md)

## Unresolved gap

A linked early predictor and later mature AT1 outcome, with a baseline transition comparator, is missing.

## Working hypothesis

A bounded component adds reproducible information about mature AT1 output after accounting for the early shared transition component.

## Competing explanations

The component is an identity label, generic injury response, compositional artifact or information leaked from the later endpoint.

## Discriminating outcomes

Incremental performance on held-out biological units with a temporally independent outcome favors the hypothesis; no increment or instability across sources favors the rival.

## Next investigation

Qualify outcome linkage and timing before fitting. Fix the comparator and prevent outcome-derived feature selection; stop if only simultaneous RNA scores are available.

## Experimental bridge

Track comparable starting cells to independently verified mature descendants, separating mature yield from survival and expansion. Functional maturation requires an assay beyond the candidate RNA panel.

## Interpretation boundary

Score separation is not mature AT1 contribution; MYRF alone cannot serve as a cell-specific maturation endpoint.

## Decision and maturity

Measurement development is useful, but prediction and the experimental bridge remain gated on an independent outcome.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.

## Primary-source and feasibility review

Published transitional trajectories do not supply individual early-to-later linkage. Qualify linked units and an independent mature-output endpoint before a predictive contract.
