Does a maturation-specific programme add information about mature AT1 contribution beyond shared transition?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does an early epithelial RNA component add information about later mature AT1 contribution, beyond a concurrent transitional-marker description?

## Established knowledge

Transition-associated and mature-state expression can overlap. Mature contribution requires an independent later endpoint rather than relabeling a contemporaneous score.

## Repository evidence

The completed exposed-source attribution retains 35 original units: ten pooled multiome libraries and 25 external animals. Of 140 unit/seed/panel contrasts, 34 qualify and 106 remain unsupported. Seeds are technical sensitivity, not independent replicates. The endpoint is the equal-member mean detection difference in percentage points between Cldn4/Krt8-labelled and Sftpc-reference cells at the original 2,000-UMI budget. Contributions are heterogeneous; some late-panel totals change sign across seeds, including the sole external animal eligible at both seeds. Those changes describe a fixed-budget detection measurement, not later AT1 output.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A8_maturation_component_at1_contribution/reports/PANEL_ATTRIBUTION_V1_RESULTS.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

The early measurement and later mature output are not linked in the required biological units. Pooled libraries, external animals and repeated technical seeds cannot be pooled as equivalent independent replicates.

## Working hypothesis

An early, frozen maturation-associated RNA component may identify AT2 descendants that later contribute mature AT1 cells beyond information in the shared transition component. The additional claim is prospective information about independent later mature output, not that the score is fate or that one marker causes maturation. The component remains unchanged and the early-to-later biological-unit link is still missing.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Prediction question; linked endpoint held. Transition and AT1 differentiation programmes are established. The fixed component needs an independently linked incremental prediction test; a differently named score is not biological novelty. See the [source comparison](../history/reviews/NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a8). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Gene overlap, depth/subsampling, selected markers, cell-state mixture and concurrent differentiation context can explain the score without prospective maturation information.

## Discriminating outcomes

Incremental information from a fixed early component about linked later output would support bounded prediction. A signal limited to concurrent markers, panel membership or technical subsampling would constrain that interpretation. The present attribution measures the latter features, not future output.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Keep every supported and unsupported panel contrast and the technical sign changes. A predictive question needs an eligible early-to-later endpoint link and an explicit fixed component. Improvement on an appropriate earlier-state baseline would support bounded prediction; a signal confined to concurrent markers or technical choices would restrict it. More same-source scoring does not supply the missing later outcome.

## Experimental bridge

Track comparable starting cells to independently verified mature descendants, separating mature yield from survival and expansion. Functional maturation requires an assay beyond the candidate RNA panel.

## Interpretation boundary

A useful panel/measurement diagnostic rather than selection of a maturation driver. The results limit interpretation of small-panel totals as coordinated maturation. Neither a favorable panel score nor a technically stable member has been validated as an early predictor of later contribution.
