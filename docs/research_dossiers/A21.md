# A21: research development dossier

Does Fzd4-dependent vascular integrity support gCap self-renewal and aerocyte production during alveolar repair?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Does Fzd4-dependent vascular integrity change gCap renewal and aerocyte production during alveolar repair?

## Established knowledge

Vascular barrier integrity, endothelial survival and lineage output are distinct. Tumor vascular restoration is contextual evidence, not proof of adult alveolar lineage behavior.

## Repository evidence

Fzd4 RNA is lower in the transitional state in the current analysis. The workspace and extension do not provide direct lineage or functional vascular evidence for the proposed coupling.

## Unresolved gap

A relationship between receptor-dependent integrity and alveolar endothelial renewal has not been distinguished from state composition or endothelial loss.

## Working hypothesis

Fzd4-dependent integrity affects gCap self-renewal and subsequent aerocyte output beyond effects on endothelial survival.

## Competing explanations

Fzd4 marks a stable endothelial state; barrier change and lineage output are parallel consequences of injury or altered composition.

## Discriminating outcomes

A lineage-output difference with integrity and survival independently measured supports coupling; RNA-only state changes or output fully explained by loss favor the rival.

## Next investigation

Qualify adult alveolar model relevance, receptor attribution and lineage readouts before interpreting cross-context vascular studies as mechanism evidence.

## Experimental bridge

Use distinct measurements of vascular function, survival, retained gCap and aerocyte descendants. An interpretable receptor contrast is necessary to connect these outcomes causally.

## Interpretation boundary

Lower transitional Fzd4 RNA does not establish reduced signaling, lineage restriction or a vascular repair mechanism.

## Decision and maturity

Functional and lineage evidence are missing. The question stays open while model relevance and measurements are assessed.

Novelty remains provisional: review primary literature around the actual discriminator before calling this a new contribution. Assay validity, meaningful effect size, biological-unit variance and model feasibility must be established before a confirmatory experiment.

## Primary-source and feasibility review

gCap progenitor and aerocyte roles are established. Fzd4-dependent vascular function must be connected to lineage outcomes; expression transport alone cannot do that.
