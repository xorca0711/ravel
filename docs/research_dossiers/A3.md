Does prior injury leave a macrophage programme that differs from normal aging?

Status: proposed refinement, 3 October 2026. This is a development aid, not scientific acceptance or an experimental protocol.

## Biological problem

Which consequences of macrophage exposure history remain after the initial input, and which observations distinguish retained regulatory context from current stimulation or a different cell mixture?

## Established knowledge

Age, tissue processing and macrophage composition can change measured programmes. Persistence after injury must be distinguished from ongoing environmental stimulation.

## Repository evidence

The reviewed separate BMDM source contains six libraries: two source controls, two initial IL4 and two post-IL4. Egr2/Irf4 post values exceed control but remain well below initial exposure. Pparg post values lie within the control range, Stat6 is lower, and Rxra has a higher-post context. Arg1 is much lower post than initially while remaining above low control values. The readout is deposited-count CPM with all 24,411 genes in the denominator, retaining Salmon-derived count/estimate semantics.

[Current result, design and limits](../../RQ_Specified/A3_macrophage_injury_history/reports/RNA_CONTEXT_DISPLAY_V1_RESULTS.md). Existing reports retain their evidence authority; this synthesis runs no new analysis.

## Unresolved gap

Preparation/batch sharing and time-matched control harvest remain unresolved. Library suffixes do not establish pairing or six independent animals. This source is distinct from the original NAM context and does not resolve same-cell RNA–ATAC coupling or a withdrawal effect.

## Working hypothesis

Prior lung injury may leave a persistent macrophage programme within comparable macrophage origins and states after the current environment has resolved. The additional question concerns history beyond age, origin and continuing tissue stimulation. A programme and independent functional consequence still need nomination; late sampling alone cannot demonstrate biological memory.

**Specificity/novelty disposition:** Programme and consequence required. Macrophage history, origin and age effects are already reported. A narrower wording or adjusted late-state score alone does not establish a new functional contribution. See the [source comparison](NOVELTY_SPECIFICITY_APPLICATION_2026-10-03.md#a3). This is a proposed research scope, not a claim-grade or retain/reject decision.

## Competing explanations

Continued or residual input, harvest timing, culture/state composition and RNA/protein differences can explain the context. Accessibility and transcription in unmatched cell populations need not agree even without regulatory memory.

## Discriminating outcomes

A transcript pattern that persists with verified cessation and time-matched controls would support a temporal RNA observation. Dependence on harvest timing, current input or composition would restrict it. Neither outcome alone identifies the regulator maintaining the state or an ARG1-mediated consequence.

## Next investigation

Use the result and rationale assessment to decide whether another comparison is justified. Use the existing temporal/source qualification to decide what a matched post-exposure contrast could estimate. Consistent transcript persistence after qualified cessation would narrow a temporal explanation; dependence on current input, timing or composition would restrict it. Regulator activity and effector consequences are separate questions. No additional unlinked enrichment score or intervention is selected by this display.

## Experimental bridge

A later experiment would distinguish prior history from present environment using comparable macrophage populations and an independently measured function. Persistent RNA alone cannot establish intrinsic memory.

## Interpretation boundary

Exploratory transcript-context narrowing, not identification of a persistence-maintaining regulator. The measured regulator RNAs are not uniformly maintained in these libraries. This does not test a requirement of the mechanistic axis or exclude sequential regulation; regulator activity is unmeasured. Egr2/Irf4 are source-specific leads after prior outcome exposure; annotations and repeated use of these libraries do not independently validate maintenance, activity or an ARG1 mechanism.
