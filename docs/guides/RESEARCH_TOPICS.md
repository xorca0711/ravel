# Biological topic navigation

This is the shared topic map for all **36 RQ homes and 21 paper packages**.
Tags describe the question or package scope, including proposed and unresolved
links. They do not mean that a mechanism is established, an analysis is complete,
or a question has priority. A question can belong to several topics while keeping
one stable folder and one A-series identity. Tissue-specific labels remain in
the owning pages; common tags do not equate biological systems.

Use [RQ findings and current arguments](../../RQ_Specified/README.md#all-33-current-questions)
for scientific status and [paper reading/analysis status](../../Research%20Article/README.md)
for source work. This page introduces no status ledger or execution queue.

## Maintenance and path ownership

Maintain tag definitions and memberships here and the matching tag columns in
the RQ/article indexes together. Membership follows the current organizing
question or source package, not the sign of a result. Current findings remain
in owning reports and question arguments. No result-based folders, copied
evidence trees or new A-series IDs are created by a tag.

Frozen contracts, scripts, outputs, shared workspaces and historical source
paths stay with their existing owners. In particular, DY1 is ungated despite
its historical `gate2_DY1_...` path. See [repository structure](REPOSITORY_STRUCTURE.md)
and the [4 November architecture audit](../audits/2026-11-04-topic-navigation/README.md).
